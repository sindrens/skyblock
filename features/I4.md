# PvE, bosses and pets · id: I4 · status: Draft · version: 1

## In one sentence
Depths mobs get abilities, the whole server fights scheduled world bosses and is paid by damage share, small groups call summon bosses with sigils, and pets level up while you play and give a small perk.

## How it works
Phase 8 is a written plan (PHASE-8-PVE.md). Only the mob base is built. Every number below is a proposed default unless listed under Decided.
- **Built:**
  - 10 combat tiers, each with a **mini-boss** (Grave Warden to Warlord) and 13 normal mobs in `mobs.yml`. Mini-bosses are placeholders: 15x health, glowing, boss bar, one every 10 min per zone.
  - Everyone with 10%+ of a mini-boss's damage gets full rewards (770, `combat.mini-boss.min-damage-share`).
  - A placeholder boss arena island with a portal in the last combat tier (blocks `/ec` and `/craft`).
  - A pet hook for loadouts (`PetHook`, shows "Pets are coming"), a Pets button in `/menu`, and a pet egg slot (8%) in the Eclipse crate, marked "coming soon".
- **Planned (decided): mini-boss scaling** (R3-10). Health +25% per extra eligible player, up to 10 players. Eligible = 10%+ of the damage, a hit in the last 90 s, not AFK, max 3 accounts per IP. Rewards stay full for each eligible player. Health is fixed today.
- **Planned (decided): Depths v2** (REVIEW3 D1, D3). Five zones per line, a dungeon boss hall and the main boss arena, and layered mobs from the start (each player fights their own tier's mobs). The plan `PHASE-4-DEPTHS-V2.md` is not written yet and may change the shared-zone kill credit below.
- **Mob framework v2** (M8.1, proposed default): armor, abilities (slam, leap, charge, volley, pull, summon, shield, ring, curse, teleport, enrage), each shown 1 s before it hits. Tiers 1-3 have none. Tiers 4-10 get one per mob, mini-bosses two. 2% of tier 4+ mobs are elites (2x health, 3x drops, a star). The player with the most damage gets the kill.
- **World bosses** (M8.4, proposed default):
  - Every 2 h from 12:00 to 00:00 (7 a day), announced 5 min before with a 60 s join window. Entry from Combat 12 via `/bosses`, the Gatehouse board or the tier 10 portal. Teleport is instant (3 s if tagged).
  - Health is set when the window closes, aiming at about 5 min of fight. The boss escapes after 10 min. Launch bosses: Iron Colossus and The Hollow King. Wyrm of the Deep waits for the resource pack (I7).
  - **Bands** from the average damage: Top 3, Gold (150%+), Silver (50%+), Bronze (10%+). Money is minutes of your own tier's income: Bronze 3, Silver 6, Gold 10, top 3 get +10/+7/+5. Shards: top 3 get 20/15/10, Gold 5, Silver 3, Bronze 1. Also books, set XP, 1-3% boss blueprint and 1-2% pet egg.
- **Summon bosses** (M8.6, proposed default): 5 bosses (Rotfather, Bog Matriarch, Tide Witch, Pyre Marshal, Ashen Tyrant) needing Combat 6/18/30/46/66. A fragment drops about 1 in 150 kills. 8 fragments plus money make a tradeable sigil at the Summoner NPC. 6 shared pits, the summoner plus up to 4 players, 5 min limit.
- **Pets** (M8.7, proposed default):
  - A Mystery Pet Egg (tradeable) opens instantly into a rarity: Common 50%, Uncommon 28%, Rare 14%, Epic 6%, Legendary 2%. Then a random species of 15 (Ember Fox, Ash Wolf, Coin Allay, Phoenix Chick...). Hatched pets are bound to the profile (130). Max 30 per profile.
  - One active pet. Its perk grows to 3/5/8/11/15% at level 100 by rarity, inside the existing bonus caps. Epic and Legendary add +5/+8% skill XP. Level 100 takes about 60 active hours. Offline, AFK and grinders give no pet XP.
  - Pets never fight. They are small vanilla mobs behind you; other players' pets are hidden in combat zones and the boss arena.
  - Pet skins are cosmetic, account-wide and kept across seasons.
- **Staff** (proposed default): `/boss start|stop|give`, `/pet give|view`, all audited, plus a fight log in `/logs`.

## What the player sees
- Today: mini-bosses with a boss bar in combat zones; `/menu` Pets shows "coming soon".
- Planned: `/bosses` (next boss and time, Teleport, Summon, Leaderboards, Last fights, Rewards guide), a boss scoreboard (health, phase, time, top 3, your rank and band), a fight report menu, `/pets`, and sigil and pet item lore.
- Fight end line: "Iron Colossus fell in 4:51! 112 fighters." with the top 3 and your band.

## Rules and limits
- Anti-leech (proposed default): you need to be in the arena at the kill, hit in the last 90 s, not be AFK, reach Bronze, one reward per account and max 3 accounts per IP (needs real Bedrock IPs, REVIEW3 §3). Flying, elytra, pearls and wind charges are off in arenas.
- PvE death keeps everything. Profile switching is blocked in the arena (132).
- Payout is one transaction per player, keyed by fight, so a crash never pays twice; offline winners get items in `/box`. A failed summon uses the sigil; a crash or staff stop returns it to `/box`.
- World, summon and mini-bosses together should pay at most 6% of a regular player's income (proposed default, PV40).
- Bedrock: boss bar, fake block ring warnings, chest menus, no damage numbers.
- Performance: max 30 adds per fight, one ability timing wheel, damage ledger in memory. Budget 8 ms per second with 150 fighters.

## Open questions
1. Schedule and entry: a boss every 2 h from 12:00 to 00:00, Combat 12+ to join (PV5, PV6). Default: yes.
2. Fight rules: health set from the fighters' sword tiers, relative bands, and the anti-leech list (PV7-PV9). Default: yes.
3. Summon bosses: five bosses, 1 in 150 fragments, 8 per sigil, shared pits, tradeable sigils (PV16-PV19). Default: yes.
4. Pets: egg odds, 15% top perk, eggs tradeable but hatched pets bound (PV24-PV27). Default: yes.
5. R3-24 limits Combat XP to Depths combat zones. Do world bosses, summon bosses and sea creatures (PV37) still give Combat XP? Default: bosses yes (their arenas are on Depths islands), sea creatures no.

## Rejected ideas
- Instance worlds for summon fights: too much memory and CPU at 300 players (PV19).
- Damage numbers: text displays are not reliable on Bedrock.
