# Starter chest · id: A4 · status: Draft · version: 1

## In one sentence
Every new island starts with a chest of tools, seeds and a guide book picked for this server, and a player who joins a team island instead gets a smaller Starter pack in their inventory.

## How it works
- **Built.** The chest sits in front of the island home on the starter island and is filled **once, when the island is created** (A2). The islands module asks the tutorial module for the contents. If the tutorial module is off or `starter-chest.enabled` is false, it uses its own old list (`islands.yml: starter-chest.items`: lava bucket, ice, bone meal, bread, saplings, sugar cane, cactus, melon and pumpkin seeds).
- **Contents** (`tutorial.yml: starter-chest.*`, slot numbers in brackets):

| Item | Amount | Why |
|---|---|---|
| Phoenix Field Guide, written book [4] | 1 | The whole loop in 6 short pages |
| Starter Pickaxe, Axe, Hoe, Sword, Rod [10-14] | 1 each | The Forge starter kit. The pickaxe is needed for the Depths |
| Wheat seeds [15] | 8 | Tutorial step 4 plants 3 |
| Bone meal [16] | 8 | First wheat grows at once |
| Oak sapling [19] | 2 | Wood |
| Lava bucket [20] | 1 | For the cobble generator by the pond (optional step O1) |
| Bread [21] | 16 | First session food |
| Crafting table [22], Chest [23] | 1 each | The island has neither |

- **The 5 tools** are made by the tools module for that profile and count as its **first starter kit claim**, so the 24 h re-claim timer starts. Lost pieces come back from the Forge's Starter kit button. The auction house refuses starter kit pieces. Forging one turns it into a normal tool.
- **Field Guide pages:** Welcome, Your island, The Depths, Money, Tools, Help.
- **Starter pack** (a team joiner, or no usable chest): 16 bread, the Field Guide and the starter kit (only if no kit claim is running). It goes straight to the inventory, overflow to `/box`. Once per account.
- **No chest, empty chest or a failed paste:** tutorial step 3 gives the Starter pack instead.
- **Nothing is dropped:** what does not fit goes to the inventory, then `/box`.
- **Planned (decided):** three starter islands (Ember Meadow, Frost Peak, Ash Dunes), each with its own schematic, chest and home, editable by staff (decisions I1, O16). Today one chest list serves the single Ember Meadow island.

## What the player sees
- The chest has a hologram "Starter Chest / » Open me «" and a flame column during the tutorial.
- After opening: "Take everything. The book explains it all."
- Welcome line on arrival: "Your starter chest is right in front of you."

## Rules and limits
- No money note: every profile already starts with $1,000 (answer 121), and a note is tradeable value for alts. (REVIEW3 F1 also stops a fresh profile sending money away until it has earned $10k itself.)
- The tutorial step counts a chest opened on your own island. Visitors cannot open containers there by default.
- Everything is per profile: a new profile on a new island gets its own chest and its own kit. Nothing moves between your profiles.
- Item lists are typed config (`slot: item: amount`, slots 0 to 26). Bad lines are skipped with a log line.
- Bedrock: the book and chest are normal vanilla UI. No chat clicks.

## Open questions
1. TQ15 to TQ18 have no recorded owner answer: kit counts as the first claim, no money note, sugar cane/cactus/melon/pumpkin seeds/ice removed, Starter pack for team joiners. Built with the suggested answers. Confirm?
2. Each new profile gets a fresh free kit (security audit F-14). Make the kit once per account, or keep it per profile? Default: per profile (tools cannot be sold on the AH).
3. Should starter tools also be refused by `/trade` and bound to the profile (proposal SEC-7, no answer yet)? The F1 earn rule covers money only, not items. The bound-item system (REVIEW3 R3-2, F5) is planned. Default: only the AH refuses them.
4. How should the chest differ for Frost Peak and Ash Dunes (O16)? Default: same items until those builds exist.

## Rejected ideas
- The old chest (lava, ice, sugar cane, cactus, seeds): crops belong to later island tiers, ice is useless next to an infinite pond.
- A money note in the chest: it is tradeable value and would feed alt accounts.
- Giving team joiners the island items (seeds, lava, table): the team island already has them.
