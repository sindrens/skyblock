# Starter chest · id: A4 · status: Review · version: 2

## In one sentence
Starter items belong to the profile and never run out: every new island starts with a full chest (tools, seeds, food, a guide book), a player who joins a team island gets their own small Starter pack straight into the inventory, and lost starter tools can be restocked.

## How it works
Three paths, one rule ("the profile always ends up with its five starter tools; nobody can run the chest empty for the next player"):

| Path | When | Gives | Claim rule |
|---|---|---|---|
| **Chest** | Every time an island is created (A2), for every profile, also the 2nd and 3rd island of a profile | Full chest, see table below | None. No 24 h timer, no claim row touched (fixes BUGS-FOUND 4) |
| **Starter pack** | A profile joins a team island as member (B3), tutorial finished or not | The starter tools it is missing + bread + seeds (A4-Q1) straight to the inventory, overflow to `/box` | Once per profile and season, atomic |
| **Restock** | Forge menu, "Starter kit" button (E10) | Only the starter tools the player does not have | Timer per A4-Q2 |

- **Chest:** it sits in front of the island home and is filled when the island is built. If the tutorial module is off or `starter-chest.enabled` is false, the old `islands.yml: starter-chest.items` list is the fallback. No chest, empty chest or a failed paste: the Starter pack is given instead (tutorial step 3, A3). What does not fit goes to the inventory, then `/box`. Nothing is dropped.
- **Chest contents** (`tutorial.yml: starter-chest.*`, slot numbers in brackets):

| Item | Amount | Why |
|---|---|---|
| Phoenix Field Guide, written book [4] | 1 | The whole loop in 6 short pages |
| Starter Pickaxe, Axe, Hoe, Sword, Rod [10-14] | 1 each | The five tier-1 starter tools; the pickaxe is needed for the Depths |
| Wheat seeds [15] | 8 | Tutorial step 4 plants 3 |
| Bone meal [16] | 8 | First wheat grows at once |
| Oak sapling [19] | 2 | Wood |
| Lava bucket [20] | 1 | Cobble generator by the pond (optional step O1) |
| Bread [21] | 16 | First session food |
| Crafting table [22], Chest [23] | 1 each | The island has neither |

- **Starter tools:** minted by the tools module, tier 1, carrying the `phoenixskyblock:starter_kit` mark ("starter" flag). The mark makes the auction house and the sell paths refuse them, and (new) `/trade` too. The mint count is logged. What happens when a tool is forged to tier 2: A4-Q4. Starter tools can be improved from bad to very good with enchants, upgrades and more (B17-R1-21, designed in E10, E11, E12).
- **Starter pack (joiner):** replaces today's once-per-account pack. A team joiner would otherwise start with an empty inventory and could not do the tutorial's farming step. The tools are only the kinds the player does not already hold (inventory or ender chest). The Field Guide stays a separate once-per-account claim (`pack_given`, untouched, B17-R1-05). The pack never contains what the island chest already holds (lava, water, crafting table, chest).
- **Restock (Forge "Starter kit" button):** gives only missing pieces, never a full second kit.
- **Fresh island left for an invite (B17-R1-03):** the untouched island and its chest are removed. Nothing is lost: the Starter pack is a separate claim, so the player still gets their tools when the invite is accepted. Taking items out of the starter chest does not count as "touched" (only putting items in does).
- **Planned (decided):** three starter islands (Ember Meadow, Frost Peak, Ash Dunes), each with its own schematic, chest and home, editable by staff (I1, O16). Today one chest list serves the single Ember Meadow island. See A4-Q3.

## What the player sees
- The chest has a hologram "Starter Chest / » Open me «" and a flame column during the tutorial.
- After opening: "Take everything. The book explains it all."
- Welcome line on arrival: "Your starter chest is right in front of you."
- Joiner: chat line "You got the Starter pack: tools, bread and seeds." plus the same sound as a chest pickup; the items are already in the inventory.
- Starter tools say in their lore "Starter tool" and, if bound forever (A4-Q4), "Bound to this profile".

## Rules and limits
- No money note: every profile already starts with $1,000 (121, B17-R1-16 once per slot and season); a note would be tradeable value for alts. REVIEW3 F1 also stops a fresh profile sending money away until it has earned $10k.
- Everything is per profile: a new profile on a new island gets its own chest and its own pack. Nothing moves between your profiles (principle 2).
- The tutorial step counts a chest opened on your own island. Visitors cannot open containers there by default.
- Item lists are typed config (`slot: item: amount`, slots 0 to 26). Bad lines are skipped with a log line. Only items that exist in 1.21.5 (X4): all items above do.
- Bedrock: the book and the chest are normal vanilla UI; no chat clicks.

## Developer decisions (security, dupes, data, tests; owner only vetoes)
- **Fix the chest bug:** chest fills mint the tools without touching `starter_claims`. Test: create island, disband, create again in under 24 h gives five tools both times.
- **One claim table for the Starter pack:** `starter_grants (profile_key, season, grant, given_at, PRIMARY KEY (profile_key, season, grant))` in the tools module, grant `join_pack`. The claim is one atomic insert, the items follow; if delivery fails (player left), the row is removed again, as the Forge does today. A season change gives the pack again, because the season wipe empties the profile (I5 puts both starter tables on its wipe list).
- **Interface change:** `StarterContents.givePack(Player)` becomes `giveJoinPack(Player)`, called by the member-join step of B3; the tutorial's step 3 fallback calls the same method. The `p.state.complete()` refusal is removed. `pack_given` stays only for the Field Guide.
- **Who can take chest tools:** any member (containers follow island permissions). That is fine because every joiner has their own pack and restock, so a taken tool never costs another player theirs. Tools carry no owner field; they are only marked.
- **Marked items cannot move:** add `phoenixskyblock:starter_kit` to `trade.blockedDataKeys`; AH and sell already refuse it. Items cannot be dropped (principle 3). Test: AH listing, `/sell`, `/trade` add and swap, and money-note paths each refuse a marked tool.
- **Mint alerts:** a profile that mints more than 20 starter tools in 24 hours raises a mod alert (`tools.yml starter-kit.mint-alert-per-day: 20`; island creation is already heavy, so this is a trail, not a block). Each mint keeps its audit row (`starter_kit`, pieces, profile).
- **Starter axe and WorldEdit (BUGS-FOUND 1):** E10/H7 decide the fix (marked wand item or another material); A4 only requires that starter tools stay unusable as a wand.
- **Config keys:** `tutorial.yml: starter-chest.enabled, .kit, .kit-slots, .book-slot, .items` and `starter-pack.enabled, .kit, .book, .items` (exist); new `starter-pack.items` default `bread: 16, wheat_seeds: 8, bone_meal: 8`; new `tools.yml starter-kit.mint-alert-per-day`; later `starter-chest.islands.<type>.items` (O16). `starter-pack.per-season` is not configurable on purpose.
- **Staff:** `/tutorial admin kit <player>` (admin, tab-completed, audited) gives the Starter pack again, ignoring the claim; reason line mandatory. This is the only new command; the owner may veto it. No new permission node beyond the existing `/tutorial admin` one.
- **Data:** nothing in the chest is saved separately; a removed fresh island takes its chest with it; staff restore a deleted profile's inventory from the profile backup (B17), not from the chest.
- **Performance:** chest fill and pack are one-time, under 25 slots; the DB writes are async; tool rows are written asynchronously (as built, saved on first use if the write fails).
- **Tests (H2):** claim atomic under 50 parallel joins (one pack); a failed delivery removes the claim; pack skips tool kinds already held; pack works after the tutorial is finished; chest refills after disband (BUGS-FOUND 4); every refuse path above; season change allows a new pack.

## Decided
- 158: make the starter chest custom and relevant to this server.
- TUT-14: contents = 5 starter tools, 8 wheat seeds, 8 bone meal, 2 oak saplings, 1 lava bucket, 16 bread, crafting table, chest, Field Guide.
- 121: every profile gets start money (so no money note); B17-R1-16: once per slot and season.
- I1: three starter islands. O16: each has its own schematic, chest and home, editable by staff.
- B17-R1-04: every new island's chest is filled completely again, no 24 h timer for chest fills, only the tools are bound to the profile (reverses TQ15 in part).
- B17-R1-05: a profile joining a team island gets once the tools it is missing plus relevant items (not what the island chest holds), tutorial finished or not.
- B17-R1-03: an untouched island can be left to accept an invite.
- B17-R1-21: starter tools are upgradeable from bad to very good (E10, E11, E12).
- A3-R1-01 and FRIEND-JOIN points 1, 4, 5: starter items belong to the player, nothing is lost when a fresh island is left.

## Rejected ideas
- The old chest (lava, ice, sugar cane, cactus, seeds): crops belong to later island tiers, ice is useless next to an infinite pond.
- A money note in the chest: it is tradeable value and would feed alt accounts.
- Giving team joiners the island items (lava, water, crafting table, chest): the team island already has them.
- Delivering the whole kit to the inventory for the player's own first island and leaving the chest as a "bonus" (FRIEND-JOIN point 1): replaced by B17-R1-04 (full chest every time) plus the joiner pack.
