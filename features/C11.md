# Player shops and buy orders · id: C11 · status: Draft · version: 1

## In one sentence
Buy orders let a player post "I buy 64 iron for $X" and anyone can fill it; chest shops let a player sell from a chest on their island with a sign. Nothing of this is built yet.

## How it works
Nothing is built. The design below is the proposal in PLAYER_SHOPS_BUY_ORDERS.md (owner said yes to the ideas, not yet to the details).
- **Order: buy orders first** (smaller, they reuse the AH), then chest shops, after the production phase (Phase 7).
- **Buy orders: a new tab in the AH** (`/ah orders`).
  - Make an order: item, amount, price per item. The money is held in escrow at once.
  - Anyone can fill it from their inventory. The items go to the buyer's collection bin, the money to the filler (minus the tax).
  - Same fee and rules as AH listings. At most 5 open orders (more with ranks). Same price range as the AH ($10 to $1T).
  - Orders can be cancelled; the unused escrow comes back. Filling can be partial: you fill what you have. (proposed default)
- **Chest shops on islands.**
  - Put a sign `[shop]` on a chest: item, amount, price, and a buy or sell mode.
  - Visitors right-click to buy. The money goes to the owner, or to the island bank with a toggle. The stock lives in the chest.
  - Each sale is one atomic step: the item leaves the chest and the money moves together.
  - Islands with shops show in the visit browser under "Shops". At most 10 shops per island (more with an upgrade).
  - Chest shops are taxed 5% (O13).
- **Money only.** No gems, crystals or shards in shops or orders (CURRENCIES doc, question 13).

## What the player sees
- (proposed default) A setup menu when you right-click a new `[shop]` sign, so nobody has to type item names on a sign: pick the item from your inventory, set the amount and price with a number picker, choose Buy or Sell.
- Shop signs show the item, amount, price and a stock hologram. A buyer sees "Buy 16 Iron Ingot for $320?" with Confirm.
- The orders tab lists open orders sorted by price, with "Fill with items from your inventory" and your own orders with Cancel.

## Rules and limits
- Dupe-safe like the AH: escrow rows, one database transaction per fill or sale, every step logged with the full item.
- No buying from or filling the orders of your own other profile (principle 2, P2). Accounts that shared an IP cannot trade through shops or orders (proposal, not decided).
- Blocked items follow the AH list: starter kit items, dupe-flagged items, staff items, filled shulker boxes, and bound items (R3-2, claimed gem items per REVIEW3 F5). Spawners stay tradeable, so they can be sold. (proposed default)
- A hopper or other automation must not be able to empty or fill a shop chest. (proposed default)
- Planned (decided for the AH): buying needs $10k earned by the profile (REVIEW3 F1) and a dupe gives a 24 h trade block (R3-28). Orders are an AH tab, so they follow. For chest shops it is the same by default. (proposed default)
- Performance: shops are found through an in-memory index by sign position. No scanning of chests, no per-tick work. Orders use the same cached browse as the AH.
- Bedrock: creation and buying through menus, not sign text.

## Decided
- 57: player shops (chest shops) are wanted. 58: buy orders are wanted.
- O13: chest shops are taxed 5%.
- P2 / principle 2: never buy from your own other profile.
- REVIEW3 F1 (R3-1) and R3-28 cover AH buying and AH trade blocks. REVIEW3 F5 / R3-2: bound items can't be sold.
- 550-563: the AH rules (fee 1%, tax 5%, bin never expires) are the base for orders.

## Open questions
1. Buy orders as an AH tab first, with escrow, the AH fee and 5 open orders? Default: yes.
2. Chest shops with a `[shop]` sign, buy and sell modes, money to the owner with an island bank toggle, max 10 per island? Default: yes.
3. Which phase: with the Phase 3 economy extras now, or after Phase 7 production? Default: after Phase 7.
4. Is the fee for orders the AH 1% on top of the 5% tax, and what does a chest shop sale pay: 5% only (O13)? Default: orders as the AH; shops 5% only.
5. Should same-IP accounts be blocked from shops and orders? Default: yes.
