# Main menu (/menu) · id: G9 · status: Draft · version: 1

## In one sentence
`/menu` and the Nether Star in hotbar slot 9 open one chest menu that leads to every feature, so nobody needs to remember commands (Bedrock too). The owner wants it changed, and a smarter layout is proposed.

## How it works
**Built:**
- `/menu` (`/gui`) opens a 6-row menu. Your profile head sits in slot 4 (rank, playtime, join date; click opens `/settings`).
- **28 buttons in 4 rows of 7** (MENU_GUI.md counts 29, the file has 28):
  - Row 2: Island, The Depths, Shop, Sell, Bank, Auction House, Black Market
  - Row 3: Skills, Wardrobe, Enchanter, Quests, Crates, Events, Pets
  - Row 4: Warps, Collection, Leaderboards, Titles & Tags, Store, Vote, Discord
  - Row 5: The Forge, Spawn, Rules, Settings, Help, Website, Profiles
- Bottom row is the standard frame: Back, Info, Close, Help.
- **Status per button** (`menu.yml: status`): `auto` = clickable when the command exists, otherwise gray "Coming soon" (decided: 41). Today six are locked: Black Market, Quests, Events, Pets, Collection, Leaderboards. A button can also need a permission.
- **Menu item:** a Nether Star, right-click or tap. It cannot be dropped, moved, stored or lost on death. It is put back on join, respawn, world or game mode change, after closing an inventory, and every 5 s (`menu-item.check-seconds`). Players can turn it off in `/settings` (per profile, answer 50). Staff mode pauses it.
- Layout is in `menu.yml` (slot, icon, command, status), names and lore in `messages/navigation.yml`. Reload: `/skyblock reload menus`. `/staff` is a separate staff hub.
- Every menu has a "?" that opens `/help <topic>`.

**Planned (proposal in MENU_GUI.md, option C "smart hub", needs owner input):**
- Row 2, one click: Island, Depths, Sell, Shop, Auction House.
- Row 3, categories: Progress (skills, wardrobe, enchanter, collection, achievements, leaderboards), Rewards (daily, vote, quests, crates, battle pass, black market), Island+, Community (friends, parties, tags, Discord, rules, website), Events.
- Row 4: Recent and pinned buttons (QoL 19: last 4 used, up to 4 pinned).
- Row 5: Bank, Forge, Spawn, Warps, Profiles. Bottom row: Settings, Help, Store. A Staff button for staff only.
- **Status as a number:** a button's item count shows what waits (Rewards 3 = 3 ready) plus the lore line `● 3 ready`. Works on Bedrock.
- Unbuilt features hidden on the live server; on the test server they show inside their category, not on the main page.
- Fixed slots, so the resource pack's background art lines up.

## What the player sees
- Buttons named in flame orange (`Island`, `The Depths`) with two lines of lore and `» CLICK to open`. Locked ones: gray name, "Coming soon", "Keep an eye on /discord for news."
- Info book (slot 48): "Every feature of Phoenix Skyblock. Gray icons are coming soon."
- Chest menus only, no Bedrock forms (answer 33).

## Rules and limits
- Items can never be taken out of a menu. Clicks are debounced.
- All 28 inner slots are used. Box, Trade and Achievements have no button; they open from `/help`, their commands or `/tags`.
- The menu item check is one timer for all players, not a scan per tick.
- Performance: the hub is built when opened, from the config. The planned "ready" counts must come from cached values, not scans `(proposed default)`.
