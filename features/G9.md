## In one sentence
`/menu` and the Nether Star in the last hotbar slot open one chest menu with 14 feature buttons in 4 coloured rows plus your head, so nobody needs to remember commands (Bedrock too); the old 28-button grid and the "smart hub" plan are replaced by the clean hub that is already built, cleaned up (no frame, no marker panes, no `?` and Main menu buttons) and restyled to the new standard with the Phoenix red and ember identity.

## How it works

**Built (matches the code, `menu.yml` GUI-2; the old card said 28 buttons):**
- `/menu` (`/gui`) opens a 6-row menu. Your head sits on slot 4 (name, rank, money, crystals, playtime; click opens the You menu).
- **14 buttons in 4 rows, one per group, spaced on columns 1, 3, 5, 7:**
  - Row 1, Play: Home (10), Island (12), The Depths (14), Travel (16).
  - Row 2, Money: Sell (19), Shop (21), Bank (23), Auction House (25).
  - Row 3, Power: Forge (28), Skills (30), Enchanter (32), Wardrobe (34).
  - Row 4, More: Rewards (39, one click claims everything ready), Top (41).
- **You menu** (3 rows): Profiles, Titles, Achievements, Settings, Store, Rules, Quests, Collection. **Travel menu** (3 rows): Spawn, My Island, Depths Gate, Crate Yard, Market, Forge Quarter, Visit Islands.
- A feature that is not built (service missing, module off, command missing) is **hidden** on the live server; the test server can show it in a "Coming soon" look (`navigation.yml: hub.show-unbuilt`). A button can need a permission (shown locked without it).
- **Menu item:** a Nether Star named `Menu` in hotbar slot 9. It cannot be dropped, moved, stored or lost on death; it is put back on join, respawn, world or game mode change, after closing an inventory and every 5 s (`menu-item.check-seconds`). Players turn it off in `/settings` (per profile, decided 50). Staff mode pauses it.
- Layout in `menu.yml` (slot, group, icon, command or action, service), names and lore in `messages/navigation.yml`. Reload: `/skyblock reload menus`. `/staff` is a separate staff hub.
- Every screen opened from the hub has Back to the hub (navigation stack).

**Decided (new standard, I9; owner GUI-Q11: "keep it clean and clean it up, remove the useless things, we make our own skyblock personality"):**
- **No marker panes and no border frame.** The four coloured marker panes on the left edge and the black frame go; a row is a group because of its spacing, and **each name takes its row colour** (Play lime, Money green, Power violet, More flame gold). Inside the features, I9's meaning colours apply (Depths aqua, Travel ember ...), so the hub is the one place where the row colour wins.
- **Base items** from the I9 icon map; the forced Java pack (I7, phase 1) repaints them with our own simple icons later. Until then, and always on Bedrock, the base items show.
- **Phoenix personality:** the head tile name in flame gold with a red `|` bar section (rank, money, crystals, playtime), the red `▸` title marker, ember and flame tones and our own icons carry the identity. Button names keep their row colour (meaning); red names stay reserved for danger (I9 section 8).
- **Tooltip** per button: coloured bold name, one grey sentence, `» CLICK to ...`. Defaults (texts in `navigation.yml`):

## What the player sees
- Short buttons in four coloured rows with space between them, one grey sentence each and a yellow `» CLICK` line. Rewards shows how many things wait. Nothing else: no frame, no extra buttons.
- No gray "Coming soon" buttons on the live server. Bedrock sees the same chest menu and the same items.

## Rules and limits
- Items can never be taken out of a menu; clicks are debounced.
- Max 14 feature buttons on the main page. A new feature goes into You, Travel or its own feature screen first; it earns a main-page slot only by replacing another.
- The menu item check is one timer for all players, not a scan per tick. The hub is built when opened from the config; the Rewards count comes from a cached value.
- Box, Trade and Achievements have no main-page button; they open from `/help`, their commands or the You menu.

## Developer decisions (defaults; the owner only vetoes)
- Remove `hub-groups.<id>.mark` panes; add `hub-groups.<id>.color` (an `area.*` tag). Old `menu.yml` files keep working: the `mark` key is ignored with one log line.

- Tests: hub slot uniqueness and group rows (pure), tooltip line limits, hidden-when-unbuilt, staff tile permission, no frame panes in the built inventory.
