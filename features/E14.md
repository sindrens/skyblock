# Loadouts · id: E14 · status: Draft · version: 1

## In one sentence
A loadout is a saved kit of set pieces, a pet and the custom tools in your hotbar. One click (or `/loadout Mining`) swaps your whole kit, for example from Mining to Combat, and the old tools wait safely in the Wardrobe Chest. Armor and tools work today; the pet part and extra slots from the store do not yet.

## How it works
- **Built:**
  - **Presets per profile:** 2 free. Ranks add more through the permission `skyblock.loadout.slots.<n>` (the highest counts). At most 8 in all.
  - **What a preset holds:** per armor slot a wardrobe set or "empty" (pieces not in the preset are taken off and stay in the wardrobe); per hotbar slot one unique item such as a custom tool; the pet (hook only). Plain items are never saved. Save current captures what you wear and the unique items in your hotbar.
  - **Putting tools back:** each tool returns to the hotbar slot it was saved in (#935).
  - **Name and icon:** a preset name is one word (letters, digits, `-` and `_`, max 16); a new one is called `Loadout-<n>`. Pick an icon from your items.
  - **Where you can swap:** anywhere except while combat-tagged (10 s after a hit from a player or hostile mob), in the warzone, while frozen, during a profile switch or another wardrobe action (#946). A 2 second cooldown is shared with wearing sets.
  - **How a swap works (no dupes):** the menu closes and your inventory is locked; the whole swap is planned first; then one database transaction stores the outgoing tools in the Wardrobe Chest, marks the incoming ones as handed out and writes the new worn pieces; only then do items move, in one tick. Swapped-out tools never drop on the ground. A full chest keeps the tool in your inventory. Plain items in a needed slot move to a free slot; vanilla armor under a new piece moves to the inventory, or to `/box` when full.
  - **Partial swap (#943):** if one part cannot happen (a tool was sold, a set was merged away, no room), the rest still happens and chat lists what was skipped. `loadout.partial: false` refuses the whole swap instead.
  - **Crash safety:** a quit or crash at any point is repaired on the next join by the chest recovery, so each tool ends up in exactly one place.
  - **Merging sets** re-points presets that used either set to the merged set.
- **Planned / not built:**
  - `/loadout 2` with a slot number (#934). Today only names work.
  - **Pet in the preset:** `PetHook` does nothing until pets exist (Phase 8). The menu shows "Pets are coming".
  - **Bought slots:** +1 per store credit purchase, max +3, account-wide. The table `loadout_slots_bought` exists but nothing writes it until the store exists.
  - **Rank slots:** Knight 3, Warlord 4, Monarch 5 (LD2). The grant lines are commented out until the donor ranks exist (Phase 11).
  - **Perk model (REVIEW3 F7, decided, plan not locked):** extra loadout slots are a convenience perk. A bought rank unlocks its convenience perks at once; the rest unlock along one progression track from start to end game, like a battle pass (F1). Gameplay perks are never sold.

## What the player sees
- `/loadout` (or the Loadouts button in `/wardrobe`): a menu of presets with an icon, the set pieces and tools count, and a red "missing" line for anything gone. Click one to swap. Empty places say "Click to save your gear"; places above your slots say "Locked".
- Edit under each preset: Use now, Save current (confirm), Rename (type in chat), Icon, Delete (confirm).
- After a swap: an action bar line and a chat line such as `Loadout Mining: 4 piece(s), 3 tool(s). Old tools went to your Wardrobe Chest.` Skipped parts in one line.
- Tab completion lists your preset names.

## Rules and limits
- Anti-abuse: only unique tracked items move, so a tool exists in exactly one place at any moment (inventory, chest, or a journal row while in flight). Plain items are never saved or copied. Every action is audited (`loadout_apply`, `loadout_save`, `loadout_rename`, `loadout_icon`, `loadout_delete`).
- A preset above your slots (rank lost) is kept but locked, never deleted.
- Per profile: presets belong to the profile; only bought slots are account-wide.
- Performance: one plan (pure code) and one database transaction per swap, no per-tick work; swaps cost one round trip, under about 100 ms.
- Bedrock: the menu is click-only; the `/loadout <name>` shortcut is for Java players who bind it.
- No waiting: 2 s cooldown only; nothing lost: swapped tools go to the chest, never to the ground.

## Decided
- L1: a loadout holds armor, pet and hotbar tools.
- L2: 2 free presets, more from ranks and store credits, max 8.
- L3: swapped-out pieces and tools go into the wardrobe.
- L4: not in combat or the warzone, 2 s cooldown.
- LD1-LD6: plan defaults in docs/plans/WARDROBE.md (atomic apply, `/loadout <name>`, wardrobe equips follow the same place rules).
- REVIEW3 F7: perks such as extra slots unlock along one progression track; convenience perks only for bought ranks.
- 934: `/loadout` takes a slot number (not built).
- 935: tools return to the same hotbar slots. 943: partial swap, list what was skipped. 946: no swaps while frozen.

## Open questions
1. Partial swap (default, built) or refuse the whole swap when one part fails? Default: partial.
2. Preset names are one word so `/loadout <name>` and tab completion always work. Default: keep. Players cannot use spaces.
3. `/loadout 2` (#934): if a preset is literally named "2", which wins? Default: the name first, then the place number.
4. Should a loadout also save plain hotbar items such as food or blocks? Default: no (dupe safety, LD1).
5. How are extra slots earned: bought with gems, the progression track, or both? Default (proposed): earned gems, 500 and 1,500 (the review's perk table), once the F7 plan is locked.
