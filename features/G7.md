# Scoreboard · id: G7 · status: Built · version: 1

## In one sentence
The panel on the right of the screen shows a different, short layout for each place you stand in (spawn, your island, someone else's island, each Depths zone, the warzone), with only the lines that matter there.

## How it works
- **One sidebar per player.** The layout is picked by place (`hud.yml: places`); the layouts themselves (title and lines) are in `messages/hud.yml: scoreboard.<layout>`.

| Place | Layout | Shows |
|---|---|---|
| Your own island | `island` | Island level and rank, next open note, name and rank, money, crystals, gems |
| Someone else's island (coop counts as a visitor) | `island-visit` | "Visiting <island name>", name and rank, money, crystals, gems |
| Spawn | `spawn` | Name and rank, money, crystals, gems, island level, AFK-pool line while standing in it |
| The Depths: mine, causeways | `depths` | Zone, tier, satchel, Mining level and %, money |
| The Gate / Forest / Combat wing | `depths-gate`, `depths-forest`, `depths-combat` | Zone and tier, the wing's skill level, money |
| Warzone | `warzone` | Combat level, money (placeholder until the warzone exists) |
| Anywhere else | world-named layout, else `default` | Profile, island level |

- **Look (STYLE2):** phoenix gradient title, one blank line under it (FIXES1), bold red `| Section` headers, rows start with a dark red bar, gray labels, white or currency-coloured values, footer `play.skybornmc.net`.
- **Size:** up to 10 lines under the title. Bedrock shows at most 15 lines (`scoreboard.max-lines`).
- **Updates:** each player refreshes every 20 ticks (1 s), spread over the ticks. Only changed lines are sent, so there is no flicker. The layout switches on the tick after a teleport, respawn or world change, and within 1 s when you walk onto another island or Depths zone.
- **Conditional lines:** `if:<placeholder>|<line>` shows only while the value exists. The island level line is hidden until the island has a level (owner answer 275).
- **Switch:** `/settings` > Scoreboard, per profile, applies at once. Chat form: `/settings scoreboard off`.

## Rules and limits
- Max 10 lines is checked by `SidebarLinesTest` for every bundled layout.
- No online count and no objective line (UX1 removed them). The objective returns with tutorial and quests as its own layout.
- A missing layout falls back: wing, then Depths base, then world-named, then `default`.
- Performance: 300 players means 300 sidebars updated each second (PERFORMANCE.md P-08, partly fixed: changed-line sending and a layout cache). Each player's own scoreboard also carries the tab and nametag teams (G8, P-07).
