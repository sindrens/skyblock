# AFK pool · id: D10 · status: Draft · version: 1

## In one sentence
Standing in the AFK pool at spawn pays small money, island fuel and crate keys every 15 minutes, up to 8 hours a day per account, so AFK time helps a little but never replaces playing.

## How it works
- **Built: the whole module** (`modules/afk`). There is no command: rewards are automatic while you stand inside the pool. It needs no movement.
- **Built: the area.** The pool is a region `afk_pool` in a spawn build, or a box in `afk.yml`. **No build has this region yet, so the pool is closed.** It opens as soon as builders add the region.
- **Planned (decided): its own area** away from the Tavern, with players inside hidden from each other (R3-36). It uses the shared visibility service (REVIEW3 D4). Expect about 90 players on average and 120 to 150 at peak. Not built.
- **Reward every 15 minutes** of credited time (checked every 2 s). Each reward gives money, 15 minutes of island fuel, and sometimes a booster.
- **Money** scales to your stage, so it is never useless late or a printer early. Your tier is your best mining, foraging or combat level. Per 15 min at 100%: tier 1 $310, tier 2 $550, tier 5 $2,970, tier 8 $16,000, tier 12 $1,070,000. Paid to your active profile (ledger reason `afk_pool`).
- **Longer stay pays more:** 100% at the start, 110% after 30 min, 125% after 1 h, 140% after 2 h, 160% after 4 h, 180% after 6 h. Leaving resets the step, not your daily total.
- **Fuel:** 15 min per reward to your island. It is saved as a pending grant and delivered once Island Fuel exists (D5).
- **Keys** once per day: 1 h Ember, 3 h Ember, 6 h Cinder, 8 h Blaze. Keys from the pool are bound (P10-6). The crates module is built, so these are handed over.
- **Planned (decided): no Blaze keys (R3-15).** The 8 h key becomes a Cinder key (one line in `afk.yml: keys.milestones`). Loot from bound keys is bound too (R3-2).
- **Booster:** 2% chance per reward after 1 h in one session of a +50% sell booster for 10 minutes. It is banked (max 60 min) and starts when you leave the pool.
- **Daily cap:** 8 hours per account, all profiles together (reset at midnight Europe/Oslo). No skill XP and no set XP (1023).
- **Planned (decided): no idle kick (R3-35).** A player in the pool who is not earning (daily cap reached, or paused by the one-per-connection rule) is teleported to their island with a short message. Delay: 60 s after earning stops, configurable (proposed default).
- **Planned (decided): family whitelist (R3-3).** Staff can whitelist a family that shares one connection.

## What the player sees
- Action bar in the pool: `AFK Pool | Next reward 12:30 | Today 3h 15m/8h | 125%`.
- Spawn scoreboard: an `AFK` line with the status and today's time ("Limit reached", "Paused", "Off").
- Chat per reward: "AFK reward: +$310 and 15 min of island fuel (100%)". Without an island it says to create one with `/is`.
- Messages: "You reached today's AFK limit of 8h. It resets in ...", "Paused: another account on your connection is earning". Help page: `/help afk`.

## Rules and limits
- One account per IP address earns at a time (the first one in). The others see why they are paused. Plus 8 hours per IP per day over all accounts, so alts can't take turns. This IP total is kept in memory, a restart resets it.
- Each reward is one database transaction (money, reward number, daily total, fuel grant, booster). A replay after a crash can never pay twice. Credited time is saved every 30 s.
- Money from the pool is at most about 4% of an active regular player's income (balance sheet 7.5). A full AFK day at the top step is worth about 1 to 1.5 active hours.
- Performance: players in the pool are checked every 2 s, all writes run in one queue off the main thread. No player cap (1020).
- Bedrock: action bar and scoreboard only, no chat clicks. Check that two Bedrock players from different homes both earn (Geyser must pass the real IP).

## Connections
- Fuel (D5), spawners (D2) and offline production (D7): the fuel is what the pool really adds to the economy.
- Crates and keys (F5), boosters (F11). Selling (C4): the booster counts toward the +300% cap (O11).
- Economy curve (C14) and economy watch (C10): `afk_pool` is its own money source on the dashboard. Personal bank (C8): pool time does not count for interest.
- Scoreboard (G7), social commands (G3): the separate `/afk` idle status. Profiles (B17): money goes to the active profile, the cap is per account.

## Decided
- O10: AFK pool with fuel, small money and keys, loot improves the longer you stay, max 8 h a day, balanced.
- O11: boosters count toward the global multiplier cap.
- RW3: the pool needs no movement; anti-abuse is one reward stream per IP; small area at spawn; rewards every 15 minutes.
- 1020: no player cap. 1021: idle kick after 20 minutes outside the pool (R3-35 adds: no kick inside the pool).
- 1023: no skill XP or set XP. 1026: money scaled to the stage.
- 1028: keys at 1 h and 3 h (Ember), 6 h (Cinder), once per day. The 8 h key was Blaze; R3-15 changes it to Cinder.
- 1033: the 8 h limit counts per account, across all profiles.
- P10-6: keys from the AFK pool are bound. R3-2: loot from bound keys is bound too.
- R3-3: keep one earning account per connection and 8 h per connection per day (answer: Recommended: keep as built, add a family whitelist). Staff check that Geyser passes the real Bedrock IP.
- R3-15: AFK never gives Blaze keys; the 8 h key is Cinder.
- R3-25: minutes in the pool do not count toward the 2 hours in 7 days that bank interest needs. Other idle time still counts.
- R3-35: "no need to, let them be, but just teleport them back to their island whenever they don't earn anything". No idle kick.
- R3-36: the pool gets its own small area, players hidden from each other.
- MA13: the "AFK Comfort" mastery (better pool rewards) was refused, to keep AFK weak.

## Open questions
1. R3-36: where is the area at spawn and how big? Size it for about 90 players on average, 120 to 150 at peak. Default: closed until a region `afk_pool` exists.
2. R3-35: how long after earning stops before the teleport? Default: 60 s, configurable.
3. R3-3: how do staff add a family to the whitelist (command, menu or config)? No default in the docs.
4. R3-3: does Floodgate pass each Bedrock player's real IP? Must be checked before launch, or all Bedrock players count as one connection.
5. Save the per-IP daily total in the database? Default: no.

## Rejected ideas
- Requiring movement in the pool (RW3): it is AFK by design.
- AFK Comfort mastery (MA13): would make AFK stronger.
- Up to 3 accounts per IP, each with its own 8 hours (1031): replaced by R3-3, one earning account per connection.
- Idle kick inside the pool (R3-35) and Blaze keys from the pool (R3-15).
