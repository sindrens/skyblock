# Freeze and inspect · id: H4 · status: Draft · version: 1

## In one sentence
Staff can freeze a player in place for a check (screen share, questions) and open a player card with profile, inventory, reports, punish and logs, even when the player is offline.

## How it works
- **`/freeze <player>`** (alias `/ss`) and **`/unfreeze <player>`**. Needs `skyblock.staff.freeze` (helpers and up). Admins have `.exempt` and can't be frozen. `/unfreeze` also works for offline players.
- **A frozen player can't:** change position (looking around is fine), build, break, interact, use items or inventories, drop or pick up, ride, glide, use pearls, chorus fruit or portals, be knocked back, take or deal damage, get hungry, or use any command. Chat goes to staff only.
- **Staff and plugin teleports** (`/tp`, `/tphere`) still move them (`freeze.allowed-teleport-causes: command, plugin`). They hover, so they can't fall away. After an unfreeze, fall damage is ignored for 10s.
- **Stored.** The freeze survives logout and restarts (`freeze.keep-on-rejoin: true`).
- **Logout while frozen:** staff are alerted and a note is added (`freeze.logout-action: alert`). The other choice is an automatic ban.
- **A ban clears the freeze** (so the logout action does not fire).
- **Reminders:** a title "FROZEN" and the chat instructions repeat every 10s, with the Discord link to talk to staff.
- **Slowness 3 and no jump** are only visual hints. The real hold is a check every 10 ticks, only for frozen players, plus a stop on every move.
- **`/inspect <player>`** (alias `/check`, or right-click with the Inspect tool in staff mode) opens the card:
  - Head: rank, playtime, first and last seen, Java or Bedrock (with device), ping, location, game mode.
  - Buttons: reports (open and total, opens `/reports player`), inventory (`/invsee`), ender chest (`/ecsee`), teleport, freeze or unfreeze, punish (`/p`), logs.
  - Admins also see Wardrobe, item Box and Tutorial. Offline players show profile, reports, punish and logs.
- Every inspect and freeze is audited (category `staff`; frozen chat in `chat`; logout in `punishment`).
- **Planned (decided): 10-minute alert (538).** Staff are alerted when a player has been frozen for 10 minutes. The freeze still never ends by itself.
- **Planned (decided): profile picker (134).** Staff pick which profile to view in `/inspect`, `/invsee`, `/ecsee` and `/logs`. Today only the Box view has a profile switch.
- **Planned (decided): satchel view (756).** Inspect shows a player's satchel read-only. Admins can fix it. There is no satchel button yet.

## What the player sees
- A frozen player: title "FROZEN, Don't log out, read the chat" and the instructions with the Discord link.
- A blocked command: "No commands while frozen. Type in chat to talk to staff."
- Their messages appear to staff as "[Frozen] Steve: ...".
- Staff see the player card as a menu. On Bedrock the same menu works with taps.

## Rules and limits
- Staff mode tools reach 16 blocks. Freeze and inspect need no typing for Bedrock staff: use the tools or the Inspect button.
- Commands are blocked, so `/pay` and `/shop` are out. Trade, crates, loadouts and profile switching also refuse a frozen player.
- The freeze never ends by itself and never unfreezes automatically.
- Performance: the freeze watchdog runs every 10 ticks and only loops over frozen players. Report counts in the card load in the background.
- Privacy: viewing a player card is logged.
