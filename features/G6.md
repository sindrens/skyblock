# Friends, parties and guest book · id: G6 · status: Draft · version: 1

## In one sentence
Three small social tools: a friends list to see who is online and where, short-lived parties of up to 5 with party chat, and a guest book where visitors leave one line on an island. Nothing is built yet.

## How it works
All of this is planned (FRIENDS_PARTIES.md). Numbers marked with an answer id are decided; the rest are `(proposed default)`.
- **Friends (`/friends`):**
  - Add by request: both sides accept. Max 100 friends (Q1; the plan doc says 50, see open question 1).
  - Menu shows online friends first, with where they are (island, Depths tier, warzone). Buttons: visit island, `/tpa`, message.
  - Join and quit notices for friends, with a `/settings` switch.
  - The list belongs to the account, not to one profile `(proposed default)`.
- **Parties (`/party`, chat `/pc`):**
  - Leader invites, up to 5 players (Q2). Party chat with `/pc`.
  - The party ends when the leader leaves or after 30 minutes with nobody online `(proposed default)`.
  - Joins events and duels together; the Depths summon group uses the party once it exists (PHASE-8 PV20).
  - No sharing of XP or loot in the Depths (Q2). The plan doc proposes shared kill credit; see open question 2.
- **Guest book:**
  - A book item or `/is guestbook`. A visitor writes one line, max 60 characters, through the chat filter `(proposed default)`.
  - One line per visitor per island per day (Q3). The leader can delete lines; staff can read them (Q3). The last 50 are kept `(proposed default)`.
- **Timing (plan suggestion):** friends and guest book soon (small), parties with Phase 9.

## What the player sees
- Menus only, so Bedrock never types long commands. Friend requests and party invites arrive as an Accept / Deny popup menu (QoL 15) as well as clickable chat.
- The Community category of the planned smart `/menu` (G9) holds Friends and Parties buttons.
- Tab list does not sort friends first. Friends and island members are found in `/is` and the friends menu.

## Rules and limits
- No value moves between profiles or accounts through these tools: no shared loot, no gifts, no teleporting items (principle 2).
- Anti alt-farm: a party gives no extra rewards. Mini-boss rewards stay full for each player with 10% damage (770). Planned (decided): boss health grows +25% per extra eligible player, up to 10, and eligible means 10% of the damage, a hit in the last 90 s, not AFK, max 3 accounts per IP (R3-10). No guest passes: a higher player cannot bring lower friends into locked zones (821).
- `/ignore` still wins: an ignored player cannot send you a friend request, party invite or guest book line `(proposed default)`. Staff cannot be ignored.
- Guest book text uses the chat filter (304). English only. Every line is audited like chat `(proposed default)`.
- Performance: friend and party data kept in memory for online players only, saved on change. Online status from the join/quit event, no scans.
- Requests are rate limited to stop spam `(proposed default)`.

## Connections
- Island visiting (B5), warps and likes (B6), island chat (B8). Visit browser gets a "friends' islands" filter (idea 62).
- Private messages and ignore (G2), teleports (G11): a friend could skip the `/tpa` accept step later (QoL doc, approved with 63, not built).
- Visibility (REVIEW3 D4, G10): the "Friends + island" mode needs this friends list. Mini-bosses (E7). Events and duels (I3, I2).
- Player picker sorts friends first (QoL 6). Hub menu (G9).

## Decided
- 63: friends list, yes (`/friend add`, see online friends, join their island quickly).
- 64: parties, yes (temporary group for dungeons and events, party chat).
- 66: guest book, yes (visitors leave a short message on an island).
- Q1: friends max 100, requests, location shown, join notifications.
- Q2: party max 5, party chat `/pc`, no sharing of XP or loot in the Depths.
- Q3: guest book one message per visitor per island per day, leader can delete, staff can read.
- 316, 317: `/ignore` rules. 304: player text goes through the chat filter.
- 770: full mini-boss rewards for everyone with 10% damage. 821: no guest passes.
- R3-10: mini-boss health +25% per extra eligible player, up to 10. Eligible = 10% of the damage, hit in the last 90 s, not AFK, max 3 accounts per IP. Rewards stay full for each eligible player. (Replaces the older "+100% cap", answer 797.)
- REVIEW3 D4: visibility Everyone (default) / Friends + island / Nobody, plus pinning a player, built as one shared visibility service. "Friends" comes from this feature's friends list.

## Open questions
1. Max friends: 100 (Q1) or 50 (plan doc, "more with ranks")? (default: 100)
2. Parties in the Depths: shared kill credit and mini-boss damage counting for the party (plan doc) or no sharing (Q2)? (default: Q2, no sharing)
3. Does the friends list belong to the account or to each profile? (proposed default: account)
4. Guest book: 60 characters, last 50 kept, `/is guestbook` plus a book item? (default: yes)
5. Does ignoring a friend remove the friendship, and may friends skip the `/tpa` accept step? (proposed default: yes to the first, no to the second at launch)

## Rejected ideas
- Referral rewards for inviting a friend (idea 54): the owner said NO.
- Island members first in each player's tab list: too much per-player sorting work, so not now (TAB_LIST.md).
