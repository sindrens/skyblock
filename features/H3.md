# Reports · id: H3 · status: Draft · version: 1

## In one sentence
Players report cheaters, spam and bad behaviour with `/report`. Staff see it at once, claim it, close it with an outcome, and the reporter is told the result.

## How it works
- **Built: `/report <player> [reason]`** opens a reason picker menu, or takes a typed reason. 9 reasons today (`staff.yml: reports.reasons`): Hacking, X-ray, Toxicity, Spam, Advertising, Exploiting, Inappropriate build, Inappropriate name, Other. "Other" asks for free text (max 120 characters).
- **Planned (decided): 12 reasons (R3-31).** Add Scamming, Threats and Griefing. Scamming and Griefing already exist as punishment reasons in `punishments.yml`. Threats does not (see open question 1).
- **Built: limits.** 60s between reports, 3 open reports per player, one open report per target, no reporting yourself. Staff with `skyblock.report.bypass-cooldown` skip the cooldown. `/report` works from the first minute.
- **Built: staff alert.** A chat line "[Report #12] Steve reported Alex for Hacking (2 open, 5 total)" with [Teleport] [Inspect] [Claim] buttons and a sound. Staff with `skyblock.staff.reports` get it (helpers and up). On join they see "N open report(s)".
- **Built: `/reports [open|claimed|closed]`** is a menu. `/reports claim|unclaim <id>`, `/reports close <id> <outcome>`, `/reports player <name>`. Outcomes: punished, warned, no-evidence, invalid, duplicate.
- **Built: the reporter is told** the outcome in chat, also on their next join, for example "Report #12 on Alex: action taken. Thank you!"
- **Built: report link.** When staff punish a player, the reports about that player that they claimed close as "punished". Unclaimed reports stay open and staff get "2 open reports on Steve [Open /reports player Steve]".
- **Built: chat evidence.** A mute or warning saves the player's last 10 public chat lines plus 5 from others, copied from the audit log (IPs removed, kept 180 days).
- **Planned (decided): DM evidence (R3-9a).** `/report` also attaches the last 10 private messages between the reporter and the reported player. This is the only way DMs become evidence. The copy is kept 180 days and the privacy page says so.
- **Built: false reports.** Only the outcome `invalid` counts. 3 invalid reports in 30 days give staff a suggestion for the reason `report-abuse` (warn, report-block 3d, 14d, mute 1d), at most one per player per 7 days, never automatic.
- **Built: report block.** A punishment that stops `/report`: "You can't use /report for 2d (too many false reports)."
- **Planned (decided): reports about staff go only to admins (540).** Not in the code.
- **Planned (decided): a "severe" reason for threats, child safety and doxxing (520).** Not in the code.

## What the player sees
- The picker "Report <player>": "Pick what this player did. Staff get your report right away. False reports can be punished."
- After sending: "Report #12 on Steve sent. Thanks! You'll hear the result."
- Refusals: "Report again in 40s.", "You have 3 open reports. Staff will check them soon.", "You already reported them."
- Planned (decided): the picker or the privacy page says the last 10 private messages with that player are sent along.
- Staff see the `/reports` menu with Open, Claimed and Closed tabs.

## Rules and limits
- Anti-abuse: cooldown, open-report cap, one report per target, false-report penalty, report block.
- Minimum playtime before `/report` is a setting (`reports.min-playtime-minutes`). It is 0 and stays 0: R3-31 keeps reports open from minute 1, so new players can report.
- Helpers see private messages only through a report (318). Today helpers can also read them in `/logs`; the fix is in Logs (H5).
- Reports are stored in the staff database (`reports`, `report_blocks`) and every claim and close is audited.
- Bedrock: reason picker and `/reports` are menus. Close buttons are menu clicks.
- Performance: nothing runs per tick. Report counts load in the background. The DM copy is one read of the last 10 lines when `/report` is sent.

## Decided
- 513: only public chat is evidence. Changed by R3-9a: DMs count only through `/report`.
- R3-9a: `/report` attaches the last 10 DMs between reporter and reported as evidence. Evidence is kept 180 days. The privacy page says so.
- R3-9b: helpers can't read DMs in `/logs` (fix in H5). Mods and up keep access.
- R3-31: `/report` works from minute 1. Add the reasons Scamming, Threats and Griefing (open question 542). This replaces 539 (10 minutes of playtime).
- 520: a "severe" reason for threats, child safety and doxxing. Not built.
- 540: reports about staff go only to admins. Not built.
- 148: island chat is saved in a log staff can read when handling a report (30 days).
- Spec 14.5: a false-report penalty exists, suggested only, never automatic.

## Open questions
1. Which punishment reason does a "Threats" report lead to? Default: the severe reason (520) once built, and "toxicity" (rule 1) until then (proposed default).

## Rejected ideas
- Counting "no-evidence" or "duplicate" closes as false reports: they are honest mistakes.
- Automatic punishment of false reporters: staff always click.
- A 10-minute playtime wait before `/report` (539): replaced by R3-31, because new players are the main targets.
