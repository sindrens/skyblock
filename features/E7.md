# Mini-bosses · id: E7 · status: Draft · version: 1

## In one sentence
Every combat tier has a mini-boss that appears every 10 minutes while players are in the zone. Everyone who did at least 10% of its health gets the full rewards. Today they are tough placeholders; real abilities, health scaling and anti-alt rules are planned.

## How it works
- **Built (placeholders):**
  - **Ten mini-bosses:** Grave Warden, Crypt Archer, Broodmother, King Slime, Tide Caller, Coven Elder, Raid Captain, Forge Lord, Bone King, Warlord (tier 1 to 10).
  - **Timer:** one per combat zone, 10 minutes after the previous one died (`mobs.yml: mini-boss-minutes`). The first comes 10 minutes after the zone wakes. Only while players are in the zone, only one at a time. If the zone is empty for 30 seconds it is removed.
  - **Stats:** about 15x the health of a normal mob (Grave Warden 300 vs 20), 2x damage, bigger size, glowing outline, name always visible, a boss bar for players in the zone, a chat line "Mini-boss Grave Warden appeared in Graveyard!" and a spawn sound.
  - **Rewards:** about 15x drops, 20x Combat XP and 20x crystals, plus a 30% chance of a Combat enchant book, one rarity band better (`enchanter.yml: drops.mini-boss-*`).
  - **Who gets them:** every **profile** that dealt at least 10% of the boss's health (`combat.mini-boss.min-damage-share: 0.1`), and the killer always. Damage is counted per profile, not per account.
  - Mini-bosses carry a boss tag so Giant Slayer and Executioner work on them.
- **Planned (Phase 8, I4):**
  - Two abilities each (for example Broodmother summons spiders and slows; Forge Lord volley and fire ring; Warlord charge and slam), warned 1 second before they hit.
  - Leashed to the build's mini-boss arena (it walks back and heals 5% a second outside); 1 to 2 summon fragments for each rewarded player.
  - **Health scaling and anti-alt rules (R3-10, decided):** health +25% for each extra eligible player, up to 10 players. Eligible means: at least 10% of the damage, a hit in the last 90 seconds, not AFK, and at most 3 accounts per IP. Rewards stay full for every eligible player. Not built.

## What the player sees
- A red-star name above the boss, a boss bar with its health, a chat line when it appears and one when it dies: "X was defeated by Name."
- If you helped: "You helped defeat X. Rewards are in your satchel." with a money sound.
- Players with "Event sounds" off in `/settings` do not hear the spawn sound. The reward sound still plays.

## Rules and limits
- Anti-alt today: shares are per profile, so a second profile only earns if it really deals 10% of the boss's health itself. The recent-hit, AFK and per-IP rules of R3-10 are not built, so one person with several accounts can still farm a group fight.
- Death keeps your inventory (#76). No stacking and no PvP in the zone.
- Performance: one clock per zone, one boss at a time, damage shares in a small map per boss; no per-tick scans.
- Bedrock: boss bar and chat work; abilities need a visible warning, not only sound.
- Mini-boss rewards are event income inside the ~15% event share, never on top of Depths income.
