# Mob and item stacking · id: D1 · status: Draft · version: 1

## In one sentence
Mobs of the same kind and dropped items on islands merge into single stacks with a counter, so big farms and spawners cost the server almost nothing.

## How it works
- **Built: mob stacks.** Mobs of the same kind within 5 blocks join one stack of up to 1,024 (S2, S3). "Same kind" = type, spawner or natural, baby or adult, sheep colour and sheared, slime size. (stacking.yml: mobs.max-stack, mobs.merge-radius)
- **Built: where.** Only in the islands world. Never in The Depths, spawn, warzone or boss worlds. Outposts are added when they exist (D9). Stacking can't be turned off (S11).
- **Built: kills.** A hit kills one mob, unless a custom kill enchant (for example Slaughter) kills more (S4). Fall, lava, void and /kill kill the whole stack (S5), so grinders work.
- **Built: drops and XP** are multiplied by the number killed (S6). The loot table is rolled 8 times and scaled, not once per mob. Equipment is never multiplied.
- **Built: AI and despawn.** Spawner mobs have no AI (S7). Stacked mobs and spawner mobs never despawn (S12). Natural mobs keep normal AI.
- **Built: never stack:** named, tamed, leashed, saddled or ridden mobs, villagers, wandering traders, zombie villagers, allays, bosses (S9).
- **Built: breeding.** One food item breeds one pair: 15 cows + 5 wheat = 5 babies (S10). The parents rest 300 s. Shearing a sheep stack shears all of them.
- **Built: item stacks.** Dropped items of the same kind within 3 blocks merge into one item with a count, no maximum (S14). Players pick up what fits, the rest stays (S16). Hoppers take up to 64 per check (S17, see D3). Items vanish about 10 minutes after the last one joined (S18). Unique custom items (tools, notes, books) never stack (S19).
- **Built: island entity limits (SK2):** armor stands 20, item frames 60, minecarts 20, boats 10, villagers 20, display entities 30. The Entity Limits upgrade adds +50% (the upgrade itself is not built yet).
- **Built: max 8 stacks of one mob type per chunk** (SK1, each up to 1,024). More spawns join a stack in the chunk or are cancelled.
- **Planned (decided): total cap of 40 mob stacks per chunk**, all types together, on top of 8 per type (R3-26). There are 30 spawner mob types, so today's maximum is 240 stacks per chunk. A normal farm with 3 to 5 types never reaches 40.
- **Planned (decided): Combat XP only in Depths combat zones** (R3-24). Stacked island mobs will give no Combat XP. Today one stack-kill hit on a big stack can pay thousands of XP.
- **Planned:** one explosion kills at most 10 mobs from a stack (1008). Staff tool to clear or split huge stacks (1010). A rare action bar line when the limit cancels a spawn (1011, 992).

## What the player sees
- Name above a mob stack: `Zombie x45` (white name, gold count). Above items: `Diamond x1,234`.
- Refusals: "You can't name a stack. Name a single mob." (same for leash, tame, saddle, ride, shear).
- Breeding: "Bred 5. Stack: 20". Limits: "Island limit reached: 20 armor stands."
- No command and no menu. Help page: `/help spawners` (also found as stacking).

## Rules and limits
- Anti-abuse: stacks can't be named, tamed, leashed or ridden. Equipment is never multiplied. Unique items never merge, so no dupes. Before the faster hoppers go live (REVIEW3 F12), test item stacks with hoppers for dupes on full and partial stacks.
- Performance: no per-tick entity scans. Mobs merge when they spawn, using a chunk-indexed cache. Walking mobs are re-checked in a small budget (200 per 40 ticks). Items merge on spawn. A warning is logged above 5 ms of main-thread time per second. (perf.warn-ms-per-second)
- Entity limits count the island's loaded chunks only (no full scan).
- Bedrock: nothing depends on chat clicks. Names are plain text above entities.

## Connections
- Spawners (D2): spawner mobs join these stacks. Hoppers (D3): 64 per pickup, and the faster hoppers (REVIEW3 F12) must be tested with item stacks.
- Collectors (D7): catch the drops of stacks. Generators and crops (D6): crops that grow without light live in the same module.
- Enchants (E3): stack kill enchants use the kill hook. Selling (C4): stack loot sells like normal loot.
- Island upgrades (B14): Entity Limits. Outposts (D9): added to the stacking worlds later.

## Decided
- S2: max 1,024 per stack. S3: merge radius 5 blocks.
- S4: kills depend on the weapon. S5: fall and lava kill the stack. S6: full drops and XP.
- S7: spawner mobs have no AI. S9: named, tamed, villagers, leashed never stack.
- S10: breeding limited by the food given. S11: stacking can't be turned off.
- S12: stacked and spawner mobs never despawn. SK1: 8 stacks per type per chunk, each up to 1,024.
- S13 and SK2: entity limits as listed, +50% with an upgrade.
- S14 to S19: item stacking as described (suggested answers accepted).
- 1008: one explosion kills at most 10 mobs from a stack. 1010: staff clear and split tool. 1011: rare action bar line.
- 992: spawner output stops at the island's limits, with a "Limit reached" line.
- R3-26: 40 mob stacks per chunk in total (answer: Recommended).
- R3-24: Combat XP only from Depths combat zones (answer: Recommended).
