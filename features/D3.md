# Hoppers · id: D3 · status: Draft · version: 1

## In one sentence
Hoppers are fast vanilla hoppers with no count limit and no filter: a full stack of 64 items moves every 8 ticks, and a per-chunk counter alerts staff to hopper-heavy chunks.

## How it works
- **Built: timing.** Hoppers check and move every 8 ticks (spigot.yml: hopper-check 8, hopper-transfer 8). Hoppers can't load chunks, ignore blocks above them, and wait when full (Paper cooldown-when-full: true).
- **Built: bulk pickup from item stacks.** A hopper under a stacked item takes up to 64 items per check (S17, `items.hopper-per-transfer`).
- **Built: price and access.** A hopper costs $120 in /shop and is worth 0 for island level. Opening one needs the island role permission `containers`.
- **Planned (decided): 64 items per transfer.** `hopper-amount: 64` with `hopper-transfer: 8` (today `hopper-amount` is 1). A hopper then moves a full stack every 0.4 s, about 160 items/s (64 times vanilla), with far fewer move operations. Moving every tick would add work, not save it, so it stays at 8 ticks.
- **Side effect:** vanilla item sorters (one item per move) stop working. Collectors and storage do the sorting (D7).
- **Planned (decided): per-chunk counter.** A whole number per chunk, counted when a hopper is placed or broken, kept in memory and saved with the chunk data (or rebuilt when the chunk loads). No world scanning and no per-tick work.
- **Planned (decided): staff alert** when a chunk has more than 64 hoppers, at most one alert per chunk per hour.
- **Not planned:** filter hoppers, hopper menus, custom hopper blocks.

## What the player sees
- Hoppers just work faster. Nothing new to learn.
- Staff get a short alert with the chunk and the hopper count (text still to be written, proposed default).
- Island menu line "Hoppers 214" (proposed default).

## Rules and limits
- No block, hopper, piston or observer count limit (O1, O2, WR2). A hard cap comes only if the load test shows lag (R3-27).
- Before the 64-item setting goes live (REVIEW3 F12): test it with collectors (D7), stacking and its item events (D1) and island storage. There must be no dupes on full or partial stacks. Measure MSPT on a hopper-heavy test island.
- Do not set Paper `disable-move-event: true`. Plugins may listen to hopper moves.
- Lag-machine detection is planned, not built (a lag guard module, PERFORMANCE P-02). It covers redstone, pistons and observers too. Its third stage (removing a part) needs the owner's approval.
- Performance: the counter changes only on place and break. Bedrock: hoppers are normal blocks, no chat clicks.

## Decided
- O1: no hopper limit.
- O2: no filter hoppers (answer 39 = no, and no hopper limit).
- WR2: no piston or observer limits, detect lag machines only.
- S17: hoppers take from a stacked item in bulk, 64 per step.
- R3-27: keep "no limits", count hoppers per chunk and alert staff above 64. A hard cap only if the load test shows lag. (Owner: "make sure hoppers are 64 items per tick and yeah they're fast, this will reduce lag".)
- REVIEW3 F12: "yes, if this is optimized and safe 64 items every 8 tick". Settings: hopper-amount 64, hopper-transfer 8, hopper-check 8, cooldown-when-full true.
- REVIEW3 F13: "we need to make sure it wont lag, optimize or find a way to make it safe". Counter on place and break only, at most one staff alert per chunk per hour.

## Rejected ideas
- Filter hoppers (answer 39, O2): not wanted.
- A hopper limit per island or chunk (O1): lag detection and the counter instead. A cap only after a load test shows lag.
- Hoppers that move every tick (the owner's first wording): the review found it adds work, and the owner chose every 8 ticks (REVIEW3 F12).
