# Selling · id: C4 · status: Review 1 · version: 1

Cluster C4 + C5 + C6 (selling, shop, auction house). Shared questions are asked once, here (C4-Q1 to C4-Q5); C5 and C6 point to them. All prices and numbers of prices in this cluster are **placeholders**: the full price and reward overhaul is planned as C15 (card not written yet) after the whole server is planned (C1-R1-25, C1-R2-31). This review fixes the **rules** and the **engine**, not the price list.

## In one sentence
Players sell items to the server for money at a live price that falls when many people sell and recovers over time. Every plain block and material can be sold at a fair price that is worked out from what it is made of, so nobody can make money by crafting and selling. All sell bonuses add up and stop at +300%.

## How it works
**Built (stays as it is):**
- **`/sell`** opens the sell chest: 4 rows of free space, then buttons. Put items in (click, shift-click, drag) or press **Sell inventory**. Live total (items, value, bonus, you get). Closing the chest returns every item (inventory first; what does not fit goes to `/box`, never the ground: see Reality check 11).
- **`/sell hand`** and **`/sell all`** sell the stack in hand or every sellable stack. Also reachable from the Sell button of `/shop` and from the satchel (own Sell all).
- **Dynamic price** = base x multiplier between 40% and 150%. Each item sold lowers it; the drop per item is recomputed hourly from the last 24 h of sales so a steadily sold item settles at about 85% of base; every 10 min the gap to base closes by 2%; an item nobody sells for 60 min drifts up 10% a day. Inside one sale the price falls item by item, so splitting a sale pays the same.
- **Hot Item:** one item a day (Oslo date) pays +25% (price, not bonus).
- **Sell bonus:** every source is added up and capped once at +300% (x4.0, #46), after the dynamic price. A grinder ends the season at about +128%.
- **Safe sale:** items are taken, the player file saved, then one ledger transaction (`sell`, key `sell:<sale id>`) pays the active profile and writes `sell_sales`. If paying fails the items come back.
- **Never sellable:** unique items (tools, enchant books, money notes, set pieces), dupe-flagged items, staff items, starter kit items, vanilla items with a custom name, enchants or damage.

**Proposed (new in this review):**
1. **Every block sellable at a fair price (BUGS-FOUND #2).** `sell.yml` today lists 85 hand-priced items; the rest cannot be sold. New: three kinds of price.
   - **Base** (hand-priced, as today): things that come from nature or mobs: crops, ores, mob drops, Depths items, and bulk blocks (dirt, sand, gravel, stone types, logs, netherrack ...). Logs matter: no vanilla log has a price today, so every wooden block (planks, slabs, fences, doors) would stay unsellable without one.
   - **Derived** (computed, never typed): any block or plain material that has a vanilla recipe (crafting, smelting, stonecutting). Price = (sum of the live prices of its parts / pieces made) x `sell.derive.factor` (C4-Q1; proposed 100%). Cheapest recipe wins. If a part has no price the item stays unsellable. Examples at today's placeholder prices: iron block = 9 x $18 = $162, hay bale = 9 x $10 = $90, one cobblestone stair = $1.
   - **Not sellable:** unique and custom-named items, vanilla tools, armor, weapons, potions, spawn eggs, books, admin blocks, and anything newer than 1.21.5 (C1-R2-24).
2. **Price families (what makes it loop-proof).** A derived item has no live multiplier of its own: its live price is built from the live multipliers of its parts. Selling it pushes down its parts in proportion. If derived items kept a fixed price, a player could dump ingots to the 40% floor, craft blocks and sell them at full price. Families remove that.
3. **Bulk blocks pay pennies (C4-Q2).** Includes logs. Proposed: $0.25 each (a stack of 64 = $16). Selling dirt clears your inventory; it is not income. Real money stays in the Depths, farms and mobs. The sale total is rounded down once per sale.
4. **Market tips button** in the sell chest (C4-Q4): the 5 items paying the most above base right now and the Hot Item, so players switch what they mine or farm during the day (fights a single meta).
5. **Hot Item shown to everyone** (C4-Q3): once a day at 00:00 Oslo one chat line, one line on first join of the day, and a "HOT" tag in the sell chest. Only items with base price at or above `sell.hot-item.min-price` can be hot, so nobody gets "dirt is hot".
6. **Push limit (613, decided as "must be planned"):** one player (island members counted together) can move an item's server price at most 10% per hour. Past that, the player's own sales keep paying less (personal price keeps falling) but the server price stops moving. Dumping hurts only the dumper, and nobody can crash a price for the whole server.
7. **Price news (612, decided):** biggest mover of at least 15% in 24 h, at most one chat line per 30 min, only for items with base price at or above `sell.news.min-price`. Format: `[Market] Sugar cane is up 22% in 24 hours. » /sell` (the `[Market]` tag is the G1 category tag, GUI-Q15).
8. **Money only.** Everything sells for money (C4-Q5). Rare Depths items give crystals or shards when they drop (E5), not when sold.
9. **Keep Forge materials toggle** (700, 752, decided, not built): `/sell all`, Sell inventory and satchel Sell all skip the current Forge tier band's materials while the toggle is on (default on, `/settings`).
10. **Faster quick commands:** the 2 s cooldown between `/sell hand` / `/sell all` goes to 0 (the sale lock already blocks a second sale while one runs; a cooldown only annoys, principle 1).
11. **Recovery every minute** instead of every 10 min, same speed per hour (2% of the gap per 10 min = about 0.2% per minute). Prices move smoothly instead of in steps (SELL_PRICES). Decision 658 (the speed) is unchanged.
12. **Sell from the shop (owner GUI-Q8, `C4-R1-01`).** In `/shop`, right click on an item opens a sell GUI for it, middle click (or Shift + Right-click) sells every one of that item in the inventory at once, and a `Sell` button on the buy GUI covers Bedrock (I9 section 12, C5). These clicks use **exactly** the sell rules of this card: the same `SellService` quote and sale (dynamic price, Hot Item, push limit, bonus cap, ledger key `sell:<sale id>`, atomic, items to `/box` on failure), the derived price for craftable items (so buy-then-sell and craft-then-sell never gain), `Not sellable` on items this card refuses, no undo, a total under $1 pays nothing. A one-item sale includes hotbar slots because the player named the item. Developer default; the owner needs to veto only if he wants other rules.

## What the player sees
- Chest buttons: Sell inventory, Total, Your sell bonus (each source, "capped" when the cap cuts it), Market tips, green "Sell N items for $X". Prices and total refresh every second while the chest is open (built). If the price moved 5% or more since an item was added, the Sell button shows the new total first.
- Each item: price, trend word (Rising, Falling, Steady: words, arrows are not Bedrock-safe) and % from base. Derived items show "Worth what it is made of".
- A total under $1 shows "Too little to sell" instead of a button.
- A small sale: one action bar line. From $100,000 one chat line with a hover list.
- Refusals say why: "... is a unique item and can't be sold. Try the auction house (/ah)." `| Can't be sold` lore appears only inside menus, never on items in the inventory.
- `/sell price` (held item, or any item name) shows what the server pays now, without selling.

## Rules and limits
- Frozen players and players switching profile can't sell. `/sell all` and Sell inventory leave hotbar slots 1-9 alone by default (`/settings` switch; built). Armor and offhand are never touched.
- Sell-spike alert at 4x the hourly average and at least $50M an hour (C10). Sell XP for the Coinmaster set is capped at 8,000 an hour (built in wardrobe).
- Selling to the server is never a way to move value between your own profiles: it pays only the profile that holds the items.
- Placeholder rule: no price in this card or in `sell.yml` is final before C15.
- Bedrock: tap items in, press Sell, no typing. `/sell price` tab-completes item names.

## Developer decisions (defaults; the owner only vetoes)
- **Derivation engine.** At enable and on `/skyblock reload economy`, read the server recipe registry (crafting, smelting, blasting, smoking, campfire, stonecutting; not smithing) once on the main thread (about 1,500 recipes, under 50 ms), build the graph item -> cheapest recipe and iterate to a fixed point (depth cap 6, `sell.derive.max-depth`). Result is an immutable map; live price = a few multiplications. Hand prices above the derived value are ignored with a console warning (that is how loops start). Config: `sell.derive.enabled` (true), `sell.derive.factor` (percent, default 100), `sell.derive.exclude` (item ids), `sell.derive.overrides.<id>` (price), `sell.derive.types` (default blocks and plain materials).
- **Families.** Each priced item carries `roots` (root id -> amount per piece). Sale impact on a derived item is applied to its roots by value share in the same async price step. Hot Item on a root lifts its family; the shop invariant uses the same ceiling, so the x7.5 rule still holds.
- **No loop, proven by tests (no server):** a committed `recipes-1.21.5.json` export (made from vanilla server data by a Gradle task, no server started) feeds `NoCraftLoopTest`: for every recipe the output is worth at most its inputs; a property test sets random multipliers (0.4 to 1.5) on every root and checks no recipe cycle gains money. Also `DerivedPriceTest` (planks from logs, block from ingots, tag alternatives take the cheapest, unpriced part -> unsellable), `RoundingTest` (split sale never pays more than one sale; total under $1 pays 0 and is refused in the menu), `PushLimitTest`, `HotItemFilterTest`, config-key test.
- **Rounding.** Prices in `sell.yml` may be decimal; money is a whole-dollar `long`; the total of one sale is rounded down once, never per item.
- **Push limit.** In-memory sliding window per (island-or-player, item): sum of impact applied in the last 60 min, config `sell.push-limit.percent-per-hour` (10), `sell.push-limit.group` (`island`). Not saved over a restart. Simpler version if the owner wants less: no push limit; the 40% floor and the sell-spike alert are the only brakes.
- **One price path.** Satchel, AFK pool, sell wands (C12), auto-sell chests (D7) and the AH hints all call `SellService`; a source-scan test fails the build if any other class multiplies a sell price.
- **Never-sellable list (704).** Items with these custom types stay unsellable: Forge materials of the current tier band (toggle), blueprint parts, summon fragments, key fragments, quest items, anything with a unique item id. Data keys in `sell.blocked-data-keys`. Filled shulker boxes and bundles are never sellable. Vanilla gear (tools, armor, weapons) is not derived.
- **Overflow fix (Reality check 11).** Returns from a closed chest, a failed sale and a profile switch go inventory, then `/box`. The feet-drop and the ender chest step are removed; `sell_return_overflow` becomes `sell_return_box`. Test: full inventory plus 36 stacks in the chest ends with every stack in the box and nothing dropped.
- **Security.** No new money path: same escrow, save, one ledger row, refund on failure. Derived prices come from the same `SellService.quote`. A duped bulk block is worth pennies; the sell-spike and earn-rate alerts (C10) already see the rest. No undo for sales (the items are gone and prices moved); protection comes from the hotbar skip, Keep Forge toggle, unique-item rule and the preview total in the chest.
- **Performance.** Price map built once; hourly impact recompute and recovery steps async (as today); price news and Hot Item run on the existing async tick; no per-block or per-tick work. Menu refresh only for open sell chests.
- **Data.** `sell_prices` gets rows for families (root-only; derived items store nothing). Push-limit state not persisted. No new tables.
- **Bedrock.** All new buttons are plain slot buttons; "HOT" is a word, not a symbol. Market tips is a normal menu page.
- **Config keys (new).** `sell.derive.*`, `sell.push-limit.*`, `sell.news.enabled`, `sell.news.min-change-percent` (15), `sell.news.min-interval-minutes` (30), `sell.news.min-price`, `sell.hot-item.min-price`, `sell.hot-item.announce` (Q3), `sell.menu.market-tips` (true), `sell.cooldown-seconds` (0), `sell.recovery-interval-minutes` (1), `sell.keep-forge-default` (true). Everything else keeps its current key.
- **Permissions and commands.** `skyblock.economy.sell` (everyone) covers `/sell`, `/sell hand`, `/sell all`, `/sell price` (new). Staff: `/eco sell price <item>` shows base, live multiplier, family roots, Hot status (mod+, `skyblock.staff.economy.sell.view`, new); `/eco sell reset <item|all>` puts multipliers back to 1.0 with a mandatory reason and an audit row (admin, `skyblock.staff.economy.sell.reset`, new). Both tab-complete item ids and `all`. Docs in `docs/commands/economy.md`, rank grants in `server/permissions/economy.txt`.
- **Messages.** All texts in `messages/economy.yml` (`sell.*`, English only, UI_STYLE: red `|` bar, bold yellow `» ACTION`, `<c:sell>` for sell price lines).

## Questions for the owner
Shared with C5 and C6: Q1 and Q2 set the sell side of every price rule in the cluster.
1. **C4-Q1 Crafted items: what are they worth?** Recommended: 100% of their parts (a block = 9 ingots exactly). Alternatives: 90% (small craft tax), one low flat price.
2. **C4-Q2 Bulk blocks (dirt, sand, gravel, stone, logs): what do they pay?** Recommended: pennies, $0.25 each. Alternatives: $1 each like cobblestone, or keep them unsellable.
3. **C4-Q3 Hot Item: how loud?** Recommended: one chat line at midnight, one line on first join of the day, HOT tag in the chest. Alternatives: only in the sell chest; or also a hologram at the sell NPC.
4. **C4-Q4 Market tips button in the sell chest?** Recommended: yes (small new feature: 5 best-paying items now). Alternative: no, trend words are enough.
5. **C4-Q5 Can some rare Depths items sell for crystals or shards (695 example)?** Recommended: no, money only; rare items drop crystals or shards directly. Alternatives: a few rare items sell for shards; a few for crystals.
