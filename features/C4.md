# Selling · id: C4 · status: Draft · version: 1

## In one sentence
Players sell items to the server for money at a live price that falls when many people sell and recovers over time. All sell bonuses add up and stop at +300%.

## How it works
- **`/sell`** opens the sell chest: 4 rows of free space, then buttons. Put items in (click, shift-click, drag) or press **Sell inventory**. A live total shows items, value, your bonus and what you get. Closing the chest returns every item (the rest goes to `/box`).
- **`/sell hand`** sells the stack in your hand. **`/sell all`** sells every sellable stack in hotbar and inventory. 2 s between the two quick commands.
- Also reachable from the **Sell** button in `/shop` and in the satchel (the satchel has its own Sell all).
- **Prices** live in `sell.yml`: a base price per item (about 80 now: 50 vanilla, 33 Depths items). Not listed means not sellable. The draft goes live on the test server first (692).
- **Never sellable:** tools, enchant books, money notes, set pieces, starter kit items, dupe-flagged items, staff items (`/give`, `/i`, creative), vanilla items with a custom name or damage. Emeralds are off for now (693 turns them on with custom mob drops).
- **Dynamic price** = base x a multiplier between 40% and 150%.
  - Each item sold lowers it a little. The drop per item is recomputed every hour from the last 24 h of sales, so a steadily sold item settles at about 85% of base.
  - Every 10 min the gap to base closes by 2%. An item nobody sells for 60 min drifts up 10% a day.
  - Inside one sale the price falls item by item, so splitting a sale pays the same as one big sale.
- **Hot Item:** one item a day (Oslo date) pays +25%. It changes the price, not the bonus.
- **Sell bonus:** rank (no paid ranks exist yet), Sell Multiplier upgrade, Market Contacts mastery, sets, Gilded Touch, boosters and events are added together, then capped once at **+300%** (x4.0, #46). The cap is applied after the dynamic price. The model says nobody reaches it by normal play (a grinder ends at about +128%).
- **Safe sale:** items are taken first, the player file is saved, then one ledger transaction pays the active profile. If paying fails, the items are given back. A crash can't both keep the items and pay.
- **Planned (decided): a "Keep Forge materials" toggle** for sell all (700, 752). Not built.

## What the player sees
- Chest buttons: Sell inventory, Total, Your sell bonus (each source, "capped" when the cap cuts it), green "Sell N items for $X".
- Prices refresh every second while the chest is open. Each item shows its price, trend (Rising, Falling, Steady) and % from base.
- A small sale shows one action bar line. From $100,000 one chat line appears with a hover list of the items.
- Refusals say why, e.g. "... is a unique item and can't be sold. Try the auction house."

## Rules and limits
- Frozen players and players switching profile can't sell. `/sell all` and Sell inventory leave hotbar slots 1-9 alone by default (a `/settings` switch); armor and offhand are never touched.
- Sell-spike alert when the server sells 4x its hourly average and at least $50M in an hour (C10). Sell XP for the Coinmaster set is capped at 8,000 an hour.
- Bedrock: tap items in, press Sell. No chat clicks needed. Performance: prices are held in memory and saved every 5 min off the main thread; steps and the hourly recompute are async.

- #46: total sell bonus +300%. Design round (30 Oct): a chest to put items in, plus Sell inventory.
- R3-7 / REVIEW3 F7: bought ranks give 0% sell bonus; gameplay perks are never sold. Ranks won in play keep their bonus. Perks follow one progression track, planned separately (not built).
- R3-21a: spawner mobs drop vanilla loot, plus a short list of special drops the owner will pick (silverfish nether stars, villagers emeralds). Prices go into `CUSTOM_MOB_DROPS.md`; emeralds stay unsellable until then (693).
