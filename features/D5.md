# Island fuel · id: D5 · status: Draft · version: 1

## In one sentence
Island fuel is a tank of minutes that keeps your spawners, crops and collectors working while nobody is active on the island, so playing and AFK-pool time turn into production hours.

## How it works
- **Built: hooks only.** `hasIslandFuel` and `addIslandFuel` exist in the island service with a default of "no fuel yet". Spawners already ask `hasIslandFuel` before they run with nobody near. There is no tank and no fuel module yet.
- **Built: sources waiting for the tank.** The AFK pool saves 15 minutes of fuel per reward as a pending grant (D10). Ember and Cinder crates have fuel rewards (60 min, 15%, and 180 min, 10%); until the tank exists these pay crystals. Island achievements can add up to +10% fuel efficiency (AC6).
- **Planned (Phase 7, M7.1): three states**, checked every 60 s:
  - **Active:** a member did a real action in the last 15 min (break or place a block, hit a mob, harvest, fish, use a menu, chat, walk 5+ blocks). Production at full speed. The tank gains **+1 minute per minute** for the island, not per member.
  - **Fueled:** nobody active, tank above 0. Full speed (simulated if the chunks are unloaded, see D7). The tank loses 1 minute per minute times the burn rate.
  - **Asleep:** nobody active, tank empty. Crops pause, spawners stop, collectors idle.
- **Tank size:** 12 h at start, +2 h per Fuel Tank level, 12 levels, 36 h at level 12. (production.yml: fuel.tank-hours-by-level)
- **Burn rate** 1.0, lowered by the Fuel Efficiency mastery (MA8: fuel lasts +10%, +20%, +30%) and island achievements (max +10%). All together capped at +40%.
- **Other sources**, always capped by the tank (a full tank refuses, nothing is lost): the AFK pool 1:1 (max 8 h a day, one earning account per connection, R3-3), and **Fuel Cells** of 1 h, 3 h and 6 h from crates, the daily calendar, the battle pass and the island shop (rank kits are in question, see the open questions). Cells are bound to the profile and can't be traded, so money can't buy production hours.
- **One tank** runs the main island and all its outposts (D9). Outposts burn no extra fuel.
- Only your own active profile's island earns fuel. The Depths, spawn and visiting earn none. Coop players and visitors never earn fuel.

## What the player sees
- Island scoreboard line `Fuel 7h 20m` or `Fuel Active`. A fuel gauge in the island menu and in `/is production`.
- Join message if the island fell asleep: "Your island ran out of fuel 2h ago".
- AFK chat line today: "AFK reward: +$310 and 15 min of island fuel (100%)".
- Gauge lore: state, "Fuel: 7h 20m / 20h", "Burn: 0.9x (Fuel Efficiency II)", how to get fuel.
- Staff fix after bugs: `/isadmin fuel <player> add|set <minutes>` (planned, audited with a reason).

## Rules and limits
- Anti-abuse (R5): standing AFK on your island burns fuel, it does not earn it. The only AFK reward is the AFK pool. Coop and visitors add nothing.
- Fuel changes in memory and is saved every 30 s and on unload. Grants are one conditional database update with an audit row. A Fuel Cell is taken before the fuel is added and given back if the add fails.
- Performance: one check per island every 60 s, no per-tick work. Offline islands cost nothing until they load.
- Bedrock: everything is a scoreboard line, a menu gauge or a join message. No chat clicks.
- Budget (balance sheet): a regular player gets about 9 production hours a day (0.9 h active, 6 h AFK pool, 1.5 h cells).

## Connections
- AFK pool (D10): 15 min of fuel per reward. Spawners (D2): run offline only with fuel.
- Collectors and offline output (D7). Generators and crops (D6): crops pause when asleep. Outposts (D9): same tank.
- Upgrades and masteries (B14): Fuel Tank, Fuel Efficiency. Crates (F5), calendar (F7), battle pass (F9): Fuel Cell sources. Ranks (F2): rank kits are in question.
- Achievements (F3): fuel efficiency boost. Profiles (B17): cells are bound to the profile.
- Economy curve (C14): fuel sets the production hours in the model.

## Decided
- DESIGN 5.4: 1 minute on the island = 1 minute of fuel, 12 h at the start, grows with the Fuel Tank upgrade. Farms, collectors and spawners run on fuel when all members are offline.
- B4: offline production is simulated, never chunk-loaded.
- O10: the AFK pool gives fuel, small money and keys. RW3: the AFK pool needs no movement.
- 991: spawners use island fuel once it exists.
- MA8: the Fuel Efficiency mastery is wanted.
- RTA-42: island boost caps include fuel 10%.
- P10-17: the daily calendar gives fuel on some days.
- REVIEW3 F7: gameplay perks are never sold. A bought rank unlocks cosmetic and convenience perks only.
- R3-3: the AFK pool stays one earning account per connection, so its fuel is too.

## Open questions
1. PD1 to PD3: +1 minute per minute per island while a member is active, AFK on the island burns fuel, the Depths earn none, tank 12 h +2 h per level up to 36 h. Default in the plan: yes.
2. PD4: Fuel Cells as bound items. The crates module pays fuel minutes straight into the tank instead of a Cell item. Keep it direct? Default in the plan: Cell items, bound.
3. Rank kits as a fuel source: REVIEW3 F7 says gameplay perks are never sold, and the review proposed removing fuel from weekly kits (R3-7). Default: no fuel in rank kits.
4. PD34: new sub-command `/is production` for fuel and the offline report. The command list has `/is fuel` as a placeholder. Default: `/is production`.
5. Answer 990 says spawners work across the whole island while a member is on it. The built rule (S24) is "a player within 16 blocks". Which one? Default: 16 blocks, as built.

## Rejected ideas
- Fuel per member (a team of 5 would make 5 times the fuel).
- Earning fuel by standing AFK on the island (rule 9).
- Tradeable Fuel Cells: money would buy production hours.
- Keeping chunks loaded for offline production (B4): too heavy for 300 players.
