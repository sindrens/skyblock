# Depths v2 zones · id: E1 · status: Draft · version: 1

## In one sentence
The Depths is the main money world: a hub (The Gate) with three lines (Mine, Forest, Combat). Today every tier is its own zone (33 zones). The owner decided a v2 rebuild: 5 zones per line, tier palettes so players at different tiers share the same field, a dungeon boss hall and a main boss arena. The v2 plan file is not written yet.

## How it works
- **Built (v1 layout, `zones.yml`):**
  - **The Gate:** a 96 x 96 hub at 0, 100, 0 under a fixed night sky. Wing gates: Mine west, Forest east, Combat north. The strip south of it stays free for a possible fourth wing (#60).
  - **33 tier zones:** Mine 12, Forest 11, Combat 10, each 80 x 80 blocks joined by 32-block causeways. Each wing bends into a U inside the 2,000-block border.
  - **Each zone has:** a 5 x 5 gate (locked players see iron bars, E3), an arrival pad, an ore or log field of about 40 x 40 (per-player blocks, E2), placeholder palette blocks and an exit. Combat zones also have a 50 x 50 fighting arena and a mini-boss pad (E6, E7).
  - **Boss arena:** its own island far north (112 x 112), reached by a portal in Combat tier 10. Placeholder only; the world boss is planned (I4).
  - **Placeholders by code:** `/depths generate [zone|all]`. A zone with `built: true` is never touched again. Real builds only change coordinates, never code.
  - **Real builds so far:** The Gate, Mine T1-T3, Forest T1-T2, Combat T1-T2. Every other zone is a placeholder.
- **Planned (decided, REVIEW3 D1 to D4; R3-34):**
  - Owner's idea: "5 zones for each mine, each wood cutting, dungeon +1 on dungeon for boss, main boss area". Write and fact-check `docs/phases/PHASE-4-DEPTHS-V2.md` before building anything.
  - **Same world, your own tier:** you mine coal in zone 1; when iron unlocks, the same blocks show as iron to you. A player still at coal sees coal in the same spot. With visibility on, you see each other (E8).
  - **Lower tier (D2):** you can pick a lower unlocked tier. Each tier in a shared zone has its own drop table and quantities (E3, E5). The Forge and blueprints still need coal after iron unlocks (E10).
  - **Combat (D3):** layered mobs from the start, each player fights their own tier's mobs (E6). **Visibility (D4):** one shared service (E8).
  - **Mapping, 15 zones instead of 33 (proposed default):** zone 1 coal, copper, iron / oak, birch, spruce / graveyard, crypt, spider den. Zone 2 gold, redstone, lapis / jungle, acacia, dark oak / slime, drowned. Zone 3 diamond, emerald / mangrove, cherry / witch, pillager. Zone 4 amethyst, quartz / pale oak, crimson / blaze, wither skeleton. Zone 5 netherite, voidstone / warped / ravager and evoker. Plus 1 dungeon boss hall after combat zone 5, and the main boss arena.
  - **Engine work (from the review's notes):**
    - A palette per resource: one "slot" block that shows a different ore or log per player. The real block is the tier-1 block, so tier-1 players need no packets.
    - `activeTier` per player and zone, refreshed on level-up, profile switch and the 20-tick check. `ResourceBreakEvent` gets `getShownType()` and `getTier()`; XP, drops, hooks and ambient mobs must read them.
    - Hardness trap: the client times a break from the shown block, so the anti fast-break check scales by the shown block's hardness.
  - **Builds:** Mine T2/T3 and Forest T2 become zone-1 districts with one `//replace`. The layout generator must output 15 zones and 2 boss areas. New builder rule: each field uses one slot material.
  - **Order (proposed default):** palette spike on the coal zone (2 Java, 1 Bedrock), events and hooks, zone groups and tier selector, visibility service, forest, combat and boss scaling, builders start zone 2, an 80-bot load test.

## What the player sees
- Today: `/dungeon` menu "The Depths" with tabs Mine, Forest, Combat; each tier shows its name and "Click to teleport" or "Needs Mining 15 (9/15)". First visit shows a title. The gate of a locked tier shows iron bars only to you.
- Planned: the same field shows your tier's ore; a tier selector to go lower; other players seen or hidden by the visibility setting.
- The Depths world is protected: only ore and logs can be mined; nothing else can be broken or placed.

## Rules and limits
- No PvP in the Depths (#822). No flying (#825). Only resource blocks can be mined (#783). Mobs only from the plugin. Combat zones have safe arrival pads (#800). No guest passes into locked zones (#821).
- Performance: zone lookup is an index; placeholder building runs in slices of 3,000 blocks a tick. v2 packets: throttle to about 300 blocks a tick per player; a tier swap is about 4 KB on Java. Run an 80-bot load test before big Depths work.
- Bedrock: everything works from the `/dungeon` menu. Geyser turns a multi-block change into one packet per block, so Bedrock needs the throttle and a real test.
- Real builds keep the fields as exact cuboids and the gates as empty openings (BUILDER_BRIEF section 3.2).

## Open questions
1. Is the mapping above right (1 to 3 tiers per zone)? Default: as in the table.
2. What does the boss hall hold, and how does it differ from the main arena? Default: the hall is a sixth zone of the Combat line for the dungeon boss; the main arena stays the boss island for world bosses (I4).
3. Does the 2,000 border and the U shape still fit 15 zones? Default: keep the border; the plan sets zone size.
4. Do unlock levels, prices and drops stay as now? Default: yes; only layout, look and per-tier drop tables change (E3, E5).
5. Layered mobs and 300 players: can the server hold it? Default: the plan answers it after the 80-bot load test (E6).
