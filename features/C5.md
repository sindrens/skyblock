# Shop · id: C5 · status: Review 1 · version: 1

Cluster C4 + C5 + C6. Shared price questions are asked once under C4 (C4-Q1 craft value, C4-Q2 bulk blocks). All shop prices are **placeholders**: the shop and price overhaul is planned as C15 (card not written yet) after the whole server is planned (C1-R1-25, C1-R2-31). This review fixes the rules and the engine, not the list of items or prices.

## In one sentence
`/shop` sells building blocks, decoration, redstone, seeds, basics and tier 1-2 spawners for money. It has no buy-back price of its own and is a steady money sink; selling from the shop goes through the C4 sell price (sell GUI, middle-click, `/sell`). Buying is fast, nothing is lost by accident (undo, a second click for big buys), and a shop item can never be bought and sold again for a profit.

## How it works
**Built (Shop v2, GUI-3; stays as it is):**
- **9 tabs, about 253 items:** Essentials 26, Farming 27, Wood 24, Stone and Earth 28, Special 28, Colors 53 (2 pages), Decoration 28, Redstone 28, Spawners 11. `/shop <tab>` jumps in, the shop remembers your last tab until restart. Tabs are on every page.
- **Click an item -> buy page:** Buy 1, Buy 16, Buy 64, Fill inventory (as many as fit and you can afford), Type an amount (chat on Java, tap picker on Bedrock, `1.5k` works, up to 2,304). Non-stacking items show Buy 1 and Type. Java shift-click on a grid item buys one stack at once.
- **No confirm screen** (8, 9, R3-29). Instead: **Undo last buy** (slot 47, 15 s, full refund while the items are still in your inventory; a spawner until placed) and **a second click for big buys** (a buy over 50% of your money turns orange and needs a second click within 5 s; typed amounts and Java shift-click follow the same rule). Both switch off per player in `/settings`.
- **Full inventory:** you only pay for what fits; the rest of an already paid purchase goes to `/box`, never the ground.
- **Safe buying:** one ledger transaction per purchase (`shop_buy`, key `shop_buy:<purchase id>`, the purchase row in the same database transaction), active profile only. Not while frozen, in staff mode or switching profile. A crash never gives both the items and the money.
- **Buy above sell (712):** an item is for sale only if its price is above the most it (or what it turns back into, `contains:`) could ever sell for: base x 150% x Hot Item 1.25 x (1 + 300%) = x7.5. `BuySellInvariantTest` checks the bundled files; a broken item shows "Not for sale right now" and staff see it in the console.
- **Spawners tab:** tier 1 (pig, cow, chicken, sheep, rabbit) $117,000 and tier 2 (zombie, skeleton, spider, cave spider, slime, squid) $213,000, fixed price. Output falls per island and tier (built in the stacking module: spawners 1-16 at 100%, 17-32 at 60%, 33-64 at 35%, 65-128 at 20%, 129+ at 10%). The tile shows "Next works at X%" and "Pays back in about N h" (green up to 100 h, yellow up to 300 h, red "Poor deal"). Tiers 3-5 are not sold. Spawners stay tradeable (988).
- **Never in the shop:** anything from the Depths, tools above the starter set, enchant books, keys, shards, crystals, credits items, spawn eggs, TNT, beacons, elytra, totems (722), anything newer than 1.21.5 (C1-R2-24). Money never buys the core grind.

**Proposed (new in this review):**
1. **Auto price for new items.** A shop entry can say `price: auto`: price = `shop.auto-markup` (10) x the item's highest sell price, rounded up, at least $1. Adding the many ordinary blocks of C5-Q2 needs no hand-pricing and stays above the x7.5 rule. Hand prices (owner S4, S6, S7: hopper $2,500, bone meal $30, flint and steel $40 ...) always win.
2. **Click map (owner GUI-Q8, `C5-R1-01`; this answers C5-Q1).** On a shop item: left click opens the buy GUI (as built); right click opens a sell GUI for that item; middle click sells every one of that item in the inventory at once; Java shift-click still buys one stack (as built). Bedrock cannot right or middle click: the buy GUI has a `Sell` button and the sell GUI a `Sell all` button. Survival Java clients may not send a middle click in a chest screen, so Shift + Right-click does the same (the build checks both on the test server). Layout, wording and rules are in I9 section 12; every sell uses exactly the C4 price and rules (`SellService`).
3. **Breadth (C5-Q2).** Proposed: the shop sells ordinary blocks only (all common building blocks, wood types, stone types, colors, glass, plain decoration) and stays at about 250 to 330 items. Blocks that come from a place or a boss (Nether, End, ocean, outposts, Depths) stay play-only, so players trade them on the AH.
4. **Prices stay fixed (C5-Q3).** Proposed: the shop never moves a price by itself. Daily deals and a progress-locked Island tab are decided together with the price overhaul (C15), not now.
5. **No search in the shop (owner GUI-Q10, `C5-R1-02`; this answers C5-Q4: no).** Search exists only in the auction house. This reverses answer 718 (a shop search button, a suggested answer, not an explicit owner answer); the shop uses its 9 tabs.
6. **Staff tools in game.** `/eco shop price <item>` (mod+) shows price, sell value and ratio; `/eco shop disable|enable <item> <reason>` (admin) switches one item off or on at once without a file edit; audited; the owner emergency button of 628 for shop prices is the same command.

8. **Item grid = `List`** (6 rows, 7 x 4, wallet tile slot 4, Back 45, Sell 46, Undo 47, Close 49, pages 52 and 53). Rules, in this order:
   1. **One group per row.** Items belong to a group (`groups:` below); a group starts on a new row; a group of more than 7 splits evenly (9 = 5 + 4, 12 = 6 + 6), never 7 + 2.
   2. **Centred rows.** An odd row is contiguous and centred (1 = column 4, 3 = 3 to 5, 5 = 2 to 6, 7 = 1 to 7); an even row splits around the centre column with a gap (2 = 3 and 5, 4 = 2, 3, 5, 6, 6 = 1, 2, 3, 5, 6, 7). Slots come from the pure `Centered.grid(rowCounts)`.
   3. **Fixed order.** Groups in the file order; inside a group by price ascending, ties in vanilla (creative-tab) order; natural orders keep the vanilla order (the 16 colours in dye order, woods in log order); helpers (bone meal) last in their group. The price decides the order, never the item name.
   4. **Pages hold whole groups**, up to 4 rows; a group moves to the next page when it does not fit the rows that are left (so a page can end with an empty row). The page count is the count on the paper arrows.
   5. **No row captions, no label tiles, no empty gap rows**: the items explain their row (seeds look like seeds); there are only 4 rows.
   6. Items in no group form a last group `More`. Disabled items leave a hole of zero: the group is simply shorter.
   7. Flowers belong to Decoration, saplings to Farming; moving items between tabs is not part of this layout and waits for C15.

## What the player sees
- Item lore (UI_STYLE): `| Buy Price` (green), `| Sell Price` (red, only when `/sell` buys it), `| Information` (stack of 64, "You can afford: N"), then bold yellow `» CLICK to choose an amount`.
- Spawner tile: "Next works at 35%" and "Pays back in about 62 h" with the colour (today's placeholder income per hour: tier 1 $1,900, tier 2 $3,500).
- After a buy: action bar "✔ +64 Torch -$256 · $1,199,744 left" with a soft click; from $100,000 also one chat line.
- Errors say the fix: price, balance, "buy fewer or sell items first" and "Bought 40 of 64: no more room".
- A **Sell** button in the shop bottom row (slot 46, I9) opens the sell chest (C4). Undo plate in slot 47 counts the seconds left. The bottom row is otherwise minimal: Back, pages, Close (no Search, no `?`).

## Rules and limits
- The shop takes about 4% of what players earn (balance sheet). Items `/sell` also buys always cost more here than they sell for, so buy-then-sell loops cannot print money, and with the new derived sell prices (C4) the same test covers every sellable item.
- Optional per item: `requires: { farming: 10 }` (skill level), `enabled: false`.
- The shop never pays a price of its own for items. A sell click on a shop tile pays the C4 sell price only (same `SellService` path as `/sell`: dynamic price, Hot Item, push limit, bonus cap, atomic; items not sellable show `Not sellable` and do nothing). Credits are never accepted here (credit shop is separate, C1).
- Nothing moves between your own profiles: you buy with the active profile's money only.
- Bedrock: amount picker instead of chat. Performance: the catalog is read once; one purchase at a time per player.

## Developer decisions (defaults; the owner only vetoes)
- **Engine stays.** `ShopEngine` (one guarded ledger transaction, purchase row states `delivering`/`delivered`/`pending`/`review`/`refunded`), `UndoLedger`, `BigBuyGuard` are not touched except below.
- **`price: auto`.** Resolved at load from `SellService#maxUnitPrice` x `shop.auto-markup`; reload re-resolves; the resolved price is shown in `/eco shop price`. Hand price wins. If an item has no sell price, `auto` is refused at load with a console line and the item is hidden.
- **Invariant test extended.** `BuySellInvariantTest` also runs over derived sell prices (C4) with the exported recipe table (no server). Known and expected exclusions go in `sell.derive.exclude`; any other conflict fails the build.
- **Sell GUI and click map (GUI-Q8).** A new `SellItemMenu` (3 rows) next to `BuyMenu`, built from the same pieces (amount buttons, type an amount, Back, Close). Right click, Shift + Right click and middle click map to it through a pure click-to-action function (unit tested); every sell calls `SellService` (no price path of its own); a shop-to-sell round trip test over the bundled catalog proves no item sells for more than it costs (`BuySellInvariantTest`). Bedrock buttons: `Sell` on the buy GUI, `Buy` and `Sell all` on the sell GUI. Shift + Left keeps buying one stack.
- **Staff commands and permissions.** `/eco shop price <item>` (`skyblock.staff.economy.shop.view`, mod+), `/eco shop disable|enable <item> <reason...>` (`skyblock.staff.economy.shop.toggle`, admin). Runtime switch in table `shop_overrides` (item, enabled, reason, who, when), loaded into memory, audit row `shop_item_toggle` (category `economy`). Tab completion: item ids from the catalog, `enable`/`disable`, reason hint. Permissions in `modules/shop/src/main/resources/permissions/shop.yml`, grants in `server/permissions/shop.txt`, docs in `docs/commands/shop.md` and `docs/commands/economy.md`. The existing node `skyblock.shop.use` (everyone) stays.
- **Security.** No change to money flow. Spawner and gear items come only from the engine's own item factory; `shop_review` rows stay staff-visible after a crash. Per-profile start money ($1,000, 121) cannot be turned into value on another profile (trade and AH both refuse own-profile partners).
- **Performance.** Catalog and `shop_overrides` in memory; one purchase at a time per player; no per-tick work; undo timer is a one-shot task per buyer.
- **Data and migration.** Config version 2 stays. New table `shop_overrides` through a module migration; no data change to `shop_purchases`.
- **Config keys (new).** `shop.auto-markup` (10). Existing keys are unchanged. Middle click and Shift + Right-click both sell all of the item; no setting.
- **Bedrock.** Every new control is a slot button or a form; no chat clicks required.
- **Docs fix.** `docs/commands/shop.md` spawner "100% until built" line is corrected in the build.
- **Grid layout (v3 proposal).** `shop.yml` gets an optional `groups:` list per category (`groups: [{id: seeds, items: [wheat_seeds, ...]}, ...]`, item ids only; prices stay in `items:`; no group list = today's flat order, so old files keep working). New pure `ShopGrid` (shop module) turns groups into pages (rules 1 to 6), `Centered.grid(rowCounts)` (common) gives the slots; `ShopMenu` and `ShopCategoryMenu` only place what they are given. Group titles come from `messages/shop.yml` `groups.<category>.<id>` (used by `/admin`, not drawn). `categories` order in `shop.yml` is changed by hand to the order of point 7. Tests (no server): `ShopGridTest` over the bundled `shop.yml` (every enabled item placed exactly once, no group split across rows except by the even split, Farming = 2 pages with the mock's slots, Colors = 2 pages, a disabled item shortens its group), `CenteredGridTest` for 1 to 9 per row. No engine, price or invariant change; `BuySellInvariantTest` untouched.
