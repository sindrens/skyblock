# Shop · id: C5 · status: Draft · version: 1

## In one sentence
`/shop` sells building blocks, decoration, redstone, seeds and utility items for money with fast one-click buying. It never buys anything back and is a steady money sink. An undo for the last purchase is decided but not built.

## How it works
- **Built: 8 categories, 332 items.** Building blocks, wood, stone and bricks, glass and colors, decoration, redstone, farming, utility. `/shop <category>` jumps straight in. Prices are per item in `shop.yml` (a draft; the owner approves them, test server first, 711).
- **Built: quick-buy grid** (28 items per page).
  - Java: left-click buys 1, right-click buys a stack (64), shift-click opens the amount screen.
  - Bedrock: a tap opens the amount screen (-64 -16 -1, +1 +16 +64, type an amount like `1.5k`, "Buy N for $X"). Up to 2,304 per purchase.
- **Built: no confirm screen.** Quick-buys never ask (8). If you can't afford a full 64, nothing is bought and you are told to buy fewer or sell items first (9).
- **Built: full inventory.** You only pay for what fits. The rest of a purchase that was already paid goes to `/box`, never on the ground.
- **Built: buy above sell (712).** An item is only for sale if its price is above the most it could ever sell for (base x 150% x Hot Item x (1 + 300%) = x7.5). A build test checks `shop.yml` against `sell.yml`; a broken item shows "Not for sale right now" and staff see it in the console.
- **Built: safe buying.** One ledger transaction per purchase (`shop_buy`) on the active profile. A crash never gives both the items and the money back. Not while frozen, in staff mode or switching profile.
- **Planned (SHOP.md, needs owner input):**
  - Three tabs in one `/shop`: Builder (today), Island (seeds, saplings, spawners, generator parts, fuel; items unlock with island level or Depths tier), Daily deals (6 items at -20%, same for everyone, new at 00:00 Oslo).
  - Search by name (718), lava and water buckets and flint and steel (721). Spawn eggs, TNT, beacons, elytra and totems stay out (722).
  - Three shop NPCs in the Market Square (Builder, Farmer, Merchant).
  - **Planned (decided): spawners** at a fixed price, tier 1 $117,000 and tier 2 $213,000 (REVIEW3 F2, D2). Output falls per island and tier: spawners 1-16 at 100%, 17-32 at 60%, 33-64 at 35%, 65-128 at 20%, 129+ at 10%. Tiers 3-5 are not sold. The numbers are fine-tuned in the D2 card.
- **Planned (decided): undo last buy (REVIEW3 F14, R3-29).** For 15 s after a buy, an Undo button in the shop refunds the full price while the items are still in your inventory (a spawner until it is placed). A buy over 50% of your money needs a second click on the same button. Both can be switched off per player in `/settings` (default on). It is a menu button, so it works on Bedrock.

## What the player sees
- (Planned, decided) The `/shop` tile for a spawner shows payback: "Pays back in 171 h (about 14 days at your 12 h/day)". Green up to 100 h, yellow up to 300 h, red "Poor deal" above. The spawner menu shows the same.
- Item lore: `| Buy Price`, `| Sell Price` (what /sell would pay), `| Information` (Stack of 64, "You can afford: N"), then bold yellow `» LEFT-CLICK buy 1` / `» RIGHT-CLICK buy 64` / `» SHIFT-CLICK pick amount`.
- After a buy: action bar "✔ +64 Oak Log -$512" with a soft click. From $100,000 also one chat line.
- Errors: price, your balance and "buy fewer or sell items first"; "Bought 40 of 64: no more room".
- A **Sell** button in every shop menu opens the sell chest (C4).

## Rules and limits
- The shop takes about 4% of what players earn (balance sheet). Items that `/sell` also buys always cost more here than they sell for, so buy-then-sell loops can't print money.
- Optional per item: `requires: { farming: 10 }`, `enabled: false`.
- Bedrock: amount screens instead of long commands. Performance: the catalog is read once; one purchase at a time per player.
