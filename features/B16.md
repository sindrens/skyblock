# /is top and leaderboards · id: B16 · status: Draft · version: 1

## In one sentence
Island leaderboards rank islands by level and value, with separate solo and team boards, shown in a menu and on holograms in the Hall of Heroes.

## How it works
- **Built: `/is top` menu.** Tabs: Level and Value (block worth). A Solo/Team toggle (separate boards; you start on your own kind). Places 1 to 28 and your own island's place. Ranked from memory; refreshed every 5 min (`top.refresh-seconds` 300).
- **Built: tie-break.** Level, then total points, then who reached it first.
- **Built: one island per account.** A player's best profile counts, so one person can't fill a board with profiles (P7, P5-29). Boards use one island per leader account.
- **Built: Hall of Heroes.** The NPC and hologram module has board sources `island_level` and `island_value` (`all`, `solo` or `team`). The boards `island_top_solo` and `island_top_team` use them; #1, #2 and #3 get their own colors.
- **Built: placeholder.** `<ph:island_rank>` is your place on the solo or team board.
- **Planned (M5.7): Weekly gain and Pillars tabs.** The tabs exist in the menu as grey "Coming soon".
  - Weekly island gain: earned points this week (pillars, upgrades, masteries, achievements). Week starts Monday 00:00 Oslo. Live sources are ignored, so moving blocks or money on Sunday night does nothing (P5-26).
  - Weekly pillar gain and a board per pillar.
  - Hall panels `weekly_island_gain` and `weekly_pillar_gain` switch between Solo and Team every 10 s.
- **Planned: weekly rewards (V1: keys).** Top 3 solo and top 3 team islands. Every member on the island 3 or more days that week with 1 h played gets the key. The plan proposes #1 Inferno, #2 Blaze, #3 Cinder `(proposed default)`. Paid through the crate service.
- **Planned: season end.** A final snapshot (top 10 solo and team) feeds won ranks, store-credit prizes and Hall of Fame statues (I5). Value history per season, exportable as CSV for the owner (V2).
- **Planned: staff islands** are hidden on boards (like `/baltop`).
- **Planned (decided): contributor rewards.** Each island's biggest contributors also get season rewards, planned with Phase 5 and Phase 12 (REVIEW3 F11, see B4).

## What the player sees
- `/is top` or the Island menu: a menu with rows like "#1 Ashreach - Lv 104 - leader Name".
- Your place at the bottom; Solo/Team toggle button.
- Hall of Heroes holograms: "ISLAND TOP - SOLO", updated every 5 min. Right-click explains the board.

## Rules and limits
- Boards are computed off the main thread and cached; opening the menu reads memory only.
- Weekly boards use earned points only. Live sources (block worth, bank, members) can go down; earned ones never do.
- Rewards are paid once per member and week (idempotency key). Alts can't stack: one island per account, 3-day and 1 h rules.
- Islands are wiped at season reset, so boards reset too (296).
- Bedrock: plain clicks; hologram boards need no clicks.
