# Island level and pillars · id: B13 · status: Draft · version: 1

## In one sentence
Every island has an Island Level that grows from many things (pillars, skills, Depths progress, bank, achievements, blocks), not only from block value, so playing well matters more than hoarding.

## How it works
- **Pillars (built, M5.1):** Mining, Foraging, Farming, Combat. A fifth, Conquest, stays hidden until KOTH or events feed it (`pillars.yml: conquest.enabled`).
- **Pillar XP** is the base skill XP that members earn (before rank, booster, enchant and set bonuses), so donors never push the island. Also: ripe crops on the island (Farming), natural logs (Foraging, placed logs give nothing), spawner mobs killed by your own hit or arrow (Combat; fall, lava and stack kills give 0). Coop players and visitors give nothing.
- **Curve:** XP for the next level = 250 x 1.12^level, no cap. 5 members give 5x the XP, but only about 14 levels more. Levels never go down in a season.
- **Balance bonus:** +2% pillar XP per pillar within 10 levels of the highest, max +10% (family cap +25%).
- **Synergy milestones:** when all shown pillars reach 10, 25, 40 and 60, every member gets crystals once: 500, 1,500, 4,000, 10,000, plus a title and chat line.
- **Island Level (built, M5.2) = floor(total points / 10)** from 8 sources:

| Source | Points | Cap |
|---|---|---|
| Pillars | 1 per pillar level | none |
| Upgrades and masteries | per level bought | none (gives 0 until B14) |
| Island achievements | from the achievements module | 10% |
| Collections | per milestone | 5% (gives 0 until F10) |
| Members' skills | average total skill level / 10 | 8% |
| Depths progress | best member's open tiers, 1 each | 6% |
| Island bank | 4 x log10(lowest balance of 24 h / $100k) | 5% |
| Block worth | 0.5 x sqrt(worth / $1,000) | 20% (O3) |

  Caps are solved together so each capped source stays under its share of the final total.
- **Block worth:** live from place, break, piston and explosion events; a nightly rescan (04:00 Oslo) for changed islands; a Recalculate button (members, once per 10 min per island). Items in chests never count. Spawners count per spawner by tier.
- **Planned (decided): per-member attribution.** Pillar XP and upgrade/mastery points will also be recorded per member (their current stint), so a kick can remove half of that member's contribution and season rewards can go to the biggest contributors. Kept points become unattributed: they still count for the level. Today nothing per member is stored here. (REVIEW3 F9, F11, see B4)
- **Recalculation:** after a pillar or skill level-up, a bank deposit, a new member or a block change, with a 5 s debounce (max wait 30 s). Online islands refresh every 10 min.

## What the player sees
- `/is level` (or the island item in the island menu): level, "% to next", place on the board, points per source with cap and how to raise it, the Recalculate button, the pillar row.
- A title, chat line and sound on a new season-high level; every 10th level also a global chat line. Pillar level-up lines can be switched off in `/settings`.
- Scoreboard Level line; placeholders `<ph:island_level>`, `<ph:island_rank>`, `<ph:pillar_mining_level>` and others.

## Rules and limits
- Money can't buy the level: bank uses a log scale and the lowest balance, blocks use a square root and a 20% cap, upgrades also need tasks.
- Members' skills and Depths tiers count only members who joined 3 or more days ago; a member who leaves takes their points with them.
- Coop players never count, nor creative or spectator play.
- XP is held in memory and written every 10 s in batches. A crash loses at most one interval and never counts twice.
- Bedrock: menus only.

## Decided
- 168: island level is dynamic, from many things besides block value.
- O3 and P5-2: block worth counts with a square root and is at most 20%.
- P5-1: level = points / 10 from the 8 sources.
- P5-3: block worth, bank, skills and Depths can go down; pillars, upgrades, masteries and achievements never do within a season.
- P5-4: skills and Depths count only members of 3 or more days.
- P5-6: pillar XP is base skill XP before rank and personal boosts.
- P5-8: Conquest is hidden until it has a source.
- P5-25: `/is level` and `/is top` are new sub-commands.
- 275: show the level line only once levels exist.
- R3-12 and REVIEW3 F9 ("Recommended"): on a kick the island keeps 50% of the member's contribution (they count for level, not for prizes). Bought perks are never revoked; only later "island level X" gates can drop.
- REVIEW3 F10: leaving by choice keeps 100% for the island.

## Open questions
1. Pace targets (pillar 10 in 1-2 h, 25 on day 2-3, 40 in week 2, 60 in month 2) are not tuned yet; the M5.9 balance pass (BAL3) is pending. Default: keep the starting curve.
2. Caps: the plan said "apply once, smallest first"; the code solves them together. Default: as built.
3. Farming pillar XP from island crops pauses once the Farming skill pays (Phase 7). Default: as built.
4. Season value history (V2), its CSV export and `/isadmin progression` (P5-31) are approved but not built. Default: with M5.7 and M5.10.

## Rejected ideas
- Keeping the level at its highest value when blocks are removed (168): the level is dynamic.
- Letting rank or booster XP feed pillars (RK20).
