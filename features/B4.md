# Contribution · id: B4 · status: Draft · version: 1

## In one sentence
The island keeps a record of what each member did for it, so a kick costs the island fairly, a leaver costs it nothing, and the biggest contributors get season rewards.

## How it works
- **Built: stats with shares.** `/is stats` counts, per island and per member, in day, week and all-time buckets. Click a category to see every member's part: head, share in whole % (always adds up to 100), this week, today. Former members keep their numbers and show as "Former member".
- **Built: categories.** Blocks mined, logs chopped, crops harvested, blocks placed, mobs killed (stack kills included), time on the island (sampled every 60 s), items sold, money earned. Island-wide only: generator output, unique visitors per day. KOTH time and bosses killed show `-` until those features exist.
- **Built: anti-abuse.** Only stored members count (never coop or visitors, never creative or spectator). A block placed recently (last 512 per island) gives nothing when broken again; ripe crops always count.
- **Built: what stays.** XP and counts a member earned stay with the island when they leave (A5). Task progress and pillar XP are shared and also stay.
- **Planned (decided): contribution on kick** (Phase 5). Contribution = earned island points from the member's current stint: pillar XP per member, upgrade and mastery points (split by deposit share at the time of purchase), and bank deposits. Live sources (skills, Depths tier) already leave with the member. Phase 5 attributes nothing per member yet, so this tracking is new work.
  - **Kick:** the island keeps 50%. The kept half becomes unattributed points (they count for level, never for a prize share). The other half is deleted. Bought perks are never revoked; only later "island level X" task gates can drop. The kicked player keeps all personal progress and gets 50% of their unspent bank deposits back.
  - **Preview before the kick:** "Kicking Alex removes 21 points (Lv 38 -> 36). Bought perks stay. Alex gets $120,000 back."
  - **Leaving by choice:** the island keeps 100%. Staff kicks, bans and members inactive for 21 days or more do not trigger the loss.
  - Halving a 30% XP share costs about 1.4 levels per pillar, because levels grow x1.12.
- **Planned (decided): season rewards for the biggest contributors** of each island, "fair play" (REVIEW3 F11). Needs a plan with Phase 5 and Phase 12. Team season prizes are already decided to be split by contribution share, minimum 5% (REVIEW A6).

## What the player sees
- Island menu, Stats button, then a category with a list of heads and "share %".
- Categories without a feature yet show `-` ("Coming soon").
- The stats menu reloads every 10 s while open. Members only.
- (Planned) The kick preview above, and the contribution share next to each member in the members menu.

## Rules and limits
- Counters are in memory and written in one batch every 30 s and on shutdown. A crash loses at most one interval and never counts twice.
- Day rows are kept 35 days, week rows 104 weeks, totals forever (also for removed islands).
- The 50% deposit refund is one ledger transaction with an idempotency key, so a kick, a restore or a restart can never pay twice `(proposed default)`.
- Unattributed points never count toward a prize share, so a kick can't be used to hand points to someone.
- Bedrock: plain clicks only.

## Open questions
1. What are the contributor rewards (title, crystals, keys) and are they weekly or per season? Default: per season, decided in the Phase 12 plan.
2. How are "unspent bank deposits" counted when other members withdraw? Default: the member's net deposits, reduced pro rata by upgrades paid and withdrawals.
3. How is "inactive 21 days" measured? Default: days since the member last logged in.
4. Should "time on the island" stop counting while AFK (M2.6)? Default: count everything until AFK detection exists.
5. Items sold and money earned: docs/commands/islands.md says "not hooked yet", OPEN_QUESTIONS says every `/sell` sale counts. Check which is true before the card is locked.

## Rejected ideas
- Wiping a kicked player's profile as the cost of a kick (R3-12, SECFIX1): only the island's points are halved.
