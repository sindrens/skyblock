# Pay, balance, baltop · id: C2 · status: Draft · version: 1

## In one sentence
Players see their balances, send money or shards to each other, check the richest list and read their own money history. The $10k earned rule that stops alt feeding and sending gems are decided but not built yet.

## How it works
- **Built: `/bal [player]`** (also `/balance`, `/money`). All four balances of your active profile. Other players, online or offline, can be looked up (`skyblock.economy.balance.others`, default everyone).
- **Built: `/pay <player> <amount> [currency]`.** Money by default, or shards. Amounts like `1500`, `1.5k`, `2M`, `3B`. Offline players are fine.
  - Minimum $10 or 1 shard. A confirm screen from $1M or 1,000 shards (valid 30 s). 3 s between payments.
  - Refused: to yourself, to your own other profiles (P4), while frozen, for crystals (never) and for gems (the switch is off today). Muted players can pay.
  - Opens at the tutorial's bank step (656). There is no daily limit and no new-account timer (573, 131).
- **Built: `/baltop [currency]`.** Top 10, one line per player (their best profile, P7). The list is cached for 30 s and refreshed in the background; your own line is always live.
- **Built: `/transactions`** (`/txs`). Your own ledger in a menu, 28 per page. Filter by type (payment, selling, shop, auction, trade, interest, bank, note, admin, start balance) and time (all, 24 h, 7 d, 30 d). Staff changes show the staff reason (663).
- **Planned (decided): the $10k earned rule (REVIEW3 F1, R3-1).** A profile can send money away (`/pay`, notes, `/trade`, AH buying) only after it earned $10k itself. Start money, rewards and money from other players do not count. Sending to members of the island the sender is on, including island bank deposits, is always allowed. It ends the loop "make profile, pay the $1,000 away, delete, repeat". Receiving is not limited.
- **Planned (decided): gems in `/pay`.** Bought gems can be paid, earned gems are bound and can't be (REVIEW3 F3). No limits or gates, but every gem transfer is logged (REVIEW3 F4). Shards stay payable, crystals never (R3-5b). The existing confirm at 100 gems stays.
- **Planned (decided): 24 h trade block (R3-28).** After the system finds a duped unique item, the holders can't use `/pay`, notes, AH or `/trade` for 24 h. It ends on its own; staff decide after.
- **Planned, decided but not built:**
  - Gems hidden from other players and no gems list (574, 575). Today `/bal <player>` still shows gems and `/baltop gems` works.
  - Staff accounts hidden from `/baltop` (576). A `Bank: $X` line in `/bal` (577). `/baltop` counts purse plus personal bank (593).
  - A join line for payments received while offline, one per payer, max 5, then "and N more" (579).

## What the player sees
- Pay: "Paid $500 to Steve. Balance: ..." and "Got $500 from Steve." The confirm line has a clickable [Confirm].
- Errors: "You can't pay yourself.", "You can't pay your own profiles.", "The smallest payment is $10.", "You don't have ...".
- `/baltop`: "Richest players (Money) updated 12s ago", own colours for #1, #2 and #3, "You're not in the top 10. You have ...".

## Rules and limits
- Every payment is one atomic step with a ledger row. A frozen or trade-blocked player is refused; if the sanction check itself fails, money leaving an account is refused (fail closed).
- Alerts, never automatic punishment: one transfer of 0.5% of the money supply (from $50M), 5 near-equal transfers to the same player in 10 minutes (from $10k), and any transfer of $5M or more to or from a staff account.
- Bedrock: amounts are typed (`1.5k`), the confirm and history are menus. Performance: `/baltop` is one cached query per currency; 300 players cause one read.

- 574 / 575: gems hidden from others, no gems list. 576: staff hidden from `/baltop`. 577: bank line in `/bal`.
- 578: whole dollars only. 579: join line for offline payments. 593: `/baltop` counts purse plus personal bank.
- 656 / TUT-4: `/pay` unlocks at the tutorial. 131: no 30-minute limit; R3-16: delete the dead `new-account-minutes` keys and doc lines. 660: confirm from $1M or 1,000 shards.
- 662: others' money is visible. 663: staff reason shown in `/transactions`. 47: `/eco` is admin and owner only.
- REVIEW3 F1 (R3-1): a profile can send money away only after earning $10k itself, unless it is to members of the island it is on.
- R3-5a / R3-5b / REVIEW3 F3, F4: bought gems can be paid, earned gems and crystals cannot, no gem limits. R3-28: 24 h trade block after a dupe. R3-39: staff play on a separate account.
