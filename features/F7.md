# Daily rewards and calendar · id: F7 · status: Draft · version: 1

## In one sentence
Log in once a day and claim a reward on a 30-day calendar, with a streak bonus and small rewards for active play time. Each profile has its own calendar.

## How it works
- **Status:** planned. The developer has started M10-6 (new module `rewards`, `/daily`), but nothing is in the repo yet.
- **Calendar (per profile, 128):**
  - Claim once a day. The day resets at 00:00 Europe/Oslo.
  - The calendar moves forward only when you claim. Missing a day never sends you back. After day 30 it starts again at day 1.
  - Keys on days 3 and 17 (Ember), 7 (Cinder), 10 and 24 (Ember x2), 14 (Blaze), 21 (Cinder x2), 28 (Inferno), 30 (Eclipse). Other days: money (3-10 min of income), Crystal Pouch S/M/L, island fuel (2-6 h), boosters (days 6, 9, 18, 27), an enchant book (15), a Disenchant Scroll (20), a Wardrobe Tome (25).
  - Crystal Pouch S / M / L = 20 / 40 / 80 × 1.3^(tier-1). All calendar keys and boosters are bound.
- **Streak:** days in a row. Every 7 streak days gives 10 gems (earned gems: bound to the account, REVIEW3 F3). Titles at streak 30 and 100 (plan). A missed day breaks the streak. You can save it within 2 days for 100 credits (R5). The save button stays hidden until the store exists.
- **Playtime rewards (R6, per profile, in the same menu):** only active time counts: not AFK and not in the AFK pool. Every hour: Crystal Pouch S + 20 pass XP (no money). Every 10 hours: 1 bound Cinder key. Titles at 50, 100, 250 and 500 h.
- **Built today:** nothing for the calendar. The `/menu` hub has no Daily button yet.

## What the player sees
- `/daily` (plan): a 6-row menu. 30 days as items: claimed (green glass ✔), today (gold, "» CLICK Claim"), next days (gray, reward in the lore). Row 5: streak count and days to the next gems, a **Playtime** tab, and a **Save streak** button (hidden for now).
- The Daily Rewards NPC opens `/daily`.
- Rewards are auto-given. What does not fit goes to `/box`.

## Rules and limits
- Per profile, as you decided (128). Calendar keys and boosters are bound, and so is the loot from calendar keys (R3-2), so a second profile or an alt cannot pass them on (P4).
- A claim is one database write per profile and day, so a double click or a crash cannot pay twice (proposed default).
- Playtime counts active time only. The AFK pool pays its own rewards and gives no playtime reward.
- Pouches and money scale with your best Depths tier, so rewards never become a printer. Phase 10 together should stay under about 15% of a regular player's income (plan).
- Bedrock: everything in the menu, nothing in chat clicks.
- Performance: playtime is counted from the minute tick that already exists (proposed default), no scans.

## Decided
- 128: the daily reward and streak are per profile, not per account.
- R5: a 30-day calendar. A broken streak can be saved for 100 credits within 2 days.
- R6: playtime rewards: 1 h small, 10 h key, 50 h title.
- P10-17: the 30-day contents as planned: keys on days 3, 7, 10, 14, 17, 21, 24, 28 (Inferno) and 30 (Eclipse), money, crystals, fuel and boosters on the other days.
- P10-6: calendar and playtime keys are bound.
- R3-2: loot from bound keys (calendar, playtime, votes, AFK pool, rank claims) is bound too.
- REVIEW3 F3: gems earned in play (votes, streaks, prizes, thanks) are bound to the account. Gems bought in the store are tradeable.
- FI 51 and FI 52: daily login rewards and playtime rewards: yes (feature ideas).
