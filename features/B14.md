# Upgrades and masteries · id: B14 · status: Draft · version: 1

## In one sentence
Islands grow through upgrades (numbers like size, slots, bank, production) and masteries (perks like Timber, Wings, Treasury), each bought with island bank money after the island finishes its tasks.

## How it works
- **Built (M5.3): the task engine and challenge book.** Button in `/is level`. 180 tasks in 12 tiers (`tasks.yml`), shared by all members from the island's creation, so earlier play is never wasted. Tasks: mine, chop, farm, kill, craft, sell, earn, deposit, deliver, skill level, pillar level, island level. Rewards: crystals to every member and pillar XP, once per island per task. Deliver tasks take items from the inventory (storage later). Each task lists the unlock keys it gates, such as `upgrade.island_size.1` or `mastery.wings.1`.
- **Planned (M5.4, being built, not merged): `/is upgrade`.** One menu with tabs Island, Economy, Depths, Production and Masteries. A level needs its tasks done plus island bank money. One click, a confirm screen (cost, bank after), instant, no timers. No 2% fee and no lost interest (P5-11). Needs the new Upgrades right in the matrix (Leader, Admin).
- **Planned upgrade catalog** (levels; first to last price):
  - Island: Island Size 3 ($3.4k to $157k), Member Slots 4 ($7k to $2.2M), Warp Slots 6 (to $462M), Island Vault 5 ($2k to $7.2M), Entity Limits 1 ($157k, +50%).
  - Economy: Bank Capacity 10, Bank Interest 10, Sell Multiplier 12 (+5% each, +60%).
  - Depths: Mine Fortune, Lumberjack, XP Boost: 12 levels each, +2% per level.
  - Production: Spawner Stack Limit 12 (8 up to 512), Spawner Rate, Mob Loot, Crop Growth, Harvest Yield, Generator: 10 levels each, +12% per level; Fuel Tank 12.
- **Planned (M5.5): masteries**, each needs its pillar level (5 x price tier), tasks and bank money:
  - Timber, Swift Hands, Warrior (5 ranks, +4% each), Deep Pockets (+10% satchel), Crystal Rush (+3% crystals), Lucky Strike (+4% rare finds), Fuel Efficiency, Beast Tamer, Angler, Homestead (2 ranks, planned (decided): shared island homes with `/is home <name>`, +1 per rank), Wings (island fly, level 20, $60k), Treasury (bank fee 1.5%, 1.0%, 0.5% at island level 15, 35, 60).
  - Market Contacts (MA5) opens when Sell Multiplier is maxed: +2% sell per level, up to +40%.
  - **Ascension:** endless levels after everything is maxed, each worth 1.5 level points, to keep money draining in months 2 and 3.
  - Effects go through the bonus families, so existing caps hold (sell cap +300%). PvE damage never applies in PvP.
- **Planned (decided): storage.** Deliver tasks and the Forge can take items from island storage (B12); the Forge counts as a take-out with the same daily limit (R3-23).
- **Planned (decided): kicks.** Bought upgrades and masteries are never revoked when a member is kicked; only later "island level X" task gates can drop (REVIEW3 F9).
- **Hooks already in the islands code:** member slots, size, warp slots, spawner stack limit, spawner rate, bank capacity, bank interest, bank fee, Wings.
- **Planned: staff tool** `/isadmin progression <player>` (mods view, admins edit and refund, all logged).

## What the player sees
- Island menu button "Upgrades" (grey "Coming soon" now). Upgrade items show level, next effect, tasks with check marks and progress, cost.
- Colors: green ready to buy, yellow tasks open, grey needs an earlier level, red no Upgrades right ("Ask your leader").
- On a buy: toast and sound for online members, island chat line, scoreboard "Next: Island Size I - 2/3 tasks".

## Rules and limits
- Payment is atomic in the database (`UPDATE ... WHERE balance >= ?`, ledger reason `island_upgrade`); level and money change in one transaction.
- Only stored members count for tasks (never coop). Bought levels belong to the island, so leaving doesn't take them (141 note).
- Bank capacity is a natural gate: late levels cost more than a small bank holds.
- Prices live in `upgrades.yml`; none go live before the owner approves the balance sheet.
- Bedrock: tabs and confirm screens are menus; two big buttons, no typing.
