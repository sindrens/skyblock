# Collectors and auto-sell · id: D7 · status: Draft · version: 1

## In one sentence
A Chunk Collector catches every item dropped in its chunk and an Auto-sell Chest sells it for the island, so farms collect and sell themselves, online and (with fuel) offline.

## How it works
- **Built:** nothing yet. Crates list a "collector" reward as "coming soon" (Inferno 5%, Phoenix 5%, Eclipse 4%). Until it exists that reward pays crystals.
- **Planned (Phase 7, M7.5): two placeable blocks**, tier 1 to 5, not craftable. They come only from crates, the store and the monthly crate. Store copies are labelled "Season item: removed at season end".
- **Chunk Collector:** picks up every item dropped in its chunk (mob loot, crop drops, piston farm drops). It catches drops when they spawn, no entity scans. One collector per chunk. Items with a unique id (tools, notes, books) are never collected.
- **Full collector:** it stops picking up, the items stay on the ground as stacks (D1), and members get one warning per hour. No overflow into island storage (I8).
- **Auto-sell Chest:** sells its contents every interval at the dynamic sell price. Hoppers and players can fill it. If a collector is in the same chunk, the chest also sells from the collector. One chest per chunk. Items without a price stay inside.
- **Money** goes to the island bank (ledger reason `collector_sell`) with island sell bonuses only (Sell Multiplier, Market Contacts, Desert biome). Personal rank and booster bonuses need a player to sell. The +300% cap still holds.
- **Merge:** right-click a placed block with a second one of the same kind and tier, confirm, and it becomes the next tier with its contents kept. Tier 5 can't merge.
- **Starter collector:** every new island gets one bound tier 1 collector at the tutorial finish (can't be traded, merged or sold).
- **Offline (M7.6):** no chunk is kept loaded. When the island loads again, the plugin calculates the fueled minutes. With a collector, crops are harvested virtually and spawner mobs are killed virtually (vanilla loot times the offline share), with no XP orbs. Without one, crops stay ripe and spawner mobs pile up in their stacks. Offline output gives no skill, tool or set XP and no books.

## What the player sees
- Item: "Chunk Collector, Collector - Tier 3. Picks up every drop in its chunk." Lore shows storage, offline share and "Merge two Tier 3 into Tier 4".
- Collector menu (5 rows): info (tier, fill %, chunk, offline share); items with amount and value (click takes a stack, shift takes all, overflow goes to /box); Sell all, linked chest, merge help, back, help.
- Join line: "While you were away: 6h 10m fueled, $412k sold, collectors 73% full". Full report in `/is production`.
- Island role key `collectors` (break, merge, take out, Sell all): Leader, Admin, Mod. Visitors never.

## Rules and limits
- Anti-abuse: each block has a unique id with dupe detection. Merge is one database transaction. Breaking one keeps its contents inside the item. Offline share is a lever against AFK production. Every place, break, merge, take-out and sell is audited.
- The starter collector is bound to the profile, so it needs the shared bound-item system (R3-2, planned).
- Hoppers can fill Auto-sell Chests. The faster hoppers (REVIEW3 F12) must be tested with collectors and chests before release, with no dupes on full or partial stacks.
- Staff: `/collector give <player> <kind> <tier>` and `/isadmin production <player>` (planned, audited).
- Performance: counts per item key in memory, saved every 30 s. Auto-sell chests sit in a queue by next sell time. Offline math runs off the main thread (target 300 islands x 25 chunks in under 200 ms). A farm with a collector has almost no item entities.
- Bedrock: everything is a menu with taps. Merge uses a confirm screen.

## Decided
- DESIGN 5.3: collectors and auto-sell chests tier 1 to 5, merge 2 of a tier into the next, only from crates, the store and the monthly crate.
- V5: sell at the dynamic price and run on fuel when offline.
- I8: no collector overflow into island storage. ST1 to ST6 were declined.
- B4: offline production is simulated, never chunk-loaded.
- REVIEW3 F12: the faster hoppers are tested with collectors before release.
