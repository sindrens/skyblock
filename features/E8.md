# Visibility · id: E8 · status: Draft · version: 1

## In one sentence
A setting that lets you hide other players while you mine and chop in The Depths (Everyone, Friends + island, or Nobody, with pinned players), so crowded zones are calmer and run better on phones and weak PCs. Only the hook is built; nothing is hidden yet. The owner decided it as one shared visibility service.

## How it works
- **Built (hook only):**
  - The block engine keeps a per-player choice `EVERYONE`, `ISLAND` or `NONE` (default everyone) and can answer "should viewer see other player?" (`shouldSee`). The islands module can plug in a "same island" check.
  - Nothing calls it yet, so no player is hidden. The choice lives in memory only and resets to "everyone" on rejoin.
  - `/settings` has a "Players in The Depths" tile marked "Coming soon". Lore: "See other players while mining in The Depths."
- **Planned (decided, REVIEW3 D4; #51, #58):**
  - Setting `Players: Everyone (default) / Friends + island / Nobody` in `/settings`, plus pinning a player so they stay visible. Also a one-click switch (a spawn NPC or hologram and a button in `/menu`). Staff always stay visible. Chat and tab are not affected. The choice is saved (#58).
  - **One shared visibility service** for staff vanish, this setting and the AFK pool, where players inside are hidden from each other (R3-36). It computes `hidden = vanished || !allowed` and applies only the changes. Bukkit tracks hides per plugin, so a second feature calling `showPlayer` would unvanish staff. Hidden bodies are non-collidable.
  - Only players who opt in pay the cost, so the server never does 300 x 300 work.
  - **Depths v2 (E1):** you mine coal while a friend mines iron in the same spot. With visibility on, you see each other even though your ore differs.
  - (proposed default) "Friends and island" means your island team plus your friends. Until friends exist (G6) it is island members only.
  - (proposed default) You can pin up to 5 players who stay visible even on "Nobody" (friends, a mining partner). The pin list is saved with the setting.

## What the player sees
- `/settings > Display > Players in The Depths` with three choices and a pin list, and the same switch as a menu button and at spawn.
- Hidden players disappear from view in mine and forest zones. Ore stays personal either way (E2).
- A short line when you change it: for example "Players in The Depths: friends and island".

## Rules and limits
- Staff and vanish rules win: a hidden or vanished staff member is never shown by this setting.
- No hiding in combat zones, the boss arena or the warzone (see open question 5).
- Performance: hide and show only on join, world change, zone change and setting change; no per-tick loops. Large groups of opted-in viewers are the cost, not all online players.
- Hiding changes only what you see. Chat, tab and damage work as before, and ore is personal anyway, so hiding cannot be used to steal.
- Bedrock: a menu toggle only; no commands. Needs a test on a Bedrock client that hidden players really disappear.

## Decided
- #51 (Round 1): add a "Hide other players" option with a quick toggle at spawn, default all.
- #58 (Round 2 list): the Depths visibility setting is saved with the settings toggle.
- REVIEW B6: visibility is forced to everyone in combat zones and world bosses.
- REVIEW3 D4: visibility Everyone (default) / Friends + island / Nobody, plus pinning a player, built as one shared visibility service.
- R3-36: players in the AFK pool are hidden from each other, using the shared service.

## Open questions
1. Saved per profile or per account? Default: per account (a comfort setting, like sounds).
2. Does the quick toggle at spawn also hide players at spawn, or only inside the Depths? Default: both, one setting.
3. Pin list size and who can be pinned. Default (proposed): up to 5, any online player.
4. Should hidden players' pets, trails and set particles also hide? Default: yes.
5. With layered mobs (E6), does combat still force Everyone (B6)? Default: yes, players still share the zone and only mobs are layered.
