# Staff mode · id: H1 · status: Draft · version: 1

## In one sentence
Staff press `/s` to swap their inventory for a moderation kit (inspect, freeze, punish, reports, vanish) and press it again to get everything back. Owner and admins will also be able to edit islands in creative from inside `/s`.

## How it works
- **Built: `/s`** (aliases `/staffmode`, `/sm`, `/mod`) turns staff mode on and off. Helpers, mods and up have it (`skyblock.staff.mode`). `/staff` is a different thing: the staff hub menu.
- **Built: crash-safe.** Inventory, game mode and flight are saved in memory and in the database before the swap. After a crash or restart you get it all back on the next join. If the save fails, staff mode does not start.
- **Built: the kit** (hotbar slots 1 to 9): 1 Inspect (blaze rod), 2 Freeze (packed ice), 3 Random player (compass, skips other staff), 4 Logs here (clock), 5 Vanish on/off (dye), 7 Punish (breeze rod, runs `/p`), 8 Reports (book), 9 Leave (barrier). Slot 6 is empty.
  - Kit items can't be dropped, moved or stored. They disappear when you leave.
  - Inspect, Freeze and Punish reach 16 blocks (`staff.yml: mode.inspect-range`). Right-click a player.
  - Inspect is not a spyglass: a spyglass zooms and the server can't stop that.
- **Built: powers while on:** vanish (`mode.vanish`), night vision (`mode.night-vision`), fly, no damage, no hunger, no item pickup, mobs ignore you.
- **Built: powers off otherwise.** God, `/fly` and `/heal` only work in staff mode or `/build` mode. They switch off within 1 second of leaving both.
- **Built: creative is narrow.** Only builders and admins, only in spawn and The Depths (`essentials.yml: staff-powers.creative-worlds`). Mods use spectator. Items taken or moved in creative, or made with `/give` and `/i`, get a hidden **staff item** tag: never sold, traded or auctioned.
- **Built: `/is bypass`** (admins) ignores island protection on any island. Today it is a manual toggle, off by default, off again at logout, and every toggle is logged.
- **Planned (decided): creative island editing (R3-6, REVIEW3 F15).** Inside `/s`, the owner and admins can use creative on islands.
  - `/is bypass` turns on by itself when they enter `/s` and off when they leave.
  - Every block they place or break is logged.
  - Items taken in creative are staff-tagged (`tag-staff-items`), so they can't be sold, traded or listed.
  - This is for owner and admin only, not builders or mods.
- **Planned (decided): separate play accounts (R3-39).** Admins, builders and the owner play on a separate account. Mods and helpers keep one account. `/eco` refuses your own account and any account on the same IP.

## What the player sees
- Staff see "Staff mode on. Inventory saved. /s to leave." and, when they leave, "Inventory is back."
- Other staff see "[Staff] Alex entered staff mode."
- Tool names read like "Inspect · Right-click a player".
- Normal players do not see vanished staff.
- Bedrock: tap a player instead of right-click. The Reports book opens a menu, not the book screen.

## Rules and limits
- Every action is audited (category `staff`).
- In staff mode you can't `/trade`, buy in `/shop` or open crates.
- `/s` waits while the saved-inventory check runs, so a saved inventory is never overwritten.
- Big money or item transfers to or from staff accounts are flagged to admins (economy watch rule `staff-transfer`).
- Planned (decided): block logging for creative editing is written through the normal batched audit writer, not one database write per block.
- Staff mode is event-driven. No per-tick scan.
- Bedrock: the whole kit works by tapping. No long commands.

## Connections
- Punishments (H2): the Punish tool runs `/p`. Reports (H3): the Reports book. Freeze and inspect (H4): the two tools. Logs (H5): "Logs here" and the block log.
- Anti-dupe (H7): staff items. Island admin tools (B10): `/is bypass`, `/isadmin`.
- Essentials (G12): vanish, `/gm`, `/build`. Economy watch (C10): `/eco` checks.

## Decided
- 331: creative only for builders and admins, only in spawn, The Depths and builder areas. Mods use spectator.
- 332: items from `/give`, `/i` and creative carry the hidden staff-item tag.
- 333: god, fly and heal only in staff mode or build mode.
- R3-6 / REVIEW3 F15: in `/s`, owner and admin can use creative on islands. `/is bypass` is automatic in `/s` and off when leaving. Every placed or broken block is logged. Creative items are staff-tagged.
- R3-39: admins, builders and the owner play on a separate account; mods and helpers keep one. `/eco` can't target your own or a same-IP account. This replaces 334 for those ranks.
- 501: helpers may use staff mode and freeze.
- 541: staff mode blocks `/ah`, `/pay`, `/shop` and `/sell`. Only `/shop`, `/trade` and crates are blocked in the code.
- #5: the Inspect tool is a blaze rod.
- O16: each starter island has its own schematic, chest and home, editable by staff.

## Open questions
1. Block `/ah`, `/pay` and `/sell` in staff mode as answer 541 says? Default: yes.
2. A separate creative inventory per owner or admin, like staff mode? Default: no, tag everything.
3. Is there a builder world for the creative list? Default: spawn and depths only.

- Creative for mods: they lost the permission (331).
