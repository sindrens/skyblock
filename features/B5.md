# Visiting · id: B5 · status: Built · version: 1

## In one sentence
Players can visit any open island from a browser or by name, and island owners can lock their island, send visitors away or ban them.

## How it works
- **Visit by name:** `/is visit <player>` (any member of that island, online or offline) teleports to the island home after a safe-spot check. Instant; 3 s only while combat-tagged or inside a combat zone (285).
- **Visit browser:** `/is visit` with no name, or Island menu then "Visit islands". Tabs: **Discover** (default: every island in random order per open, favouring more playtime), Most visited (unique visitors per day over 7 days), Top rated (ranking rule changes, see B6), Most liked, Newest. Click an island to go there. 6-row paged menu.
- **Lock:** `/is lock`, `/is unlock` (Leader, Admin, Mod). Only members and coop players can enter; visitors on it go to spawn. Locked islands are hidden in the browsers.
- **Expel:** `/is expel <player>` sends a visitor on your island to spawn.
- **Ban:** `/is ban <player>`, `/is unban <player>`. Banned players can't enter by command, `/tpa`, ender pearl, chorus fruit or login. Max 100 bans. Members must be kicked and coop players removed first (281).
- **Visitors can do:** walk and look by default. The matrix (B2) can allow more.
- **Member alerts** (each a `/settings` toggle, on by default): someone tried to visit a locked island (with an Unlock button), a visitor arrived, a visitor left. The same visitor is announced at most every 30 s (attempts every 60 s). Vanished staff are never announced.
- **Visit counter:** unique visitors per island per day (Europe/Oslo day). Members never count.
- **Closed islands:** an island whose leader is banned is closed to visitors, hidden in the browser, and its warps refuse (522). Members and coop keep playing.
- **Staff:** helpers and up ignore locks and bans (`skyblock.island.visit.bypass`). `/isadmin hide <island> [note]` hides an island from the browser (it can still be visited by name).

## What the player sees
- Action bar "Visiting <island>" while on another island.
- "<island> is locked. Only its members can enter." / "You're banned from <island>." / "<island> is closed to visitors right now."
- Members see "<player> is visiting" with a short cooldown.

## Rules and limits
- Every teleport into an island slot is checked (commands, `/tpa`, pearls, chorus fruit, plugins, flying in over the void, login). A banned or locked entry is refused or sent to spawn.
- No limit on visitors at once (291).
- The browser totals are loaded every 120 s, not per click. Your own rating, like or visit updates at once.
- Islands are never loaded just to be listed. Offline islands are not simulated.
- Bedrock: one plain click in every menu.

## Decided
- 280: locking sends current visitors to spawn.
- 281: banning refuses members and coop; a banned player inside is sent off at once.
- 282: most visited = daily visitors over 7 days.
- 284: helpers and up may enter locked or banning islands unseen and uncounted.
- 285: instant visit, 3 s only in combat.
- 290: Discover shows every island, random, favouring playtime.
- 291: no visitor limit.
- 293: staff can hide an island from the browser, with a note.
- 522: a banned leader's island is locked for visitors.
