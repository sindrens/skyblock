# Spawners · id: D2 · status: Review 1 · version: 1

Review 1 (31 Oct 2026, cluster ECON-RANKS-PACE). Owner: "fix spawner economy: we want them to get more expensive the better they are, it has to make sense." The proposal is the section **Economy v2**. Questions are EP-Q1 to EP-Q9 in `questions/ECON-RANKS-PACE.json`. Nothing here is locked; the older text stays where Economy v2 does not replace it.

## In one sentence
Spawners make mobs (money and drops) on your island. Better spawners cost more and take longer to pay back, you unlock them as you go deeper in The Depths, and each extra spawner of the same tier on one island gives less, so the island decides when buying more stops paying off.

## How it works

### Built today (deployed, branch q/gui-phase1)
- **5 tiers, 30 mob types** (`stacking.yml spawners.tier-1..5`): tier 1 pig, cow, chicken, sheep, rabbit; tier 2 zombie, skeleton, spider, cave spider, slime, squid; tier 3 creeper, witch, drowned, husk, stray, magma cube; tier 4 blaze, enderman, guardian, piglin, zombified piglin, hoglin, glow squid; tier 5 iron golem, wither skeleton, shulker, evoker, ravager, breeze.
- **/shop sells tier 1 and 2 only** (`shop-settings.yml spawners.max-tier: 2`) at a fixed price per tier: every tier 1 mob $117,000, every tier 2 mob $213,000 (`shop.yml categories.spawners.items`). Tiers 3 to 5 come from crates, drops and the Black Market. No price rise per spawner owned.
- **Stacks:** one spawner block holds 8 spawners, up to 512 with the Spawner Stack Limit upgrade (steps 8, 32, 64, 128, 256, 512). There is no limit on spawner blocks.
- **Output:** one spawn cycle per block every 10 to 40 s (about 144 per hour), `mobs-per-spawner: 1.0` per spawner in the stack (owner 641, applied in code). Spawner mobs have no AI and stack up to 1,024.
- **Diminishing output per island and tier** (`SpawnerEfficiency`, built): spawners 1 to 16 of one tier work at 100%, 17 to 32 at 60%, 33 to 64 at 35%, 65 to 128 at 20%, 129 and more at 10%. All blocks and outposts of the island count. Example: 38 tier 2 spawners = 16 + 16 x 0.6 + 6 x 0.35 = 27.7 spawners' worth.
- **Drops:** vanilla loot only (R3-21a), sold through `sell.yml mob-loot` at fixed prices per item ($10, $18, $33, $61, $180). A few rare drops are config (evoker totem 2%, shulker shell 5%).
- **Spawners are tradeable** (AH, /trade). The brake follows the island they work on.
- **Crash safety:** a spawner whose block is gone is frozen and logged, never deleted. Spawners on islands disbanded or reset on purpose are cleared.
- **Shop lore:** "Next works at X%" and "Pays back in about N h" (green up to 100 h, yellow up to 300 h, red "Poor deal"). The payback uses the **configured income per hour** (`shop-settings.yml spawners.income-per-hour.tier-1..5` = $1,900, $3,500, $6,500, $11,800, $35,000), not the real sell prices.

### Reality check: why it does not make sense today
Numbers: `ECONOMY_BALANCE.md` section 4.3 and 2.1, `sell.yml`, `shop.yml`, `SpawnerEfficiency`. "Production hour" = an hour the spawner runs (player within 16 blocks or island fuel); a regular player has 12 per day. Income per hour = 144 mobs x loot per mob x 0.85 dynamic price.

## Economy v2 (proposed)

### The rule in one line
**Spawner tier N unlocks with Depths tier N. Every tier costs about 1.28 times the one before and earns about 1.21 times the one before, so each step takes a little longer to pay back (60 hours for tier 1, 122 hours for tier 12), and one island holds at most 32 spawners of a tier.**

### The ladder, tiers 1 to 12 (money-equivalent prices; first spawner of the tier at 100%)
Loot per mob = 1.5 items worth the tier's loot value; income = mobs per hour x loot x 0.85 dynamic price. Payback = price / income in production hours.

- **Price in hours of income, honestly:** the price in hours of a regular player's income at its gate falls from 2.3 h (tier 1) to 0.26 h (tier 8) and 0.01 h (tier 12) (REF(t)). With vanilla loot the money price cannot keep up with a player who earns $36M an hour late in the season. That is why tiers 9 to 12 are sold for **shards** (scarce by design) and crates, not money; the money-equivalent price is only used for the payback label and the AH hint.
- **Old to new:** mobs move by their loot value, so the old five tiers do not map one to one (see the starting mobs column). A first spawner stays at $117,000; today's $213,000 tier 2 price is now $150,000 to $320,000 depending on the mob.
- **Mobs:** the "starting mobs" column is a starting assignment from estimated loot per mob; the price of each mob is `payback(tier) x its own income`, so a better mob in a tier is a better deal, never a free upgrade. 8 mobs earn under $10 per mob today (squid, glow squid, magma cube, piglin, zombified piglin, shulker, ravager, breeze): they are taken out of the shop, crates and Black Market until they have a sellable drop (EP-Q8). Iron golem and guardian are worth about $72 and $75 per mob and sit at tier 11 and 12 with a price 1.4 to 1.6 times the row.
- **Speed:** a better spawner also spawns faster (EP-Q3): speed = 1.08^(tier - 1), the 10 to 40 s cycle divided by it (tier 12: 4.3 to 17 s), never below a 4 s minimum cycle.
- **Payback and the upgrades:** the table is the base. Spawner Rate (+12% per level, 10 levels) and Mob Loot (+12% per level, 10 levels) multiply income by up to 4.84, so a maxed island pays back 4.8 times faster; the lore shows the island's current multipliers.

### Cap, curve and the money check
- **Cap: 32 spawners of one tier per island** (16 at 100% + 16 at 60%), counted over every block and outpost (EP-Q4). Tiers are counted separately, so the island maximum is 12 tiers x 32 = 384. The 33rd and later bands (35%, 20%, 10%) of the built curve stay in the config for the credit perk: the season perk `+N spawner cap` (C1-R2-14, hard maximum +64) raises the cap of every tier by N, those extra spawners run in the 35% band.
- **Conflict with S20 (stack limit 8 to 512 per block):** one block holds one mob type and all mobs of a tier count together, so a block can never hold more than the per-tier cap. With a cap of 32 the Spawner Stack Limit upgrade (B14: 12 levels, 8 up to 512, $1.2B for the last step, the sheet's "spawner stack step" ups at day 3, 9 and 38) would do nothing above 32. Proposed: that line becomes 8, 12, 16, 24, 32 per block (5 levels, same curve, smaller prices) and the credit perk raises the cap. This reverses owner answer S20 (max 512) and removes a late money sink of the sheet (the sheet's late sink is mainly Ascension); EP-Q4 asks, and the model rerun checks the sink ratio.
- **Grandfathered:** an island that already owns more than 32 of a tier keeps them (never deleted), new placements of that tier are refused until it is under the cap.
- **Whole-collection money:** tiers 1 to 8 at the cap (32 each) cost $84M in money; tiers 9 to 12 cost 15 + 25 + 40 + 60 = 140 shards per spawner.
- **Share of a regular player's late income ($36.5M per hour):** all 12 tiers at the cap earn $1.97M per hour at base (5.4%) and $9.5M per hour with Spawner Rate and Mob Loot maxed (26%). The sheet's band for spawners is 8% to 25% (R10 alert above 25%): the ceiling sits on the limit, so nobody gets over it by buying; the model must be rerun before lock (C14 ripple).

### Unlock gate
Buying a tier in /shop or the Black Market needs your **best Depths tier** (Mine, Forest or Combat, the same rule the AFK pool and island level use) to be at least that tier. A locked tier shows in the shop as "Needs Depths tier N" with your progress. Spawners won in crates or bought on the AH can be placed at any time; the price and the cap are the brake there. The Depths tier unlock times are in E3 (regular player: tier 2 at 2.2 h, tier 4 at 9 h, tier 8 at 52 h).

## What the player sees
- **/shop Spawners tab:** one tile per tier, locked tiers greyed "Needs Depths tier 5 (Mining 20: 14/20)". Click a tile to pick the mob, then the normal buy screen. Lore: "Tier 5 · Wither skeleton · $320,000 · $4,045/h · Pays back in 79 h · Next works at 100% (16/32 on this island)". Green up to 100 h, yellow up to 300 h, red "Poor deal" above; the 17th to 32nd show yellow because they work at 60%.
- **Spawner menu:** "Tier 5 on this island: 20 of 32 = 17.6 at full output. Next one works at 60%. 196 mobs/h per spawner."
- **AH listing lore:** the shop price of that tier (money tiers) or "Black Market: 25 shards" and the payback at the buyer's own island.
- **Refusal at the cap:** "This island holds 32 tier 5 spawners. Raise the cap with the Spawner Cap perk in /store."
- Bedrock: everything in the menu and lore; nothing depends on chat clicks.

## Rules and limits
- Max 8 mob stacks per type per chunk, and 40 stacks per chunk in total (not built yet).
- Spawner mobs give no Combat XP and no custom drops.
- The counts per island and tier are kept in memory (no scanning), updated when a spawner is placed, broken, merged or moved.
- Spawner mobs and spawners never give Depths drops. Offline output follows D7 (vanilla loot times the collector's share).

## Developer decisions (defaults; the owner only vetoes)
- **Tier table in config.** `stacking.yml spawners.tier-1..12` (mob lists), `spawners.speed-percent` (12 numbers), `spawners.max-per-tier: 32`, `spawners.cap-perk-max: 64`. `shop-settings.yml spawners.max-tier: 8` and a new `spawners.gate: depths-tier`. Existing `tier-1..5` lists migrate into the new lists by the table above; unknown mobs in `tier-N` stay allowed.
- **Real income, not constants.** The payback label is computed from the mob's expected loot (`mob-loot` sell prices x vanilla drop averages x speed x island Spawner Rate and Mob Loot x 0.85) with a pure `SpawnerIncome` class; `incomePerHour` keys are deleted. Unit tests: a cow beats a rabbit, a mob with no sellable loot shows "No sellable loot" and is not buyable, payback is monotone by tier at base, cap and grandfathering.
- **Generator, not hand prices.** A small tool (like `tools/economy_model.py`) writes `shop.yml spawners.items` from payback(tier) x each mob's income and the sheet's REF table; the C15 price overhaul reruns it. No hand-typed spawner price survives a loot price change.
- **Speed.** The spawn timer scales the interval by the tier's speed; a hard floor of 4 s per block keeps the main thread safe; the 100 due blocks per tick budget stays; a spike test with 384 spawners at tier 12 on one island is part of the build.
- **Gate.** One check at buy time through the `DepthsTierService` (best tier across the three lines, per profile); audited as `shop_review` rows if a gate is bypassed by staff `/spawner give`.
- **Cap.** One check at place time through `StackingService`; the count is the island's per-tier total; grandfathered islands are tagged by a log line, never changed.
- **Security.** All paths stay through the item factory; the cap and gate cannot be bypassed through AH because those only give items, and the cap holds at place time. A credit chargeback revokes the cap perk (C1-R2-13) and the surplus spawners become frozen, not deleted.
- **Performance.** No scans; one array of 12 counts per island; speed lookup is a table read.
- **Data.** No table change. `spawner` items keep their `tier` and `mob` tags; tier numbers of existing items are remapped once by the table above on load (item UUIDs unchanged).
- **Bedrock.** The tier tile and mob picker are two normal menus.

## Open questions
Moved to the owner questions above. Kept as developer defaults: the old menu example "18 mobs/min" was wrong (27.7 spawners' worth at 144 per hour is 66 mobs per minute); the new example is in "What the player sees".

## Rejected ideas
- +10% shop price per spawner owned (642): a wall at about 25 spawners, and alt islands bypass it. (EP-Q1 option b would reverse this; recommended not.)
- Server buy-back at 25%: adds money and makes a price floor for key farms.
- Upkeep or season decay: breaks "always worth having".
- One price for a whole tier (today): pig and rabbit differ by a factor 2 in loot.
- Money prices for tiers 9 to 12: they would be pocket change for late players (0.01 to 0.1 hours of income).
