# Island creation and grid · id: B1 · status: Draft · version: 1

## In one sentence
Every profile gets its own island in a fixed grid slot, built from a starter schematic, with a home point and a border that only people on that island see.

## How it works
- **Built: grid.** The islands world is cut into 512 x 512 block slots (one region file each), numbered in a spiral from the middle. Slot states: used, clearing, free. Free slots are reused (lowest id first) before a new one is added.
- **Built: creation.** Walk into the island portal in the spawn gatehouse: the island is made at once (no menu) and you are teleported. Later the same portal takes you home. `/is` with no island opens a "Create your island" confirm screen.
- **Built: starter island.** Pasted async with FastAsyncWorldEdit from `islands_starter_meadow.schem` (surface Y 100). If the file or FAWE is missing, or the paste takes over 30 s, a built-in placeholder islet "Ember Meadow" is used. A starter chest is filled: lava bucket, ice, 2 bone meal, 16 bread, 2 oak saplings, sugar cane, cactus, melon seeds, pumpkin seeds (`islands.yml: starter-chest.items`).
- **Built: size.** Starts at 3 x 3 chunks (48 x 48 blocks), centered in the slot. Normal growth stops at 9 x 9, the hard cap is 15 x 15 (see B15). Build height is Y 0 to 256.
- **Built: home.** `/is home`, `/home` and `/is go` teleport home (instant, 3 s while in combat). Leader, Admin and Mod move it with `/is sethome` (inside the border, safe spot). An unsafe home moves to the nearest safe spot (radius 8). Falling below Y -16 returns you to the home with no damage.
- **Built: border.** A per-player world border (packet), shown only to players standing on that island.
- **Built: rules.** One island per profile (database-enforced). No creation cooldown after leave, kick, disband or staff delete. Every profile gets start money ($1,000). You always respawn at spawn, never at the island.
- **Planned (decided):** choose from 3 starter islands (Ember Meadow, Frost Peak, Ash Dunes), each with its own schematic, chest and home, editable by staff, with a spectator preview (I1, I2, O16). A starter chest made for this server (158). Border colors and particle styles (I5), later painted by the resource pack. Shared island homes come from the Homestead mastery (R3-17, see B14), not from personal homes.

## What the player sees
- Title "YOUR ISLAND", subtitle "Rise from the ashes", then "Welcome to your island! Your starter chest is right in front of you."
- Confirm screen "Create your island" (from `/is`).
- A new player with a pending invite gets a choice at the portal: create my own, or join that island (`members.portal-invite-choice`).
- If the build fails: "Couldn't build your island. Try /is again soon, or tell staff."

## Rules and limits
- Nothing can be built outside the border, and nothing crosses it (liquids, pistons, explosions, growth, fire). Details in B2.
- Offline islands are never kept loaded. Paste and clearing run async in small slices, never one big job on the main thread.
- Bedrock: the portal and the confirm screen need no typing.
- Islands are wiped at season reset (414).
