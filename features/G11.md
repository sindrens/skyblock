# Teleports · id: G11 · status: Built · version: 1

## In one sentence
Every player teleport is instant (`/spawn`, `/tpa`, island home, visits, warps, `/dungeon`, portals); you only stand still for 3 seconds when you are in combat or in a combat zone, and a teleport is never blocked.

## How it works
- **One shared service and one file** (`teleport.yml`) for every warmup and cooldown. Modules only pass a destination name, so all teleports look and behave the same.
- **Instant by default** (owner answers 285, 341). Spawn, tpa, island home, visit, warp and dungeon all have a 0 s warmup.
- **Danger warmup of 3 s** (`danger.warmup-seconds`) only when:
  - you are combat-tagged (you hit, or were hit by, a player or hostile mob; `core.yml: combat.tag-seconds`), or
  - you stand in a combat zone: the warzone, the Depths boss arena, the Depths combat tiers (345).
  - It is cancelled by moving more than 1 block, taking damage, a new combat tag, a staff freeze or a world change. A cancelled warmup gives its cooldown back.
- **`/spawn`:** to the spawn world's spawn point. 5 s cooldown (`spawn.cooldown-seconds`). After a death you also respawn at spawn (160).
- **`/tpa <player>`:** ask to go to them. **`/tpahere <player>`:** ask them to come to you.
  - The receiver sees `[Accept] [Deny]` in chat. `/tpaccept [player]` (`/tpyes`), `/tpdeny [player]` (`/tpno`), `/tpcancel`.
  - Request expires after 60 s. 15 s cooldown between your requests. A player can hold 5 open requests (`essentials.yml: tpa.*`).
  - Refused with the same words as "requests off" if the target turned off "Teleport requests" in `/settings` or `/ignore`s you (316), so the ignore list stays private.
- **Guards:** the warzone is entered only through its portal, so `/tpa`, homes and warps into it are refused (413). `/tpahere` into a Depths tier you have not unlocked is refused (346). A locked island or an island ban refuses a visit. Frozen players cannot teleport.
- **Island teleports** (`/home`, `/sethome`, `/is visit`, `/is warp`) belong to islands (347). Staff have their own homes (`/shome`).
- **Staff:** `/tp`, `/tphere`, `/tppos`, `/top`, `/back` by rank. `skyblock.staff.teleport.bypass` (mod and up) skips warmups, cooldowns and restrictions.

## What the player sees
- Warmup (chat): "Teleporting to spawn in 3s. Don't move." or, in danger, "You're in combat. Teleporting to ... in 3s." Action bar: `⌛ Teleporting to spawn in 3...`.
- Cancel: "Teleport cancelled: you moved." / "you got hit." / "you're in combat." / "you were frozen by staff." / "you changed worlds."
- Arrival: "Welcome to spawn." or "Teleported to Steve."
- Request: "Steve wants to teleport to you. [Accept] [Deny]". Sender: "Request sent to Steve. Expires in 60s. [Cancel]".
- Refusal: "Steve doesn't accept teleport requests." · "You can only enter the warzone through its portal at spawn." · "Slow down. Try again in 4s."
- Bedrock: tap `[Accept]`, or type `/tpaccept` if taps do not work.

## Rules and limits
- Never blocked in combat, only delayed (342). Staff `/tp` into the warzone still works.
- Anti-abuse: the request cooldown and the open-request cap stop spam. Island bans, island locks, tier locks and the warzone portal rule apply to teleports (TEST_GUIDE tests 6 and 7).
- `/ec` and `/craft` are a separate rule: blocked in combat zones (G12).
- Performance: requests live in memory with a 60 s expiry. One timer for warmups, no per-tick scanning.

## Decided
- 285: visit and warp are instant, except while in combat or inside a combat zone (3 s).
- 341: all warmups in one shared file, with logical values (instant by default).
- 342: teleports are not blocked in combat.
- 345: `/spawn` and `/tpa` are not blocked in the warzone, boss arena or events; they get a delay instead.
- 346: `/tpahere` into an unlocked Depths tier is refused.
- 347: `/home` and `/sethome` are the island's; staff use `/shome`.
- 413: the warzone is reachable only through its portal.
- 160: players respawn at spawn, never at the island home.
- 316: ignore blocks `/tpa` and `/tpahere`.
- R3-17 (P5-21): no personal homes. The Homestead mastery gives shared island homes (`/is home <name>`, +1 per mastery rank); the new sub-command is approved.
- R3-35: "just teleport them back to their island whenever they don't earn anything". No idle kick in the AFK pool.

2. Give every player `/back` (last place before a teleport, never a death point; QoL 7)? (docs default: yes)

4. Events have no combat zone yet. Add each event world to `danger.worlds` when events are built? (default: yes)
5. Bedrock Accept / Deny popup menu for `/tpa` (QoL 15), after you test whether tapping chat buttons works (QoL 31)? (docs default: yes)

## Rejected ideas
- Blocking teleports in combat (342): delay only.
- Blocking `/spawn` and `/tpa` in the warzone, boss arena and events (345): delay instead.
- The old 3 s warmups for `/is visit`, `/is warp`, `/dungeon` and `/tpa` (285, 341): instant now.
- Respawning at the island home (160).
