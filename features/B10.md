# Disband, reset, staff tools · id: B10 · status: Draft · version: 1

## In one sentence
A leader can remove an island they are alone on, staff can delete or reset any island, and every removal keeps a backup so nothing is lost by accident.

## How it works
- **Disband:** `/is disband` (or the Disband button in the island menu). Leader only, and only while nobody else is a member (transfer and leave first, 141). Built today: one confirm screen with a confirm button, no typed word (261).
- **Planned (decided): two-step disband.** After the first screen, a second screen shows the real amounts ("you lose $X, N items, bank $Y") with the buttons in different slots, so a double-tap can't confirm. (R3-30)
- **What a disband does:** the island is removed for good, coop players are told, everyone on it goes to spawn, the grid slot is cleared and reused, the island bank money is deleted (263), and this profile is reset (its money, inventory and ender chest are taken, 591). A new island can be made right away.
- **Backup first:** a reset needs a saved backup row, or it does not happen. The backup holds role, money taken, inventory and ender chest (when the player is online) and the bank money. Items are marked restorable only after the inventory was emptied and saved. A crash in between shows "Pending: check by hand", never a copy next to the player's items.
- **Staff reset:** `/isadmin reset <island>` removes the island, gives the leader a fresh starter island, removes other members, and resets every member profile (backups kept).
- **Staff restore:** `/isadmin restore <player>` opens the player's backups (newest first). A kick backup gives back the membership once. A disband or reset backup gives back exactly the money that was taken (once), pays the saved island bank money into the profile's purse (once), and returns items into an empty inventory.
- **Clearing:** the slot is emptied in slices (4 chunks every 2 ticks), with FAWE off the main thread, or a built-in clearer that skips empty sections. Unfinished slots resume after a restart. Slot states: used, clearing, free.
- **Planned (decided): spawners.** Spawners on an island that is disbanded or reset on purpose are cleared, not frozen for staff (a listener on the island-removed event in stacking). Frozen spawners from crashes stay, and staff get a restore button for them. (R3-13)
- **Planned (decided): staff creative editing.** In `/s` staff mode, owner and admin can use creative on islands. `/is bypass` and `/isadmin bypass` switch on automatically in `/s` and off when leaving. Every placed or broken block is logged and creative items are staff-tagged. (REVIEW3 F15)
- **Staff commands:**

| Command | Who | Does |
|---|---|---|
| `/isadmin info`, `tp`, `stats view` | helper | look, teleport, read stats |
| `/isadmin hide`, `unhide` | mod | hide from the visit browser |
| `/isadmin delete`, `reset`, `setsize`, `addmember`, `kick`, `setowner`, `restore`, `slots`, `teleport-slot`, `stats reset`, `bypass` | admin | change or fix an island |

  Destructive ones open a confirm screen (console: `... confirm` within 30 s).

## What the player sees
- Confirm screen "Disband island" with: "Your island and everything on it is deleted forever, its bank money too. This profile is reset: its money, inventory and ender chest are taken."
- (Planned) A second screen with the real amounts lost.
- "<island> was disbanded." with a `[Create one]` button.
- Members get "<player> disbanded <island>. Join another island or create your own."

## Rules and limits
- Every removal is one database transaction (`deleted_at`, reason disband, delete or reset). History rows stay.
- A staff delete keeps the island bank in a closed bank for staff to decide.
- Restore never gives more than was taken (SECFIX1 F-02), so spend-then-restore can't dupe.
- Everything is logged (`island_disband`, `_delete`, `_reset`, `_clear_*`, `island_profile_reset`, `staff: isadmin_*`).
- The clearer is throttled and loads only one slice of chunks at a time.
- Bedrock: confirm screens and the restore menu are plain taps.

## Decided
- 141: an island with other members can't be disbanded; promote someone else first.
- 261: a confirm button is enough.
- 263 and 591: bank money is deleted on disband or reset, every member profile is reset, staff can restore from the backup.
- 251: backup on kick, staff restore.
- 258, 259: no creation cooldown after any removal.
- 163 and 260: no player reset; reset is staff only.
- 228 and 265: helpers and mods view island info; resets are for admins only.
- R3-30 ("Recommended"): two-step disband with a second screen of real amounts and buttons in different slots. This replaces 261's "one button is enough".
- R3-13 (both follow-ups): spawners on disbanded or reset islands are cleared; staff get a restore button for frozen spawners.
- R3-6 and REVIEW3 F15: owner and admin can edit islands in creative inside `/s`, with automatic bypass, block logging and staff-tagged items.

## Open questions
1. A reset only clears money, inventory and ender chest. Skills, Depths unlocks and other per-profile progress are not reset yet. Default: not reset until planned.
2. Restore of bank money: into the leader's purse (built) or into the new island bank? Default: purse.
3. Players offline at a staff reset keep their items (can't be read safely) but lose money. OK? Default: yes.

## Rejected ideas
- Typed word or island name to confirm (261).
- A single confirm screen for disband: replaced by the two-step screen (R3-30).
- 24 h cooldown after a disband (258/259).
- Paying the bank money to the leader on disband (263).
