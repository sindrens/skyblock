# Black Market · id: C13 · status: Draft · version: 1

## In one sentence
The Black Market is a hidden alley shop in Market Square where players spend shards on rare, limited items. The stock changes twice a day. Nothing is built yet: only the menu slot and the NPC role exist, and both show "coming soon".

## How it works
Everything below is the Phase 10 plan (M10.11, `modules/blackmarket`). The owner accepted the suggested answers (P10-34 to P10-38). His final REVIEW3 answer on island size is that it is sold for shards here and also for gems (R3-19, REVIEW3 F6).
- **Planned: shards only.** No gems and no money. Gem items stay in the gem shop (Phase 11), money items stay in `/shop` (P10-34). Shards come from events and bosses, so the stock is limited by play, not by grinding or paying.
- **Planned: always open, stock rotates** at 00:00 and 12:00 Oslo time. 8 slots: 1 headline, 3 rare, 4 common, picked by weight from `blackmarket.yml` (P10-35). A chat line announces the headline.
- **Planned: a fixed row** that is always in stock: the Island Charter and the Disenchant Scroll (8 shards).
- **Island Charter:** one step at a time, 9x9 must come first. 11x11 / 13x13 / 15x15 cost 150 / 300 / 450 shards, the expensive way. The same sizes can also be bought with gems, bought or earned, for 250 / 500 / 750 in the island menu (M5.6). It lasts one season, and a confirm screen says so (P10-38, P5-14, B15, REVIEW3 F6).
- **Prices in shards (P10-36):**

| Slot | Items |
|---|---|
| Headline | Phoenix Regalia Set Token 400, Voidforged Plate Set Token 250, random Mythic enchant book 200 |
| Rare | Coinmaster Silks Set Token 120, Auto-sell chest tier 4 120, Chunk collector tier 3 100, Eclipse key 75, Legendary enchant book 60, Phoenix key 50, tier 5 spawner 40 |
| Common | Black Market tag 60 (only sold here), random Blueprint 40, Elytra 30, Sell Wand 2,000 uses 25, Greater Wardrobe Tome 20 |

- **Limits (P10-37):** Phoenix Regalia 1 per account per season. Eclipse key 1 per account per month. Phoenix key 2 per account per rotation. The headline has a server-wide stock of 25 per rotation.
- **One safe buy:** shards out, item in, a `black_market_buys` row and an audit entry in one transaction. Items go to the inventory or `/box`.
- **Shard income today: none in the game.** The balance sheet proposes boss rewards (20 / 15 / 10 for the top 3, 3 for 1% damage), KOTH 10, envoy 1-3 and a rare tier 9+ drop (about 1 an hour). Until those exist, staff can grant shards. A late-season regular earns about 5-10 a day, so a headline is a 1-2 month goal and a common item takes a few days.

## What the player sees
- The `/menu` slot "Black Market" (wither rose) and the alley NPC show a help text and "coming soon" today. The slot is hidden at launch until the feature is done (owner answer 41).
- `/blackmarket` (6 rows): top row "Restocks in 4h 12m", then the headline with "Stock 18/25", 3 rare items, 4 common items, the fixed row, and "Your shards".
- The alley is in the south-west corner of Market Square: a dark oak door with iron bars, a hooded stall and dim orange lanterns. A hologram shows the time left.

## Rules and limits
- Shards are per profile (P2): you spend the active profile's shards. Limits are per account, so a second profile cannot buy the same limited item again. Nothing moves between your own profiles (P4).
- Shards stay tradeable, and get money notes (R3-5b, C1). A rich player can pass shards to a friend, but the buying limits count per account.
- Bought items count in the collection log, because they are bought with earned shards (P10-32).
- Mojang risk to log (REVIEW3 F3, F6): island size for gems is paid space, and bought gems are tradeable. Nothing bought with real money may give a competitive gameplay advantage. Read Mojang's current terms before the store opens (M11.1).
- Staff sub-command `/blackmarket admin` (give, take, reset, test roll) is a new command and needs the owner's OK (P10-41).
- Performance: the rotation is picked once per 12 hours and cached. Bedrock: menu taps only.
