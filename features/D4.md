# Custom mob drops · id: D4 · status: Draft · version: 1

## In one sentence
Mobs drop custom items that are used for crafting and upgrades, while spawner mobs stay on vanilla loot (plus a few special drops) so spawners cannot run away with the economy.

## How it works
- **Built: Depths combat mobs.** Each of the 10 combat tiers has its own custom drop item (graveyard zombies drop `depths_rotten_flesh`, crypt skeletons `depths_bone`, and so on). A normal mob drops 3 to 5 per kill, a mini-boss 50 to 70. Looting-style bonuses multiply the amount, capped at `combat.max-drop-multiplier`. (mobs.yml, depths.yml) These items are Forge materials (772, 776) and sell in `/sell` from $26 (tier 1) to $3,675 (tier 10).
- **Built: spawner mobs and island mobs.** Vanilla loot only. It sells at a fixed price per spawner tier in `/sell`: $10 (tier 1), $18, $33, $61, $180 (tier 5). (sell.yml: mob-loot)
- **Built: rare spawner drops (SK10).** Spawner evokers drop a totem 2% of the time, spawner shulkers a shell 5% (vanilla: 100% and 50%). (stacking.yml: spawners.rare-drops)
- **Built: emeralds** have a price ($82) but are switched off in `/sell` (`sellable: false`) because villager trading could turn free items into emeralds.
- **Built: stack kills** multiply drops. The loot table is rolled 8 times and scaled to the number killed (see D1).
- **Planned (owner 693):** every mob killed drops 1 custom item, more with Looting or the Plunder enchant. One custom drop per mob type, for example Zombie gives Rotten Sinew, Skeleton gives Bone Shard, Spider gives Silk Gland, Creeper gives Blast Powder (names from the plan, to be approved). Uses: Forge recipes, enchant upgrades, summon sigils, quests and collections. Sell value low, below crafting value. A few rare elite drops sell for shards.
- **Planned (decided): spawner mobs drop vanilla loot only, except a short list of special drops the owner will pick later (R3-21a).** Owner examples: silverfish drop nether stars, villagers drop emeralds. The list, chances and amounts are written into `planning/CUSTOM_MOB_DROPS.md` and balanced there. Nothing is built yet.
- **Planned (decided): one mob per spawner cycle.** The server still has 4 mobs per cycle (`mobs-per-spawner: 4.0`); answer 641 is applied now by setting it to 1 (R3-21b). This lowers spawner loot to what the balance sheet expects.
- **Planned (decided): full satchel (R3-22).** Combat drops go to the satchel, then the inventory, then `/box`. Today they are lost when the satchel is full (a bug being fixed).
- **Planned (proposed default):** the Mob Loot upgrade (+12% per level) raises vanilla spawner loot. It adds no custom drops.

## What the player sees
- Depths: custom drops go to the satchel with auto-pickup. They show in `/sell` with a price. When the satchel is full, drops will go to the inventory and then `/box`.
- Islands: vanilla loot falls on the ground, merges into one item with a count (`Bone x1,234`). Island auto-pickup covers blocks only for now. A collector (D7) catches the loot of its chunk.
- Rare special drops get a short chat line (proposed default).

## Rules and limits
- Spawner mobs give no new custom drops, only vanilla loot and the special drops above (R3-21a). Reason: spawners are already the biggest money risk in the balance sheet.
- Special drops must be rare and are counted in the economy model before they go live (proposed default).
- Offline spawner output (Phase 7) gives vanilla loot times the collector's share. No special drops offline (proposed default).
- Performance: drops merge into stacked item entities. No per-mob loot rolls for big stacks.
- Bedrock: items are normal items with names, nothing needs chat clicks.
- Anti-abuse: custom drops never sell above what they are crafted into, so no craft-and-sell loop. Unique items are never merged or sold.

## Open questions
1. Which mobs get custom drops: Depths, natural island mobs, bosses? Default: Depths and natural island mobs, bosses have their own tables (Phase 8). Note: natural hostile spawning is off on islands.
2. Which special drops, how rare, and what amounts? The owner gave silverfish (nether stars) and villagers (emeralds) as examples. Silverfish is not a spawner type today, and villager spawners are banned (SK8). Default: none until the owner gives the list.
3. Emeralds are `sellable: false` today ($82 if switched on). What price do they get, and is villager trading turned off? The plan says off; WORLD.md says allowed (decided 2026-10-29). Needs one clear answer.
4. Do Plunder and Looting both add custom drops? Default: yes.
5. Who writes the first list of drop names and uses? Default: the developer proposes, the owner approves.

## Rejected ideas
- Custom drops from every spawner mob (the original 693 answer): spawners would earn far above the model. The owner kept it only for a short list of special drops (R3-21a).
