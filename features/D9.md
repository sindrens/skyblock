# Outposts and biomes · id: D9 · status: Draft · version: 1

## In one sentence
Outposts are up to three small extra islands, unlocked late in the season, where a biome you choose boosts farming, spawners or rare drops on that outpost only.

## How it works
- **Built:** only the ground work. An `outposts` template world is in the worlds config. The Outposts portal at spawn exists but stays closed ("Soon"). The pillars used for unlocking are built. Island upgrades are in progress; Outpost Slots and Biome Tier will show "Coming soon" until Phase 7.
- **Planned (Phase 7, M7.9): three outposts per island,** in the `outposts` world on the same grid as islands. Each is 5x5 chunks, upgradable to 7x7 and then 9x9. Each is pasted from its own builder schematic when you unlock it.

| Outpost | Unlock (all pillars at least, plus) | Theme | Its 3 biomes |
|---|---|---|---|
| 1 Greenhold | 10 | Farming | Plains, Jungle, Desert |
| 2 Wildwood | 25 and Combat pillar 30 | Spawners | Dark Forest, Swamp, Snowy Taiga |
| 3 Emberdeep | 40 and a member at Depths combat tier 10 | Late game | Nether Wastes, Crimson Forest, Soul Sand Valley |

- **Unlock** = buy the next Outpost Slots level from the island bank once the condition is met: $1.5M, $7.3M, $60M (balance sheet 5.3).
- **Same island:** roles, bank, fuel tank, settings, members and coop rules are shared. Block worth, pillars and stats count for the island. An outpost burns no extra fuel.
- **Getting there:** the island menu button "Outposts" and the Outposts portal at spawn open a menu of your outposts (teleport, size, biome). Instant teleport (3 s only in combat).
- **Biomes (M7.10):** each outpost has its own 3 biomes. The leader picks the active one in the Biomes menu. A biome unlocks at its outpost's gate on the related pillar: Farming for Greenhold, Combat for Wildwood, Combat for Nether Wastes and Soul Sand Valley, Farming for Crimson Forest.
- **Tiers I to V:** each +5% (+25% at V). **Fusion I** merges 2 biomes, **Fusion II** all 3, each merged effect at 75%. 18 steps per outpost, priced 1.22^j x the reference income of the gate. Fusion II costs $42.9M, $215.5M and $1.8B. All 18 steps: $231.4M, $1.2B, $9.5B.
- **Biome effects (that outpost only):** Plains crop growth. Jungle double-crop chance. Desert crop sell value. Dark Forest spawner speed. Swamp mob drops. Snowy Taiga vanilla XP from mobs. Nether Wastes spawner stack limit (never above 512). Crimson Forest rare drops. Soul Sand Valley boss key chance (shows "Coming soon" until bosses exist).
- **Main island biome changer (FI 15):** cosmetic only, free, in the same Biomes menu, needs the role key `biomes`.

## What the player sees
- Outposts menu: Greenhold with "Click to teleport", Wildwood locked with "All pillars 25 - 18/25", Emberdeep "Locked". Buttons Biomes, Outpost size, Upgrades.
- Biomes menu with the active biome, tier and fusion step. The biome is applied to the outpost's chunks (Bedrock fine).
- Planned command list has `/is outposts` and `/is biomes`; the Phase 7 plan uses menus only.

## Rules and limits
- Outposts load only while a player is there. Offline outposts are simulated like islands (D7). Teleports preload chunks in the background.
- Spawners on outposts count toward the island's per-tier output brake (D2, REVIEW3 F2).
- Effects feed existing bonus families, so every cap still holds (+300% sell, crop growth +100%).
- Visiting another island's outpost follows that island's lock and ban. New island role keys `outposts` and `biomes` for Leader and Admin (proposed default, PD37).
- Islands and outposts are wiped at season reset (414). Builders get a brief per outpost: size, spawn pad, fishing water, farm space.
- Bedrock: menus and teleports only, no chat clicks. Performance: the schematic paste is async (FAWE).
