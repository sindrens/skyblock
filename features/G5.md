# Chat broadcasts · id: G5 · status: Draft · version: 1

## In one sentence
The server posts a short message in chat every few minutes (vote, store, tips, market news, events, Discord), and staff can send their own announcement with `/broadcast`.

## How it works
**Built:**
- Staff `/broadcast <message>` (`/bc`, `/announce`): admin only. Sends a chat block (blank line, "ANNOUNCEMENT" in the phoenix gradient, the text, blank line), plus a screen title and a sound. The text allows MiniMessage colours. The title can be switched off (`social.yml: broadcast.title`).
- Join, AFK and ban/mute lines already exist as their own systems. Ban and mute announcements are public unless `-s` (owner answer 500).

**Planned (proposal in CHAT_BROADCASTS.md, nothing is built):**
- One rotation timer, one auto message every 5 minutes at most (proposed default). The lines live in `messages/social.yml` (REVIEW3 F10) and reload without a restart. Timing and weights are module settings.
- Six kinds, each with a weight and its own lines (proposed default):
  - Vote (`Vote for us and get an Ember key + 3 gems. /vote`)
  - Store (`Support the server and get a rank. /store`)
  - Tips (`Tip: sneak + right-click an NPC to see what it does.`)
  - Market (`Sugar cane is up 22% in 24 hours. /sell`), for movers of 15% or more in 24 h, at most one per 30 minutes
  - Events (`KOTH starts in 10 minutes at the warzone.`)
  - Discord (`Join our Discord for news and giveaways. /discord`)
- Players can switch off Tips and Market (new "Market" toggle next to Tips). Vote, Store, Events and Discord cannot be switched off.
- Staff preview a line with `/broadcast preview <id>`.

## What the player sees
- Planned look (UI_STYLE §3.3): a blank line, `» VOTE  Vote for us and get an Ember key + 3 gems.`, then `[Vote now]  (or type /vote)`, a blank line. The kind word has its own colour (`» VOTE`, `» TIP`, `» MARKET`, `» EVENT`).
- Java gets a clickable button. Bedrock always gets the command written out.
- No prefix `PHOENIX »` on broadcasts (UI_STYLE §3.1).

## Rules and limits
- English only (owner answers 31, 305).
- Planned anti-spam (proposed default): the same line is not repeated within an hour; kinds rotate; store line at most once per 30 minutes; the auto-announcer never posts more than one line per 30 s (UI_STYLE §3.3).
- Not shown to players in the tutorial; they get Pip's help instead (proposed default).
- Vote line skips players who already voted on every site today (needs voting, F6).
- Performance: one timer for the whole server. The line is built once; only players with the toggles on receive it. No per-player parsing.
- Bedrock: needed information is never only in hover text.

## Decided
- 612: a new message in chat every X minutes or hours: donate to the store, remember to vote, and price news like "sugarcane prices have gone up 22% the past 24 hours".
- 31, 305: English only.
- 500: ban and mute announcements stay public unless `-s` (separate system).
- UI_STYLE §3.3: announcement look is a blank line, a short header, 1-2 lines, a clickable action, a blank line.
- REVIEW3 F10: all player-facing text stays in `messages/<module>.yml`, so the broadcast lines go in the messages file, not in a separate `broadcasts.yml`.
