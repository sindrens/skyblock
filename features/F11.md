# Boosters and thanks · id: F11 · status: Draft · version: 1

## In one sentence
Booster items give a temporary bonus to you (personal) or to everyone online (global). `/thanks` rewards the player who started a global one.

## How it works
- **Personal boosters:** bound items, 4 types: Sell, Skill XP, Tool XP, Crystals. Crates give +25 / 50 / 100% for 10-60 min. Right-click the item, or click it in `/boosters`, to start it. The item cannot go above +100% or 120 min.
- **They never stack:** the same type and strength adds time, up to 3 h per type (RX39). Another strength of a running type is refused and the item is kept.
- **Time counts only while you play that profile.** Not while offline, paused during a profile switch.
- **AFK pool:** its sell booster (2% per reward after 1 h in a session, +50% for 10 min) is banked and starts by itself when you are outside the pool.
- **Global boosters:** won from Phoenix and Eclipse crates (4% of opens: Sell +50% for 30 or 60 min). Everyone online gets the bonus. One per type runs, up to 5 wait in a queue per type. Different types run side by side. The item is bound to its winner.
- **Thanks (RW2):** `/thanks`, or saying thanks in chat ("thanks", "ty", "thx" and similar), gives the starter 1 gem. Once per player per booster. Never for your own booster. No gem if you share the starter's IP. At most 100 gems per booster (`thanks.max-gems-per-booster`). Thanks still count 2 min after it ends.
- **Planned (decided, R3-43):** keep `/thanks` and typed thanks, fold them into one chat line, and cap the gems at 20 per booster instead of 100.
- **All bonuses go through the bonus system.** Family caps apply once, and sell has the +300% total cap (O11).
- **Planned:** booster rewards in the daily calendar (days 6, 9, 18, 27) and in quests. Global boosters in the store (Phase 11). Tool XP and Crystals boosters have no source yet.

## What the player sees
- `/boosters` (alias `/booster`): a menu that updates every second. Your boosters (running, time left, banked time), the global boosters (starter, time left, thanks so far, queue) and the booster items in your inventory. Click an item to start it.
- A boss bar for every running global booster: type, strength, time left, starter and `/thanks`. `/settings` → Global booster bar hides it.
- Chat when one starts: "<name> started a global +50% sell booster for <time>! Say thanks or type /thanks."
- Item looks: Sell is a gold ingot, Skill XP lapis, Tool XP an echo shard, Crystals prismarine crystals, global a nether star.

## Rules and limits
- Personal boosters are bound: no trade, no AH, no pickup by another profile. Global boosters are always bound by default (`global.always-bound`).
- A booster item can start only once, even after a copy or a crash (its unique id is stored). A refused start gives the item back.
- Used time is saved every 30 s, so a crash costs at most that, in the player's favour. A thanks gem is paid once, even after a crash.
- Thanks gems are earned gems, so they are bound to the account (C1, REVIEW3 F3). They are paid through the ledger (reason `booster_thanks`).
- Bedrock: clicking a global booster in `/boosters` thanks it, no typing. Performance: an open menu refreshes once a second and used time is saved every 30 s.

## Decided
- R3: the same type does not stack, it adds time.
- R4: `/boosters` shows personal and global boosters. `/thanks` gives the starter a small reward.
- RW2: thank a global booster with `/thanks` or by saying thanks in chat. The starter gets 1 gem.
- O11: boosters count toward the global multiplier cap.
- #46: the total sell multiplier is capped at +300%.
- FI 49 and FI 50: personal boosters and global boosters: yes.
- R3-43: keep `/thanks` and typed "thanks", fold them into one chat line, cap at 20 gems per booster, and give Bedrock a menu button (the click in `/boosters` is built).
- REVIEW3 F3: gems earned in play (votes, streaks, prizes, thanks) are bound to the account. Gems bought in the store are tradeable.
- R3-2: loot from bound keys is bound, so boosters won with a bound key are bound.
