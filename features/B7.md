# Island settings and fly · id: B7 · status: Built · version: 1

## In one sentence
Each island has switches for mob spawning, fire, explosions, weather, time and more, and players with the right can fly inside their island border.

## How it works
- **Command:** `/is settings` opens the settings menu. `/is settings <setting> [on|off|value]` changes one right away (toggles flip, weather and time go to the next value). Tab completes names and values.
- **Who:** the `settings` right in the matrix (Leader and Admin by default). Every member can look. Changes are logged (`island_setting`) and announced to members.
- **Settings and defaults** (`islands.yml: flags.defaults.*`):

| Setting | Default |
|---|---|
| Mob spawning (natural) | off |
| Animal spawning | on |
| Fire spread | off |
| Explosions break blocks | off |
| Leaf decay | on |
| Crop trampling (players and mobs) | off |
| Visitors pick up items | off |
| Visitor alerts to members | on |
| Visitor fly | on |
| Weather: sunny, rain, storm | sunny |
| Time: day, noon, sunset, night, real | day |

- **Weather and time** are client-side for everyone on the island and reset when they leave. "Storm" is rain plus thunder sounds only they hear. "Real" follows the Oslo clock (refreshed every 30 s).
- **Spawning:** "mob spawning off" blocks natural, patrol, reinforcement, trap, jockey, mount and village-invasion spawns. Spawners, eggs, breeding and commands always work. Mob stacking is not a setting (S11).
- **Island fly:** with `skyblock.island.fly` or the Wings mastery, double-tap jump inside the island border. Members and coop always; visitors only while "visitor fly" is on. Leaving the border, losing the right or a setting change turns it off at once; the next fall within 10 s does no damage. Creative, staff mode and freeze are never touched.
- **Related, personal:** island auto-pickup (blocks you break on your own island go to your inventory) is a `/settings` switch, on by default.
- **Planned:** Wings mastery at island level 20 for $60k (B14). Island fly as a rank perk is a convenience perk: a bought rank unlocks it at once, and it is also earnable through the Wings mastery. Which rank gets it waits for the perk-track plan (REVIEW3 F7).

## What the player sees
- The Settings button in the island menu and in `/is members`; a menu tile per setting with its state.
- A short chat line "<setting> is now <value>" when a setting changes.
- "Island fly on: double-tap jump" and "Island fly off: you left the island".

## Rules and limits
- Fly is checked on block moves plus once a second (every 20 ticks), only in the islands world. No per-tick scan.
- Weather and time never change the real world; they are packets for players on that island only.
- The islands world itself has natural spawning and fire spread on (`worlds.yml`); the module switches them off per island by default.
- Bedrock: toggles are one plain click.

## Decided
- I3 and Round 3 flags: the list and defaults above (mob stacking not a setting).
- 188: weather default always sunny and time always day, changeable in `/is settings`.
- 189 and #16: server time is Oslo; "real" follows the Oslo clock.
- I4: island fly for everyone on the island, also visitors, via `skyblock.island.fly` or Wings.
- 243: some ranks will get fly; ranks are planned later.
- 244: mob spawning and fire spread are off per island by default.
- MA12 and P5-20: Wings at island level 20 for $60k.
- REVIEW3 F7: perks unlock along one progression track from start game to end game, like a battle pass (not by hours played). A bought rank unlocks its cosmetic and convenience perks at once. Gameplay perks are never sold. Needs its own plan before anything is built.
