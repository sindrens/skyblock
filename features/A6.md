# Portals · id: A6 · status: Built · version: 1

## In one sentence
Step into a portal in a finished build and you travel to its target at once (island, Depths, spawn, warzone and more), with a short wait only if you are in combat.

## How it works
- **Built.** The builders mark portals in each build file. `/skyblock builds apply` stores them in `build-points.yml`. The worlds module reads that list, so a new portal needs no code.
- **Trigger:** a small air box in the portal frame. Stepping in is detected when a player changes block in a world that has portals (one hash lookup).
- **Targets:** `island`, `spawn`, `depths`, `warzone` (`portals.world-targets`), `world:<name>`, `point:<category>:<id>`. A world target lands in front of that world's portal leading back, else at the world spawn.
- **Portals in the current builds:**

| Portal | Target | State |
|---|---|---|
| `to_island` (Gatehouse) | your island | Open. Creates the island on first use (A2) |
| `to_depths` (Gatehouse) | Depths arrival pad | Open after tutorial step 5 |
| `to_warzone` (Gatehouse) | Warzone | Says "The warzone isn't built yet" until a warzone build is applied, then opens after the tutorial |
| `to_outposts`, `to_events` (Gatehouse) | Outposts, Events | Closed with iron bars: "This portal isn't open yet. Soon!" |
| `to_spawn` (Depths gate) | In front of the Gatehouse Depths portal | Open |

- **Travel:** instant teleport (reason `PORTAL`), a title with the target name ("The Depths / Mine, chop and fight"), and a sound. Warzone: "PvP is on here".
- **Combat rule:** a combat-tagged player, or anyone inside the warzone, waits 3 s inside the portal (`portals.combat-delay-seconds`). Leaving the portal cancels it. Staff with `skyblock.staff.teleport.bypass` skip the wait.
- **Refusals:** frozen players, closed portals and missing worlds. The player gets one message (at most every 2 s) and is pushed back out.
- **Grace:** after a portal teleport, portals ignore you for 1 s so you cannot bounce back.
- **Warzone only by portal:** teleports into the warzone from other worlds (`/tpa`, homes, warps) are refused. Staff `/tp` still works.
- **Particles:** each portal can have a particle (for example happy-villager at the island portal), puffed every 10 ticks, 6 per puff, only when players are in that world.
- **Config overrides** in `worlds.yml: portals.*` without touching the build: `island-portal`, `force-open`, `disabled`, `target-overrides`, `need-build`. Apply with `/skyblock reload worlds`; a new `/skyblock builds apply` is picked up at once.

## What the player sees
- Names above each portal ("Warzone", "The Depths", "Your Island", "Outposts", "Events") as holograms.
- Locked by the tutorial: pushed back out with "Unlocks at tutorial step 5 (Menu)" or "Unlocks when you finish the tutorial".
- In combat: action bar "In combat: the portal takes you in 3 s. Stay inside."
- Closed or unbuilt: the messages in the table above.

## Rules and limits
- No portal can be used while frozen by staff.
- Tutorial locks: Depths portal after step 5, warzone, outposts and events after the finish (A3). If the tutorial module is off, they are open.
- Anti-abuse: no bouncing (1 s grace), refusal messages are rate limited, warzone entry cannot be skipped with `/tpa`.
- Performance: no per-tick scan. Particles run on one repeating task and skip worlds with no players or unloaded chunks.
- Bedrock: walk-in only, no chat or typed commands.
- Vanilla Nether and End portals are disabled in the islands world.
