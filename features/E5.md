# Depths drops · id: E5 · status: Draft · version: 1

## In one sentence
Every ore, log and Depths mob drops one custom item for its tier (Coal, Copper ... Voidstone; Oak Log ... Warped Stem; Grave Flesh ... Warlord Sigil). They go into your satchel, sell for more at each tier, feed the Forge, and sometimes come with crystals. Geodes and key fragments are placeholders for later.

## How it works
- **Built:**
  - **One item type per tier** (#809). Mine: Coal, Copper, Iron, Gold, Redstone, Lapis, Diamond, Emerald, Amethyst, Quartz, Netherite, Voidstone. Forest: Oak, Birch, Spruce, Jungle, Acacia, Dark Oak, Mangrove, Cherry, Pale Oak logs, then Crimson and Warped stems. Combat: Grave Flesh, Crypt Bone, Venom Eye, Bog Slime, Tide Shard, Hex Dust, Raider Scrap, Forge Rod, Wither Ash, Warlord Sigil.
  - **Mining and chopping:** 1 item per block, multiplied by the tool's fortune (+5% per Forge tier above 1, `tools.yml: fortune.per-tier-percent`), at most 3 per block (`depths.yml: drops.max-per-block`).
  - **Crystals:** a chance per block of 1 crystal: 5% at tier 1, growing x1.3 per tier to about 90% at tier 12. Crystal bonuses (enchants, sets) scale the chance.
  - **Combat kills:** about 4 drops (3 to 5) and a crystal chance per kill. The mini-boss gives 15x the drops, 20x the XP and crystals.
  - **Payout:** crystals are added up and paid every 3 seconds as one ledger row (`depths_drop`), shown as "+N crystals" on the action bar.
  - **Sell prices** (balance sheet): mine tier 1 $20, tier 2 $27, tier 3 $36, tier 4 $49, tier 5 $66, tier 6 $90, tier 7 $121, tier 8 $163 (x1.35 per tier), then x2.2 per tier: $360, $791, $1.7k, $3.8k. Forest logs match the mine tier by tier. A combat kill is worth $104 (tier 1) to about $14.7k (tier 10) in drops.
  - **Use:** drops are Forge materials (E10), sellable (C4), tradeable and listable on the auction house (#812). A placed Depths log becomes a plain vanilla log and loses its value.
  - **Other drops** in the same moment: enchant books (E12) and blueprints (E11), each rolled by its own module.
- **Planned (decided, not built):**
  - **Geodes** (tier 9+) and **Key fragments** (tier 11+) exist as items with drop chance 0 and no use. A geode gives 5 to 50 crystals (#811).
  - **Fragments (R3-18):** 3 fragments make 1 Phoenix key (not 10 for any key). They drop in mine and forest tiers 11+ (later fishing, 1 in 4,000). The chance is not set (open question 1).
  - **Own drop table per tier (REVIEW3 D2):** when tiers share a zone (E1), each tier has its own drop table and quantities, chosen by the player's active tier.
  - Custom items from Depths logs and mine materials have their own sell values (#695).

## What the player sees
- Action bar while gathering: "+N crystals". Rare find message: "A rare find. Its use arrives in a later update."
- Item names with a rarity footer (common to legendary). The satchel shows amount, price and value per item (E4).
- Combat: kill drops land in the satchel; mini-boss message "Rewards are in your satchel."

## Rules and limits
- Drops follow the owner's income meta: the Depths are about 50% of income (economy sheet). Mini-boss and boss rewards count as event income, not extra.
- A full satchel gives no drops today; the owner decided drops go to the inventory, then `/box` (R3-22, E4). Spawner mobs and island blocks never give Depths drops.
- Anti-abuse: every drop item has a fixed id and a sell key. Staff-given copies are tagged and unsellable (ANS3). Crystal payouts have an idempotency key, so a retry never pays twice.
- Performance: one roll per accepted break; crystals batched; no scans.
- Bedrock: items look like their vanilla base (coal, raw copper, echo shard) and need no resource pack.

## Decided
- 809: each tier drops one item type.
- 812: Depths drops are tradeable and listable on the auction house.
- 811: a geode gives 5 to 50 crystals.
- R3-18: 3 fragments make 1 Phoenix key; fragments drop in mine and forest tiers 11+ (later fishing, 1 in 4,000).
- REVIEW3 D2: each tier in a shared zone has its own drop table and quantities.
- 644: late Depths prices grow x2.2 per tier instead of x2.4.
- 695: Depths materials have their own sell values.
- 776: mine and forest Forge recipes use Depths drops.
- 772: combat kills drop custom items used for sword recipes.

## Open questions
1. How many hours of tier 11+ play should one Phoenix key take? This sets the fragment chance. Default: the owner gives a number; otherwise one is suggested with the crate build.
2. Which tiers drop geodes? Config lists tiers 9 to 12; chance is 0 today. Default: enable with the crates work.
3. Should blueprint chances move from `tools.yml` into the drop tables? Default: stay in `tools.yml`.
4. Block placing Depths drops (they become vanilla)? Default: allowed.
