# Anticheat · id: H8 · status: Draft · version: 1

## In one sentence
GrimAC watches movement, combat and packets and warns staff when someone looks like a cheater. It never punishes by itself: staff get a suggestion and decide.

## How it works
- **Built: GrimAC 2.3.x** is installed with its config tracked in `server/plugins/GrimAC`. Alerts go to staff with `grim.alerts` and to the console. Violation history is kept in a local SQLite database (`/grim history`). The Discord webhook is off.
- **Built: no automatic punishment.** Grim's groups (Simulation, Knockback, Post, BadPackets, Reach, Hitboxes, Misc, Combat, Autoclicker) only run `[alert]`, `[log]`, `[webhook]` and `[proxy]`. No kick or ban command is set. Violations expire after 300s.
- **Built: `/psuggest <player> <reason-id> [details]`** (console, Grim or staff) creates a normal punishment suggestion: "Steve (Grim: Reach vl 12). Suggested: Hacked client, ban 30d [Apply] [Dismiss] [Punish menu]". One waiting suggestion per player and reason. Repeats are quiet and add their details (newest 5 lines). The ladder of the reason decides the punishment. Each call is audited.
- **Planned: connect Grim to `/psuggest`.** Nothing calls it yet because the owner has not chosen thresholds. A safe start (server/CONFIG_NOTES.md): only groups with few false flags (Reach, Hitboxes, Knockback, Timer) at a high threshold, for example `"10:10 psuggest %player% hacking Grim: %check_name% vl %vl%"`, and Autoclicker `"40:40 psuggest %player% macro ..."`.
- **Built: Depths anti-fast-break.** A break faster than 0.7 of the vanilla time (plus 100 ms grace) is ignored without a message. 3 flagged breaks in 10s alert staff (one alert per player per 30s) and write an audit row.
- **Planned (audit idea F-11b):** a drops-per-minute cap above a human maximum and a "same yaw and pitch for N breaks" counter. The output goes to staff review, not a ban.
- **Server settings that fit Grim:** `allow-flight: true` (Grim handles fly cheats), old clients below 1.21.5 are blocked by ViaVersion because their protocol is easy to exploit.

## What the player sees
- Nothing. Grim is silent for players. A suspected cheater is not told until a punishment is applied.
- Staff see Grim alerts in chat and then the suggestion line with [Apply] [Dismiss] [Punish menu].
- Setbacks (Grim moves a player back) are visible to the player as rubber-banding.

## Rules and limits
- Anticheat suggestions are never applied automatically, in any mode. `extras.never-auto: [hacking, xray, macro]` documents which reasons may never be automatic.
- Lag and ping spikes cause false flags, which is why it stays suggestion-only.
- Hacking, X-ray and macro ladders never expire (offences count forever). Bans of 30 days or more need an admin to approve.
- Bedrock: players join through Geyser. No Bedrock test of Grim is recorded in the docs. Floodgate must also give each Bedrock player their real IP, because the per-IP rules (AFK, ratings, warzone, gems, mini-boss) rely on it (REVIEW3 §3 fix 9).
- Performance: Grim is a separate plugin with its own packet checks. For the load test, bots must be exempt or Grim relaxed (PERFORMANCE.md).
- Alt checks are a different job (H2: `/alts`, raid lockdown).
