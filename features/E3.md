# Tiers and gates · id: E3 · status: Draft · version: 1

## In one sentence
Each Depths tier opens at a skill level (Mining, Foraging or Combat). Open tiers can be visited any time through `/dungeon`, including lower ones; locked tiers show iron bars and push you back.

## How it works
- **Unlock levels** (`zones.yml: required-level`):

| Tier | Mine (Mining) | Forest (Foraging) | Combat (Combat) |
|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | 0 Coal | 0 Oak | 0 Graveyard |
| 2 | 5 Copper | 5 Birch | 6 Bone Crypt |
| 3 | 10 Iron | 10 Spruce | 12 Spider Den |
| 4 | 15 Gold | 15 Jungle | 18 Slime Bog |
| 5 | 20 Redstone | 20 Acacia | 24 Sunken Ruins |
| 6 | 25 Lapis | 25 Dark Oak | 30 Witch Hollow |
| 7 | 30 Diamond | 30 Mangrove | 38 Pillager Outpost |
| 8 | 35 Emerald | 35 Cherry | 46 Blaze Forge |
| 9 | 42 Amethyst | 42 Pale Oak | 55 Wither Keep |
| 10 | 50 Quartz | 50 Crimson | 66 Warlord's Citadel |
| 11 | 58 Netherite | 58 Warped | none |
| 12 | 66 Voidstone | none | none |

- **Access follows the live skill level** of your active profile. Unlocks and first visits are remembered per profile and per season (`tiers.season-id`) and audited.
- **Gate:** each zone has a 5 x 5 opening. If you are locked out, only you see iron bars there (`tiers.gate-block`). The real gate stays open, so the code checks every move.
- **Locked player:** pushed back when walking in, teleporting in or throwing a pearl; gets no XP and no drops there. If a zone becomes locked while you stand in it (staff lower your XP), you are sent to The Gate.
- **Unlock:** a level-up that opens a tier shows a chat line with a [Teleport] button and a sound. The first visit shows a title.
- **`/dungeon`:** opens on the wing you stand in. Teleport is instant. It takes 3 seconds only while you are combat-tagged or leaving a combat tier (moving, damage or a new combat tag cancels it).
- **Pick a lower tier:** nothing blocks it. Lower tiers just pay less per block. A lower Forge tool also mines higher ore, only slower (#826), because break speed comes from the tool.
- **Planned (decided, REVIEW3 D1, D2):** when tiers share a zone (E1), you pick your active tier in a tier selector. Each tier in a shared zone has its own drop table and quantities (E5). The Forge and blueprints still need the lower material, such as coal after iron unlocks.
- **Tool and set gates:** the Forge uses the same levels for tool tiers; Wardrobe sets and Enchanter rolls read the same numbers from their own config copies.

## What the player sees
- `/dungeon` tier item: "Tier 4 . Gold", "Needs Mining 15 (9/15)" with a 10-dot progress bar and "(6 to go)", or "You are here".
- Locked action bar: "Needs Mining 15 (9/15) . Mine Tier 4".
- Unlock line: "Unlocked Mine Tier 2 Verdigris Cut! [Teleport]". A locked `/tpahere` says that tier is still locked.

## Rules and limits
- Anti-abuse: no guest passes (#821); `/tpahere` and pearls into locked tiers are refused (#346). Staff `/depths tp` gives a pass for one zone only. Builders and admins with `skyblock.depths.bypass` skip locks and the warmup.
- Skill levels are per profile, so a second profile starts at tier 1 (profiles are islands). A season reset clears skills, so tiers lock again.
- Performance: one gate check per player every 20 ticks plus move events in the Depths; no region scans.
- Bedrock: the whole flow is the `/dungeon` chest menu; bars are packets.

## Connections
- Skills (E9) give the levels. Zones (E1), blocks (E2), drops (E5), combat (E6).
- Tools and Forge (E10), enchants (E12) and wardrobe (E13) use the same unlock numbers.
- Teleports (G11), tutorial (A3: Depths opens after step 5), island level (B13: Depths tiers count), progression track (F1) and AFK pool (D10) read the best tier.

## Decided
- 346: `/tpahere` into a tier you have not unlocked is refused.
- 345: teleports out of combat zones, the warzone and the boss arena wait 3 seconds.
- #62: a locked gate shows iron bars. #63: a player in a zone that becomes locked goes to The Gate.
- #61 (Round 2 list): Combat unlock levels 0/6/12/18/24/30/38/46/55/66.
- 821: no guest passes into locked zones. 824: ore tiers have no player cap.
- 826: a lower tool can mine higher ore, only slower. #65: max skill level 150; tiers stop at level 66.
- REVIEW3 D2: a player can pick a lower unlocked tier; each tier in a shared zone has its own drop table and quantities.
- REVIEW3 D1: tiers move into 5 zones per line with tier palettes (E1).

## Open questions
1. The unlock numbers live in four places (`zones.yml`, `enchanter.yml`, `wardrobe.yml`, tools). Default: keep them in sync by hand until a shared tier service exists.
2. First unlock of every tier is audited, including tier 1 (3 rows per new player). Default: audit all.
3. On first join after a release, existing players unlock every tier they already reached, with one toast. Default: toast with the first 3 lines and "+N more".
4. In a shared zone, which tier is active after a new unlock? Default: the newest unlocked tier, with the selector to go lower.

## Rejected ideas
- Unlock by blocks broken, logs or kills (early open question 7): skill levels do this job instead (REVIEW A2, one clear job per system).
- Invisible barrier blocks for locked gates: confusing, so iron bars are used.
