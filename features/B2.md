# Protection and roles · id: B2 · status: Draft · version: 1

## In one sentence
Every action on an island is checked against the player's role (Leader, Admin, Mod, Member, Coop or Visitor), and the leader can change what each role may do in a permission matrix.

## How it works
- **Roles:** Leader (always everything), Admin, Mod, Member, Coop (temporary, never stored), Visitor (not on the island).
- **Permissions (rows of the matrix):** break, place, interact (doors, buttons, levers, beds, workstations), containers, redstone, animals, villagers (trade), pickup, drop, armor stands (also item frames and paintings), vehicles, fire, ender pearls, spawners, hurt monsters. Extra rows with their own defaults: island settings, notes (read, write, manage), bank (deposit, withdraw, history).
- **Defaults** (`islands.yml: permissions.*`):

| Role | Default |
|---|---|
| Admin, Mod | everything (settings and bank rows have their own lists) |
| Member | all except spawners |
| Coop | build, farm, containers, redstone, pickup, drop, vehicles, pearls; no fire, no spawners |
| Visitor | nothing (walk and look), but may hurt hostile mobs |

- **Matrix:** `/is members` then Permissions. Columns Admin, Mod, Member, Coop, Visitor; 4 rows per page; one plain click flips a cell. Only differences from the defaults are stored. Leader edits all columns, Admin edits Mod and below, Mod edits Member, Coop and Visitor. Members see it read-only.
- **World rules:** nothing outside the border; blocks only between Y 0 and 256. Liquids, pistons, falling blocks, explosions, tree growth, fire and spreading never change a block outside their own island. Explosions can't hurt entities on another island. PvP is off. Nether and end portals can't be built or used. Mobs, items and empty vehicles pushed outside are put back; a player inside a border can't be pushed or walk out.
- **Staff:** `/is bypass` (admin) ignores protection. Off by default, off again on quit, every toggle logged.
- **Planned (decided): staff creative editing.** In `/s` staff mode, owner and admin may use creative on islands. `/is bypass` switches on by itself when entering `/s` and off when leaving. Every block placed or broken is logged. Creative items are staff-tagged (can't be sold, traded or listed). (REVIEW3 F15; also R3-6 asked for this.)
- **Planned (decided): hopper counter.** Hoppers are counted per chunk on block place and break only (an int in memory, rebuilt lazily on chunk load; no world scan). Staff get at most one alert per chunk per hour above 64 hoppers. No hard cap. (REVIEW3 F13)
- **Planned:** an "Upgrades" box in the matrix (Leader always, Admin on, Mod and Member off) with `/is upgrade` (B14).

## What the player sees
- A short action-bar message when blocked, at most one per 1.5 s. Stepping on plates and item pickup are silent.
- The matrix GUI with a plain click per cell (works on Bedrock).

## Rules and limits
- Every change of a cell is logged (`island_perm`).
- Coop players never get settings, notes-manage or bank rights. Only stored members can hold the management rows.
- The entity return sweep and the border checks run on a timer (every 40 ticks around occupied islands only). No per-tick scan of all islands.
- No block, hopper, piston or observer limits (O1, O2, WR2): lag is handled by optimized systems, lag-machine detection and the hopper alert above.
- Today `/is bypass` is a manual toggle; it is logged and never kept after relog.
