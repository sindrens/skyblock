# Anti-dupe and security · id: H7 · status: Draft · version: 1

## In one sentence
Valuable items have a hidden id so a copy can be found, flagged and blocked from sale and trade, and money and item paths are built so a crash or a trick can't create value.

## How it works
- **Built: unique items.** Tools, enchant books, set tokens, tag items and money notes carry a hidden custom id, a UUID, a creation time and a source (for example `crate:rare`, `admin:Alex`). `/logs item <uuid>` shows the item's whole life.
- **Built: dupe detector.** Every online player is read again once per 60s (a slice each second) and shortly after joining or opening or closing an inventory. It checks inventories, ender chests, open containers and cursors. If one UUID shows up twice, **both copies are flagged**, staff with `skyblock.staff.notify` get "DUPE <item> found 2x from <source>. All copies are flagged.", the console logs it, and an audit row (`item/dupe`) is written. One alert per item per 10 minutes.
- **Built: a flagged item** can't be sold, listed on the auction house or traded: "This item is flagged as a possible dupe. Ask staff." No staff command to clear a flag was found.
- **Built: staff suggestion.** A dupe report creates a suggestion in the punishment system with the `exploiting` ladder (ban 7d first) and "also freeze balance" in the details. It is never applied by itself.
- **Planned (decided): automatic 24 h trade block (R3-28).** When the system finds a duplicated unique item, the holders are blocked from `/pay`, notes, the auction house and `/trade` for 24 h. It ends by itself. Staff decide afterwards and can upgrade it to a balance freeze.
- **Built: money notes.** Each note is a database row, so a copy is taken from the hand, staff are alerted and `note_dupe` is logged. A duped note can't make money: the second cash-in is refused.
- **Built: staff items and bound items.** Items from `/give`, `/i` and creative are tagged and can't be sold, traded or auctioned. Loot from bound keys (votes, AFK pool, calendar) is bound to the profile and can't be traded.
- **Built: crash safety (SECFIX1).** The player file is saved before an item moves into the database (auction, spawner add, box, satchel, forge). A kick restore gives back membership only, never money or items. Orphaned spawners are frozen, not deleted. Box has a hard cap of 5,000 stacks and stops the source instead of deleting. Shop hand-overs stuck by a crash go to `review`. Sanctions fail closed: if the check breaks, paying and trading are refused.
- **Built: server config.** Autosave every 30s, tripwire and chorus updates off, TNT and sand height limits 320, TNT can't move in water, piston duplication off.
- **Planned (decided): start-money rule (REVIEW3 F1).** A profile can send money away (`/pay`, notes, `/trade`, buying on the auction house) only after it has earned $10,000 itself. Sending to members of the island the sender is on, including island bank deposits, is always allowed.
- **Planned (decided): `/eco` limit (R3-6, R3-39).** An `/eco` change up to $1T applies at once and is flagged. Above $1T it waits until an owner or admin clicks Allow in the alert. `/eco` refuses your own account and any account on the same IP. Today `/eco` has no limit. The alert goes to Discord once the webhook exists.
- **Planned (decided): one bound-item system (R3-2, R3-14).** Loot from bound keys is bound, a key found in a bound crate is bound, and a spawner from a bound key goes straight onto the opener's island stack. Crystals can never be transferred, as a hard rule in code (R3-5b).
- **Planned (decided): notes and spawners (R3-40, R3-13).** A soft limit of 20 notes a minute replaces the 10 s wait, and cashed `money_notes` rows are purged after 90 days. Spawners on islands disbanded or reset on purpose are cleared, not frozen. Staff get a restore button for frozen spawners.
- **Planned (audit docs, still open):** a `/pay` daily cap and 24 h hold (F-06), starter kit once per account (F-14), a nightly count check and an offline scan for non-unique valuables such as spawners and keys (F-07, F-23).

## What the player sees
- The flagged-item message above, in trade, auction and sell.
- A copied money note is taken from the player's hand. Staff see "NOTE <player> tried to cash note #id".
- Planned (decided): a holder of a duped item sees that trading is blocked for 24 h and when it ends (text in `messages/`).
- Players do not see the detector. There are no false alarms for moved items, because a copy only counts while it is still in the slot.

## Rules and limits
- Bedrock: the same item data and the same menus. Menu clicks that Bedrock lacks are not needed. Check that Floodgate gives each Bedrock player a real IP (REVIEW3 §3 fix 9), or the per-IP rules treat all Bedrock players as one.
- Menu buttons run only on plain left, right and shift clicks. All other click types are cancelled.
- Performance: no full scan per tick. One slice of players per second; text is built only for unique items.
- The detector sees online players only. Items of offline players are not scanned.
- Profiles are islands: bound items and sanctions keep value from moving between a player's own profiles.

## Decided
- 332: items from `/give`, `/i` and creative are hidden staff items.
- 458: the balance freeze is a staff click. R3-28 adds an automatic 24 h trade block before staff decide.
- R3-28: a system-detected dupe of a unique item gives the holders a 24 h trade block (`/pay`, notes, AH, `/trade`) that expires by itself. Staff can upgrade it to a freeze.
- 734: locked wardrobe armor, menu items, dupe-flagged items and filled shulker boxes are untradeable.
- 684: staff can void a single money note by its id, logged.
- 531: logs of valuable unique items searchable for 180 days. The code keeps item rows 30 days.
- REVIEW3 F1: a profile sends money only after earning $10k itself, except to members of its current island.
- R3-6: `/eco` up to $1T applies at once and is flagged; above it waits for an Allow click. R3-39: `/eco` can't target your own or a same-IP account.
- R3-2 / R3-14: loot from bound keys is bound; skins, tags and sets bought with gems or credits stay account-bound. REVIEW3 F5 refines this: bought items arrive unclaimed (tradeable anywhere); right-click claims and binds them.
- REVIEW3 F3 / REVIEW3 F4 / R3-5b: bought gems are tradeable, earned gems are bound, crystals never (`transferable=false` in code). No gem limits, but every gem transfer is logged and economy-watch gem thresholds are lowered. Chargeback: freeze the account's gem transfers.
- R3-40: 20 notes a minute, cashed notes purged after 90 days.
- R3-3: AFK stays one earner per connection, plus a staff family whitelist and the Bedrock real-IP check.
- REVIEW3 F8: island bank withdraw is the leader's choice per role, no 3-day rule (closes F-13).
- R3-13: frozen spawners, never deleted by a bug. On purpose disband or reset clears them. Staff get a restore button.

## Open questions
1. The duped item stays flagged and untradeable until staff clear it, but there is no clear command. Add a staff clear button? Default: yes (proposed default).
2. Which of the waiting audit fixes (F-06, F-14) must be done before launch? Default: both.
3. Nightly count check and offline scan for non-unique valuables (F-07, F-23)? Default: yes.
4. Log every creative inventory event, not only blocks (F-21)? Default: yes.
5. R3-6 reading: confirm with the owner that the $1T limit means "applies at once, flagged" and the Allow click is only above it. Default: yes (proposed default).

## Rejected ideas
- Deleting orphaned spawners (the old behaviour): replaced by freezing them (R3-13).
- A full automatic balance freeze on a dupe: the owner chose an automatic 24 h trade block only (R3-28); a freeze stays a staff click (458).
- A 7-day wait before a new island member can use the bank (F-13): the leader decides per role (REVIEW3 F8).
- Gem sending limits or gates: the owner said no (REVIEW3 F4).
