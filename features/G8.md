# Tab list and nametags · id: G8 · status: Draft · version: 1

## In one sentence
The player list (Tab) shows a SKYBORNMC header, the online count, every player with their rank, name and tag sorted by rank, and a footer with the server address; the same rank shows above heads. A redesign is proposed.

## How it works
**Built:**
- **Entry** (chat.yml: `format.tab`): `<rank><name><tag><afk>`. Rank prefix, name in the rank colour, tag in ( ), gray `[AFK]`. Nicknames start with `~`. The title stays in chat only (FIXES1). Players with the default rank show only their name (no prefix).
- **Sorting:** rank weight high to low, then A to Z. One scoreboard team per player does the sorting (`pxs<999-weight><name>`).
- **Header:** `SKYBORNMC` with a white line on the left and a blue line on the right (the colours of the first and last letter), `Phoenix Skyblock` in the phoenix gradient, then `12 online · Season 1 · Preseason` (`tab.season`).
- **Footer:** `» play.skybornmc.net «`. Staff with `skyblock.staff.tab` also see `TPS 20.0 · Ping 34 ms` (TPS green at 19+, orange at 15+, red below).
- **Nametags** above heads show the rank prefix (`format.nametag`).
- **Rank changes** show within about 1 s: LuckPerms events mark the player, and the second refresh comes 0.5 s after the last change.
- **Vanished staff** are hidden from players.
- Admins can edit the chat, tab and nametag formats in `/display` with a live preview (see F4).

**Planned (proposal in TAB_LIST.md, needs owner input):**
- Every entry shows a rank. Players without a bought rank show **Ashborn** (the free rank), given automatically when the tutorial ends (instead of the 30-minute `/freerank` claim). Rank as a short coloured word (`MOD`, `FIREWING`, `ASHBORN`); a badge icon once the resource pack exists.
- Sort: staff (Owner, Admin, Mod, Helper, Builder), won season ranks, donor ranks high to low, Ashborn, then A to Z.
- Header: `SKYBORNMC`, `Phoenix Skyblock`, `187 online · Season 1 · Day 12`. Personal footer: `Island Lv 24 · $1.2M · Ping 34 ms` and the address. Staff footer: `TPS 20.0 · MSPT 31 · 3 vanished · 2 reports`.
- Vanished staff shown to other staff in gray with `(V)`.
- Header and footer update at most every 5 s.

## What the player sees
- Java shows at most 80 names (4 columns of 20). With 300 online most are not visible, so order matters. Staff stay at the top.
- Bedrock: a simple scrolling list. Names keep their dot (303). Header and footer may not show on Bedrock (to verify), so nothing important lives only there.
- Symbols (arrows, dots) must not show as boxes on Bedrock.

## Rules and limits
- Updates are diff only: a cheap fingerprint per player (prefix, colour, weight, nick, AFK, title, tag). Work is spread over ticks with a 400 microsecond budget per tick; refresh checks use `tab.update-ticks` (40).
- Known cost: every player has their own scoreboard and every board holds a team for every player. About 90,000 team jobs at 300 players (PERFORMANCE.md P-07). Proposed fix: sort with `setPlayerListOrder` and keep nametag teams only for nearby players `(proposed default)`. Left for an owner decision.
- Rank and tag text goes through the same filters as chat (nicknames, 304).
- Principle 8: ranks are cosmetic here; sorting gives no gameplay edge.

## Review v2 (31 Oct 2026, PROPOSAL, not decided; owner's tab sketch "Phoenix // (ping) x (ip address) and under (players active)", "the top of the scoreboard over every player", "the logo of the server"; questions HT-Q1, HT-Q2, HT-Q7, HT-Q8 in `questions/HUD-TAB.json`)
**What is built (`TabService`, `messages/chat.yml tab.*`, read in `q/gui-phase1`):**
- Header (6 lines): blank, `──── SKYBORNMC ────` (frost left, blue right), `Phoenix Skyblock` gradient, blank, `N online · Season 1 · Preseason`, blank. Footer: blank, `» play.skybornmc.net «`, blank; staff with `skyblock.staff.tab` get `TPS 20.0 · Ping 34 ms` under it. Sent per viewer, resent only when the viewer's header key changes (online count, max, season, platform) or the texts reload.
- Entry (`format.tab`, `Display#tabEntry`): `<rank><name><title><tag><afk>` as ONE list name for everybody (the client ignores team prefixes when a list name is set, so there is no per-viewer rank form on the tab). Sort: one team per player `pxs<999-weight><name>` on every viewer's board, so rank weight high to low then A to Z.
- Ping: the per-name latency bars are drawn by the client (Java and Bedrock). The only ping text is the staff footer. The header key has no ping, so a personal ping needs a new rule.
- Bedrock: Bedrock has no player-list header or footer, so the header and footer are probably invisible there (to verify with a real client); nothing important may live only in them.
- Not built: Ashborn on every entry, the staff/won/donor sort groups, the personal footer, the `(V)` marker for staff viewers (still the planned list in "How it works").

**Proposed header, owner's format** (3 lines; blank lines above and below; line 1 is the logo glyph U+E001 for Java with the pack loaded, else bold phoenix-gradient text, same rule as the scoreboard, see G7 Review v2):
```
                                           
            PHOENIX SKYBLOCK                ← logo (pack) or gradient text
      Ping 23 ms  ·  play.skybornmc.net     ← ping coloured, address in sky_ice
          187 players online                ← count bold white, label muted
                                           
 [OWNER] Sindre    [MOD] Pouched   [ASHBORN] Steve   ...
                                           
            Season 1 · Day 12               ← only when the season service exists (I5)
        TPS 20.0 · MSPT 31 · 3 vanished     ← staff only
```
- **Ping:** the viewer's own `Player#getPing()`, coloured green below 60 ms, orange below 150 ms, red above (`chat.yml tab.ping-good: 60`, `tab.ping-ok: 150`; texts `tab.ping.*`). It joins the header key rounded to 10 ms, and the header is resent at most every 5 seconds per viewer (`tab.header-min-seconds: 5`), so 300 viewers cost about 60 small packets per second at worst, usually far fewer because most pings sit inside one bucket.
- **Address moves to the header** (owner's format). The footer then holds the season line (hidden until I5) and the staff line; with both empty the footer is one blank line. This replaces the UX1b header (`SKYBORNMC` rule lines); the network name leaves the tab (HT-Q7 option b keeps it).
- **Count:** `N players online` (`1 player online`), everybody except vanished staff (`Visibility.knows`); AFK players count (they are online). A staff viewer sees the real count and, in the staff line, `3 vanished`.
- **Entry:** `[MOD] Pouched` : rank word in the rank colour, name in the rank colour, then the AFK marker. No island level, no tag, no title (HT-Q8; titles stay in chat, FIXES1). Everyone gets a rank word (Ashborn when no bought rank, open question 1, F2 fixes the word list). Latency bars stay vanilla.
- **Order:** staff (Owner, Admin, Mod, Helper, Builder), season-won ranks, donor ranks high to low, Ashborn, then A to Z inside each group (open question 2, default yes). Java shows 80 names, so staff and top donors are what a crowded server shows first; "friends first" stays rejected (needs per-viewer sorting).
- **Bedrock-safe:** only letters, `[`, `]`, `·`, digits; no glyph ever reaches a Bedrock viewer; Bedrock sees names with rank words, the header is bonus.
- **Nameplates (above heads):** unchanged and decided (G8-R1-01): the rank word and the name, nothing else. "Over every player" most likely means the header sits above the whole player list, or an extra line above each head: HT-Q2 asks. A floating hologram above every player is not recommended: one more entity per player seen by 300 viewers, and Bedrock may show a box for the glyph.

**Developer decisions (defaults; the owner only vetoes):**
- The header text is built per viewer and cached by key (`headerKey` gains `logo` and `pingBucket`); the existing generation counter keeps reloads cheap. The rank-sorting fix P-07 (`setPlayerListOrder`, nametag teams for nearby players only) stays proposed; this review does not depend on it.
- Config keys: `tab.ping-good`, `tab.ping-ok`, `tab.header-min-seconds` (5), `logo.enabled` shared with hud (G7). Messages: `tab.header` (3 lines), `tab.header-logo` (1 line = the glyph), `tab.footer`, `tab.ping.good|ok|bad`, `tab.footer-staff` (adds MSPT and vanished).
- Tests: header key changes only at a ping bucket change, the plain header has no glyph, the header never exceeds 6 lines, count never includes a vanished player for a normal viewer, plural form.
- Docs: UI_STYLE §6.2 and TEST_GUIDE still show the old header (they also still say the tab shows the title).
