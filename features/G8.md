# Tab list and nametags · id: G8 · status: Draft · version: 1

## In one sentence
The player list (Tab) shows a SKYBORNMC header, the online count, every player with their rank, name and tag sorted by rank, and a footer with the server address; the same rank shows above heads. A redesign is proposed.

## How it works
**Built:**
- **Entry** (chat.yml: `format.tab`): `<rank><name><tag><afk>`. Rank prefix, name in the rank colour, tag in ( ), gray `[AFK]`. Nicknames start with `~`. The title stays in chat only (FIXES1). Players with the default rank show only their name (no prefix).
- **Sorting:** rank weight high to low, then A to Z. One scoreboard team per player does the sorting (`pxs<999-weight><name>`).
- **Header:** `SKYBORNMC` with a white line on the left and a blue line on the right (the colours of the first and last letter), `Phoenix Skyblock` in the phoenix gradient, then `12 online · Season 1 · Preseason` (`tab.season`).
- **Footer:** `» play.skybornmc.net «`. Staff with `skyblock.staff.tab` also see `TPS 20.0 · Ping 34 ms` (TPS green at 19+, orange at 15+, red below).
- **Nametags** above heads show the rank prefix (`format.nametag`).
- **Rank changes** show within about 1 s: LuckPerms events mark the player, and the second refresh comes 0.5 s after the last change.
- **Vanished staff** are hidden from players.
- Admins can edit the chat, tab and nametag formats in `/display` with a live preview (see F4).

**Planned (proposal in TAB_LIST.md, needs owner input):**
- Every entry shows a rank. Players without a bought rank show **Ashborn** (the free rank), given automatically when the tutorial ends (instead of the 30-minute `/freerank` claim). Rank as a short coloured word (`MOD`, `FIREWING`, `ASHBORN`); a badge icon once the resource pack exists.
- Sort: staff (Owner, Admin, Mod, Helper, Builder), won season ranks, donor ranks high to low, Ashborn, then A to Z.
- Header: `SKYBORNMC`, `Phoenix Skyblock`, `187 online · Season 1 · Day 12`. Personal footer: `Island Lv 24 · $1.2M · Ping 34 ms` and the address. Staff footer: `TPS 20.0 · MSPT 31 · 3 vanished · 2 reports`.
- Vanished staff shown to other staff in gray with `(V)`.
- Header and footer update at most every 5 s.

## What the player sees
- Java shows at most 80 names (4 columns of 20). With 300 online most are not visible, so order matters. Staff stay at the top.
- Bedrock: a simple scrolling list. Names keep their dot (303). Header and footer may not show on Bedrock (to verify), so nothing important lives only there.
- Symbols (arrows, dots) must not show as boxes on Bedrock.

## Rules and limits
- Updates are diff only: a cheap fingerprint per player (prefix, colour, weight, nick, AFK, title, tag). Work is spread over ticks with a 400 microsecond budget per tick; refresh checks use `tab.update-ticks` (40).
- Known cost: every player has their own scoreboard and every board holds a team for every player. About 90,000 team jobs at 300 players (PERFORMANCE.md P-07). Proposed fix: sort with `setPlayerListOrder` and keep nametag teams only for nearby players `(proposed default)`. Left for an owner decision.
- Rank and tag text goes through the same filters as chat (nicknames, 304).
- Principle 8: ranks are cosmetic here; sorting gives no gameplay edge.

## Decided
- UX1b: remove the command footer line, nicer TPS and address, the lines beside SKYBORN match the colours.
- FIXES1: tab ranks fixed; the title stays in chat, not in tab.
- 303: Bedrock names keep their dot.
- RTA-2, RTA-3: donor ranks Hatchling to Immortal; the free rank is renamed from Ember to Ashborn.
- R3-16: the tutorial is the only gate for new accounts; the old 30-minute rule is dead and its keys are deleted. This supports Ashborn at the end of the tutorial (open question 1).
- REVIEW3 D4: one shared visibility service computes who is hidden from whom (`hidden = vanished || !allowed`), so a second feature cannot unvanish staff.
