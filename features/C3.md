# Money notes · id: C3 · status: Draft · version: 1

## In one sentence
`/withdraw` turns money into a paper note you can hand to someone, drop or keep. Right-click cashes it. Each note is one database row, so a copy can never be cashed twice.

## How it works
- **Built: `/withdraw <amount> [currency]`.** Money is taken and a "Money Note" (paper, with glint) appears. Amounts like `5k`.
  - Minimum $100, maximum $1T per note. No fee. A free inventory slot is checked before any money is taken.
  - Opens at the tutorial's bank step. Frozen players can't.
  - Money only today (`economy.yml: notes.currencies`). Gems and crystals can't be noted. **Planned (decided):** shard notes, by adding `shards` to `notes.currencies` (R3-5b). Crystals never.
- **Built: the database row.** Each note has an id (the item's unique id), amount, maker, date and status (active, redeemed, void). The amount always comes from the row, never from the item.
- **Built: cashing.** Right-click the note in your main hand. One transaction locks the row, marks it redeemed and pays you. Two clicks or two servers can't pay twice.
- **Built: copies.** A note that is already cashed, or is not in the database, is taken from the hand and staff get an alert (audit `note_dupe`). A note is a bearer item: whoever holds it can cash it, even if its maker is frozen or banned (683).
- **Built: staff.** `/eco note <player> <amount> <reason>` gives an online player a note without taking money (the money appears when it is cashed). Staff can void one note by its id (684).
- **Built: where a note can go.** `/trade`, dropping it, a chest. Not the auction house (`auction.yml: items.allow-money-notes: false`), not the sell chest, not crafting, villagers or cartography tables (669). Your own other profile can't cash it (it is handed back).
- **Planned (decided): 20 notes a minute (R3-40).** A soft limit per player replaces today's 10 s wait between two `/withdraw` (`notes.withdraw-cooldown-seconds: 10`).
- **Planned (decided): purge (R3-40).** Cashed `money_notes` rows are deleted 90 days after cashing. Uncashed notes are kept until the season reset. A late copy of a purged note is still taken, because an unknown note is confiscated.

## What the player sees
- Item name "$5,000 Note". Lore: Amount, Signed by, Date, Note id (#first 8 characters). Action line "» RIGHT-CLICK to redeem".
- Chat: "Made a note of $5,000. Balance: ... (#a1b2c3d4) right-click it to cash it." and "Cashed a note of $5,000 (signed by Steve)."
- Refusals: "The smallest note is $100.", "Inventory full. Free a slot and try again. Nothing was taken.", "Make another note in 7s."
- A copy: "This note was already cashed, so it's a copy. It was taken and staff were told."

## Rules and limits
- Staff alert when one profile cashes 10 notes in an hour (`economy-watch.yml: rules.note-redeem`). Uncashed notes count in the money supply on the dashboard.
- A note can't be made out of thin air by a player: money leaves the account first, in the same step.
- Because a note is a bearer item, a stolen note is gone. Notes move value between players, so the earned-$10k rule covers them (REVIEW3 F1, C2): a profile can make and hand over notes only after it earned $10k itself, except to members of its own island.
- Planned (decided): after the system finds a duped unique item, the holders get a 24 h trade block that includes notes (R3-28). A duped money note itself already cannot make money, because the second cash-in is refused.
- Bedrock: the amount is typed once; cashing is a right-click or tap. Performance: one row per note, indexed by id; no scans.

## Decided
- 552: money notes are not listable on the AH.
- 665: notes from $100 to $1T, no fee, no wait (the 10 s wait in the code is replaced by R3-40).
- 666: notes for every currency type, tradeable by drop, `/trade` and duel wagers. R3-5b settled part of it: crystals never, shards get notes. Gems are open (question 1).
- 667: a note missing from the database is confiscated with a staff alert. 669: no crafting, villager or cartography use.
- 683: anyone holding a note can cash it. 684: staff can void a note by id.
- R3-40: at most 20 notes per minute, and cashed rows purged after 90 days (not built). R3-5b: shard notes on, crystals never. R3-16: delete the dead `notes.new-account-minutes` key. R3-28: 24 h trade block on dupes.
