# /box · id: C9 · status: Draft · version: 1

## In one sentence
Items are never dropped on the ground. Whatever does not fit your inventory waits in your item box until you take it out, so nothing is lost by accident.

## How it works
- **One box per profile.** Items wait until you take them out. They never expire during a season (N2-3) and are cleared at the season reset.
- **What goes in:** the leftovers of the sell chest, `/shop` purchases that do not fit, Forge and Enchanter returns, wardrobe and loadout returns, trade deliveries, AH collection overflow, staff gives (tools, blueprints, spawners, notes, tags, wardrobe tomes) and restored sanction items. While you switch profile or are in staff mode, items go to the box instead of the inventory. Crate rewards, gifts and AFK keys use it as well.
- **`/box`** opens the stacks for your active profile, oldest first, 28 per page. Click a stack to take it, or press **Take all that fit**. Only what fits is taken; the rest stays.
- **Safe delivery.** All stacks of one delivery go in one database transaction. If the database fails, the stacks are retried 3 times, then written to a spool file on disk and imported every 60 s, each with a unique ref so a repeat can never double them.
- **Safe take.** The rows are deleted first, then exactly those stacks are given. Anything that no longer fits goes back into the box. A double click or a second take can never give a stack twice. The player file is saved right after items are given.
- **Soft cap:** 500 stacks per profile. From 90% you are warned. At the cap the box is "full" but nothing is deleted or refused: returns are still stored, and staff see `box_over_cap` in the logs.
- **Hard safety cap:** 5,000 stacks. Only actions that can simply not happen are refused, for example breaking or taking a stacked spawner ("Your /box is too full ... nothing was taken"). Items that already left somewhere are still stored.
- **Staff:** `/inspect <player>` then Box (admins) shows a player's box per profile and removes one stack or clears it, with a confirm and a log. Removed stacks go to a `box_cleared` table and can be restored by hand.
- A profile with items in its box can't be deleted until they are taken out.

## What the player sees
- On join or profile switch: "You have N item(s) in /box" (click to open), also on the action bar.
- When items arrive: a chat line, an action bar line and a sound. An open `/box` refreshes.
- Each stack's lore says where it came from (sell, shop, trade, auction ...) and when it arrived ("3h ago").

## Rules and limits
- Any item staff created (`/give`, `/i`, creative) that reaches a box from a trade, the AH or a gift is flagged (owner answer 332).
- Anti-spam: 250 ms between take clicks.
- Bedrock: tap a stack or "Take all that fit"; the join line opens the menu by click, and `/box` is short.
- Performance: the menu loads at most 2,000 stacks, oldest first; deliveries and takes run off the main thread.
- **Planned (decided): full satchel (R3-22).** Drops go to the inventory, then to `/box`. Breaking stops only when `/box` reaches its hard cap of 5,000 stacks, so the box can't grow forever. The full-satchel warning comes every 3 s. Combat drops use the same chain. Today a full satchel stops the break, the warning comes every 1.5 s, and **combat drops are lost** (a fix, not built). Until it is built, "nothing is lost" is not true for combat drops.

- N2-3: items in `/box` stay until the season reset, never expiring.
- 332: staff-made items are flagged when they pass through trade, AH or gifts.
- R3-22: satchel and combat drops go inventory, then `/box`; breaking stops only at the hard cap (5,000); warning every 3 s.

## Open questions
1. Where does `/box` sit in `/menu`? Every hub slot is used. Default: no hub button yet; join line, `/help box` and the command.
2. Should a hard limit ever refuse more than a stacked spawner (for example AFK keys wait as pending)? Default: soft cap only.
3. An offline killer's stacked-mob enchant book and spawner items still drop in the world. Send them to the box? Default: no, world drop as before.
4. Delete a profile that still has items in its box, or move the items? Default: refuse the delete (moving would be a transfer between own profiles, P4).
