# Logs · id: H5 · status: Draft · version: 1

## In one sentence
Everything that matters on the server is written to one audit log, and staff search it in game with `/logs` to answer "who paid whom", "what happened to this item" and "what did this player say".

## How it works
- **The audit log** (`common/audit`, table `audit_log`, monthly partitions) records economy, trade, auction, island, punishment, staff, config, command, chat, item, auth (joins), world, tutorial, crates and boosters. Each row has time, actor, target, amount, location and details.
- **Writes are batched and async.** Rows are queued and written every 2s or at 500 rows. If the database is down they spool to a file (memory cap 200,000 rows). Nothing blocks the main thread.
- **Retention:** chat, command and item rows are kept 30 days, then archived to a compressed monthly file (`audit.yml: retention.*`, purge daily at 05:00). Everything else is kept forever. Join and IP history is kept forever (security).
- **`/logs`** opens a menu: pick who (online player, any player, a staff member, near me, an item, an island, everyone), a category, and a time range (1h, 24h, 7d, 30d, all). Results are paged, newest first. Hover shows details, click teleports (if you may).
- **Shortcuts:** `/logs player <name> [category] [time]`, `/logs pay <name> [other]`, `/logs trade|chat|commands <name>`, `/logs island <name|id>`, `/logs item <uuid>` (the full life of one unique item), `/logs near [radius]`, `/logs staff <name>`, `/logs page <n>`.
- **`/logs export`** writes a CSV to `plugins/PhoenixSkyblock/exports/` (admins; up to 50,000 rows; the export is logged).
- **Punish from logs:** left-click a chat line in the results to open `/p` with that line as evidence.
- **What staff may see** depends on rank, per category (`skyblock.staff.logs.category.<name>`): helpers chat, commands and joins; mods add economy, trade, auction, island, item, punishment and world; admins add staff actions and export. IPs need `skyblock.staff.whois.ip` (mods and up).
  - **Gap today:** helpers can also read every private message here (`chat/dm`, `server/permissions/logs.txt`). This breaks answer 318.
  - **Planned (decided): hide `chat/dm` from helpers (R3-9b).** Mods and up keep it. Helpers see private messages only through a report.
- **Planned (decided): staff creative edits are logged block by block (REVIEW3 F15).** When the owner or an admin edits an island in creative inside `/s` (see H1), every placed or broken block is written through the normal batched writer. Ordinary players' blocks are still not logged.
- **Viewing logs is logged too** (`staff/logs_view`).

## What the player sees
- Players see nothing, except a privacy line and link on the welcome and in `/rules` (skybornmc.net/privacy).
- Staff see the filter menu "Logs": Current search, Online player, Any player, Staff member, Near me, plus category and time buttons, then the results list.
- Bedrock staff get the same menu (taps). Chat output is for the console, `/logs page`, and a setting that gives Bedrock chat instead of the menu (off).

## Rules and limits
- Every console and in-game command is logged; nothing is hidden from the log by rank, only from the viewer.
- Staff access is limited by category, and viewers only see categories they have the permission for.
- Block break and place history of ordinary players is not in this log. CoreProtect would give it, but there is no build for 26.3 yet, so `/logs near` shows plugin actions only.
- Private messages are logged for 30 days under chat. The 10 DMs a `/report` attaches are copied and kept 180 days (R3-9a). Staff can also use `/socialspy`.
- Performance: searches run async and are capped (500 rows per page). Writes are batched.
- Punishment evidence is copied from the chat rows at punish time, so it survives the 30-day purge (kept 180 days).
