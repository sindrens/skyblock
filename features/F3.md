# Achievements · id: F3 · status: Draft · version: 1

## In one sentence
Goals with tiers that track what you do. Every tier gives boost points for small capped bonuses, and the last tier gives a title.

## How it works
- **32 personal achievements** in 6 categories: Skills (8), Island (5), Combat (3), Economy (7), Gear (5), Exploring (4). Most have several tiers. 4 are secret and show `???` until the first tier: Grandmaster, Overkill, Jackpot, Unstoppable.
- **6 island achievements**, shared by all members: Rising Island (island level), Deep Roots (crops), Stone Heart (blocks mined), Open Doors (visitors), Loved Isle (likes), Team Flame (team islands only).
- **Boost points per tier:** 1 / 2 / 3 / 5 / 8 (`achievements-settings.yml: points.tier-1..5`). A category's points give its boost, up to a cap:

| Points from | Boost | Per point | Cap |
|---|---|---|---|
| Skills | skill XP | +0.3% | +10% |
| Gear | tool XP | +0.4% | +10% |
| Combat | damage to mobs (never in PvP) | +0.3% | +8% |
| Exploring | rare finds | +0.3% | +8% |
| Island (own stats) | crystals | +0.25% | +6% |
| Economy | auction slots | +1 at 12 points, +2 at 30 | +2 slots |

- **Island boosts** (whole island, from island points): production +0.25% per point (cap 8%), island crystals +0.25% (6%), island XP +0.2% (5%), fuel efficiency +0.5% (10%). Island points also feed the island level (capped at 10% of it). Production, island crystals and fuel only work once those systems exist; the menu says "starts to work with ...".
- **Titles:** the last tier of most achievements gives a title (35 are defined). Personal titles go to the account. Island titles go to every member who was on the island 3+ days.
- **Per profile:** progress is separate on each profile. Titles are shared by the account (PR1).
- **Planned:**
  - 5 more island achievements wait for their data: Spawner Nest, Mastery Path, Full Vault, Weekly Fire, Night Shift.
  - Themed title names (Magma Delver, Gold Phoenix ...). The ids stay.
  - +50 pass XP per tier when the battle pass exists (F9).
  - The season reset function exists but nothing calls it yet (needs the season script).

## What the player sees
- `/achievements`, or `/tags` → Achievements button. Three tabs in the top row:
  - **Personal:** summary head and one item per category with its points and "boost now / cap". A tap opens the category: tier, progress bar, goals, next reward.
  - **Island:** island points, the four island boosts, the island achievements, top 5 members.
  - **History:** one book per finished season and profile.
- On unlock: a screen title, a chat line `Achievement: <name> <tier> (+N pts)`, a sound. At most 3 lines at once, then "+N more". A title unlock adds `New title: ... [Equip]`.

## Rules and limits
- Progress comes only from server events and existing services. Skill levels are polled every 30 s, island stats every 5 min (one read per island). Saved in a batch every 60 s and on quit.
- Each tier unlocks once per profile. Counters only go up. Boosts go through the bonus system, so family caps still apply.
- Personal achievements never boost sell or money (RTA-39). No crystals, money or keys per tier (RTA-40).
- Island progress counts only stored members, never coop or visitors. A member who leaves takes nothing. Team Flame counts alts on one IP once.
- Season reset wipes progress and boosts, keeps titles, and saves a History summary (RTA-44).
- `boosts.enabled: false` turns all boosts to 0 and keeps the points visible.
- Bedrock: click-only menu, lore lines 36 characters or less.
