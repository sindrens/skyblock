# Progression track · id: F1 · status: Draft · version: 1

## In one sentence
One track from the first minute to the endgame, like a battle pass. You climb it by playing, and it unlocks perks on the way. It replaces "perks by hours played".

## How it works
- **Direction decided, plan not written, nothing built.** Owner (REVIEW3 F7): "NOT playtime, but if you imagine the whole grind from start game to end game as a battle pass, thats the princip, it has to be planned better". Do not build until this card is locked.
- **Decided shape (REVIEW3 F7):**
  - Perks unlock along one track from start game to end game. Not by hours played.
  - A bought rank unlocks its Cosmetic and Convenience perks at once (F2). The track is how everyone else earns the same perks.
  - Gameplay perks (sell %, skill XP %) are never sold (F2). Whether the track gives any is open.
- **Built today as separate ladders** (the track would join these, not replace them):
  - Tutorial: 11 steps. Features unlock step by step: team after step 4, the Depths after step 5, /ah after step 8, /pay and /withdraw after step 11, /trade, warzone and new profiles at the finish (TUT-4).
  - Skills up to level 150. Depths tiers open by skill level (Mining has 12 tiers, the last opens at level 66).
  - Island level: 8 sources. Pillar milestones at 10/25/40/60 pay island crystals once. The Challenge Book has 180 island tasks.
  - Achievements (F3): every tier gives boost points for small capped boosts.
- **Planned in other cards:** battle pass with 100 levels (F9), collection log (F10), island upgrades and masteries (B14), island milestone rewards at level 10/25/50/100/200 (no reward list written).
- **Perks that could sit on the track** (REVIEW3 §4.5, proposed default): `/hat`, `/craft`, `/ec`, plain `/nick`, island fly, extra AH slots, profile slots, loadouts, `/pv` vaults, borders, trails, join message, nick colors. The earlier idea of unlocking them by hours (2 h, 10 h, 25 h) is dropped.
- **Some perks already have an earn path:** island fly from the Wings mastery, up to +2 AH slots from Economy achievements (F3).
- **Proposed shape (proposed default):**
  - A view over progress that already exists, not a new XP bar. Steps: tutorial finish, Depths tiers, skill levels, island level milestones, achievements, collection milestones, pass levels.
  - Progress belongs to the profile. A perk, once unlocked, stays with the account like titles (PR1).
  - One button in `/menu`. Each perk shows its tag and how to earn it. REVIEW3 §4.5 wanted a `/perks` page: that is a new command, so ask first.

## What the player sees
- Today there is no single view. Progress is spread over `/skills`, `/is level` (island level and Challenge Book), `/achievements` and `/tutorial`.
- Planned (PHASE-5 M5.8): an island scoreboard line "Next: Island Size I · 2/3 tasks".
- Proposed: a "Track" menu with the steps in a row (done / next / locked) and the perk of each step in the lore. A toast when a step is reached.

## Rules and limits
- Nothing sold may give a gameplay advantage (principle 8). A bought rank only skips Cosmetic and Convenience steps. No step can be bought with gems or money.
- Profiles are islands (principle 2): progress belongs to the profile. Nothing moves between your own profiles.
- Pacing follows #61: fast at first (first steps in the first session), harder later, with ups and downs (a new tier or the Forge is an "up").
- No per-tick scans (principle 9). Steps are checked from events that already exist: level-ups, tier unlocks, island level changes (proposed default).
- Bedrock: one click-only menu, no typing, short lore lines.

## Connections
- Ranks and perks (F2): the perks and the bought-rank shortcut. Tags and titles (F4): cosmetic perks.
- Tutorial (A3): the start. Depths tiers (E3) and skills (E9): the middle.
- Island level and pillars (B13), upgrades and masteries (B14): the island side.
- Achievements (F3), battle pass (F9), collection log (F10), quests (F8): sources of steps.
- Seasons (I5) and the economy curve (C14): the track is paced with them.

## Decided
- REVIEW3 F7: perks unlock along one progression track from start game to end game, like a battle pass, not by hours played. A bought rank unlocks its cosmetic and convenience perks at once. Gameplay perks are never sold. It needs its own plan, made through this card.
- R3-7 (owner): "all ranks have x perks, and of default the permissions for ranks are possible to be gained by playing the game and they are not immediately given, but some are".
- #61: progression is fast at first, progressively harder in every system, with ups and downs.
- TUT-4: the tutorial unlocks features step by step.
- 168: island level is dynamic and comes from many sources.
- 44: battle pass (free + premium): yes (feature ideas).
- 47: milestone rewards at island level 10/25/50/100: yes (feature ideas). Suggested default: keys and cosmetics, plus level 200.

## Open questions
1. Which step unlocks which perk? This is the plan the owner asked for. Default: start from the perk list above and place each perk on a step.
2. Per profile or per account? Default: (proposed default) progress per profile, perk unlock stays with the account.
3. Does it restart every season? Default: (proposed default) progress restarts with the profile, unlocked perks stay.
4. Is the track the free track of the battle pass (F9), or separate? Default: (proposed default) separate. The track is permanent unlocks, the pass is seasonal rewards and stays the only paid lane (cosmetic).
5. May the track give any Gameplay perk? Default: no. Sell % and skill XP % keep coming from upgrades, Market Contacts, sets and achievements.

## Rejected ideas
- A separate rank-up ladder (DESIGN §9, 2026-10-29): island level and skills are the progression.
- Perks unlocked by hours played (REVIEW3 F7): the owner said not playtime.
