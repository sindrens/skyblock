# Voting · id: F6 · status: Draft · version: 1

## In one sentence
Vote for the server on 5 sites and get a key, gems and pass XP for each vote, plus a streak and a server-wide vote party.

## How it works
- **Built:** only `/vote`, a link command that points to `skybornmc.net/vote`. It gives no reward.
- **Planned (M10.5, new module `rewards`, not started):**
  - Our own Votifier receiver (protocol v2 with a token per site, and v1 with RSA). Port and keys in `votes.yml`.
  - 5 sites (proposed default, the owner registers the server): PlanetMinecraft, Minecraft-MP, MinecraftServers.org, TopG, Minecraft-Server-List.
  - **Per vote (P10-13):** 1 bound Ember key + 3 gems + 25 pass XP. One reward per site per account per 24 h.
  - **Paid to the active profile** (129). If you are offline, the vote is queued and paid to the profile you are on at your next join. Unknown names wait 7 days.
  - **Bedrock names:** Floodgate adds a prefix to the name. Vote sites often drop it, so the name is looked up with and without it.
  - **Streak** (days with at least one vote): day 7 Cinder key, day 14 Blaze, day 21 Cinder, day 30 Inferno, then it repeats. A missed day resets it. (plan, not answered by the owner)
  - **Vote party (P10-15):** a server-wide counter. At 500 votes everyone online who voted today, finished the tutorial and is not AFK gets 1 Blaze key. The counter shows on the Vote NPC hologram in the Tavern and in `/vote`.

## What the player sees
- Today: `/vote` prints "Vote daily for rewards:" and a link. The `/menu` Vote button opens the same.
- Planned: `/vote` becomes a menu with one button per site (link in chat, URL in the lore for Bedrock), today's votes ✔/✘, your streak and the party progress. A chat line when a vote is paid.
- The Vote NPC in the Tavern opens `/vote` (today it shows help).

## Rules and limits
- Vote keys are bound: they cannot be withdrawn or traded (P10-6), and the loot they give is bound too (R3-2). Gems from votes are earned gems: bound to the account, never tradeable (REVIEW3 F3). That is about 15 a day if you vote on all 5 sites, so vote alts cannot feed a main.
- Fake votes: a token per site, one reward per site and account per 24 h. A vote for an unknown name expires after 7 days.
- Alts: rewards for at most 3 accounts per IP per day (plan default). The party only pays players who voted today, are active and finished the tutorial.
- One vote = one reward (129). Every vote is audited (`rewards/vote`).
- Bedrock: the menu shows the URL in the lore, nothing depends on chat clicks.
- Performance: votes arrive as network events, nothing is polled. The party counter lives in memory and is saved in batches (proposed default).
