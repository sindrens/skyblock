# Social commands · id: G3 · status: Draft · version: 1

## In one sentence
Small commands to see who is online and what they are doing (`/list`, `/ping`, `/playtime`, `/seen`, `/afk`), one-click links (`/discord`, `/store`, `/vote`, `/website`, `/rules`), and `/whois` for staff.

## How it works
- **`/list`** (`/online`, `/who`): "12/300 online", grouped by rank, highest first, names A to Z. Each name is clickable (starts `/msg`). AFK players get `[AFK]`. Vanished staff are hidden.
- **`/ping [player]`**: connection delay. Green under 80 ms, orange under 180 ms, red above.
- **`/playtime [player]`** (`/pt`): total play time. Works for offline players.
- **`/seen <player>`**: "online now" or "was online 3h ago", plus first join date. Helpers and up also see UUID and platform.
- **`/afk`**: toggles AFK. It also turns on after 5 minutes without activity (`social.yml: afk.auto-minutes`, 0 = never). Moving, chatting or using a command brings you back. 3 s command cooldown.
  - Everyone sees "Steve is AFK." / "Steve is back." The setting "AFK announcements" lets each player hide these lines (`afk.broadcast`).
  - `[AFK]` shows in the tab list and on the nametag. AFK status itself gives nothing. The AFK pool (D10) is a separate feature.
- **Links:** `/discord`, `/store` (`/buy`), `/vote`, `/website` (`/web`) print a clickable address (`social.yml: links.*`, owner answer 320).
- **`/rules`:** rules 1-9, the privacy line ("Chat, private messages and commands are logged for 30 days and staff can read them") and a link to the full rules and `skybornmc.net/privacy`.
  - Planned (decided): the privacy line changes. Only mods and up can read private messages (R3-9b). A `/report` attaches the last 10 DMs between the two players as evidence, and that evidence is kept 180 days (R3-9a). The privacy page says the same.
- **`/whois <player>`** (helper and up): UUID, rank, platform, first and last seen, play time, location, game mode, ping, old names. Mods and up also see addresses and other accounts on the same address.
- Staff-only: `/socialspy` (G2), `/broadcast` (G5).

## What the player sees
- `/list`: `▸ Mod (2): Pouched, Steve` per rank, with a header "12/300 online:".
- `/seen`: "Steve was online 3h ago (12 Oct). First joined 2 Sep."
- Rules 1-9: be kind; no cheats; no duping; no spam or scams; no real-money trading; staff decisions are final unless you appeal; English only in public chat; no alt accounts to dodge punishments or farm; no macros or AFK farming outside the AFK pool.
- Bedrock: all of this is plain text and clickable names. Nothing needs hover.

## Rules and limits
- Vanished staff never show in `/list`, `/ping` or `/seen` for players (they count as offline). `skyblock.staff.vanish.see` sees them.
- `/ping`, `/playtime` and `/seen` need no permission beyond the default. They show no location or island.
- `/whois` addresses are staff only and every look-up is audited. IPs are never shown to players.
- Reads come from memory or one cached player row. Only `/list` loops over online players, and only when used.
- Nicknames: `/whois` and `/seen` use the real account name. Staff use `/realname` to find who is behind a nickname (G12).

## Decided
- 318: social spy mods and up only (see G2).
- R3-9a: `/report` attaches the last 10 DMs between the reporter and the reported player as evidence. That is the only way DMs become evidence. Evidence is kept 180 days, and the privacy page says so.
- R3-9b: helpers cannot read DMs in `/logs` (`chat/dm` is hidden from them); mods and up keep it. This keeps answer 318.
- 319: rules and first join state the 30-day logging.
- 320: final web addresses discord.skybornmc.net, store.skybornmc.net, skybornmc.net/vote, skybornmc.net, skybornmc.net/rules.
- 321: rule 6 says staff decisions are final unless you appeal with `/appeal`.
- 322: rules for language, alt abuse and macros/AFK farming outside the AFK pool.
