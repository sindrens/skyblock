# Tutorial · id: A3 · status: Draft · version: 1

## In one sentence
An 11-step guided tour (about 3 minutes) that starts at the island portal, teaches the money loop, and opens risky features one step at a time; finishing it once opens everything on the account.

## How it works
- **Built:** all of TU1 to TU8 (the old list said only TU1 to TU4). Module `tutorial`, settings `tutorial.yml`, texts `messages/tutorial.yml`.
- **Starts** when a new player joins: a 6 s spawn camera, then a flame trail and boss bar compass lead the ~95 blocks to the island portal (A2).
- **Core steps** (a step done early counts and is skipped later):

| # | Step | Done when | Opens |
|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | portal | the island portal fires its event | |
| 2 | arrive | the 6 s island camera ends or is skipped | |
| 3 | chest | starter chest opened and a starter pickaxe in the inventory (A4) | |
| 4 | crop | 3 wheat planted on your island | `/is invite`, coop, visit, warps |
| 5 | menu | island menu opened from /menu or /is | The Depths (portal, /dungeon) |
| 6 | depths | standing in Mine Tier 1 (5 s camera) | |
| 7 | mine | 15 Mining XP gains in the Depths | |
| 8 | sell | any sale | Auction house `/ah` |
| 9 | forge | the Forge menu opened | |
| 10 | help | any NPC clicked | |
| 11 | bank | one personal bank deposit | `/pay`, `/withdraw` |

- **Finish:** title "TUTORIAL COMPLETE / Every feature is unlocked", **$2,500 + 25 crystals + title Firstflight**, once per account, paid to the profile you finish on, in one transaction with the finished flag (key `tutorial_reward:<uuid>`). Then `/trade`, the warzone, outposts, events and new profiles open, and a "Where next?" menu shows.
- **Guides:** boss bar compass, action bar hint, scoreboard "Next:" line, flame trail, flame column at the target, dust on farm spots and ore, holograms only you see, and **Pip**, a small red parrot only you see. Three core cameras (spawn 6 s, island 6 s, Depths 5 s), sneak skips. Bedrock gets cut-and-fade shots and name-tag holograms.
- **Rejoin mid-tutorial:** after 2 s you are teleported to your step's place (sneak to stay). Progress is saved at every step. An idle reminder comes after 5 minutes.
- **Team joiners** get a Starter pack instead of the chest (A4). Invited new players can do the tutorial on the team island.
- **Optional steps O1 to O9** (generator, spawn tour, settings, skills, team, upgrades, enchanter, wardrobe, warzone): 5 crystals each, once per account, with a first-visit tip (turn off in `/settings` Tips). O6 is switched off in config (`enabled=false`) until island upgrades are live. O7 and O8 show only when `/enchanter` and `/wardrobe` exist.
- **Staff:** `skyblock.tutorial.bypass` means no locks. `/tutorial admin status|skip|reset|step|stats`; skip gives no reward, reset never pays twice.
- **Old players:** everyone already in the players table when the module first started was marked finished (legacy), no reward.
- **Planned, no recorded owner answer:** finish also gives 1 bound Ember key plus optional steps O10 Rewards and O11 First crate (PHASE-10 M10.12); one bound tier 1 Chunk Collector at the finish (PHASE-7); the last step opens island upgrades (PHASE-5).
- **Planned (decided): earn rule before sending money (REVIEW3 F1, R3-1).** On top of the step locks, a profile can send money away (`/pay`, money notes, `/trade`, buying on the AH) only after it has earned $10k itself. Start money, the tutorial reward and money from other players do not count. Exception: sending to members of the island the sender is on, including island bank deposits, is always allowed. Today only the step locks exist, so a second profile can `/pay` at once.
- **Planned (decided): private messages (R3-32).** `/msg` goes through the link, IP and phone filter (not the swear list). Until an account finishes the tutorial, only staff and players it messaged first can DM it. Public chat stays open from the start.
- **Planned (decided): cleanup (R3-16).** Delete the dead 30-minute settings (`auction.yml`, `economy.yml` pay and notes `new-account-minutes`) and the old doc lines. The tutorial is the only gate.

## What the player sees
- `/tutorial` menu: progress "Step N/11", **Take me there**, **I'm stuck**, **Steps**, **Watch again**, **Optional steps**, **What unlocks when**, **Where next?**
- Locked feature: action bar "Unlocks at tutorial step 8 (Sell)" and a chat line with a [Tutorial] button (at most every 10 s).

## Rules and limits
- Locks are checked in code by `TutorialGate`, not by LuckPerms groups. If the module is off, every lock is open.
- "I'm stuck" may skip only arrive, depths and help after 3 presses. Sell and bank can never be skipped, they open money features.
- Locks open only when the step and every step before it are done, so a money step done early opens nothing early.
- Cameras save game mode, position and flight, and restore them on quit, death or restart.
- Performance: one 5-tick task per player who is in the tutorial, trail points parsed once, about 50 particle packets per second per player. Launch bursts are a known risk (PERFORMANCE P-21).
- Bedrock: no chat clicks needed, titles at most 20 characters, no glow outlines. Geyser's camera API is not on the classpath.

## Decided (GUI-PACK restart, 31 Oct 2026; owner GUI-Q13, ledger `A3-R1-02`; the card keeps its own status Draft, only this section is decided): the tutorial boss bar
MC Hub's bar is one vanilla boss bar: a bold title, a thin separator, one white instruction, one progress bar. Ours is already a vanilla boss bar (`Guides.java`), but it mixes three meanings of "progress" and uses red.
- **One bar, one instruction, one progress.** Text = `Tutorial 3/11` (the word `Tutorial` and the count in bold ember orange, owner: a coloured bold step word) + a dark-gray `·` separator + the one current instruction in white (plain text, no custom font). While walking to a target the instruction is the compass text with the distance (`» Starter Chest · 24m «`); for counted steps it carries the count (`Plant wheat · 2 / 3`). The distance never moves the bar.
- **The fill always means the same thing:** steps done out of the total steps (11 now; not distance, not the step count). Today `progress` is the distance while walking, the step count on counted steps and the step index otherwise, so the bar jumps backwards.
- **Colour (GUI-Q13, owner):** yellow, so red stays the colour of event and boss bars (KOTH, envoys, mini bosses use red). Boosters use yellow today and move to another colour in F11 (ripple). The owner allows a touch of styling, not a custom font: the coloured bold step word, the separator and the vanilla bar overlay style. Default overlay `PROGRESS` (smooth); `tutorial.yml guide.bossbar-overlay` (`progress`, `notched_6`, `notched_10`, `notched_12`, `notched_20`) lets staff change it.
- Bedrock-safe arrows only (`«`, `»`, `·`), no glyphs, no custom font. The bar hides when the tutorial is done, and while a camera shot plays.
- Config: `tutorial.yml guide.bossbar-color` (default `yellow`) and `guide.bossbar-overlay` (default `progress`); texts `messages/tutorial.yml bar.*` and `compass.*`. A short unit test pins "fill = done / total steps".
- Reality check: `bossbar-color` default is `red` in `TutorialConfig`; the A3 card (How it works) says "boss bar compass" without naming the progress meaning; the `GlobalBoosters` bar also uses a configured color (default yellow), so two bars can share a colour when both are shown.

**What is annoying (less is more):**
- The same instruction reaches the player in up to six places: boss bar, scoreboard `Next:`, action bar hint every 2 s, a title at each step start, Pip's name tag and Pip's chat line, plus a chat line "step done (n/11)". Proposed default: keep the boss bar (instruction and progress), the scoreboard `Next:` row and ONE title per step change; the action bar hint only after 15 s idle on the step; Pip speaks in his name tag only (`pip.chat: false`); the step-done chat line stays one short line.
- Steps 1 and 2 are 60 to 75 seconds with nothing to do and no reward; step 7 is 15 identical clicks; step 10 is empty.
- No step pays or levels the player: the reward comes only once, at the end.
- The fixed trail and the hologram arrows (A3-R0-02, A3-R0-03) are separate build work and are not part of the pace choice.

**FAST progress, proposal (owner chooses the pace, HT-Q9):** the tutorial pays a little and levels the player at almost every step, with one title and one sound per step change, so progress is felt and the Money row on the scoreboard moves.
- **Step popup (one per step, replaces the step-start title):** title `+$400` (flame gold, at most 20 characters for Bedrock), subtitle `Next: Sell your loot` (at most 36 characters), `unlock` sound. A step with a level-up shows the existing skill level-up title instead (it already has title, chat and sound) and the money goes to the action bar. Level-ups and the finish keep their own titles and sounds.
- **Reward split:** the same $2,500 total is paid over the steps instead of all at the end (HT-Q10), each step its own idempotent payment `tutorial_step:<uuid>:<step>` to the profile that completed it, a staff reset never pays twice, crystals and the title stay at the finish.
- **Free levels (HT-Q11):** `SkillService#addXp(player, MINING, n, "tutorial")` shows the vanilla-style level-up with no new code. Mining Lv 1 costs 600 XP, Lv 2 1,320 XP in total; at tier 1 one block is 1 XP, so Lv 2 saves a player about 20 minutes of mining (more at slow speed). Island Level is floor(points / 10) (B13), so one island level is 10 points; a "tutorial bonus" source (10 points at three steps) gives Island Lv 1 to 3 and needs B13 to add the source.

**Developer decisions (defaults; the owner only vetoes):**
- Rewards are paid with `EconomyService#executeBatch`-style atomic payments keyed per account and step; the per-step flag lives next to the existing `rewarded` flag so a reset never pays a step twice; the earn rule treats tutorial money like the current reward (not "earned", F1).
- The tutorial's own mining counter (`onXp`) ignores XP with source `tutorial`, otherwise the free XP would count as a mined block. Pillars and island level get the bonus through their normal sources only.
- The sell step keeps accepting every source; its hint stays the satchel (mined items are there). The shop step completes on the first successful buy click; no purchase event exists yet, so the shop module fires a new `ItemsBoughtEvent` (common, like `ItemsSoldEvent`); the step opens with the shop NPC or `/shop`.
- Step ids stay in `flow.steps` (`flow.version` goes to 2; players mid-way continue at their first step not done, finished players stay finished).
- A staff `tutorial admin step` and the stats menu keep working with the new ids.
- Tests: reward amounts sum to the total, a step pays once, `tutorial` XP is not a mined block, the shop step done event, bar fill stays steps done over total (12 now).
- Docs: `docs/commands/tutorial.md` step table and the "about 3 minutes" line.
