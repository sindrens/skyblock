# Tools and the Forge · id: E10 · status: Draft · version: 1

## In one sentence
Players get a free starter kit, then forge better tools at the Forge from Depths drops and money. Every tool is "living": it levels up from use, opens enchant slots, keeps its progress when upgraded, can prestige, and is kept on death.

## How it works
- **Built:**
  - **Starter kit** (Quartermaster NPC or `/forge > Starter kit`): free basic pickaxe, axe, hoe, sword and rod. You can claim again after 24 hours and only get the pieces you are missing. Pieces can be traded but not listed on the auction house.
  - **The Forge** (Blacksmith NPC or `/forge [type]`): tabs per tool type. Each tier shows owned, craftable or locked with progress: the skill level, the materials and the money. Forging the next tier needs the previous tier's tool in your inventory and keeps level, enchants, stats, skin and ability.
  - **Tiers follow the Depths tiers:** pickaxes 12 (Coal to Voidstone), axes 11 (Oak to Warped), swords 10 (Grave to Warlord). Hoes and rods only have the starter piece until Phase 7.
  - **Recipe** (balance sheet): $15,000 x 2.35 per tier and 256 x 1.35 per tier of the previous tier's Depths drop. Copper pickaxe: $15,000 + 256 Coal. Voidstone pickaxe: $77,049,500 + 5,147 Netherite. Materials come from the inventory, then the satchel. Needs the matching skill level (the tier unlock).
  - **Levels 1 to 50:** XP per block or log = the tier XP of the zone; a kill = 5x tier XP; blocks outside the Depths give 25%. Level 1 to 2 costs 400 XP, each level 1.21x more. Perk slots open at level 5, 15, 30 and 45.
  - **Stats:** break speed from the tier's mining efficiency plus 1% per level; drops +5% per Forge tier above 1; swords have damage per tier.
  - **Prestige** at level 50: once per tool per season. Back to level 1, keeps enchants and stats, +1 slot (slot 5, for Mythic enchants) and a star.
  - **Tool menu:** shift + right-click, or `/tool` on Bedrock: level, stats, slots, skin, history, prestige, "Open Forge".
  - **Safety:** each tool is a unique item (id + UUID, dupe checks). No durability. Kept on death. Not allowed in anvils, grindstones, smithing, crafting grids or enchanting tables. The Forge takes money first, then items in one tick, and refunds if anything vanished.
  - **Staff:** `/tool give <player> <tool> [tier]` for admin and owner, audited.
- **Planned / decided, not built:**
  - Only custom tools work in the Depths (#810). Today a vanilla pickaxe still mines but gets no fortune.
  - Custom tools cannot be dropped on the ground; they move only by trade or auction (#901).
  - **Island storage (#891, R3-23):** the Forge also takes materials from island storage. It counts as a take-out: same permission, same daily limit. The limits must fit Forge amounts (a tier 10 tool needs 2,824 items). Built together with island storage.
  - **Lower materials stay needed (REVIEW3 D2):** after iron unlocks, recipes still ask for coal and other lower drops, because every tier keeps its own drop table (E5).
  - Hoe and rod tiers and abilities with Phase 7.

## What the player sees
- `/forge`: a chest menu with tabs Pickaxes, Axes, Swords, Hoes, Rods, a Starter kit button and a Blueprints button (E11).
- Tool lore: level bar, stats, empty or filled perk slots, history (blocks mined, kills). Level-up sound and action bar.
- Forge message: "Forged <tool>! Still Lv 12 with all perks." Missing parts are listed, for example "Missing: ..." or "Mining 15 needed (you have 9)."

## Rules and limits
- Everything is per profile (money, materials, tools). Nothing moves between your own profiles.
- Anti-abuse: unique ids, an upgrade whose stored tier does not match is treated as a copy; the AH refuses starter-kit pieces (free tools from alts).
- Performance: tool XP is added in memory and saved in batches every 30 seconds; lore refreshes at most every 2 seconds (`render.interval-ms`).
- Bedrock: menus and `/tool` replace shift + right-click.
- Prestige is a time achievement, not a purchase. It is tradeable, not soulbound (#902).

## Decided
- Phase 4 (29 Oct): the Omnitool is removed. Starter kit, Forge, living tools, blueprints and skins; all tradeable; no durability.
- Phase 4 (29 Oct): starter kit reclaim every 24 hours. Perk slots at 5, 15, 30, 45 and +1 from prestige.
- 810: only custom tools work in the Depths, starter kit free.
- 834: a blueprint tool forged higher keeps its ability.
- 891: Forge materials from inventory, satchel and island storage together.
- R3-23: Forge use of island storage counts as a take-out (same permission, same daily limit).
- REVIEW3 D2: the Forge and blueprints still need lower materials after a higher tier unlocks.
- 892: vanilla items in recipes only until Depths drops exist (done).
- 901: no dropping custom tools on the ground.
- 902: prestiged tools stay tradeable.
- 904: season reset wipes player data, so tools are forged again each season.
- 776: mine and forest recipes use Depths drops.

## Open questions
1. Should a higher Forge tier give more tool XP per block? Default: no (`xp.tier-bonus-percent: 0`).
2. Starter kit tradeable via `/trade` but not on the AH. Default: as built.
3. Do spawner mobs give tool XP? Default: no (`xp.spawner-mobs: false`).
4. Island storage daily limits must cover a tier 10 tool (2,824 items). Default: the island storage plan sets limits that fit Forge amounts.
