# Seasons · id: I5 · status: Draft · version: 1

## In one sentence
The server runs in 3-month seasons: a week of pre-season, a full wipe of progress, prizes for the winners, and a clear list of what you keep forever.

## How it works
The plan is an outline (PHASE-12-SEASONS.md). Only the hooks are built. A reset is the riskiest thing the server does, so it must be one tested, logged process.
- **Built (hooks only):**
  - Data tables carry a `season_id`: skills, island level, pillars, tasks, islands and the economy ledger. `skills.yml: storage.season-id` stays 0 until the season system exists.
  - `AchievementService#seasonReset` writes each profile's season history and wipes personal progress (titles stay). It has no command.
  - `ProfileService#seasonResetSlots` removes profile slots that were not bought with gems (P8).
  - Crate pity counters have `pity.season-id`. Cosmetics have a "season persistent" flag.
  - The tab shows the season text ("Season 1 · Preseason"). The MOTD says "Season 1 coming soon".
  - `/admin` has an owner "season" setting. The VPS makes a daily local backup at 04:30 (7 days).
- **Not built:** a season service, pre-season, Final Week, the reset tool, prize payout, exports, `/season`, contributor rewards.
- **Season rhythm:** pre-season week with a faster-progress boost, then a full wipe, then about 3 months live, with a Final Week of double Conquest XP and extra KOTHs (DESIGN §1).
- **Wiped:** islands, outposts, levels, pillars, skills, tools, money, crystals, shards, items, loot, unused keys, achievement progress and boosts, island sizes above 9x9, ratings and likes, pity counters. Pets reset too (proposed default, PV32).
- **Kept:** titles, tags and cosmetics, bought ranks, gem-bought profile slots, claimed skins, tags and gem-set looks (bound to the account), pet skins, island value history (exported). Gems are open (question 2): bought gems are tradeable and earned gems are bound (REVIEW3 F3).
- **Won ranks:** Torchbearer, Flameheart and Firecrown last one season, then are removed.
- **Planned (decided): contributors.** No leader kicks in the last 14 days of a season (staff still can). Each island's biggest contributors get season rewards, fair play (REVIEW3 F11). This needs a plan with Phase 5 and Phase 12.
- **Proposed default milestones:**
  - M12.1 season service: number, dates and phase (pre-season, live, Final Week, ended) shown everywhere.
  - M12.2 Final Week: warnings for keys, `/box` and bought sizes, leaderboards locked at the end time.
  - M12.3 end of season: freeze economy and trades, final snapshot, CSV export, grant titles, won ranks and prizes.
  - M12.4 reset tool: full backup first, a dry run that only counts, owner-only, two-step confirm. M12.6 carry-over check.

## What the player sees
- Season name and phase in the tab list and scoreboard.
- Planned (proposed default): Final Week warnings in chat and on join, a "Season N veteran" title for 10 h played, and prizes paid as store credit (gems, so earned and bound).

## Rules and limits
- Wipe island and outpost worlds only. The Depths, spawn and warzone worlds stay; their player data resets (414).
- `/box` items stay until the reset (N2-3). The AH collection bin never expires during a season (563).
- Safety (proposed default): a full off-site backup before the reset, kept for the whole next season; the reset is audited.
- Reset runs from a checked wipe list and a checked keep list. No player command can start it.
- Performance: the reset runs off the main thread in batches, with the server closed to players (proposed default).

## Decided
- DESIGN §1: 3-month seasons, 1 week pre-season then a full wipe, Final Week with double Conquest XP and extra KOTHs, solo prizes 150/100/50 and team 400 as store credit.
- 414: wipe islands and outposts only; keep Depths, spawn and warzone worlds.
- 354 / RTA-44: achievement progress and boosts reset every season; titles stay.
- 387 / RTA-12: bought ranks are permanent; won ranks last one season. RTA-9: a won rank needs 10 h played and 7 days on the winning island.
- P8: gem-bought profile slots survive the reset. P10-43: unused keys are wiped, with a warning in the Final Week.
- N2-3: `/box` items never expire before the reset. 563: collection bin never expires. P5-14: sizes above 9x9 last one season.
- R3-19 / REVIEW3 F6: sizes above 9x9 are bought with shards (Black Market) or gems, bought or earned.
- R3-14 / REVIEW3 F5: gem purchases arrive unclaimed and tradeable; right-click claims them and they stay bound to the account. R3-20: gem sets are looks only. R3-44: the premium pass is cosmetic only.
- REVIEW3 F11: no leader kicks in the last 14 days of a season; rewards for the biggest contributors.
- V2: island value history is saved after the season and can be exported. P5-31: `/isadmin progression` exports the season CSV.
- RTA-32: "The First Flame" title for 1 h played in Season 1's first week.
- 411: backups every 6 h, 7 days, an off-site copy and one before every update.

## Open questions
1. Pre-season: one week with faster progress, then a full wipe before the season starts? Default: yes (the other option is to let it flow into the season).
2. Are unspent gems kept across seasons? Default: yes. DESIGN §1 leaves it open, and kept bought gems are tradeable, so they let players carry wealth across the wipe.
3. Season prizes: team places 2 and 3 have no numbers yet (solo is 150/100/50). What are they, and what do an island's biggest contributors get (REVIEW3 F11)?
4. Reset tool: dry run first, owner-only, two-step confirm, and an off-site backup kept for the whole next season? Default: yes.
5. Are gem-set looks (959) still claimed free every season, or kept once claimed? Default: kept once claimed, since they are looks only now (R3-20).
