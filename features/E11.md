# Blueprints and skins · id: E11 · status: Draft · version: 1

## In one sentence
Blueprints are rare Depths drops that turn into a special tool with one ability at the Forge. Skins are cosmetic looks for tools that you collect and keep across seasons. Both are tradeable items.

## How it works
- **Built, blueprints:**
  - **Four blueprints** (`tools.yml: blueprints`):

| Blueprint | Tool | Ability | Drops from | Chance | Forge cost |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| Sage Pickaxe (rare) | pickaxe, tier 2 | Scholar: +25% tool XP | Mine tier 1+ | 1 in 15,000 blocks | $25,000 + 256 Coal |
| Magma Pickaxe (epic) | pickaxe, tier 4 | Auto-smelt: ores drop smelted | Mine tier 4+ | 1 in 20,000 blocks | $150,000 + 320 Gold |
| Windcutter Axe (rare) | axe, tier 3 | Swift: +15% break speed | Forest tier 2+ | 1 in 15,000 logs | $50,000 + 320 Birch Logs |
| Vampire Blade (epic) | sword, tier 5 | Lifesteal: heals 5% of damage dealt (max 1 heart per hit) | Combat tier 4+ | 1 in 1,500 kills | $250,000 + 160 Bog Slime |

  - The roll happens on every accepted Depths break or kill, in the tools module. A full satchel means no roll.
  - A blueprint is a unique item (id in `blueprints_found`). At the Forge (Blueprints button) it is used up and becomes a level 1 tool of that tier and ability. A used blueprint cannot be reused (audit `blueprint_reused`).
  - Blueprints are tradeable and listable (#833). A blueprint tool forged to a higher tier keeps its ability (#834).
  - Staff: `/blueprint give <player> <blueprint>` (admin and owner), audited.
- **Built, skins:**
  - Three skins: Phoenix Flame (legendary), Frostbite (epic), Royal Gold (epic). Each fits every tool type. Cosmetic only.
  - The collection is stored per player (`skins_owned`) and kept across seasons. Pick one in the tool menu under **Skins**; the skin stays on the tool when you forge or trade it.
  - Without a resource pack a skin changes only the name and lore (Java item model comes with the pack; Bedrock shows the name).
  - Staff: `/skin give <player> <skin>`, audited.
- **Planned / decided, not built:**
  - Blueprints about 1 in 50,000 blocks instead of 1 in 15,000 to 20,000 (#832). Config is unchanged.
  - Skins from crates (tool skin rolls of 1.5% to 6% and a monthly skin at 10%), events and achievements, plus seasonal gem skins (#835). Crate rewards are "coming soon" placeholders.
  - **Paid skins (R3-14, REVIEW3 F5):** a skin bought with gems or credits arrives as an unclaimed item. Unclaimed, it can be sold or traded anywhere (AH, `/trade`). A right-click claims it into the account and it is then bound. Only skins won in play can become tradeable skin tokens (#836). `skins_owned.source` tells the two apart.
  - **Lower materials stay needed (REVIEW3 D2):** a blueprint recipe keeps its lower material after higher tiers unlock, for example 256 Coal for the Sage Pickaxe.
  - **Bound keys (R3-2):** a blueprint won from a bound crate key is bound too.
  - Hide skins until the resource pack exists (#837). Boss-only blueprints from world bosses (Phase 8); the crates list a blueprint reward as "coming soon".

## What the player sees
- A blueprint item with its rarity and drop source in the lore. A chat line when one drops: "You found <blueprint>! Craft it at the /forge."
- Forge, Blueprints button: a menu listing every blueprint in your inventory with its ability and recipe. Click one, confirm, and craft. Result: a new tool at Lv 1 with its ability.
- `/tool > Skins`: skins that fit your tool, owned ones apply with a click.

## Rules and limits
- Anti-abuse: unique ids stop copies; no blueprint from island blocks or spawner mobs. Paid skins are bound once claimed (R3-14), so gems never turn into tradeable power.
- Skins may never change stats (Mojang commercial rule, principle 8).
- Per profile: tools and blueprints belong to the profile that found them. Skins are a collection that is kept across seasons.
- Performance: one extra random roll per accepted break or kill.
- Bedrock: menus only; skins show as names until the pack.

## Decided
- 833: blueprints are tradeable and auctionable.
- 834: a blueprint tool forged higher keeps its ability.
- 835: skins from crates, events and achievements, plus seasonal gem skins.
- 836: skins can be turned into a tradeable skin token (limited to skins won in play by R3-14).
- R3-14 (Recommended, see REVIEW3 F5): skins, tags and sets bought with gems or credits stay account-bound; only play-earned ones trade.
- REVIEW3 F5: items bought with gems arrive unclaimed and tradeable anywhere; a right-click claims them and they become bound.
- R3-2: loot from bound keys is bound too.
- 837: hide tool skins until the resource pack exists.
- 832: blueprints about 1 in 50,000 blocks.
- 899: start with four blueprints: Sage Pickaxe, Magma Pickaxe, Windcutter Axe, Vampire Blade.
