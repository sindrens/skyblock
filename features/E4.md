# Satchel · id: E4 · status: Draft · version: 1

## In one sentence
The satchel is your private bag for everything The Depths drops: it fills as you mine, chop and fight, and you sell, use or take items out of it. What happens when it is full is partly built and partly decided.

## How it works
- **Built:**
  - One satchel per profile (table `depths_satchel_items`). Drops go straight into it, not the inventory. One shared capacity for all item types (#754).
  - **Capacity** = 256 + 16 per Mining level + 16 per Foraging level + bought upgrades, max 100,000 (`depths.yml: satchel.*`).
  - **5 money upgrades**, bought in the satchel menu: +256 for $25,000, +512 for $100,000, +1,024 for $400,000, +2,048 for $1.6M, +4,096 for $6.4M.
  - **Menu:** every item with amount, today's price and value; **Sell all**; per item **Sell**, **Take one stack**, **Take all that fit**; **Upgrade**. Opens from `/dungeon > Satchel` or `/menu > The Depths`. The scoreboard shows `Satchel 412 / 640`.
  - **Selling** uses the dynamic sell prices and your sell bonus (C4). A sale is one database transaction. Items taken out are normal items you can trade (#750).
  - The Forge counts satchel items as materials (inventory first).
  - **Full satchel today:** mining is refused (the block stays, no XP, no drops) and combat drops are lost. Action bar: "Satchel full 412/640 . sell in /dungeon, Satchel", at most every 1.5 seconds, with a sound.
  - Saves in batches every 5 seconds; one audit row per player per minute. A crash can lose a few seconds of drops, never copy items.
- **Planned (owner decided, not built):**
  - **Full satchel (R3-22):** drops go to your inventory, then `/box`. Breaking stops only when `/box` is at its hard cap (5,000). The warning comes at most every 3 seconds (`full-warning-ms: 3000`). Combat drops follow the same chain (#748, #769).
  - This is a fix item: today combat drops are lost (`CombatZones`) and mining is refused (`DepthsDrops`).
  - Sell all has a "Keep Forge materials" toggle (#752). One warning at 90% full (#755).
  - Staff see a satchel read-only in `/inspect`; admins can fix it (#756).
  - Deep Pockets mastery adds capacity (hook `SatchelService#capacityBonus` exists). Auto-sell as a later perk.

## What the player sees
- Action bar while gathering and the satchel menu (title "Satchel", fill percent, "Worth about $...").
- Sale line: "Sold 640 items for $12,800 (+25% bonus)."
- Take-out: "Took 64x Coal out of your satchel." or "Your inventory is full. Make room first."

## Rules and limits
- Per profile: nothing moves between your own profiles. A season reset wipes it with player data (#759).
- Anti-abuse: take-outs and sales are atomic, so a crash cannot duplicate items. Menu clicks are blocked while an action runs.
- Performance: adds are O(1) in memory, saves are batched, the open menu refreshes every 2 seconds.
- Bedrock: menu only, no commands needed.
- Overflow must never lose items (principle 3). Today combat drops are lost when full, which breaks that rule until the R3-22 fix is built.
