# Currencies · id: C1 · status: Draft · version: 1

## In one sentence
The server has four currencies: money (the market), crystals (progress), gems (bought or earned, account-wide) and shards (rare, for the Black Market). Crystals never move between players. Money, shards and bought gems can be traded. Nothing moves between your own profiles.

## How it works
- **Built:** the four currencies, one ledger, one on/off trade switch per currency (`economy.yml: tradeable.*`, today money and shards on, crystals and gems off). **Planned (decided):** two gem balances, gem trading, shard notes, the gem and shard sources, the store.
- Amounts are whole numbers (578). Every change is one atomic database step with a ledger row (who, why, balance after). Start balance per profile and season: $1,000 and nothing else (`economy.yml: start.*`).
- **Planned (decided): two gem balances (REVIEW3 F3).** Gems bought in the store are tradeable. Gems earned in play (votes, streaks, prizes, thanks) are bound to the account and can't be sent. Crystals can never be transferred: a hard rule in code (`transferable=false`), not only a config switch (R3-5b).
- **The option A/B/C question is settled** by R3-5a, R3-5b and REVIEW3 F3: crystals never tradeable, shards tradeable with notes, gems tradeable only when bought. That is none of A, B or C exactly.

- **Money** (the only currency with a market). Earned: /sell and the satchel, AFK pool, interest, crates, tutorial reward ($2,500); planned: quests, events, island production. Spent: /shop, Forge tools, bank tiers, AH fee and tax, wardrobe sets; planned: shop spawners (fixed price, REVIEW3 F2), island upgrades (from the island bank only, 169).
- **Crystals** (aqua, per profile).
  - Earned: Depths blocks, logs and kills (a chance per block, 1 in 20 at tier 1, rising to about 1 per block at tier 12), island missions and pillars, crates, tutorial (25, plus 5 per optional step).
  - Spent: enchants (step 1 costs 100, then x1.8 per step), enchant rolls, crystal sets (Deepforge 25,000), set merge fees. Planned: island shop, Forge extras (CR2).
- **Gems** (paid or earned, one balance for all profiles today).
  - Earned, built: 1 gem to the starter when someone thanks a global booster (max 100 per booster, nothing if the thanker shares the starter's IP). Planned (decided): cap of 20 per booster, one chat line, a Bedrock menu button (R3-43). Staff `/eco give`.
  - Earned, planned: 3 per vote, 1 for "gg" after a store purchase (max 5 a day), 10 every 7 daily-streak days. These are bound.
  - Bought, planned: store gems are tradeable. A chargeback removes the rank, flags the account and freezes its gem transfers (REVIEW3 F3, RK15).
  - Spent, built: profile slot 2 (500) and slot 3 (1,500), gem sets in /wardrobe (400 and 900). Planned: island size above 9x9 for 250 / 500 / 750, bought or earned gems (REVIEW3 F6), cosmetics, skins, nickname colours, extra vaults, battle pass.
  - **Unclaimed items (REVIEW3 F5, planned):** an item bought with gems arrives unclaimed and can be sold or traded anywhere (AH, /trade, drop). A right-click claims it: it is added to the account and becomes bound. Store tags already work this way. Skins, tags, sets and cosmetics will use it.
  - The server never gives money, upgrade levels, task progress, crystals, set XP or crate keys for gems. Gameplay perks are never sold (REVIEW3 F7).
- **Shards** (rare, earned).
  - Built: only the hidden Conqueror crate (15% for 5) and staff.
  - Planned: world boss top 3 (20/15/10), KOTH capture 10, envoy crate 1-3, Depths tier 9+ rare drop. About 5-10 a day late in a season.
  - Spent: the Black Market (C13). Planned (decided): island size above 9x9, as the expensive option (REVIEW3 F6).
- **Anti-boost.** "Boosting" = alt feeding, paid value turning into game value, and win trading. Built: own-profile transfers are refused everywhere (P4), `/pay` and `/ah` unlock with the tutorial, frozen or trade-blocked players are refused, alerts for big and repeated transfers. Planned (decided):
  - A profile can send money away (/pay, notes, /trade, AH buying) only after it earned $10k itself. Start money, rewards and money from other players don't count. Sending to members of the island the sender is on, including island bank deposits, is always allowed (REVIEW3 F1).
  - A system-found dupe of a unique item gives the holders a 24 h trade block (R3-28). Loot from bound keys is bound (R3-2). Admins, builders and the owner play on a separate account (R3-39).
  - Proposal only: no transfers between accounts that shared an IP in the last 30 days (staff whitelist for families), wager limits per pair.

## What the player sees
- `/bal`: all four balances. Short numbers in menus (1.2M), exact on hover and in chat. Money $ green, crystals ◆ aqua, gems ✦ emerald, shards ✧ red. The scoreboard shows money and crystals (and gems on the island board). `/transactions` lists every change with a reason. Today `/pay 100 gems` says "Gems can't be paid to players."

## Rules and limits
- **Mojang risk (REVIEW3 F3, to log):** bought gems that can be sold for in-game money are a path from real money to in-game money, and paid island size is another. Mojang's rule: nothing sold may give a competitive gameplay advantage (principle 8). Read the current commercial terms before the store opens (M11.1). This is a hard blocker.
- No limits or gates on sending gems (REVIEW3 F4). Every gem transfer is logged, and the economy-watch gem thresholds are lowered so big moves are seen (C10). The old audit idea to hard-code gems as never transferable (F-16) now holds only for crystals.
- Bedrock: balances, prices and confirm screens are menus. Performance: balances are cached in memory; a background job sums the ledger once a minute.

- REVIEW3 F3: bought gems tradeable, earned gems bound, two balances (replaces the proposed non-tradeable "credits"). REVIEW3 F4: no sending limits, log every transfer. REVIEW3 F5: unclaimed gem items trade anywhere until claimed. REVIEW3 F6: island size with shards or gems.
- REVIEW3 F1 (R3-1): the earned $10k rule with the island-member exception. R3-28: 24 h trade block on dupes. R3-14: paid items end up bound (by claiming, F5).
- P2 / PR2: money, crystals and shards per profile; gems per account. P4: nothing moves between own profiles. 121 / 121b: every profile gets start money.
- 660: "people can't trade currencies but we need it so people won't boost, we need a solution". 666: notes for all types, tradeable by drop, /trade and duel wagers (still open for gems, question 2).

## Open questions
1. How do the two gem balances show and which is spent first? Default (proposed): `/bal` shows "Gems: 600 (200 bought)", and earned gems are spent first so bought gems stay tradeable.
2. Can gems go into money notes, drops and duel wagers (666)? Default: no, only /pay and /trade for gems (money and shards also notes and drops).
3. If Mojang's current terms forbid selling gems for in-game money, what is the fallback? Default: one config flag makes bought gems bound too, so the store can still open.
4. Gems at season end: kept or wiped? Default: unspent gems kept (P8 keeps gem-bought slots), everything else wiped.
5. Shard supply: how many per day, from which source? Default: the list above (about 5-10 a day late in a season).

## Rejected ideas
- Tokens as a separate currency (O8). Gems that buy upgrade levels (REVIEW A1) or crate keys (keys are earned only, DESIGN §9). A server exchange from gems to money (Mojang rule, pay-to-win).
- Option B: crystals tradeable with 10% burned and a weekly receive limit (R3-5b: the owner chose crystals untradeable).
- Gem sending limits and gates (REVIEW3 F4). A non-tradeable store "credits" balance (REVIEW3 F3: the owner chose the opposite).
