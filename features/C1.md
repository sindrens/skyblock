# Currencies · id: C1 · status: Locked · version: 5

## In one sentence
Four currencies, each with its own work and purpose: money (everyday buying and selling), crystals (Depths power, personal), shards (the Black Market, prizes for danger) and credits (the credit shop, bought with real money or earned by loyalty). Money, shards and bought credits can be traded; crystals and earned credits never move. Nothing moves between your own profiles. Nothing is called "gems" any more.

Notation: `$` always means game money. Real prices are written in USD ("5 USD"). Tags: **[built]** exists in the code today, **[new]** must be built.

## How it works
Ledger ids: `money`, `crystals`, `shards`, `credits` (bought), `credits_earned`. Every change is one atomic database step with a ledger row (who, why, balance after, reference up to 64 characters). Amounts are whole numbers. **[built]** Start balance per profile and season: $1,000 and nothing else.

| | Money | Crystals | Shards | Credits (bought + earned) |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| One line (same words in `/help currencies`, `/bal` hover, tutorial) | Everyday buying and selling | Depths power | Black Market | Credit shop |
| Earned from | Selling, production, quests, interest | Depths mine, forest, combat; island tasks and pillars | Bosses, KOTH, envoys, rare Depths drops | Store (bought); votes on all sites, thanks, daily streak, battle pass (earned) |
| Spent on | /shop, Forge tools, bank tiers, AH, island upgrade levels (island bank only) | Enchants, rolls, crystal sets, set merges, Forge extras | Black Market, island size above 9x9 | Ranks, cosmetics, island perks, boosters, convenience, pass |
| Tradeable | yes | never (hard rule in code) **[built]** | yes **[built]** | bought yes, earned never (hard rule in code) **[new]** |
| Belongs to | profile; the island bank is shared | profile | profile | **player** (all profiles, never lost) |
| Public lists | yes | no | yes | yes, total only |
| Season end | wiped | wiped | wiped | kept |

### Credits **[new]**
- **Two ledger currencies.** `credits` (bought, transferable) is created only by the reasons `store_grant` and owner-only `admin_buy`. `credits_earned` (not transferable) is created by `vote`, `thanks`, `streak`, `pass`, `event`, `admin_give`. A spend takes earned first, then bought, in one transaction with two ledger rows. A chargeback takes bought first.
- **Who sees what.** The player who owns the balance sees the split ("600: 200 bought, 400 earned") in `/credit bal` and in the shop. Everyone else sees only the total ("Credits: 600"). Baltop counts the total with a 30 s async cached sum. Credits are always shown exact with separators (12,500, never 12.5k).
- **Migration (deployed together with the jar, never the jar alone).**
  - One idempotent migration per module in the style of `V003__tokens_to_crystals.sql`: economy (`eco_accounts`, `eco_ledger`, `money_notes`, `eco_watch_*`), auction, moderation, shop, blackmarket, wardrobe, profiles. The reader lists the exact columns again before the build. Every old `gems` row becomes `credits_earned` (nothing was ever sold). A dry run prints row counts per module first and runs on a copy of the live data before the real run.
  - The key `gems` stays an alias for 90 days (config date `legacy-gems-alias-until`): deposits go to `credits_earned`, store grants through it are refused. `/gems` is a hidden alias for 4 weeks. Config keys and Tebex package commands are renamed in the same release.
  - Colour `#FF8C1A`, pack glyph U+E204, symbol and art in I7. The four "store credits" comments and the old "Save streak: 100 store credits" text are fixed.

### Rules for `/credit pay` **[new]**
Bought credits only. The refusal explains the fix ("You have 40 bought credits; your other 360 are earned and can't be sent"). Every pay opens a confirm screen (existing `ConfirmMenu`, Bedrock form). Self-pay refused. Sender frozen or trade-blocked: refused. A frozen receiver follows the existing `freezeReceive` set. No cooldown (`/pay` for money keeps its 3 s anti-spam cooldown). No send gate (F4). The receiver gets a [Receipt] click that shows only that receipt.

### Store **[new]**
Tebex in USD, base rate 100 credits = 1 USD, bigger packs give more: 500 credits = 5 USD, 1,000 = 10 USD, 2,700 = 25 USD, 14,000 = 130 USD. Rank price in credits = target rank price minus owned permanent rank price (EUR or credit bought; season-won ranks do not count), floored at 0, and the menu spells out the sum. Top rank 13,500 credits (about 125 USD); the six lower prices are set in F2. The EUR price list is retired. Rank grants are idempotent by order id; the rank row keeps the order id so a chargeback finds it. The store shows the price in credits and in USD text.

### What credits buy **[new]**
Ranks, cosmetics (tags, titles, trails, chat colours, looks, pet skins), island perks (below), personal boosters inside their caps, convenience (profile, loadout, wardrobe and vault slots, extra homes), battle pass.
- **Never directly granted by credits** (one code check on the shop entry type, not config): money, crystals, shards, crate keys, skill, tool or enchant levels or XP, Depths tier unlocks, quest or event progress. Indirect paths are allowed: ranks, perks, boosters and the battle pass (buying the pass is indirect; its rewards are paid by F9 under F9's rules; the pass may pay back at most 50% of its credit price in earned credits).
- Island upgrade LEVELS are paid from the island bank in money only (principle 10).
- **Warzone, duels, boss arenas:** credit-bought sets and rank perks are looks only there. Tool skins may give abilities in the Depths only (E11 decides which). Mojang warning on tool skins with abilities.
- **Physical items:** only those on the config list `credits.shop.physical-items` (default: sell chest, chunk collector). Everything else is an entitlement with no item. Items paid with any earned credits arrive bound to the account; items paid fully with bought credits arrive unclaimed and tradeable. Ranks, rank vouchers and perks can also be won from crates (F5).

### Island perks (module islands, needs `/is upgrade` M5.4) **[new]**
Entitlements, never items. They apply to the island of the profile the buyer is on; the shop names the island and profile before the confirm; a player with no island is told so and pointed to the portal. Table `island_perks` (island, line or size or cap, `ends_at` or `season_id`, bought by, order id). All members benefit; the island menu has a Perks tab with timer and buyer. Leave and kick never remove a perk; no automatic refund on disband or reset (a refund is a manual owner action); a chargeback revokes the perks of the disputed order.
- **Timed +1 level (1, 3, 7 days or until season end).** Gives the effect of the next level of a line, ignoring the task gate and the bank price: level 7 acts as 8. Never above the line's max level (a maxed line shows "maxed"). A longer end date wins, perks do not stack. **First release: Production lines only** (spawner rate, mob loot, crop growth, harvest yield, generator, fuel tank). Not included: sell multiplier, XP lines, Depths lines, size, member slots, masteries. On expiry, limit effects keep existing things and block new ones above the limit.
- **Price** = base x factor, factors 1 / 2.5 / 5 for 1 / 3 / 7 days; season perk = 25 x base x (days left / season days), never below the 7-day price. Base = price of the next level of that line. Config `credits.perks.duration-factors`; final numbers in C15.
- **Season perks (end at the season wipe):** island size 11x11 / 13x13 / 15x15, once per step per island, bought with shards (Black Market) or credits, whoever pays first (the other is then not offered); spawner cap "+N maximum stack size of one spawner", hard maximum +64 per island and season (`credits.perks.spawner-cap-max`).
- More island perks are proposed in `feature-overview/rounds/C1/improvements.md` section 2 (cosmetic first). Mojang warning on every perk that sells progress.
- **Budget anchors (numbers set in C15):** a player voting on all sites daily affords about one cosmetic per 2 weeks and a 7-day island perk about weekly, never a season perk or a rank on votes alone; the pass and streak add on top. The watch job reports credits earned per source per week.

### Purchase safety **[new]**
- `CreditShop.buy()` is the only caller of credit spend reasons (a source-scan unit test enforces it). One DB commit via `executeGuarded`/`executeAll` writes the debit rows, a `credit_orders` row (id, player, item, price, kinds spent, state `created|debited|delivered|void`) and the entitlement. The player file is saved before the order is marked `delivered`. After a crash, redelivery mints a new item UUID and voids the old one; void items are removed silently with no punishment. Rank grants go through LuckPerms with a retry keyed by the order id.
- Table `entitlements` (id, player, type, item_uuid, state `unclaimed|claimed|void`, order id). A claim removes the item first, saves the player, then flips the state in the same commit that writes the account entitlement; a database failure returns the item; 0 rows means a copy: the husk is deleted and staff are alerted, with no automatic trade block. Unclaimed items may go in `/box`, ender chests and island storage, may be traded and listed on the AH, and cannot be dropped on the ground (principle 3). Lore: "Bought for N credits". Mojang note: AH resale is a credits-to-money path the owner accepts.
- **Sources are closed in code.** Allowed ledger reasons for `credits`, `credits_earned` and `shards` are an enum (reasons within 32 characters); shard reasons: `boss_reward`, `koth`, `envoy`, `depths_rare`, `crate_reward`, `admin_*`. An unlisted reason goes to a staff retry queue with a critical alert and is never dropped.

### Trail, alerts, chargeback **[new]**
- **Trail.** `/credit trail <player>`: ledger rows newest first, filters All, Bought, Earned, Paid, Received, Spent, each row with its kind. Reference formats `order:<id>`, `pay:<id>`, `shop:<item>`. Clicking a Paid row opens the receiver's trail at that receipt with his still-held bought balance on top; a 3-step tree view and an export serve chargebacks; later activity is labelled "not proven to be these coins". Credit rows are never pruned or moved at season rollover.
- **Alerts to mod+ (critical also Discord).** Credits 1,000 single / 10,000 per day / 1,000 repeated; shards 100 / 300 / 100; patterns keyed to the account: a receiver gets credits from 3+ senders in 24 h; an account with under 2 h played receives 1,000+; 90% pass-through within 10 minutes. Crystals leave the transfer alerts and get a creation-rate alert per player per hour. All thresholds are config; the watch config layout is `rules.<rule>.*`. **[built]** for money, **[new]** for the rest.
- **Chargeback.** `/eco chargeback <player>`: staff pick the credit order in a menu. It takes bought credits first (balance never below 0), removes the rank, perks and unclaimed items linked to that order (a claimed, bound item is listed for staff, not removed automatically), flags the account (RK15), bans on the second chargeback, and shows the trail with every receiver. Nothing is taken from receivers.
- **Hold and freeze.** A profile freeze blocks sending money and shards from that profile. A credit freeze or `/credit hold` is per player and blocks `/credit pay` and spending from every profile. Spending money and shards in shops stays allowed under a freeze (spending is not sending).
- **`/eco revert`.** Preview, confirm, new compensating ledger rows with a reason; never below 0; never reverses a transfer without staff review of the receiver's balance; any row that would create `credits` is owner-only; the preview lists items bought so staff can revoke them.

### Anti-boost for money and shards
- **[built]** Own-profile transfers refused everywhere (principle 2). The $10k earned gate with the island-member exception (`send-gate.*`); 24 h trade block after a system-found dupe (R3-28); frozen or trade-blocked players refused; shard notes; crystals never transferable.
- **[new]** The earned list is narrowed to `sell`, `depths_drop`, `island_task`, `pillar_milestone`, `quest_reward` (config only). AH buying uses the same gate (the seller being an island member exempts it), and `/trade` re-checks it at the swap. The message says how much more to earn.
- **[new]** Watch flags for staff (no auto-punishment): a profile under 2 h played (`watch.alt-funnel.max-playtime-minutes: 120`) that sends 80% or more (`share: 0.8`) of all money it ever received, minimum $2,000 (`min-amount: 2000`); a profile whose sell income is mostly traded-in. Laundering by item trade is accepted and watched, not blocked.
- **[new]** Frozen profiles cannot send money, shards or credits.

### Safety nets **[new]**
- **Profile delete:** copies money, crystals and shards into `eco_deleted_accounts`, zeroes the live rows, drops them from baltop and the supply snapshot; staff restore balances together with the profile (B17); only the owner purges backups; a late payout to a deleted profile is logged and discarded, never redirected. Credits are never deleted.
- **Balance proof:** the balance-mismatch rule is extended per currency: balances equal ledger deltas up to a watermark id. Every non-ledger balance change (profile delete backup, season rollover, the migration) writes a ledger row with a reserved reason. A mismatch is a critical alert naming the first differing account.
- **Season rollover (built with I5):** real season id in the ledger (today a hard-coded 0). Money, crystals and shards of player purses, island banks, personal banks, money notes, AH listings and `/box` follow the I5 wipe list: archived into the old-season files, then zeroed, with a dry run first. Credits are kept.
- **Config moves:** `economy.yml` gets `display.exact-below` (100,000), `baltop.check-ticks` (100), the note purge timings, the retry count and delays; the English tab hints `NOTE_REASONS` and `STAFF_REASONS` move to `messages/economy.yml`. `Currency.transferable()` for crystals stays hard-coded on purpose.

### Tests (run by `./gradlew build` on H2, never a server)
50 parallel spends never go below 0; a store grant replayed 100 times writes once; earned-first split and chargeback order; `credits_earned` is never transferable; the gems migration run twice gives the same result and the alias deposits to `credits_earned`; a crash between debit and delivery redelivers once; a double claim succeeds once; an unlisted source reason is queued; the balance proof detects a broken row; freeze with the earned/bought split.

## What the player sees
- `/bal`: all currencies, one line each; hover = exact number and the one-line purpose; compact numbers for money and shards above 100,000, credits always exact. A short gain popup in the action bar (once per second, in the currency colour, on by default, no sound, switch in `/settings`) and "This session +N" on hover from an in-memory counter. The scoreboard shows what is relevant where the player is (G7). Symbols and colours are redone simple and readable in the resource pack (I7).
- `/credit shop`: 6 categories (Ranks, Cosmetics, Island perks, Boosters, Convenience, Pass), balance split on top, confirm screen on every credit spend. Built after the GUI redo (I7). Catalogue and prices from I10 and the shop overhaul C15.
- `/transactions` lists every change with a reason. Messages are short, plain and say the fix.

## Rules and limits
- **Mojang warnings (owner accepts the risk; reminders stay in every such item):** the guidelines allow selling gameplay entitlements only if they don't "ruin other players' experience or give a competitive advantage in the game"; a sold virtual currency must have "no real-world value" and must not be transferable or convertible. Bought credits are tradeable; island size, island perks, boosters and tool skins with abilities sell progress; an unclaimed credit item can be resold for money. Read the full terms before the store opens (I6). A buyer must get an online payment and purchase history (Tebex receipts plus `store_purchases`).
- Owner principle (C1-R1-21, pending confirmation in `docs/OWNER_PRINCIPLES.md`): the server allows everything; what is not allowed must not even be possible. Every transfer, drop, trade, AH, crate and store path is closed by code.
- Performance: balances cached in memory; the watch job adds ledger deltas once a minute; no blocking database calls on the main thread; trail and shop queries are async.
- Bedrock: balances, prices, confirm screens and the shop are menus or forms.
- Only blocks and items that exist in 1.21.5 appear in shops, crates and menu icons (ViaBackwards shows newer ones as other blocks); see `feature-overview/CROSS-CUTTING.md` X4.
