# Join flow and spawn · id: A1 · status: Draft · version: 1

## In one sentence
Every player joins at the spawn (a protected void world), new players get a welcome and then start the tutorial, and anyone who falls into the void or dies is put back safely.

## How it works
- **Join:** everyone is teleported to the spawn point of world `spawn` (0.5, 100, 0.5, facing north, the dais of the spawn build) `(essentials.yml: join.teleport-to-spawn)`. Players never join at their island.
- **First join** (new player row): 1 s later a title "PHOENIX SKYBLOCK / Welcome, <name>" and a sound, then a chat welcome (links: /menu, /help, /discord, plus one line that chat, private messages and commands are logged for 30 days, with the privacy link). A server-wide line "Welcome <name> to Phoenix Skyblock! (#1,234)" shows the unique join number `(join.first-join-broadcast)`. The tutorial starts (A3).
- **Returning player:** a short "Welcome back, <name>" subtitle after 0.5 s `(join.welcome-back-title)`.
- **Join and quit lines** ("[+] name", "[-] name") only for players with `skyblock.join.message` (ranks and staff). They are shown only to players who keep "Join & leave messages" on in /settings. Vanished staff are silent. The first-join line follows the same toggle.
- **Respawn:** always at spawn, never at a bed, anchor or island `(respawn.at-spawn)`. Inventory is kept (world rule), so dying costs nothing.
- **Spawn protection** (world `spawn`, `protection.*`): no breaking, placing or buckets. Only doors, buttons, levers, pressure plates, fence gates, ender chest, crafting table and signs can be used. No damage, hunger, item drops (the drop is refused and the item stays), mob spawns or explosions. Frames and armor stands are protected. A second layer in `worlds.yml` (protection.world-flags + the `protected` region) covers build, interact, entity-edit, fire and explosions.
- **Builders and admins** need `/build` switched on to edit spawn (permission `skyblock.build.spawn`). `/setspawn` (admin) moves the spawn and saves it to `worlds.yml`.
- **Void rescue:**
  - In `spawn`: falling below Y 0 sends you back to spawn, fall damage reset, action bar "Caught you! Back to spawn." `(protection.void-y)`.
  - In the `islands` world: falling below Y -16 cancels the damage and puts you on the island you are over, else your own island home, else spawn `(islands.yml: void.teleport-below-y, void.fallback-world)`.
- **Planned (decided):** a quick "Hide other players" toggle at spawn (answer 51, default all). No code found yet. REVIEW3 D4 says visibility is one shared service with three modes: Everyone (default), Friends + island, Nobody, plus pinning a player. The spawn toggle should use that service so it cannot unvanish staff.

## What the player sees
- Title "PHOENIX SKYBLOCK" with the fire gradient, then the chat welcome with clickable /menu, /help, /discord (Java). The Nether Star in hotbar slot 9 opens /menu, so Bedrock never needs the chat links.
- Blocked actions at spawn: nothing happens (builders see "Protected area, use /build to edit").
- Void: "Caught you! Back on solid ground." on islands.
- `/spawn` is instant with a 5 s cooldown `(teleport.yml: spawn.*)`. While combat-tagged it waits 3 s.

## Rules and limits
- Join lines and the first-join line go only to players who want them, so a 300-player join storm does not flood chat.
- Spawn checks are cheap events (no per-tick scans). The void check runs on movement only.
- Bedrock: nothing here needs a chat click. Welcome links are a Java extra.
- Whitelist is on during testing (`server.properties`). Non-whitelisted accounts see a "server in testing" screen.
- Join storm after a restart (300 logins): `max-joins-per-tick` is lowered. A soft join queue is only a proposal (see open questions).
