# Join flow and spawn · id: A1 · status: Review · version: 2

## In one sentence
Every player joins at the spawn (a protected void world); a new player gets ONE welcome (title, chat, sound) and is handed to the tutorial; anyone who dies or falls is put back safely, instantly and without losing anything.

## How it works
- **Join:** everyone is teleported to the spawn point of world `spawn` (0.5, 100, 0.5, yaw 180 = facing north, the dais of the spawn build; `worlds.yml: worlds.spawn.spawn`). Players never join at their island (273). Mid-tutorial players are moved to their step's place 2 s later by the tutorial (A3, sneak to stay). `essentials.yml: join.teleport-to-spawn` (first joins are always sent to spawn, whatever this says).
- **First join** (new `players` row), as one timeline:

| Time | What happens | Owner module |
|---|---|---|
| 0 s | Teleport to spawn | essentials |
| 1 s | Sound `unlock`; **one** welcome: the tutorial's title "PHOENIX SKYBLOCK / Welcome, <name>" and chat block "Your island in the sky is waiting. Next: follow the flames to the Island portal. Stuck? /tutorial" | tutorial (A3) |
| 1 s, only if the tutorial is off or the player has `skyblock.tutorial.bypass` | The same title plus the fallback chat block with /menu, /help, /discord | essentials |
| 0 to 6 s | Spawn camera (A3) | tutorial |
| 45 s | One muted line with the log and privacy notice (see Developer decisions for the wording) and the Privacy link | essentials (`join.privacy-delay-seconds: 45`) |
| async | Server line "Welcome <name> to Phoenix Skyblock! (#1,234)" to every player who keeps "Join & leave messages" on, and to the new player; the number is the count of rows in `players` | essentials (`join.first-join-broadcast`) |

- **Launch-wave guard (new):** when 5 or more first joins happen within 10 s, the per-player line is replaced by one line "Welcome 12 new players to Phoenix Skyblock!" (`join.broadcast-burst-count: 5`, `join.broadcast-burst-seconds: 10`). Chat is never flooded on launch day.
- **Returning player:** a short "Welcome back, <name>" subtitle 0.5 s after joining, shown by the rule chosen in A1-Q1 (`join.welcome-back-min-away-hours`).
- **Join and quit lines** ("[+] name", "[-] name"): only for players with `skyblock.join.message` (ranks and staff), only shown to players who keep "Join & leave messages" on in /settings, vanished staff are silent, the first-join line follows the same toggle. As built.
- **Respawn:** always at spawn, never at a bed, anchor or island (160; `respawn.at-spawn`). Respawn is immediate (`immediate_respawn`), inventory is kept, no death message, no fall damage anywhere (B17-R1-01). Dying costs nothing.
- **Spawn protection (world `spawn`):** no breaking, placing or buckets; only doors, buttons, levers, pressure plates, fence gates, ender chest, crafting table and signs can be used; no damage, hunger, item drops (the drop is refused, the item stays), mob spawns or explosions; frames and armor stands protected. Builders and admins edit with `/build` on (`skyblock.build.spawn`); `/setspawn` (admin) moves the spawn and saves it to `worlds.yml`.
- **Void rescue:** in `spawn`, falling below Y 0 sends you back to spawn with fall damage reset and the action bar "Caught you! Back to spawn." (`protection.void-y`). In the `islands` world, falling below Y -16 cancels the damage and puts you on the island you are over, else your own island home, else spawn (`islands.yml: void.teleport-below-y`, `void.fallback-world`).
- **`/spawn`:** instant, no cooldown (Developer decisions). While combat-tagged it waits 3 s (G11).
- **Hide other players (decided 51, to build):** a two-state quick toggle "Players: Everyone / Nobody" built on the shared visibility service (REVIEW3 D4), so it can never unvanish staff and later grows into the third mode "Friends + island" and "pin a player". Where it lives: A1-Q3.
- **Join storm:** Paper `misc.max-joins-per-tick: 2` is already set (`server/config/paper-global.yml:86`); no custom queue.

## What the player sees
- New player: the fire-gradient title "PHOENIX SKYBLOCK", the chat welcome, the flame trail and boss bar leading to the portal. Nothing to click: Bedrock sees exactly the same.
- Returning player: a small subtitle (A1-Q1), the Nether Star in the hotbar (opens /menu; menus module).
- Blocked action at spawn: nothing happens; builders see "Protected area, use /build to edit".
- Void: "Caught you! Back to spawn." at spawn, "Caught you! Back on solid ground." on islands.

## Rules and limits
- Join and first-join lines go only to players who want them; the launch-wave guard caps the rest.
- Spawn checks are cheap events (no per-tick scans). The void check runs on movement only.
- No blocking database call on the main thread during join; the join-number count is already async.
- Bedrock: nothing here needs a chat click; welcome links are a Java extra and exist only in the fallback block.
- Whitelist is on during testing (`server.properties`, `enforce-whitelist=true`); non-whitelisted accounts see the "server in testing" screen (I8).

## Developer decisions (security, data, tests; owner only vetoes)
- **Privacy line wording (R3-9):** "Chat, private messages and commands are logged for 30 days; reported messages are kept 180 days. Privacy" (+ the link). Shorter, true, and does not promise who reads what. Text in `messages/essentials.yml: join.privacy`, so a legal change is a text edit.
- **Spawn protection, one rule per place:** block and entity protection lives only in `worlds.yml` (flags and the `protected` region, including the "allowed to use" blocks). `essentials.yml protection.*` keeps only the safe-zone rules (no damage, hunger, item drops, mob spawns) and `void-y`. The coder lists the overlapping keys, removes the duplicates, and behaviour stays identical (test: every action in the table above is still refused or allowed).
- **No fall damage anywhere (B17-R1-01):** add `fall_damage: false` to `worlds.yml defaults.gamerules`; the island void rescue keeps cancelling void damage.
- **`/spawn` cooldown 5 s -> 0** (`teleport.yml spawn.cooldown-seconds`): it is an instant teleport, principle 1 says no cooldown unless it stops abuse, and the combat 3 s wait is the real guard. G11 owns the file.
- **Visibility service:** per-player mode kept in memory and saved as an account setting (`settings.visibility`, default `everyone`); staff in vanish are never revealed (vanish is applied after the player's choice); mode applied again on world change and on join; Bedrock players use it like anyone.
- **Join-wave guard:** counted in memory (a ring of the last 10 s); a restart cannot reset players' data; the guard only changes chat lines.
- **Join number:** unchanged source (`players` row count); not shown to players who turned join messages off.
- **No new commands, no new permissions** except the settings key; `skyblock.join.message` stays in `modules/essentials/.../permissions/essentials.yml`.
- **Config keys (all in `essentials.yml`, typed settings):** `join.welcome-back-min-away-hours`, `join.broadcast-burst-count`, `join.broadcast-burst-seconds`, `join.privacy-delay-seconds` (exists), `join.first-join-broadcast` (exists), `join.welcome-back-title` (exists), `respawn.at-spawn` (exists), `protection.void-y` (exists).
- **Tests (H2, no server):** burst guard (4 joins = 4 lines, 5 in 10 s = 1 line); welcome-back rule at 5 h 59 min and 6 h; the visibility toggle never reveals a vanished player; the config merge keeps every protection rule; privacy line contains no promise about who reads it.

## Decided
- 273: players joining the server land at spawn, not at their island.
- 160: always respawn at spawn.
- 319: first join says that chat, private messages and commands are logged for 30 days, with a privacy link (wording updated by R3-9, see Developer decisions).
- 51: a "Hide other players" option with a quick toggle at spawn (not built yet).
- REVIEW3 D4: one shared visibility service: Everyone (default) / Friends + island / Nobody, plus pinning a player.
- B17-R1-01: keep inventory everywhere, no fall damage anywhere.
- U2 (earlier owner answer, built): one welcome for a new player.

## Open questions
None beyond "Questions for the owner" above. Old questions 1 to 5 of v1 are answered by: Q1 and Q2 -> A1-Q1 and A1-Q2; privacy wording -> Developer decisions; join queue -> no custom queue; `/spawn` cooldown -> 0; spawn protection lists -> one rule per place.

## Rejected ideas
- Respawning at the island home or a bed (160): the owner wants one fixed respawn point.
- Teleporting returning players to their island on join (273 kept spawn).
- A custom soft join queue with a "Connecting, please wait" screen (proposed by the developer; Paper already throttles logins).
