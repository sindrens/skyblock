# Profiles · id: B17 · status: Locked · version: 2

## In one sentence
A player has up to 3 profiles; each profile is its own island with its own money, crystals, shards, inventory, skills and progress; nothing can move between your own profiles. Credits, rank, titles and settings belong to the player.

## How it works
- **`/profiles`** (menu: You, slot 10): one button per slot showing name, icon, island level, board rank, playtime, last played, money and stats. Click a profile to Switch, Rename, Change icon or Delete; an empty unlocked slot creates a profile after a confirm screen ("starts with $1,000 and an empty inventory, no island, you can switch back any time"). Only way to switch (P5). The whole menu is redone with the GUI overhaul (I9): short titles and lores.
- **Slots:** slot 1 free. Slot 2 = 500 credits, slot 3 = 1,500 credits (account entitlement, bought once, never refunded, not "covered again" by a rank slot; the menu shows each slot's source: bought, rank or track). Ranks or progression-track steps may include slots (decided in F2/F1). Hard maximum 3 (constant). Credit buys go through `CreditShop.buy()` (C1); until it exists the current charge stays.
- **Per profile:** money, crystals, shards, inventory, armour, ender chest, XP, health, food, effects, skills, Depths unlocks, island membership and role, start money, AH listings, collection bin, money notes, satchel, personal bank, achievements, tools, wardrobe, daily reward progress, crate keys, quests, battle pass, scoreboard and menu-item settings, playtime, tutorial-independent stats.
- **Per player (account):** credits (bought and earned), rank, titles, tags, cosmetics, nickname, chat, social and sound settings, punishments, island join history, ratings, likes, island bans, staff homes, vanish, tutorial progress.
- **Switching:** one DB transaction saves the current state and makes the target active, then loads it and teleports to that profile's island home (spawn if none). Wait 3 seconds, loaded from `profile_accounts.switched_at` (a relog does not skip it); bypass `skyblock.profiles.bypass.cooldown`. **Refused** while combat-tagged, in boss arenas (config region list until the boss card), in the warzone/PvP area (`switch.blocked-worlds` default `[warzone]`), frozen, in staff mode, dead. Allowed in the Depths out of combat.
- **During a switch** the player cannot drop, pick up, click, build, run commands or take damage. Pending `/trade`, teleport requests and open AH/trade screens are cancelled; pending island invites stay on the account. Deliveries (credit shop, crates, kit tools, mail) go to `/box` of the **target** profile via one `deliver(profile, item)` helper.
- **Keep inventory everywhere, no fall damage** (B17-R1-01): nothing drops on death, so no death-drop tagging is needed; the only exception is the PvP loadout (I11, new card), which resets on death.
- **Delete:** two-button confirm (no typing, also on Bedrock). The screen lists real numbers: island level, playtime, money, crystals, shards, items, and the island's fate. The second confirm is a separate view with an input lock. A team leader is refused with a "Transfer leadership" button (offline members confirm on next login). Cannot delete the active profile or a profile whose `/box` has items. **Soft delete:** the profile is hidden, not erased; `profile_states` and settings stay, most module delete handlers stop deleting stored rows and keep side effects (cancel AH listings, economy balance backup, island handling). `profiles.deleted-retention-days: 30` (0 = keep), at most 10 deleted profiles per account, then automatic purge; only the owner purges by hand. `/profile restore <player> <slot|name>` (admin, tab-completed, audited) restores into a free slot only and lists what returns (inventory, skills, balances, settings) and what does not (cancelled AH listings; a removed solo island unless `/isadmin restore` covers it). Player text: "Staff may be able to restore it for a limited time." Pending invites and coops of the profile are cleared on delete.
- **Start money:** $1,000 per profile per season; for slots 2 and 3 the grant key is `start:<season>:<currency>:<account>:<slot>` (delete and re-create in a slot grants once); slot 1 keeps its old key.
- **Starter kit and friends (B17-R1-03 to 05).**
  - Every new island's starter chest is filled completely again (plain items, Field Guide, five tier-1 tools); no 24 h claim timer for chest fills. Only the starter TOOLS are bound to the profile (the reader checks what the `starter` flag restricts); mint count logged. All tools get clearer lores and can be upgraded from bad to very good with custom enchants, enchants, upgrades and more, on the island and in the Depths (E10, E11, E12).
  - A profile joining a team island as member gets once (atomic `kit_given` claim per profile, tutorial finished or not) the starter tools it is missing plus other relevant items, but not what the island chest already holds (lava, water, ...), to inventory with overflow to `/box`. `pack_given` (Field Guide, once per account) is untouched.
  - **Fresh island:** one boolean `touched_at` per island, set by a member block place or break outside tutorial steps, a bank deposit, a spawner, a completed island task, an added member or an item put in a chest. An untouched island can be left to accept an invite: validate the invite and join rules first, then remove it with new reason `fresh_left` (does not wipe the profile), clear its invites and coops, then join, behind one confirm without the word "disband". A touched island needs a normal disband; the text offers "switch to an empty profile". No refund of creation or join counters.
  - Joining someone happens after the tutorial; "join a friend" at spawn is not built (B17-R1-06); the flow is designed in A3/B3.
- **Names:** chat filter, unique per account, regex and limits in config (16 max); mod `/profile rename <player> <slot> <name>`. Islands can also get an **island name**, chosen once a certain island level is reached, with more unlocks after progressing (B18 / B13, B14).
- **Staff:** `/inspect`, `/invsee`, `/ecsee`, `/logs` have a profile picker (3 slots); inactive profiles open read-only from `profile_states`, the active one from the live player; every view audited.
- **Credits farm guard:** per-profile reward sources (daily, streak, quests, pass) pay `credits_earned` once per account per day (best profile); one-time rewards once per season; other rewards stay per profile (rules finalised in F7/F9).
- **At season reset (P8, built with I5):** profile names and icons stay, island and progress wiped, `profile_states` is on the I5 wipe list, slots: credit-bought stay, rank slots follow the rank, others lost. `seasonResetSlots` is called by the season service.

## Developer decisions (security, data, tests; veto by exception)
- `switch_id` in the player's persistent data and in the `profile_states` row: equal ids at login = already applied, clean the row; different = apply the row; no kick; alert only on an unexpected mismatch (migration for the column). A failed consume can never roll back or mix profiles.
- Block the profile switch while an item sits on the cursor is replaced by returning it before capture (as built); the dead check is removed.
- Guard test: every table with per-profile data (columns `profile_key`, `player`, `owner_id`) has a delete handler or an allowlist entry with a reason; wardrobe is fixed now.
- Hard-coded values (`LOGIN_TIMEOUT_SECONDS`, `AUTOSAVE_TICKS`, name regex, date format, menu slots, sound ids) move to config or messages; `MAX_SLOTS = 3` stays. Settings default `settings.profile-keys` loses the `setting.` prefix after each key is classified; live config edit only with the owner's OK. Switch time is logged as a metric (no staff alert).
- Nodes in `permissions/profiles.yml`, grants in `server/permissions/profiles.txt`, `docs/commands/profiles.md` fixed (typed-name claim removed).
- Tests on H2: delete then restore; purge; restore refuses an occupied slot; fresh and touched islands for each trigger; failed join keeps the island; inventory and money survive `fresh_left`; start money once per slot; disband then create fills the chest; kit claim atomic; forced consume failure; settings round trip.

## Rules and limits
- No transfers between own profiles (principle 2, P3, P4): `/pay`, AH, notes, trade, bank, invites, coop, pickup of dropped items; audited as `profile_transfer_blocked`. Two own profiles never share an island and cannot visit or coop each other.
- A crash mid-switch keeps your items (state in the database). Leaderboards use the best live profile (P7).
- Bedrock: everything is menus.
