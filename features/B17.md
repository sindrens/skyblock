# Profiles · id: B17 · status: Draft · version: 1

## In one sentence
A player has up to 3 profiles, and each profile is its own island with its own money, inventory, skills and progress; nothing can move between your own profiles.

## How it works
- **`/profiles`** (also `/menu` slot 43): one button per slot with name, icon, island, island level, playtime and created date. Click a profile to Switch, Rename, Change icon or Delete. An empty unlocked slot creates a profile. It is the only way to switch (P5).
- **Slots (P6, PR5):** slot 1 is free. Slot 2: 500 gems, or free with rank Knight+ (`skyblock.profiles.slots.2`). Slot 3: 1,500 gems, or free with rank Warlord+ (`.slots.3`). Paid once ever (ledger `profile_slot`). Hard maximum 3.
- **Separate per profile (P2):** money, crystals, shards, inventory, armor, ender chest, XP, health, food, effects, skills, Depths unlocks, island membership and role, start money, auction listings, collection bin, money notes, `/settings` values (scoreboard, menu item), playtime. Built in other modules and following the same rule: satchel, personal bank, achievements progress, tools, wardrobe. Planned the same way: daily rewards (128), crate keys (127), quests, battle pass.
- **Shared by the account:** gems, rank, titles and tags (achievement titles become account titles), cosmetics, nickname, punishments, island join history, ratings and likes, island bans, staff homes, vanish.
- **Switching:** saves the current state and makes the target active in one database transaction, then loads the target and teleports you to that profile's island home (spawn if it has no island). While it runs you can't drop, pick up, click, build, run commands or take damage.
- **Refused when:** in combat or the boss arena, in the warzone (`switch.blocked-worlds`), frozen, in staff mode, dead, an item is on the cursor, or the target slot is locked. Allowed in The Depths out of combat. Anti-spam wait: 10 s (`switch.cooldown-seconds`).
- **Delete:** confirm screen, then type the profile's name. No cooldown (P9). Deletes everything on it, auction listings and skills included. You can't delete the profile you are on. A team leader's profile can't be deleted until leadership is transferred. A solo island is removed; a member leaves the island.
- **Start money:** every profile gets $1,000, once per season (121).
- **Planned (decided): earn before you send.** A profile can send money away (`/pay`, money notes, `/trade`, buying on the AH) only after it has earned $10k itself. Start money and rewards don't count. Sending to members of the island the sender is on is always allowed, including island bank deposits. This stops the "create a profile, pay the $1,000 away, delete it" loop. (R3-1, REVIEW3 F1)
- **At season reset (P8):** gem-bought slots stay; slots from ranks or anything else are lost (the profile in it is locked, still deletable).

## What the player sees
- Menu slot status: "» CLICK to manage" or "Slot locked: you can't switch to it".
- "Saving and switching to <name>..." then "You are now on <name>."
- "Wait <time> before you switch again." / "You can't switch profiles here." / "Respawn first."
- Delete: "Last step: type <name> in chat to delete it forever."
- "Created <name>. Open it to switch." after making a profile in an empty slot.

## Rules and limits
- **No transfers between own profiles (P3, P4).** Refused and audited as `profile_transfer_blocked`: `/pay`, auction buy, money notes, island invite, coop and join, pickup of items another own profile dropped or threw. Two own profiles never share an island, and can't visit or coop each other's island.
- The active profile's state lives in the database, so a crash mid-switch keeps your items ("Your items are safe. Please join again").
- Leaderboards use the best live profile per player (P7).
- Bedrock: all of it is menus; only the delete confirmation and rename use chat typing.

## Decided
- P1 to P12 (DECISIONS): a profile is one island and everything on it; P2 lists what is separate; P3 never on the same island; P4 nothing moves (implement with extreme care); P5 switch only with `/profiles`; P6 slots; P7 best profile on boards; P8 gem slots kept at reset; P9 delete allowed, no cooldown; P10 names and icons; P11 join limits per player; P12 don't pause other work.
- PR1 to PR5: titles shared, crystals per profile and gems account-wide, rank perks on every profile with separate vault contents, switching teleports to that profile's island home, slot prices.
- 117: a profile that leads a team island can't be deleted. 118: deleting removes everything; you can't buy your own other profile's listings.
- 119: two own profiles never share an island. 120: a lost rank locks the slot, deletable. 121: every profile gets start money.
- 122, 132: switching blocked in warzone and boss arena, allowed in the Depths out of combat.
- 133: an island invite is accepted only on a profile with no island. 134: staff pick the profile in `/inspect`, `/invsee`, `/ecsee`, `/logs`. 135: a confirm screen when creating a profile.
- 123: chat, social and sound settings follow the account; scoreboard and menu item per profile.
- 128: daily login reward and streak per profile. 131: no 30-minute limit; the tutorial unlocks permissions.
- 138, 139: max 3 profiles; names go through the chat filter, 16 characters.
- R3-1 and REVIEW3 F1: "yes unless its to island members on the island they are on": a profile may send money away only after earning $10k itself; sending to members of its own island (and island bank deposits) is always allowed.
- REVIEW3 F7: perks (including extra profile slots and loadouts) unlock along one progression track like a battle pass; bought ranks unlock cosmetic and convenience perks at once. Needs its own plan.

## Open questions
1. The switch wait is 10 s, but P5 asked for no real cooldown and principle 1 says no waiting. Default: keep 10 s as anti-spam until switching is proven fast, then lower it.
2. "Knight+" and "Warlord+" are old rank names. The rank plan renames the ranks (RTA-2) and REVIEW3 F7 moves perks onto a progression track. Which rank or track step gives slot 2 and slot 3? Default: decided in the perk-track plan.
3. Skills, Depths unlocks and other progress are separate per profile, but a reset or kick does not wipe them (see B3, B10). Default: as built.
4. Should delete confirmation avoid typing for Bedrock? Default: keep the typed name, it is a rare and dangerous action.

## Rejected ideas
- Daily login reward once per account (128).
- A cooldown or delay on delete or recreate (P9, 121).
- Auction listings as a way to move items to another profile (118).
