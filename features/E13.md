# Wardrobe and sets · id: E13 · status: Draft · version: 1

## In one sentence
`/wardrobe` holds 16 armor sets that give bonuses when you wear 2 or 4 pieces. Sets are bought or won, level up from the activity they boost (level 0 to 50, up to double strength), and two max-level sets can be merged into one. The wardrobe works today; 4 sets wait for fishing and conquest, and the 3 gem sets are to become looks only.

## How it works
- **Built:**
  - **16 sets** (`sets.yml`), tiers I to IV (strength 1 / 1.5 / 2 / 2.5):

| Tier | Sets | How you get them |
|---|---|---|
| I (5) | Delver (mining), Woodsman, Tiller (farming), Footman (combat), Netcaster (fishing) | $20,000 each |
| II (2) | Banneret (conquest), Lorekeeper (all skill XP) | $2.5M; 400 gems |
| III (6) | Deepforge, Cropwarden, Dreadguard; Thornward, Stormkeel; Coinmaster (sell) | 25,000 crystals; 900 gems; $40M |
| IV (3) | Voidforged, Conqueror, Phoenix | crates only (Set Tokens) |

  - **Gates:** Banneret needs Depths tier 5, Lorekeeper tier 5 or any skill 25, tier III sets tier 6 in their wing, Coinmaster tier 8, tier IV tier 7.
  - **Storage:** the set is a database row per profile. Wearing puts real armor on you that is only a view of the row: it cannot be dragged, dropped or stored, and a copy is deleted on sight. Vanilla armor is never locked; if it must move for a piece and the inventory is full, it goes to `/box`. Equip cooldown 2 s, blocked in the warzone and while in combat (10 s), frozen or switching profile.
  - **Bonuses:** 2 pieces of a set give its first bonus, 4 pieces add the second. You may mix pairs of different sets. They go to the shared bonus families (E12 caps); new families are crop growth (cap 100%) and conquest (cap 60% per stat).
  - **Set level** 0 to 50: bonus strength 0x at Lv 0, 1x at Lv 25, 2x at Lv 50. XP per level `200 x 1.10^L` (about 65 active hours to Lv 50). XP comes from the set's activity while you wear 2+ pieces (50% at 2 to 3, 100% at 4): Depths ore or log 1, Depths kill 8, island generator block 0.25, grown crop 0.5, sell 1 per item (max 8,000 an hour), farming max 10,000 an hour. Milestones at Lv 10, 25, 40, 50 (trim upgrade, 2nd armor enchant slot, glint, particle trail, merge allowed).
  - **Merging:** two different owned sets, both Lv 50, tier I to III, sharing no bonus family, become one set with both bonuses at Lv 0. Fee 5,000 crystals x (tier A + tier B - 1). One database transaction, no unmerge, a merged set needs 1.5x XP. The second set's armor enchants come back as books.
  - **Wardrobe Tomes** give +2,500 or +10,000 set XP, never past Lv 40. Only staff give them for now.
  - **Wardrobe Chest:** unique items only (custom tools, enchant books, scrolls, Set Tokens, tomes): 27 slots, +9 per storage grant. Escrow like the auction house; crash recovery on join.
  - **Gem sets today** (Lorekeeper, Thornward, Stormkeel): real bonuses. Buying one gives an account-wide Sigil. Every profile claims the set free once per season; Sigils are kept across seasons.
  - **Set Tokens** (from crates): unique, tradeable until redeemed. Sets are bound to the profile.
  - **Warzone:** every set counts as 1x, damage reduction at 50%, only conquest bonuses, no trails. **Staff:** Wardrobe button in `/inspect`; helpers view, admins give, remove or set a level (confirm, audit).
- **Planned (decided):**
  - **Gem sets become looks only (R3-20):** Lorekeeper, Thornward and Stormkeel are worn over an earned set (reuse `look_set_id`). Their bonus versions move to crates and crystals.
  - **Paid sets are claimed items (R3-14, REVIEW3 F5):** a set bought with gems arrives unclaimed and can be sold or traded anywhere. A right-click claims it into the account and it becomes bound.
  - **Bound keys (R3-2):** Set Tokens and Tomes from a bound key are bound too. Needs the bound-item system.
  - Netcaster, Stormkeel (fishing) and Banneret, Conqueror (conquest) show "Arrives with ..." until `features.available` lists the feature (empty today).
  - Set XP from bosses, KOTH, envoys and warzone kills (hooks exist, nothing sends yet). Tomes from crates and quests. Chest size as a rank perk. Staff `/wardrobe admin` needs owner approval.

## What the player sees
- `/wardrobe` (alias `/sets`): every set grouped by activity, owned first, with filters; a set screen with pieces, bonuses at your level, XP bar, Buy (confirm), Claim, Redeem, Merge, Trail on/off; a "Worn now" screen that says "capped" when a family cap cuts a bonus. Level-ups play a sound; titles at Lv 10, 25, 40, 50.

## Rules and limits
- Anti-abuse: unique items and database-first moves; a copied token or tome is taken and staff are told; a stale or foreign piece is deleted and audited; no refunds, no selling back.
- Per profile: sets, levels, chest and loadouts. Account-wide: Sigils only.
- Gem sets must never turn real money into power (R3-20: looks only) or bound items into in-game money (R3-14).
- Performance: set XP is kept in memory and saved every 30 s; the trail is the only per-tick effect and is off in the warzone and when vanished (PERF4 saw 6 to 12 ms, tuning in progress).
- Bedrock: click-only menus; names up to 16 characters.
