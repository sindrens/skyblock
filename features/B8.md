# Island menu and chat · id: B8 · status: Built · version: 1

## In one sentence
`/is` opens one hub menu for everything about your island, and `/is chat` gives the team a private chat with role colors.

## How it works
- **Island menu:** `/is` with an island opens a 6-row menu (red frame, one plain click per button, refreshes every 2 s). `/is home` and `/is go` still teleport straight home.
- **Buttons:** Island info (name, your role, leader, solo or team, members, size, warps, level, created, open or locked; click opens `/is level`), Home, Members, Permissions, Warps, Visitors (open or lock, who is here, bans), Settings, Invites, Island notes, Island chat (toggle), Chat colors, Visit islands, Island stats, Island bank, Upgrades, and Leave island (members) or Disband island (leader). Back from any sub-menu returns here.
- **Built vs grey:** Stats, Bank and Level are live. The Upgrades button is grey "Coming soon" until `/is upgrade` is built (B14).
- **`/is info [player]`:** your island or that player's island in chat, with level, size, warps and your role there.
- **Island chat:** `/is chat` with no text switches your chat to the island and back (not saved: off after relog or profile switch). `/is chat <message>` sends one message. Members only (Leader, Admin, Mod, Member); coop and visitors don't hear it.
- **Format:** `[Island] <role> <name> » text`, in the role's color. Default colors: Leader gold, Admin red, Mod aqua, Member green.
- **Chat colors:** the Leader picks the next color per role from 8 (gold, red, aqua, green, yellow, light purple, blue, white). Others see them read-only.
- **Staff spy:** `skyblock.island.chat.spy` plus the "Island chat spy" switch in `/settings` shows every island's chat as `[Spy] [island] ...`. The console gets it too.
- **Planned (approved, not built):** an "Island achievements" button (AC1-2 question), island announcement shown on join (idea 22, I12: chat, 200 characters), custom role names (idea 20, I10: 16 characters).

## What the player sees
- Menu title "Island". Gray buttons say "Coming soon".
- Island chat lines in role colors; the mute and chat filter rules of public chat apply.
- Scoreboard: Level line (and the next note, if switched on in `/settings`).

## Rules and limits
- Island chat goes through the chat module's checks (mute, freeze, filter, caps, links, length max 256) and is logged with channel `island`.
- The chat lock (`/mutechat`) and slow mode do not apply to island chat by default (`chat.yml`).
- Staff chat (`/sc`) wins over island chat.
- Bedrock: every menu button is one plain tap; no chat clicks needed except the optional `[Open]` links.
- Menus refresh on a 2 s timer only while open.
