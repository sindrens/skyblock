# Quests · id: F8 · status: Draft · version: 1

## In one sentence
Daily and weekly quests, plus weekly island missions, that give money, crystals, keys and battle pass XP for doing what you already do.

## How it works
- **Status:** planned (M10.7, new module `quests`). Nothing is built. The Challenge Book in `/is level` (180 island tasks for upgrades and masteries) is a different system.
- **Daily quests (P10-21):** 3 per profile per day, picked from a pool and scaled to your tier so each takes about 10-15 min. 1 free reroll a day. Each pays money (3 min of income at your tier), crystals and 100 pass XP. All 3 done: 1 Cinder key.
- **Weekly quests (P10-22):** 5 per profile, reset Monday 00:00 Europe/Oslo, about 45 min each. Each pays money (8 min of income), crystals and 400 pass XP. All 5 done: 1 Blaze key.
- **Island missions (CR1, FI 41):** 3 per island per week. Progress is shared by the members (never coop). Each member who helped gets crystals and 150 pass XP.
- **Keys from quests are tradeable** (P10-6). Trading opens only after the tutorial.
- **Quest pool:** only content that exists and that you have unlocked. Live now: mine, chop or kill in a tier, sell for an amount, level a tool, forge, enchant, visit or like islands. Added later: island work (Phase 7), fishing (Phase 8), boss, KOTH and envoy quests (Phase 8-9).
- **Progress** comes from server events only, never from the client. Rewards are given automatically when the quest is done.
- **Quest Master NPC** in the Tavern opens `/quests` (412). A quest board (7 × 5) in the Tavern shows your own quests.

## What the player sees
- `/quests` (plan): tabs **Daily**, **Weekly**, **Island**, **Pass**. Each quest shows a progress bar in the lore and reward lines like `[+] $ · ◆ · 100 pass XP`. Buttons: **Reroll (1 left)** and "All 3 done: Cinder key".
- Action bar at every 25% of progress and a toast when a quest is done. An optional scoreboard line, for example `▸ Mine 400 Iron Ore 230/400`.
- Today the `/menu` Quests button shows "Coming soon".

## Rules and limits
- Quests, progress and rewards are per profile (P2). Island missions are per island.
- Only real play counts. Depths fast-break detection already gives no reward (#57). The AFK pool and stack-kill extras do not count (proposed default).
- Money is minutes of income at your tier, so quests never become a money printer. Quests and the other Phase 10 rewards should stay under about 15% of a regular player's income (plan).
- Alts: money and crystals stay on the profile. Quest keys are tradeable (P10-6), but `/trade` and `/ah` unlock only when the tutorial is finished (TUT-4).
- Bedrock: the menu is click-only, the board and scoreboard line are extras.
- Performance: progress is added from events that already exist and saved in batches, no scans (proposed default).

## Decided
- P10-21: daily quests: 3 per day scaled to your tier, 1 free reroll, 3 min of income + crystals + 100 pass XP each, all 3 done = Cinder key.
- P10-22: weekly quests: 5 per week, 8 min of income + crystals + 400 pass XP each, all 5 done = Blaze key.
- P2: quests and the battle pass are separate per profile.
- CR1: crystals are earned from quests, Depths drops and island missions.
- FI 41 and FI 42: island missions and daily + weekly missions are planned.
- 412: the Tavern is for quests.
- P10-6: quest keys can be withdrawn and traded. R3-2: keys earned by playing (quests, pass, events) stay tradeable.
- R3-44: the premium pass has no extra daily quest reroll. It stays cosmetic only.
- P10-41: staff `/quests admin` commands.
