# Duels · id: I2 · status: Draft · version: 1

## In one sentence
Duels are private 1v1 fights in the warzone's four arenas, with an optional wager held in escrow, where your gear is normalized and the only thing you can lose is the stake.

## How it works
Nothing is built yet. The owner decided the idea (`/duel` with wagers, DESIGN §8). Every rule below is a plan from DUELS.md, not decided, unless it is listed under Decided.
- **Pieces that exist:** PvP normalization of enchants and sets, the combat tag, and the idea of escrow from `/trade`. The warzone world exists but its build (with the 4 arenas) is not delivered yet.
- **Arenas:** 4 identical 30 x 30 courtyards in the warzone's south-east, two exact spawn pads, a spectator balcony (builder brief, proposed default). If all 4 are busy, an extra copy is pasted so nobody waits (proposed default).
- **Start a duel** (proposed default):
  - `/duel <player>` (or the player picker) opens a setup menu: wager, best of 1 or 3. The other player gets a request with Accept / Deny.
  - `/duel queue` finds a random fair opponent by duel rating (rating comes with Phase 9).
- **Fight rules** (proposed default):
  - Own gear, normalized the same way as in the warzone. No kits.
  - No elytra, ender pearls, flying or commands, except `/duel leave` (= forfeit).
  - Keep inventory always.
  - Rounds last 3 minutes. Then both get Wither I until someone dies, so there are no draws.
  - Quit or disconnect = you lose (same as combat logging in I1).
- **Wagers** (proposed default):
  - Money, shards and items. Crystals can never be wagered, because they can never be transferred (R3-5b). Bound items and earned gems cannot move either. Bought gems: see open question 1.
  - Both stakes go into escrow before the fight, then the winner gets both minus **5%**, which is a sink.
- **Extras** (proposed default): spectate from the balcony or `/duel spectate <player>`. Duel stats (wins, losses, streak) and a season leaderboard. Ranked rating with Phase 9.

## What the player sees
- Planned: a setup menu (wager, best of 1 or 3), a request popup with Accept / Deny, an escrow confirm like `/trade`, a boss-bar round timer, a result line, and a duel page in the warzone scoreboard.
- Text and exact menu names are not written yet.

## Rules and limits
- Wagers go through the shared transfer guard that `/pay`, notes, trade and the AH use, so nothing can move between your own profiles (proposed default).
- A money wager is a money transfer, so REVIEW3 F1 should apply: a profile can send money away only after it earned $10k itself (members of the island you are on are exempt) (proposed default).
- No wagers between accounts that shared an IP in the last 30 days. Max 3 wagered duels per pair per day (proposed default).
- Stakes are locked in escrow. A crash or restart before the result returns both stakes (proposed default). Every wager is logged.
- Duel wins give no Conquest XP (proposed default), because it is easy to farm with a friend.
- Bedrock: setup, request and spectate are menus. Nothing needs a chat click.
- Performance: arena copies are pasted on demand and removed after the fight. A limit on copies at one time is not decided.

## Decided
- DESIGN §8: `/duel` with wagers exists.
- 666: currencies can be wagered in `/duels` (limited by R3-5b below; gems are open question 1).
- R3-5b: crystals can never be transferred (hard rule in code); shard notes are on, so shards can move and be wagered.
- REVIEW3 F3: bought gems are tradeable, earned gems are bound to the account.
- REVIEW3 F5: items bought with gems arrive unclaimed and tradeable; right-click claims them and they become bound.
- 876: in the warzone and duels, damage enchants at 40%, healing 50%, area and movement enchants off.
- 963: in the warzone and duels every set counts 1x, 50% damage reduction, only conquest bonuses.
- 822: PvP only in the warzone (and duels).
- WR1 / #76: keep inventory on death.

## Open questions
1. Can bought gems be wagered? 666 allows wagers of all types and bought gems are now tradeable (REVIEW3 F3), but wagering gems that cost real money is gambling and a Mojang risk. Default: no gems in wagers; money, shards and unclaimed items only.
2. Does 5% of the pot burn as a sink? Default: yes.
3. Anti win-trading: no wagers between same-IP accounts (30 days) and max 3 wagered duels per pair per day? Default: yes.
4. Paste extra arena copies when all 4 are busy, so nobody waits? Default: yes.
5. Should duel wins give Conquest pillar XP? Default: no.

## Rejected ideas
- Crystals as wagers: crystals can never be transferred (R3-5b).
- Bound items and earned gems as wagers: they are bound to the account by design (REVIEW3 F3, F5).
- Draws: the Wither I rule ends every round with a winner.
