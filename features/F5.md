# Crates and keys · id: F5 · status: Draft · version: 1

## In one sentence
Seven crates you open with keys you earn by playing. The roll is saved before anything is shown, a pity counter guarantees a top reward, and keys are never sold.

## How it works
- **Built (M10.1, `/crates admin`):**
  - 7 crates (CA1-CA7): Ember (common), Cinder, Blaze, Inferno, Phoenix, Eclipse (monthly), Conqueror (KOTH, hidden until KOTH exists).
  - Keys live per profile as two counters. **Bound** keys (AFK pool, votes, daily, playtime, rank claims, tutorial) cannot be withdrawn or traded. **Tradeable** keys (quests, battle pass, events, weekly top, fragments, KOTH) are meant to be withdrawn and traded. A source in neither list counts as bound. Opening uses bound keys first.
  - Today only the AFK pool and staff give keys. AFK pool: 1 h Ember, 3 h Ember, 6 h Cinder, 8 h Blaze, once a day.
  - R3-2: loot from a bound key is bound too (books, spawners, tokens, boosters), and so is a key won with a bound key. Bound loot cannot be traded, listed or picked up by another profile. Keys won with a tradeable key stay tradeable.
  - Rewards: money and crystal pouches scaled to your tier, island fuel, enchant books, boosters (global ones in Phoenix and Eclipse at 4%), Disenchant Scrolls, spawners tier 3-5, set tokens, tags, keys of a higher crate.
  - Not built yet, so they pay their crystal value and keep their odds: tomes, tool skins, sell wands, collectors, blueprints, pet eggs, holograms, kill effects.
- **Pity and top band** (`crates.yml`), pity opens / top-band share / rewards: Ember 30 / 8% / 11, Cinder 28 / 9% / 13, Blaze 23 / 11% / 16, Inferno 18 / 14% / 18, Phoenix 18 / 14% / 18, Eclipse 10 / 8% / 16, Conqueror 20 / 12% / 12.
- **Pouches:** Money Pouch = N minutes of reference income at your tier. Crystal Pouch = base × 1.3^(tier-1). A crate feels the same on day 1 and in month 3.
- **Pity:** per profile and crate. At the pity number the next open is forced into the top band. Reset at the season reset.
- **Planned:**
  - M10.3: crate blocks and holograms at the crate yard, a ~3 s spinner you can skip, a "Fast opening" setting, a global chat line for ★ wins from Inferno up.
  - M10.4: withdraw tradeable keys as items (a key item cannot be used on another of your profiles). Eclipse monthly rewards. Conqueror when KOTH exists.
- **Planned (decided):**
  - R3-18: 3 key fragments make 1 Phoenix key, not 10 for any key. They drop in mine and forest tiers 11+, later fishing (1 in 4,000). The drop chance is 0 today.
  - R3-2: a spawner won with a bound key goes straight onto the opener's island spawner stack. Today it is a bound spawner item.
  - R3-15: the AFK pool never gives Blaze keys. The 8 h milestone gives a Cinder key (`afk` `keys.milestones`).
  - R3-20: the bonus versions of the gem sets move into crates and crystals. The gem sets become looks only (E13).

## What the player sees
- `/crates [crate]`: keys on top, the 7 crates in a row. Java: left-click preview, right-click open 1, shift-click open 10. Bedrock: tap opens the preview with buttons **Open 1**, **Open 10**, **Open all** (up to 64).
- Preview: every reward with `★ Name (xN) | chance%`, keys and `Pity 7/23`. Pouches show what they pay at your tier.
- Open 10 and Open all skip animation and show one summary screen. A crate block at the crate yard: right-click opens 1, left-click shows rewards.
- No key: "You have no Ember Key. Get keys from voting, quests, the AFK pool and daily rewards."
- Staff: `/crates admin give|take|view`.

## Rules and limits
- Keys are earned only and never sold for money or gems (DESIGN §9). Per profile, wiped with the profile at the season reset, with a warning in the Final Week (P10-43).
- Server rolls first. One database transaction per click takes keys, moves pity, writes the open and the reward. A crash never loses or doubles a reward. Overflow goes to `/box`.
- Odds are shown in the preview. Every roll is logged (`crate_opens`). Duplicate tag or title re-rolls into one you do not own, or pays crystals.
- Not while frozen, in staff mode or switching profile. One open at a time. Crate money stays about 5% of income (plan target).
- Staff give: max 1,000 per command, confirm above 64, audited.
- Bedrock: no chat clicks needed. Performance: key counters use conditional updates, no scans.

- P10-2: pity per profile and crate at about 2.5× the average gap, shown in the preview, reset each season.
- P10-4: open from `/crates` anywhere. Open 10 and Open all skip the animation.
