# Combat zones and layered mobs · id: E6 · status: Draft · version: 1

## In one sentence
The Combat wing has 10 tiers (Graveyard to Warlord's Citadel) where custom mobs spawn while players are there and pay Combat XP, drops and crystals. Today everyone in a zone fights the same shared mobs. The owner decided layered mobs from the start (REVIEW3 D3): each player sees and fights their own tier's mobs. The plan is not written yet.

## How it works
- **Built (shared mobs):**
  - Mobs spawn only in a combat zone that has players: **8 mobs + 2 per player, max 30 per zone**, max 300 in all zones (`mobs.yml` and `depths.yml: combat.*`). They fill up 4 per zone every 2 seconds, never closer than 6 blocks to a player.
  - Mobs leave 30 seconds after the last player. A mob that wanders out of the zone is put back at a spawn spot.
  - Nothing else spawns in the Depths: no natural spawns, reinforcements, jockeys or slime splits. No creepers. Mobs never stack here.
  - **Mobs per tier:** Zombies, Skeletons, Spiders and Cave spiders, Slimes, Drowned, Witches, Pillagers and Vindicators, Blazes, Wither skeletons, Ravagers and Evokers.
  - **Damage** per hit 2 (tier 1) up to 10 (tier 10). Health is sized for about 2.5 seconds per kill with that tier's Forge sword. Tiers 1 to 3 are safe for new players.
  - **Per kill:** Combat XP (zombie 5, skeleton 10, spider 22, slime 40, drowned 72, witch 135, pillager 250, blaze 420, wither skeleton 700, ravager and evoker 1,200), about 4 drops into the satchel (E5), a crystal chance, vanilla XP straight to you, sword XP, and a book chance (1 in 300, 350, 400 or 450 kills by band).
  - **Safe zones:** arrival and exit pads: mobs cannot target you there. No ender pearls or wind charges in combat zones. Bows, crossbows and tridents work (#798).
  - **Ambient mobs** in Mine and Forest are different: 2 harmless, silent mobs per player that only you see.
- **Planned (decided, not built):**
  - **Layered mobs (REVIEW3 D3):** in the v2 zones (E1) players at different tiers share a zone but each fights their own tier's mobs. It goes in the Depths v2 plan with a critical review and questions to the owner.
  - Known costs: hiding a mob per player (the `setVisibleByDefault(false)` plus `showEntity` pattern used for ambient mobs) does not stop it from targeting, making sounds, shooting and ticking. Spawn rate must also grow with players (today about 2 mobs a second per zone, enough for about 16 players).
  - **Combat XP only in Depths combat zones (R3-24):** today any non-spawner mob pays XP. The Soul Reaper enchant ("+Combat XP from stacked kills") needs a new effect (E12).
  - **Full satchel (R3-22):** combat drops go to the inventory, then `/box`. Today they are lost (`CombatZones`), a fix item (E4).
  - **Phase 8 (I4):** abilities for tiers 4 to 10 with a 1 second warning, 2% elites, kill credit to the top damager.

## What the player sees
- `/dungeon > Combat` tab with an icon per tier (zombie head, skeleton skull, cobweb, slime ball ...). Named mobs such as "Grave Walker" or "Citadel Ravager".
- Action bar "+N crystals", XP lines, satchel drops, mini-boss boss bar (E7). Scoreboard variant "Combat".
- Teleporting out of a combat tier takes 3 seconds (moving or damage cancels).

## Rules and limits
- No PvP in the Depths (#822). Death keeps your inventory and only adds a death stat (#76). `/ec` and `/craft` are blocked for 10 seconds after combat (#377).
- Spawner mobs on islands give no Depths drops and no Combat XP (turned off by default).
- Performance: one batched pass for all zones every 40 ticks; standing-height scan cached 300 seconds per column; hard caps 30 per zone and 300 total; no task per mob.
- Bedrock: hits, boss bar and menus work as on Java; abilities must be telegraphed.
- Kill stealing is possible today (last hit). A busy zone has only 30 mobs.

## Decided
- 774: 8 mobs per zone + 2 per player, max 30, damage 2 to 10.
- 798: bows, crossbows and tridents work with their own damage.
- 799: a tier 10 mob takes about 8 hits to kill an average player in a matching set.
- 800: safe arrival pad. 822: PvP only in the warzone.
- 918: Combat XP per kill pays the same XP per hour as mining.
- R3-24: Combat XP only from Depths combat zones (replaces the Combat part of 919).
- R3-22: combat drops follow the satchel chain: satchel, inventory, `/box`.
- REVIEW3 D3: layered mobs from the start, each player fights their own tier's mobs.
- R3-34: the owner chose the Depths zones redesign (E1) instead of a 40-player cap (824) or second copies.
- 772: combat kills drop custom items for sword recipes.
- #76: PvE death keeps inventory and only adds a death stat.
- REVIEW B6 default: mine and forest mobs are per-player ambience; combat zones and bosses are shared.
- S1: mobs never stack in the Depths or in boss fights.

## Open questions
1. Do players on the same tier in one zone share one set of mobs (a layer per tier) or does each get their own? Default: one layer per tier, shared by the players on that tier, so groups can still fight together.
2. Can the server hold hidden mobs at 300 players? Default: the plan answers this after an 80-bot load test. The fallback is the review's earlier idea: tier sub-arenas with shared mobs.
3. What is the mob cap per layer? Today it is 8 + 2 per player, max 30 per zone and 300 in all. Default: the plan sets a cap per layer and keeps the hard total.
4. Mob abilities in tiers 4 to 10, none in 1 to 3 (PV2)? Default: yes.
5. Kill credit: top damager instead of last hit (Phase 8)? Default: top damager.

## Rejected ideas
- Tier sub-arenas with shared mobs first, layered mobs only after a pilot (the review's proposal): the owner wants layered mobs from the start (REVIEW3 D3).
- A 40-player cap with "zone full" (824) and second copies of busy zones: replaced by the zones redesign (R3-34).
