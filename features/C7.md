# Secure trade · id: C7 · status: Draft · version: 1

## In one sentence
Two players near each other trade items, money and shards in a safe window where both must confirm and any change cancels the countdown. Value hints that warn about unfair trades are decided but not built.

## How it works
- **Built: ask.** `/trade <player>` (or pick from a menu of nearby players). They get a clickable request. Both must be within 16 blocks in the same world. A request lasts 60 s, 5 s between requests. A player who `/ignore`s you gets no request. Trade requests can be turned off in `/settings`.
- **Built: the window** (6 rows, taps only). Your side left, theirs right.
  - Tap a stack in your inventory to offer it (up to 12 stacks); tap it again to take it back.
  - Tap the gold ingot to offer money (number picker; a currency menu first). Money and shards can be offered. Crystals never (R3-5b). Gems are off today. **Planned (decided):** bought gems can be offered, earned gems can't (REVIEW3 F3, R3-5a).
  - Tap **Confirm**. When both confirmed a 3 s countdown runs.
- **Built: any change by either side clears both confirmations** and stops the countdown.
- **Built: the swap.** One database transaction moves every item and every money leg together. If anything fails, nothing changes. Offered items leave the inventory into an escrow table first, so a crash cannot dupe or lose them.
- **Built: delivery.** Items are handed over once. If a player has no room, the items wait and arrive when there is room (checked on join, after a profile switch and every 15 s). Confirming needs room for everything the other side offers.
- **Built: it cancels** when players are more than 24 blocks apart, after 10 minutes, on logout, damage, teleport, profile switch, freeze or staff mode. No requests within 10 s of combat. Every item goes back.
- **Not tradeable:** locked wardrobe armor, menu items, dupe-flagged items, staff items (`/give`, `/i`, creative), admin blocks, filled shulker boxes and bundles. Starter kit pieces, money notes, enchant books, tools, blueprints and tag items can be traded. **Planned (decided):** an unclaimed item bought with gems trades freely; once claimed by right-click it is bound and can't be traded (REVIEW3 F5). Loot from bound keys is bound (R3-2).
- **Unlock.** `/trade` opens when the tutorial is finished.
- **Planned (decided): value hints (736, 737, R3-38).** Each offered item shows an estimated value on hover. A red warning shows before the countdown when one side gives far more value than it gets.
  - The value comes from the server's sell and shop prices. Items with no server price (spawners, books, sets, tools) show "value unknown".
  - An AH price is used only with 5+ different sellers and buyers, ignoring same-IP pairs, so alts can't pump it.
- **Planned (decided): earned $10k before sending (REVIEW3 F1).** A profile can trade money away only after it earned $10k itself, except to members of the island it is on.
- **Planned (decided): 24 h trade block (R3-28).** After the system finds a duped unique item, its holders can't use `/trade` for 24 h. It ends on its own; staff decide after.

## What the player sees
- Request line with [Accept] and [Deny] clicks, or `/trade accept` and `/trade deny`.
- Window: Confirm and Cancel buttons, a money picker with -/+ steps ($1k, $100k, $10M; 1, 10, 100 for shards) and a green "Confirmed" or red state for each side.
- When it ends you get the reason and "Your items are back." (for example "The trade ended: you moved too far apart.", "The trade ended because of combat."). After the other side confirms: "Steve confirmed. Check the trade and confirm too."

## Rules and limits
- Trading between two profiles of the same player is refused (P4) and audited. Staff can block a player's trading and freeze balance; both sides are checked at the swap.
- No new-account timer (131; the dead 30-minute keys are to be deleted, R3-16).
- Every trade is logged with both item lists (full serialized stacks) and both money offers (`/logs trade <player>`), so staff can restore items.
- Alerts: the big-transfer and repeat-transfer rules of the economy watch see money legs (C10).
- Bedrock: taps only, no chat clicks needed (the request also has `/trade accept`). Performance: open trades are checked every 5 ticks, only for players in a trade.

## Decided
- 731: start within 16 blocks, end past 24 blocks or 10 min, 3 s countdown. 733: any damage ends the trade, no requests within 10 s of combat.
- 734: locked wardrobe armor, menu items, dupe-flagged items and filled shulker boxes can't be traded.
- 736: an estimated value as a hover line (not built). 737: a red warning when one side gives far more (not built). R3-38: value from server prices, "value unknown" without one, AH only with 5+ sellers and buyers.
- 316: a player who `/ignore`s you gets no trade request. 332: staff items can't be traded.
- 656 / TUT-4: unlocked by the tutorial. 131: no 30-minute rule.
- 666: duel wagers and drops are allowed ways to trade currency (open for gems, see C1).
- REVIEW3 F1 (R3-1): $10k earned before sending. R3-28: 24 h trade block on dupes. REVIEW3 F3 / F5: bought gems and unclaimed gem items trade; earned gems and claimed items don't.
