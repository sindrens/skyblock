# Auction house · id: C6 · status: Review 1 · version: 1

Cluster C4 + C5 + C6. Shared price questions are asked once under C4 (C4-Q1 craft value, C4-Q2 bulk blocks). Prices here are market prices set by players; server prices (C4, C5) are placeholders until the overhaul C15 (card not written yet; C1-R1-25, C1-R2-31).

## In one sentence
Players list items at a fixed price in `/ah`, other players browse, search and buy, and the money reaches the seller even when they are offline. It is where everything that has no server price goes (books, rare drops, spawners, tools, blueprints, skins), so players trade instead of only selling to the server. Buying needs $10k earned first, and a listing can never be bought by your own other profile.

## How it works
**Built (stays as it is):**
- **List.** `/ah sell <price> [12h|24h|48h]` lists the item in your main hand (`1500`, `1.5k`, `2M`). Without a duration a dialog shows the fee, the tax and what you receive, one button per duration. The menu's "Sell held item" does the same. Example: price $1,500, fee $15, tax $75, you receive $1,425.
- **Money.** 1% listing fee (minimum $1) is paid when listing and not refunded (549). 5% sales tax is taken from what the seller receives. Price $10 to $1T. Money only.
- **Limits.** 5 listings at once (more with `skyblock.auction.slots.<n>`, up to 54). 10 s between two listings. The same item type at most 3 times in 30 min.
- **Browse.** Categories (all, tools, armor, blocks, materials, consumables, cosmetics, enchant books, special), search by item name or a seller's exact name, sort by newest, ending soon, lowest or highest price. Whole listings only (559).
- **Buy.** Confirm dialog, then one database step; an item can be sold once. The seller is paid at once, online or offline.
- **Collection bin.** Expired, cancelled, removed and bought-with-a-full-inventory items wait here and never expire (563).
- **Not listable:** starter kit items, money notes, dupe-flagged items, staff items, filled shulker boxes and bundles, admin blocks. Tools, enchant books, blueprints, skins and tag items can be listed.
- **Staff** (`skyblock.staff.auction.remove`, mods and up) remove a listing with a reason (Scam listing, Bugged item, Duped item, Offensive name, Testing); the item goes to the seller's bin. A staff trade block cancels all of a player's listings into the bin.
- **Profiles.** Listings and the bin belong to the profile. Buying a listing of your own other profile is refused in the menu and again inside the purchase (118, audited `profile_transfer_blocked`).
- **Unlock.** `/ah` opens after the tutorial's sell step (built; `Gates.AUCTION`). No new-account timer (the dead key is deleted on load, R3-16).
- **Sanctions.** Frozen or trade-blocked players cannot list or buy (built; C1-R1-09).
- **Every list, buy, cancel, expire and collect is logged** with the full serialized item; payments are ledger rows `ah_fee`, `ah_buy`, `ah_sale`.

**Proposed (new in this review):**
1. **Buying gate (C1-R1-07, locked in C1, not built).** A profile can buy on the AH only after it earned $10k itself (the same service and numbers as `/pay`, notes and `/trade`: `send-gate.*`). If the seller is a member of the buyer's island the gate does not apply. The message says how much more to earn ("Earn $4,200 more by selling or playing, then you can buy here"). Checked in the menu and again inside the purchase step. Listing is not gated. Money from AH sales does not count as earned. Main-to-alt trading by AH stays open (C1-R1-07).
2. **Price hints (560, 561, 562, decided, not built).** Every listing shows its price per item and the typical price (median of the last 7 days of sales of that item, needs at least 5 sales from 5 different sellers and 5 different buyers, pairs that shared an IP are ignored, R3-38). The listing dialog shows "Typical price $X" and "Server pays $Y / shop sells $Z" when the item has server prices. A price 10x above or below typical needs a second confirm ("This is 12x the usual price"). Enchant books get a filter by enchant name and level.
3. **Sale popup.** When one of your listings sells you get an action bar line with a coin sound wherever you are ("✔ Steve bought your Diamond Pickaxe +$1,425"). Offline sales show as one join line ("While you were away: 3 items sold, $12,430, [Open /ah]"). Small, rewarding, no new system.
4. **One place for waiting items (C6-Q2).** Proposed: expired, cancelled, removed and "inventory was full" items go to `/box` (tagged "From auction") instead of a separate bin. A "Take all" already exists in `/box`.
5. **Listing pace (C6-Q3).** Proposed: 2 s between listings (stops scripts only) and the same item type up to 6 times in 30 min. The 5 active slots remain the real limit.
6. **Longest listing time (C6-Q4).** Proposed: add a 7-day duration with the same 1% fee (12h, 24h, 48h, 7d), so rare items are not relisted and re-taxed every two days.
7. **Fixed price only (C6-Q1).** Proposed: no bidding. The name stays "Auction house".
8. **Credit items.** An item bought fully with bought credits arrives unclaimed and can be listed until its owner right-clicks it; after that it is bound and cannot be listed. Items paid with any earned credits arrive bound and cannot be listed (C1-R1-39, C1-R2-09, C1-R2-29). A chargeback voids the entitlement: the listing is removed at once. Loot from bound keys is bound (R3-2). Spawners stay tradeable (988). AH resale of credit items is a credits-to-money path the owner accepts (C1-R2-09, Mojang note).
9. **Outlier alerts (C10).** New watch rules for mods: a sale at 20x or more of the 7-day median (at least $1M), and a sale between linked accounts (same IP in 30 days) or an alt-funnel profile (C1-R1-06). Alerts only, never a block.

## What the player sees
- Chat: "Listed 64x Cobblestone for $1,500 (24h). Fee: $15", "Bought 1x ... from Steve for $...", "Steve bought your ... for $1,500. You got $1,425 (tax $75)".
- Join and expiry: "Your listing expired. It's in /box (From auction)." with an [Open /box] click.
- Buy dialog bullets: what you pay, what you get, "/box if your inventory is full".
- Locked buyer: "Earn $X more before you buy on the auction house." plus how.
- Listing tile lore (UI_STYLE): `| Price` per item and total, `| Typical price`, `| Seller`, `| Ends in`, bold yellow `» CLICK to buy`.

## Rules and limits
- A crash can't dupe or lose: listings stuck in pending or buying for 2 min are resolved, also at start. No per-tick work: ended listings move on every 20 s, the browse cache reloads every 60 s.
- Wash trading is not blocked (C1-R1-06: laundering by item trade is watched, not blocked). AH payments are not player-to-player transfers, so the transfer alerts miss them: proposal 9 closes that gap.
- Deleting a profile cancels its listings (a profile with active listings cannot be deleted until they are cancelled) and, with soft delete (B17-R1-07), its waiting items are kept for staff restore, not deleted.
- Season end: AH listings and waiting items follow the I5 wipe list (C1-R1-12), archived before the reset.
- Bedrock: search through a form input (not a chat prompt), everything else is a tap. English only.
- Money only: no credits or shards on the AH (card question 4 of v1 answered by default; C1: credits move only by `/credit pay`).

## Developer decisions (defaults; the owner only vetoes)
- **Gate.** `AuctionEngine.buy` calls `SendGate.check(buyerProfile, sellerProfileOrPlayer, MONEY)` before the conditional `UPDATE ... WHERE status = 'active'`; the island-member exemption uses the same `sameIsland` lookup as `/pay`. A failing check refuses with the missing amount; a failing check service fails closed (like sanctions). Config `auction.buy-gate.enabled` (true); the amount stays in `economy.yml send-gate.required`. The gate also hides nothing: listings stay visible to locked players.
- **Hints.** Table `ah_price_stats` (item key, median, sample count, distinct sellers, distinct buyers, updated at) refreshed by an async job every 5 min from sold rows of the last 7 days; the menu reads a cached map. Distinct sellers/buyers and the same-IP filter use the moderation module's account-link lookup; if it is missing the IP filter is skipped and the 5 and 5 rule still applies. Config `auction.hints.enabled`, `.median-days` (7), `.min-sales` (5), `.min-distinct-sellers` (5), `.min-distinct-buyers` (5), `.warn-factor` (10), `.refresh-minutes` (5). The server price band comes from `SellService#price` and the shop catalog (read-only).
- **Entitlement check.** Items that carry an entitlement id are checked at listing, at purchase and at collection: state `claimed` or `void` refuses; `unclaimed` is listable. The purchase moves the right to claim to the buyer inside the same transaction. A voided entitlement removes the listing, deletes the item with an audit row and tells the seller. Test: chargeback while listed.
- **Bin into `/box` (if C6-Q2 recommended).** Migration moves `ah_collection` rows into `box_items` by the one `deliver(profile, item)` helper with source `auction` and the original timestamp; idempotent (key `ah_collection:<row id>`), dry run first, old table kept 30 days read-only; AH menu keeps a "My listings" page but loses "Collection bin". Offline deliveries for a profile that is not active wait in its box, as today (B17-R1-18). If the owner chooses to keep the bin, a **Take all** button is added to it.
- **Pace.** `auction.anti-abuse.listing-cooldown-seconds` (2), `anti-abuse.relist.max-same-item` (6) (decided 554 values were 10 and 3).
- **7-day duration.** `auction.listing.durations` gets `7d` (the key already allows up to 7d); the 20 s expiry task is unchanged.
- **Sale popup and join summary.** Built on the existing seller notice (`notify.join-delay-ticks`); action bar plus sound switchable in `/settings` (`auction.notify.popup` default true).
- **Alerts.** Two rules in `economy-watch.yml`: `rules.ah-outlier.*` (factor 20, min amount 1,000,000) and `rules.ah-linked.*`; they read `ah_sale` rows of the last 2 h like the other rules; alerts never punish.
- **Security.** No new money path beyond the gate; purchases stay one conditional update; payments keep idempotency keys `ah_fee:<id>`, `ah_buy:<id>`, `ah_sale:<id>`. Wash trading between two accounts costs 6% each time (fee plus tax), which is why it stays watched, not blocked. `/trade` and the AH share one `TradeBlock` check so a dupe block applies to both.
- **Performance.** Browse cache every 60 s, per-category index in memory, hint map cached; no per-tick work; at 300 players and about 5,000 listings the menu reads memory only.
- **Data.** New table `ah_price_stats`; `ah_collection` retired after the migration (30 days read-only); `ah_listings` gets `entitlement_id` (nullable). Everything through a module migration.
- **Tests (H2, no server).** Gate: below/at/above $10k, island-member seller exempt, gate failing = refused; 50 parallel buys of one listing succeed once; chargeback while listed; median needs 5 and 5 and ignores same-IP pairs; 10x warning thresholds; 7d duration; bin migration run twice gives the same rows; profile with entitlement `void`.
- **Config keys (new).** `auction.buy-gate.enabled`, `auction.hints.*`, `auction.notify.popup`, `auction.listing.durations`, `auction.anti-abuse.*` (values above).
- **Commands and tab completion.** `/ah` (`/auction`, `/auctionhouse`), `/ah sell <price> [12h|24h|48h|7d]`: price suggests `1k 10k 100k 1m`, duration suggests the list; every menu action is reachable from `/ah` and `/menu`; nothing needs a long command. Permissions unchanged: `skyblock.auction.use`, `skyblock.auction.sell`, `skyblock.auction.slots.<n>`, `skyblock.staff.auction.remove`. Command doc `docs/commands/auction.md` updated at build.
- **Bedrock.** Search by form input; confirm dialog and every filter are slot buttons.

## Questions for the owner
Shared price questions are under C4 (Q1, Q2). AH-only questions:
1. **C6-Q1 Fixed price only, or real bidding?** Recommended: fixed price only (simple, no waiting). Alternative: also an optional bid mode for items over $1M (money held while you lead, a timer that extends on late bids): a new system.
2. **C6-Q2 One place for waiting items?** Recommended: yes, unsold and returned items go to `/box`, and the AH collection bin goes away. Alternatives: keep the bin and add a Take all button; keep it exactly as built.
3. **C6-Q3 How fast can you list?** Recommended: 2 s between listings and 6 of the same item per 30 min. Alternatives: keep 10 s and 3 (decision 554); remove both and rely on the 5 slots.
4. **C6-Q4 How long can a listing run?** Recommended: add 7 days next to 12h, 24h and 48h, same 1% fee. Alternatives: keep 12h, 24h, 48h; or add 7 days at a higher fee (2%).
