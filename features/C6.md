# Auction house · id: C6 · status: Draft · version: 1

## In one sentence
Players list items at a fixed price in `/ah`, other players browse, search and buy, and the money reaches the seller even when they are offline. Unsold and bought items wait in a collection bin that never expires.

## How it works
- **List.** `/ah sell <price> [12h|24h|48h]` lists the item in your main hand (prices like `1500`, `1.5k`, `2M`). Without a duration a dialog shows the fee, the tax and what you receive, with one button per duration. The menu's "Sell held item" does the same.
- **Money.** 1% listing fee (minimum $1) is paid when listing and is not refunded on cancel or expiry. 5% sales tax is taken from what the seller receives. Price range $10 to $1T. Money only.
- **Limits.** 5 listings at once (more with `skyblock.auction.slots.<n>`, up to 54, planned with ranks). 10 s between two listings. The same item type at most 3 times in 30 min.
- **Browse.** Categories (all, tools, armor, blocks, materials, consumables, cosmetics, enchant books, special), search by item name or a seller's exact name, sort by newest, ending soon, lowest or highest price. Whole listings only, no part of a stack (559).
- **Buy.** A confirm dialog, then one database step: the item can only be sold once. The seller gets the money (net of tax) at once, online or offline, with a chat line or a join notice.
- **Collection bin.** Expired, cancelled, removed and bought-with-a-full-inventory items wait here. Items never expire (563); only deleting the profile empties it.
- **Not listable:** starter kit items, money notes, dupe-flagged items, staff items (`/give`, `/i`, creative), filled shulker boxes and bundles, admin blocks. Tools, enchant books, blueprints, skins and tag items can be listed. **Planned (decided):** an item bought with gems arrives unclaimed and can be listed until its owner right-clicks it to claim it; after that it is bound and can't be listed (REVIEW3 F5). Loot from bound keys is bound too (R3-2). Spawners stay tradeable (988).
- **Staff** (`skyblock.staff.auction.remove`, mods and up) remove a listing from the menu with a reason (Scam listing, Bugged item, Duped item, Offensive name, Testing). The seller is told and the item goes to their bin. A trade block by staff cancels all of a player's listings into the bin.
- **Profiles.** Listings and the bin belong to the profile. You can never buy a listing made by your own other profile (118). Deleting a profile cancels its listings and deletes their items; a profile with active listings can't be deleted until they are cancelled.
- **Unlock.** `/ah` opens after the tutorial's sell step. No new-account timer (the dead key `auction.yml anti-abuse.new-account` is to be deleted, R3-16).
- **Planned (decided): price hints (560-562).** Each listing shows its price per item and the typical price. A warning and confirm shows when a price is 10x away from typical. A book filter by enchant name and level. Not built.
- **Planned (decided): earned $10k before buying (REVIEW3 F1).** A profile can buy on the AH only after it earned $10k itself. Start money, rewards and money from other players do not count. Not built.
- **Planned (decided): 24 h trade block (R3-28).** After the system finds a duped unique item, its holders can't list or buy for 24 h. It ends on its own; staff decide after. Not built.

## What the player sees
- Chat: "Listed 64x Cobblestone for $1,500 (24h). Fee: $15", "Bought 1x ... from Steve for $...", "Steve bought your ... for $1,500. You got $1,425 (tax $75)".
- Join and expiry: "Your 64x Cobblestone listing expired. It's in your collection bin (/ah)." A bin line has an [Open /ah] click.
- Buy dialog bullets: what you pay, what you get, "collection bin if your inventory is full".

## Rules and limits
- Every list, buy, cancel, expire and collect is logged with the full serialized item, so staff can restore it. Payments are ledger rows `ah_fee`, `ah_buy`, `ah_sale`.
- A crash can't dupe or lose: listings stuck in pending or buying for 2 min are resolved, also at start. No per-tick work: ended listings move to the bin every 20 s, the browse cache reloads every 60 s.
- Wash trading is not blocked. AH payments are not player-to-player transfers, so the transfer alerts (C10) miss them; only the earn-rate alert can notice a big seller. The balance sheet's alert for sales far above the 7-day median or between linked accounts (R4) is not built.
- Bedrock: search is a chat prompt, everything else is a tap.
