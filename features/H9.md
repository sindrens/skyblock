# Performance · id: H9 · status: Draft · version: 1

## In one sentence
The server must hold 300 players at 20 TPS with no limits on how players build, so the game is tuned for speed, slow work is reported by name, and a lag-machine guard and a 300-bot load test are planned.

## How it works
- **Built: server config for 300 players** (reasons in `server/CONFIG_NOTES.md`): view distance 8, simulation distance 5, max players 350, 2 joins per tick, region file cache 512, player autosave every 30s spread over ticks, Alternate Current redstone, spawner mobs without AI, inactive villagers off, lower entity ranges, villager and grass tick rates slowed, hopper checks every 8 ticks (1 item per move today), TNT 50 per tick.
- **Built: slow-task watch.** Main-thread work slower than 5 ms is logged by name (a `core.yml` setting). Slow database work is logged too (`database.yml: slow-query-ms`). This is how the problems below were found.
- **Built: code fixes** from the audit (QUICK1, PERF2 to PERF4): chat renders once instead of per viewer, the dupe scan rolls through players, the hopper listener is gone, menu frames and colors are cached, per-player timers are spread over ticks, startup work moved off the main thread.
- **Built: island limits** (owner SK2, `stacking.yml: limits.*`): armor stands 20, item frames 60, minecarts 20, boats 10, villagers 20, display entities 30 (+50% with an upgrade). Mob stacks: 8 per type per chunk, 1,024 each.
- **Planned (decided): total mob-stack cap (R3-26).** At most 40 mob stacks per chunk, all types together, on top of 8 per type.
- **Planned (decided): fast hoppers (R3-27, REVIEW3 F12).** `spigot.yml`: `hopper-amount: 64`, `hopper-transfer: 8`, `hopper-check: 8`, and Paper `hopper.cooldown-when-full: true`. That moves a full stack every 8 ticks. `disable-move-event` stays off, because `ItemStacker` and `MenuService` listen to `InventoryMoveItemEvent`. Before release: test with collectors, stacking and storage (no dupes on full or partial stacks), and measure MSPT on a hopper-heavy test island.
- **Planned (decided): hopper counter (REVIEW3 F13).** Count hoppers per chunk on block place and break only (an int in memory, saved with the chunk or rebuilt lazily on chunk load). No world scan, no per-tick work. Staff get at most one alert per chunk per hour above 64. No hard cap unless the load test shows lag.
- **Planned (PERFORMANCE.md section 4): lag-machine guard**, three stages:
  - 1. Warn the island members with the chunk coordinates.
  - 2. Freeze the redstone in that chunk for 60s.
  - 3. After 3 freezes in 10 minutes, remove the worst part and give the item back, with a staff log.
  - Staff tools `/lagguard top`, `/lagguard island <name>`, `/lagguard unfreeze`.
- **Planned: redstone heat meter** in the island menu: Cool, Warm (300 events/s), Hot (600/s). Freeze at 1,500 for 10s. Soft counters show pistons and observers. No limit and no block.
- **Planned: villagers rest** when enclosed and idle, and wake when a player trades.
- **Planned: load test** on a second box: 300 bots (about 30 Bedrock through Geyser), 150 seeded islands, 10 scenarios (join storm, spawn crowd, Depths, farms, chat, menu storm, redstone clock bomb, boss, backup, tutorial burst).
- **Targets (in the plan, not yet approved):** TPS 20 for 95% of the time; MSPT p95 under 35 ms and p99 under 50 ms; GC pause p99 under 100 ms; database wait p95 under 50 ms; login to ready under 10s with 300 online; no task over its limit more than once an hour.
- **Not decided: production hardware.** JVM heap and GC flags are unchanged until the real machine is known.

## What the player sees
- Normally nothing. A limit shows as "Island limit reached: 20 villagers."
- Planned: "Redstone in chunk 12, 4 is running very fast. Slow it down or it will be paused." and "We removed a clock at 120, 64, 88 to protect the server. The item is back in your inventory."
- Planned: a "Redstone heat" line and a hopper count such as "Hoppers 214" in the island menu (PERFORMANCE.md). REVIEW3 F13 only decides the staff alert.

## Rules and limits
- No count limits on blocks, hoppers, pistons or observers. Only lag machines are stopped. The hopper counter only alerts staff.
- With `hopper-amount: 64`, vanilla one-item sorters stop working. Collectors and storage do the sorting.
- No per-tick scans: work is event-driven, cached, spread over ticks, or async. A new feature that loops over all players every tick is rejected.
- Nothing is lost when the guard acts: removed parts return to the owner.
- Bedrock costs more CPU per player (Geyser). Plan for about 15% Bedrock (to confirm).
- Never start a test server without the owner's permission.

## Decided
- O1, O2: no block limits, no hopper limit, no filter hoppers; handle lag with optimized systems and lag-machine detection.
- WR2: no piston or observer limits; detect lag machines only.
- O17: lag tools yes.
- 1010: a staff tool to clear or split huge stacks on a laggy island. Not built.
- R3-26: a total cap of 40 mob stacks per chunk, on top of 8 per type.
- R3-27 / REVIEW3 F12: hoppers move 64 items every 8 ticks (`hopper-amount: 64`, `hopper-transfer: 8`, `hopper-check: 8`), if tested safe and optimized.
- REVIEW3 F13: count hoppers on place and break only; one staff alert per chunk per hour above 64; no hard cap unless the load test shows lag.

## Open questions
1. May the guard remove the worst redstone part after 3 freezes and return the item? Default: yes.
2. May villagers sleep when enclosed and idle? Default: yes, restock on a timer.
3. What is the production hardware (cores, RAM, disk, heap)? Default: 8 fast cores, 64 GB, NVMe, 20 GB heap.
4. May a staging server run with bots (second box, copy of data, no public access)? Default: yes, with permission each time.
5. Shorter view and simulation distance per world (islands 6 / 4, spawn and Depths 8)? Default: yes.

## Rejected ideas
- Count limits on blocks, hoppers, pistons and observers, and filter hoppers (O1, O2, WR2): the owner wants players free to build. Only lag machines are stopped.
- Moving hoppers every tick: the owner settled on 64 items every 8 ticks, because every tick would add work, not save it (REVIEW3 F12).
