# NPCs and holograms · id: A5 · status: Built · version: 1

## In one sentence
Help NPCs, signposts and top-10 boards are placed automatically from the finished builds, so players can right-click anything in spawn and the Depths to open a feature or read a short explanation.

## How it works
- **Source of positions:** the build files (`build-points.yml`, written by `/skyblock builds apply`). Today that is 19 NPCs, 40 holograms and 10 leaderboards. Module `npcs`, settings `npcs.yml`, texts `messages/npcs.yml`.
- **NPC body:** a Paper mannequin (player-shaped, no Citizens). Invulnerable, silent, no gravity, never saved. Spawned when the chunk loads, removed when it unloads. Option `npcs.body: villager` if Bedrock cannot see mannequins. Optional skin and held item per NPC (`npcs.skins`, `npcs.held-items`).
- **Name text** above the head: the name plus a hint line ("Right-click" or "Right-click for help"). Heads turn to the nearest player within 6 blocks, checked every 10 ticks.
- **Click:**
  - Right-click runs the role's action as the player (the command checks its own permission).
  - Sneak + right-click shows a short help text instead.
  - Hitting an NPC counts as a click (Bedrock touch taps).
  - One click per 600 ms per player. NPCs cannot be traded with, hurt or burned.
- **Roles and actions** (`npcs.actions`): forge (quartermaster, blacksmith, depths_forge) opens `/forge`; bank `/bank`; shop `/shop`; sell `/sell`; auction `/ah`; enchanter `/enchanter`; dungeon (depths_guide) `/dungeon`. Guide and Info show help with an [Open] button. Foreman (every mine tier) shows help for `/dungeon`. Quests, daily, vote, black market and crates are help-only "coming soon".
- **Guide NPC at spawn** opens `/tutorial` for everyone (sneak-click still shows help). During the tutorial NPCs say one line for your step (A3).
- **Holograms:** text displays at each hologram point (info board, rules board, portal names, district signposts, event board, hints in the Depths). Text is in `messages/npcs.yml` (`holograms.<id>`). Some have a click box that shows a help text (`hologram-help.<id>`). Texts made by other modules are skipped (boss timer, event timer, rush banner).
- **Leaderboards** (Hall of Heroes): top 10 with #1, #2, #3 in their own colours, title and footer "Updates every 5 min". Boards: island top solo and team (island level), five skills (mining, foraging, combat, farming, fishing), weekly island gain, weekly pillar gain and boss damage. The last three show "Coming soon" until their data exists. Money boards (`baltop`, `money`) are supported but the Hall of Heroes build has no point for them.
- **Staff:** `/skyblock npcs list|tp <id>|respawn` and the `/staff` hub entry. `respawn` re-reads builds and config.
- **Planned (PHASE-10):** crates, quests, daily, vote and black market roles switch from help to their commands when those features go live.

## What the player sees
- A named NPC with a floating label, e.g. "Banker / Right-click".
- Java: help as chat lines with a clickable [Open]. Bedrock: a 3-row menu with the text on a book and a big Open button.
- Boards where #1, #2 and #3 have their own colours.
- Help lines stay short and plain, for example "Forge better tools here: /forge".

## Rules and limits
- These entities live only in the spawn and Depths worlds, so they never count against island entity limits.
- Start-up spawn is spread out (8 per tick, 2 ms budget) and parsing runs off the main thread.
- Leaderboards: one database read per distinct source every 5 minutes, off the main thread. No per-tick scans. Head turning is the one repeating job (every 10 ticks, rotation sent only when it changes 3 degrees or more).
- Bedrock: every NPC action also exists in `/menu`. Nothing depends on chat clicks. Mannequin and text display support on Bedrock is not verified.
- Crowds: NPC bodies are real entities seen by everyone. Packet-only NPCs are a possible later fix (PERFORMANCE P-24).
