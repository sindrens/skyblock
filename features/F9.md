# Battle pass · id: F9 · status: Draft · version: 1

## In one sentence
A season pass with 100 levels: a free track for everyone and a premium track that holds cosmetics only. You level it by playing.

## How it works
- **Status:** planned (M10.8, new module `pass`, after quests). Nothing is built.
- **Season:** 90 days (R1), 100 levels, a flat 800 pass XP per level (P10-24). A new pass with new rewards each season (`pass.yml: seasons.<id>`).
- **Pass XP sources (plan):** daily quest 100, weekly quest 400, island mission 150, vote 25, calendar claim 50, active hour 20, achievement tier 50, events later.
- **Pace (plan estimate):** a regular player reaches about level 95-100 by season end, a grinder about day 70, a casual about level 55.
- **Free track (per profile, P10-25):**
  - A small reward every level: money (2 min of income), crystals or 1 h island fuel, rotating.
  - Keys every 5 levels: Ember x2 at 5, 15, 35 and so on; Cinder at 10, 20, 30, 40, 60, 70, 80, 90; Blaze at 25 and 75; Inferno at 50; Phoenix at 100.
  - Titles at level 50 and 100. Pass keys are tradeable (P10-6).
- **Premium track (P10-26):**
  - Bought with store credits (1,000, about EUR 10, P10-27), once per account per season (P10-28). Staff can grant it for testing. Cosmetics bought this way arrive unclaimed and bind to the account when claimed (REVIEW3 F5).
  - A cosmetic every 5 levels (tags, titles, border styles, trails, tool skins, kill effects), a premium skin and an animated title at 100. No extra daily quest reroll (R3-44).
  - No keys, boosters or currencies, so nothing random and no progress is paid for.
  - Each cosmetic is given once per account, on the profile that reaches the level first.
- **Auto-claim:** rewards are given at level-up with a toast. No claim buttons.
- **After level 100:** every 2,000 XP gives a Crystal Pouch M and 1 bound Ember key.
- **Catch-up:** +50% pass XP while you are 15 or more levels behind the day line (day of season × 1.1), so late joiners can finish.
- The top donor rank does not include the premium pass (plan RK26).

## What the player sees
- `/pass` (plan), and a Pass tab in `/quests`:
  - Row 1: "Level 34 · 420/800 XP · Premium: no".
  - Row 2: the free track, 7 levels around yours (claimed ✔ / next / locked).
  - Row 3: the premium track, same 7 levels, with a padlock if you do not own it.
  - Row 6: arrows to scroll through the levels.
- A toast and sound at each level-up.

## Rules and limits
- Free track and pass XP are per profile (P2). Premium is per account for one season. Nothing transfers between profiles.
- Paying never buys keys, boosters, currency or levels. This keeps the pass inside Mojang's rules (principle 8).
- Pass keys are tradeable, so `/trade` and `/ah` unlock only after the tutorial (TUT-4).
- The free track pays about 1-2 min of income a day in the model, which is small.
- Bedrock: the level rows are a normal menu, no chat clicks.
- Performance: XP is added in memory from events that already exist and saved in batches (proposed default).

## Connections
- Quests (F8), voting (F6), daily rewards (F7), achievements (F3): the XP sources.
- Crates and keys (F5), tags and titles (F4): the rewards.
- Ranks (F2): Immortal does not include the pass. Progression track (F1): a separate track of permanent perk unlocks.
- Store (I6): premium price. Seasons (I5): a new pass each season.

## Decided
- R1: season length 3 months, 100 levels, premium bought with store credits.
- FI 44: battle pass with a free and a premium track: yes.
- P10-24: a flat 800 XP per level for all 100 levels.
- P10-25: free track: a small reward every level, keys every 5 levels, titles at 50 and 100.
- P10-26: premium track: cosmetics every 5 levels, a premium skin and animated title at 100, no keys, boosters or currencies. The extra daily reroll is removed by R3-44.
- R3-44: drop the extra daily reroll from the premium pass, so it is cosmetic only.
- R3-2: keys earned by playing (quests, pass, events) stay tradeable.
- P10-27: premium price 1,000 store credits (about EUR 10) per season.
- P10-28: premium once per account per season, cosmetics once per account, free track per profile.
- P10-6: pass keys can be withdrawn and traded.
- P2: quests and the battle pass are separate per profile.

## Open questions
1. REVIEW3 F3 only knows gems (bought gems trade, earned gems are bound), so store credits now look like bought gems. What does "1,000 credits" mean, and can earned gems pay it? Default: 1,000 gems, bought gems only.
2. The plan rules for auto-claim, the after-100 reward and the catch-up (RX29-RX31) are not answered. Default: as in this card.
3. Who designs the cosmetics and titles of each season's premium track? Default: the owner lists them with the resource pack art.
4. Should F1 (the progression track) reuse this free track? See F1. Default: (proposed default) separate. F1 is permanent unlocks, the pass is seasonal.

## Rejected ideas
- Keys, boosters or currencies on the premium track (P10-26): paying must not buy random rewards or progress.
- An extra daily quest reroll on the premium track (R3-44): the premium pass stays cosmetic only.
