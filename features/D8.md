# Fishing · id: D8 · status: Draft · version: 1

## In one sentence
Fishing is the relaxed way to earn on your island: a custom rod catches custom fish, junk, treasure and rare fish by tier, pays about 60% of an active Depths hour, and gives the Fishing skill, rod enchants and fishing sets something to work on.

## How it works
- **Built:** only the pieces around it. A basic fishing rod exists as the first rod in the Forge (starter tool). The Fishing skill shows as "Coming soon". The fishing sets Netcaster ($20,000) and Stormkeel exist but are locked until fishing exists. Angler (MA10) and rod enchant pools wait for this feature.
- **Planned (Phase 7, M7.8): where.** Any water on islands, outposts and marked spawn ponds (a build region of kind `fishing`). Not in The Depths or the warzone.
- **Custom loot needs a custom rod** (the starter rod counts). A vanilla rod gets vanilla loot and no XP.
- **12 fishing tiers** by Fishing level (0, 5, 10 and up to 66). Each tier adds one custom fish, a named vanilla fish item (Bedrock-safe) with a rarity and a price. Proposed names: Pond Cod, River Salmon, Reef Snapper, Spinefish, Ember Trout, Ashfin, Glowscale, Cinder Eel, Magma Carp, Starlight Koi, Voidfin, Phoenix Koi.
- **Loot per catch:** 70% fish (weighted to your newest tiers), 15% junk, 10% treasure (small crystal pouch, enchanted book, name tag), 5% rare fish (5x value). 1 in 4,000 gives a Phoenix key fragment (3 fragments make 1 Phoenix key, R3-18). (fishing.yml: loot)
- **Pace:** base wait 10 to 25 s, lowered by rod tier (-3% per tier), the Quick Bite enchant, Netcaster and Stormkeel sets and Angler. About 120 catches per active hour.
- **Money:** a catch is worth 0.6 x (mining money per active hour at that tier) / 120. So fishing pays about 60% of The Depths per active hour. Crystals, books (1 in 60 to 90) and Tide Chests come on top.
- **XP:** Fishing skill XP per catch by fish tier. Set XP 25 per catch. Angler mastery: +5% speed, +4% loot.
- **Rods:** 12 tiers forged with money and fish of the tier before, gated by Fishing level.
- **Planned later (Phase 8, M8.9):** sea creatures (from Fishing 15, 1 in 25 catches), lava fishing at Emberdeep and marked lava ponds, and fishing contests.

## What the player sees
- Catch messages with the fish name and rarity, Fishing XP in `/skills`, and rods in the Forge Rod tab.
- After 40 catches in one spot: only junk and no XP until you move 8 blocks (message text still to be written, proposed default).
- Contests (Phase 8): a boss bar timer, points by rarity, top 3 get a key.

## Rules and limits
- Anti-AFK: after 40 catches in the same 3-block spot without moving 8+ blocks, catches give junk only and no XP. Vanilla auto-fishing tricks only ever get vanilla loot. The spot counter lives in memory.
- Contests (Phase 8) use the same anti-AFK rules.
- Data: one small row per profile (`fishing_stats`: catches, best fish tier, rare catches). No per-tick work, one roll per bite.
- Bedrock: bite and catch use normal vanilla events. Fish are named vanilla items. Rod upgrades are menus.
