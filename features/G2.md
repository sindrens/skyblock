# Private messages and ignore · id: G2 · status: Draft · version: 1

## In one sentence
Players can message each other privately with `/msg` and `/r`, and `/ignore` stops messages, teleport requests, trade requests and island invites from a player without telling them.

## How it works
- `/msg <player> <message>` (aliases `/w /tell /whisper /m /pm`). `/r <message>` answers the last person you talked to.
- Max length 256 characters. Rate limit 5 messages per 5 s (`social.yml: dm.*`).
- **Click a name** in a private message to start a reply.
- **Settings:** "Private messages" off in `/settings` blocks messages from other players. Staff can always reach you.
- **Muted players** cannot send private messages. A raid lockdown also blocks `/msg` for new accounts (moderation module).
- **Ignore** (`/ignore <player>`, `/ignore list`, `/unignore <player>`):
  - Blocks their private messages, `/tpa` and `/tpahere`, trade requests and island invites.
  - They get the same answer as "requests off", so they never learn they are ignored. In `/msg` the sender sees the line as sent, but you get nothing.
  - "Hide ignored chat" (on by default) also hides their public chat, for you only.
  - Staff cannot be ignored (permission `skyblock.staff.unignorable`). Coop trust is not an invite and is not blocked.
  - The ignore list belongs to the account and is saved in the database (`social_ignores`).
- **Social spy:** mods and up (not helpers) can turn on `/socialspy` to read all private messages (owner answer 318). Blocked lines show "(ignored)".
- **Logging:** every private message is audited (category chat, action `dm`) and kept 30 days. `/rules` and the first-join text say so (owner answer 319).
- Other plugins use the shared `IgnoreService`: teleports, trade and island invites.
- **Planned (decided):**
  - R3-32: `/msg` runs through the link, IP and phone filter (not the swear list). Until an account finishes the tutorial, only staff and players it messaged first can DM it. Today private messages skip every filter.
  - R3-9a: `/report` attaches the last 10 private messages between reporter and reported player as evidence. That is the only way private messages become evidence. Evidence is kept 180 days and the privacy page says so.
  - R3-9b: helpers cannot read private messages in `/logs`. Mods and up keep them. Today helpers can (fix in `server/permissions/logs.txt`).

## What the player sees
- Sent: `[me » Steve] text`. Received: `[Steve » me] text`. Hover shows the time.
- Errors: "Steve has private messages off." · "Nobody to reply to. Use /msg <player> <message>." · "That player is offline now." · "Too fast. Try again in 3s."
- After `/ignore`: "Ignoring Steve. No messages, teleport or trade requests or island invites from them. [Undo]".
- Vanished staff count as offline.

## Rules and limits
- Today there is no word or link filter on private messages (see Planned above). The swear list never applies: they may be in any language (owner answer 305).
- Downside of R3-32: a new player's friends cannot message him for about 3 minutes, until he finishes the tutorial.
- Rate limit and 256 character cap stop spam. `skyblock.cooldown.bypass` skips the rate limit.
- Anti-abuse: an ignored player cannot spam you through tpa, trade or invites. Staff alerts are not affected.
- Bedrock: names are clickable text. If taps do not work, typing `/r` works. QoL 6 (player picker menu) would fix this properly.
- Performance: one map lookup per message. No scanning.

- 316: `/ignore` also blocks tpa, trade requests and island invites (never staff).
- 317: `/ignore` hides that player's public chat for you only, as an option.
- 318: `/socialspy` for mods and up, not helpers.
- 319: `/rules` and first join say private messages and commands are logged 30 days and staff can read them.
- R3-32 (Recommended): `/msg` goes through the link, IP and phone filter, not the swear list. Until an account finishes the tutorial, only staff and players it messaged first can DM it.
- R3-9a (Recommended): `/report` attaches the last 10 private messages as evidence, only through `/report`.
- R3-9b (Recommended): hide private messages from helpers in `/logs`; mods and up keep them.
- 50: chat and social settings follow the account (see open question 1).
