# Economy watch · id: C10 · status: Draft · version: 1

## In one sentence
Staff see how much money is created and removed, whether the economy is inflating, and get alerts for strange money moves. Alerts never punish by themselves. Hard limits on `/eco` are decided but not built.

## How it works
- **Built: `/eco admin [alert]`** (moderators and up, `skyblock.staff.economy.dashboard`). Money supply and trend, an inflation badge, created and removed today / 7 days / 14 days, sell price index, velocity (money moved between players in 7 days divided by supply), interest and auction volume, biggest sources and sinks, every reason, top earners (24 h or 7 d), biggest transactions, open alerts. A number after the command opens one alert with its evidence.
- **Built: the numbers.** A background job runs every 60 s, off the main thread. It reads only ledger rows after its saved cursor (batches of 5,000) and adds them to hourly summary tables per currency and reason. The sums and the cursor commit in one transaction, so every row is counted once, also after a crash. The money supply (all balances, banks and uncashed notes) is saved every 60 min. Old rows are purged daily (flows 120 days, alerts 180).
- **Built: transit money.** The AH and notes only count their difference (sales tax, staff notes), so the sink ratio is honest.
- **Built: inflation badge** from the 14-day sink ratio (removed divided by created): **OK** at 75% or more, **Watch** below 75%, **ACT** below 60% on 3 days in a row (staff get an alert once a day), "not enough data" before 3 days. The season target is 80% or more (C14).
- **Built: alerts** (all thresholds in `economy-watch.yml`, tune after the first real week, 624):

| Rule | Fires when | Default |
|---|---|---|
| earn-rate | a profile earns more in an hour than 3x the 14-day average | 3x, at least $5M |
| big-transfer | one pay or trade of 0.5% of the supply, or one huge amount created | 0.5%, at least $50M |
| repeat-transfer | same sender and receiver, near-equal amounts | 5 in 10 min, within 5%, from $10k |
| note-redeem | one profile cashes many notes in an hour | 10 |
| sell-spike | the server sells much more than usual | 4x hourly average, at least $50M |
| balance-mismatch | a balance changed without a ledger row (money from nowhere) | on |
| staff-transfer (334) | a pay or trade to or from a staff account, shown to admins | $5M |
| sink-ratio | the badge says ACT | once a day |

- **Alert flow:** a chat line to online staff with [Open] and [Punish], the console, the audit log, and Discord later. The same rule and profile alerts at most once per 60 min. The evidence screen has Open /p, Suggest punishment, Mark reviewed, False alarm and Open again.
- **Built: `/eco give|take|set|reset|note`** (admin and owner, 47). A reason is required, every use is logged and shown to online staff and the player. The reason appears in the player's `/transactions` (663).
- **Planned (decided): `/eco` limit (R3-6).** An owner or admin change up to $1T applies at once and is flagged. Above $1T it waits until the owner or an admin clicks **Allow** in the alert. This is the developer's reading of the owner's words ("maximum 1 trillion and then click in alert to allow"), see question 1. Today there is no ceiling.
- **Planned (decided): 24 h trade block (R3-28).** When the system finds a duplicated unique item, its holders can't use `/pay`, notes, AH or `/trade` for 24 h. It ends on its own. Staff decide after and can upgrade it to a freeze. A duped money note already can't make money (the second cash-in is refused).
- **Planned (decided): staff play accounts (R3-39).** Admins, builders and the owner play on a separate account. Mods and helpers keep one. `/eco` can't target your own account or a same-IP account.
- **Planned (decided): gem watch (REVIEW3 F4).** Every gem transfer is logged and the gem alert thresholds are lowered so big moves are seen.

## What the player sees
- Players see nothing of the dashboard. A player whose balance staff changed sees "Staff changed your Money. Now ... (see /transactions)".
- Staff see the evidence rows, the profile's history and the suggested punishment before anything is applied.

## Rules and limits
- Alerts never punish. `alerts.auto-suggest-rules` is empty, so staff must press Suggest and then Apply. The only automatic step is the 24 h trade block above, and it ends on its own.
- Helpers see no dashboard and no alerts. Only admins see staff-transfer alerts.
- Performance (principle 9): no per-tick work; the job reads small indexed windows; dashboard numbers are reused for 30 s.
- Bedrock: staff tool, chests and clicks only.
