# Ranks and perks · id: F2 · status: Draft · version: 1

## In one sentence
A free rank for everyone, 7 permanent bought ranks and 3 ranks won for one season. Bought ranks give looks and convenience only. Gameplay perks are never sold: you earn them by playing.

## How it works
- **Built:**
  - Staff ranks: Builder, Helper, Moderator, Admin, Owner (LuckPerms groups with weights, colors, prefixes). Only the owner hands them out (391). No Trainee (390).
  - Free rank: `/freerank` after 30 minutes of total playtime (`perks.yml: freerank.min-playtime-minutes`). Gives the group `ember` (prefix `[Ember]`, color #E0A070, weight 1) and 1 of 3 tags (ember, spark, ash). Once per account, kept forever.
  - Perk commands exist as permission nodes that no donor rank has yet: `/craft`, `/ec`, `/hat`, `/nick` (also `.color`, `.hex`). `/trash` is admin and owner only (376). Nodes for AH slots, loadouts, profile slots and island fly also wait for donor groups.
- **Planned (decided): the rank line-up.**
  - Free rank renamed **Ashborn** (RTA-3). "Ember" stays a crate name. Perks: name color, 1 of 3 tags, lockdown bypass, +1 AH slot (plan default).
  - **7 bought ranks** (RTA-2): Hatchling, Fledgling, Firewing, Flamecrest, Sunfeather, Pyrelord, Immortal. EUR 5 / 10 / 20 / 30 / 45 / 65 / 100 (RTA-7). Account-wide, every profile, never expire, kept every season (RTA-6, 387). An upgrade pays the difference (388).
  - **3 won ranks** (RTA-12), held for one season (387): Torchbearer (top 4-10 islands), Flameheart (top 2-3 and board #1s), Firecrown (#1 solo and #1 team island). They copy the perks of Fledgling, Flamecrest and Pyrelord (RTA-8). Need 10 h played that season and 7 days on the island (RTA-9). Winners also keep a title like `Firecrown · S1` (plan default).
  - Bought and won together: each perk uses the better of the two, no stacking.
- **Planned (decided): the perk model (REVIEW3 F7, R3-7, R3-8).**
  - Every perk has one tag: **Cosmetic**, **Convenience**, **Access** or **Gameplay** (REVIEW3 §4.5, proposed default).
  - A bought rank unlocks its Cosmetic and Convenience perks at once. Gameplay perks (sell %, skill XP %, fuel or keys in kits) are never sold, so **bought ranks give 0%**.
  - Everyone can earn the same Cosmetic and Convenience perks along one progression track, like a battle pass, not by hours played. See F1. That plan is not written. Do not build it until it is locked.
  - Won ranks are not sold, so they keep their gameplay bonuses and monthly keys (R3-7, R3-8). Bonuses copy the tier: Torchbearer +4% sell and +2% skill XP, Flameheart +8% and +4%, Firecrown +12% and +8% (plan table).
  - Bought ranks get one monthly cosmetic instead of crate keys (R3-8).
  - Earn paths that already exist: island fly from the Wings mastery, up to +2 AH slots from Economy achievements (F3). The weekly kit loses fuel (proposed default).

## What the player sees
- `/freerank`: "Play 30 min first. <time> left." Then "You're now Ember! Pick your tag." and the menu "Pick your tag". Later "You already claimed your free rank."
- A colored prefix in chat and tab: `[Ember]` today, `[Hatchling]` and so on later. Donors use normal brackets, staff use bold caps (`MOD`, `ADMIN`) (plan default).
- `/nick`, `/craft`, `/ec`, `/hat` work only with the permission. Nicknames show a `~` in front.
- Planned: `/menu` → Rank rewards (weekly kit and won-rank keys, one click, no `/kit`), and exact rank numbers on the store page and `/help ranks`. The progression track (F1) shows how to earn each perk.

## Rules and limits
- **Cap (plan RK-CAP):** a rank never gives more than +15% sell and +10% skill XP (won ranks only), and nothing else that changes money, drops, crystals, gems, island progress or combat. No island or pillar XP from ranks (RTA-20).
- **Mojang (principle 8):** bought ranks sell only looks and convenience, which fits the rules. The check before the store opens still stands (RTA-1, M11.1). It is a blocker for other store items such as gems and island size.
- Bonuses go through the bonus system, so the +300% total sell cap holds (O11).
- `/ec` and `/craft` are blocked in the warzone, boss arena and 10 s after combat (377). Nicknames: 3-16 letters, reserved words blocked, 60 s between changes, reset when the rank is lost (built).
- Rank is account-wide (PR3). Vault contents and kits are per profile. Nothing moves between profiles (P4).
- Chargeback or refund (REVIEW3 F3): remove the rank, flag the account for staff (RK15) and freeze its gem transfers. Won ranks need an admin to approve the winners list (plan default).
- Bedrock: prefixes are 10 characters or less, rank menus are click-only. Performance: perks are permission nodes read when used, no scans.

## Decided
- REVIEW3 F7 (final): perks unlock along one progression track, not by hours played. A bought rank unlocks its cosmetic and convenience perks at once. Gameplay perks are never sold.
- R3-7 (owner): "all ranks have x perks, and of default the permissions for ranks are possible to be gained by playing the game and they are not immediately given, but some are". This replaces the paid sell and skill XP bonus (RTA-14, RTA-16, 389).
- R3-8: bought ranks give a monthly cosmetic, not crate keys. Won season ranks keep monthly keys.
- REVIEW3 F3: chargebacks remove the rank, flag the account and freeze its gem transfers.
- RW4: `/freerank` is the lowest rank: name color, a few small perks, 1 of 3 tags, forever.
- 386: 7 ranks with themed names. RTA-2: the phoenix names above. RTA-3: free rank renamed Ashborn. 390: no Trainee.
- 387: bought ranks are permanent, won ranks last one season. 388: an upgrade pays only the difference.
- RTA-6: bought ranks are account-wide, work on every profile, never expire. RTA-7: prices EUR 5 to 100.
- RTA-8: a won rank gives all perks of the tier it copies. RTA-9: 10 h played and 7 days on the island. RTA-12: three won ranks.
- RTA-17: monthly keys claimable after 5 h played that month (now only for won ranks, R3-8).
- RTA-18 (and 243): island fly from Flamecrest. RTA-20: no island or pillar XP from ranks. RTA-1: check Mojang's rules before the store opens.
- 376: `/trash` is admin and owner only. 377: `/ec` and `/craft` blocked in combat places. 391: only the owner hands out staff ranks. 460: free and donor ranks skip lockdown.
- PR3: a rank works on every profile. PR5: profile slots 2 and 3 are free with a rank. 120: a profile is locked if its rank is lost.

## Open questions
1. The track plan (F1): which perk unlocks on which step, and is there a `/perks` page (a new command, needs your OK)? Default: place each perk on a step with the F1 card.
2. Exact perk numbers per rank: AH slots 6 to 15 in the plan, but REVIEW3 §4.5 shows only +1 to +4. `/pv` vaults 1 to 4 is a new command. What is the monthly cosmetic? Default: the table above, one cosmetic a month from a list you write, 5 h played.
3. Are the won-rank bonuses right (Torchbearer +4/+2, Flameheart +8/+4, Firecrown +12/+8)? Default: yes, they copy the tier.
4. Should Ashborn come after the tutorial (REVIEW3 §4.5) instead of 30 minutes played (built)? Default: as built.
5. Keep the LuckPerms group id `ember` after the rename? Old names (Squire to Monarch, Knight, Warlord) are still in DESIGN §11, TEST_GUIDE, permission comments and RESOURCE_PACK_ART_BRIEF §N. Default: keep the id, update the texts.

## Rejected ideas
- Paid sell % and skill XP % on bought ranks (RTA-14, RTA-16, 389): replaced by R3-7, because gameplay perks are never sold.
- Monthly crate keys for bought ranks (RTA-17): keys from a paid rank count as paid loot boxes (R3-8).
- Perks unlocked by hours played (REVIEW3 F7).
- Names Squire, Knight, Paladin, Warlord, Monarch (386): not themed enough, and only 5 ranks.
- A Trainee staff rank (390). `/trash` as a donor perk (376).
- A rank boost on pillar or island XP (RTA-20): one donor should not push a whole island.
