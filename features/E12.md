# Enchants · id: E12 · status: Draft · version: 1

## In one sentence
Custom enchants are found as unique books, put into the perk slots of custom tools and armor at the Enchanter, and raised with crystals. There are 48 in the config. The 38 for gathering, swords, stacked mobs and armor work today; the 10 for hoes and rods wait for Phase 7.

## How it works
- **Built:**
  - **48 enchants** in `enchants.yml`: shared gathering 9, pickaxe 4, axe 4, hoe 5, sword 12 (8 for the Depths, 4 for stacked island mobs), rod 5, armor 9. Rarity: 7 common, 10 uncommon, 14 rare, 8 epic, 3 legendary, 6 mythic. Each can be switched off with `enabled: false`.
  - **Slots:** custom tools only (vanilla gear keeps vanilla enchanting). Perk slots open at tool Lv 5, 15, 30, 45; slot 5 comes from prestige and takes Mythics only. Dragonfire Blast and Wildfire use 2 slots. A tool holds one enchant per conflict group (mine area, forest area, burst, hoe burst, mythic).
  - **Level gate:** `ceil(max level x tool level / 50)`; a prestiged tool counts as 50.
  - **Books:** unique items (one database row each, tradeable, AH category "Enchant books", never sold for gems). Sources: Depths drops, mini-boss drops, the Book shop and crates.
    - Drop chance per accepted action, by tier band (1-3, 4-6, 7-9, 10-12): ore block or log 1 in 2,000 / 3,000 / 4,000 / 5,000; combat kill 1 in 300 / 350 / 400 / 450. Only with that wing's custom tool (#857). Only Depths blocks, logs and kills roll.
    - Rarity odds rise by band (band A 70% common, 4% rare; band D 8% legendary). Each enchant has a lowest tier it drops from.
    - Fast start: your 100th action in an activity gives a Common book and your 1,000th an Uncommon (once per account per season). Pity: a book is guaranteed after 5,000 to 12,500 blocks or 750 to 1,125 kills.
    - Island stack books: 1 in 25,000 stacked mobs killed with a custom sword, max 5 a day per profile; only Slaughter, Soul Reaper, Bloodlust, Plunder (#874).
  - **Enchanter** (`/enchanter`): Apply a book, Upgrade (crystals, or a book of the same level for 25%), Combine two equal books for one level up (25%), Remove, Book shop, Guide, Armor. No fail chance. Remove returns the book if you use a Disenchant Scroll, otherwise the enchant shatters and refunds 20% of the crystals put in.
  - **Crystal cost** of a level: `100 x 1.8^(step-1)` over 10 steps, times a rarity factor 0.5 to 4. Apply fee = level I cost or 25% of the book level's cost.
  - **Book shop rolls** (crystals 500 / 4,000 / 25,000; need tier 1 / 4 / 7 in that wing; 5 / 5 / 3 a day; never Mythic). Disenchant Scroll costs 2,500 crystals.
  - **Family caps** (each family adds up from enchants, sets, masteries, then the cap applies once): crystals 200%, drops 200%, speed 200%, skill XP 300%, tool XP 100%, rare finds 150%, area 100%, mob damage 200%, damage reduction 60%, extra hearts 10. Sell uses the economy's +300%.
  - **Armor:** 1 slot per set piece, a 2nd at set Lv 25, a Mythic chestplate slot at Lv 50. Vanilla armor never.
  - **Warzone and duels:** each enchant has a `pvp-percent` (damage enchants 40%, healing 50%, area and movement off).
- **Planned:**
  - Hoe and rod enchants (10): defined, no drops or effects until farming and fishing exist (Phase 7, queue item E6).
  - Staff `/enchanter admin give|apply|clear` (EN20): needs owner approval.
  - **Soul Reaper (R3-24):** its "+Combat XP from stacked kills" needs a new effect, because stacked island kills will no longer pay Combat XP (E9).
  - **Bound keys (R3-2):** books won from a bound crate key are bound too. Needs the bound-item system.
  - Mythics from world bosses (3%) and the weekly Black Market book: Phase 8 and later. Crates already list a Mythic at 2% in the Eclipse crate.

## What the player sees
- Book drop line in chat with the rarity colour, a sound, and a "next guaranteed book" count in the guide.
- Tool lore shows each enchant with a short value, for example "+12% speed" (#883).
- `/enchanter`: chest menus with a tool picker, slot picker and a clear message when a book does not fit (conflict, tool level, wrong tool, 2 slots, prestige slot only).
- The guide lists every enchant by tool; Mythics are secret until found.

## Rules and limits
- Anti-abuse: a copied book (row already used) is taken away on use and staff are told. Books cannot go into anvils, grindstones, smithing tables, crafting grids or enchanting tables. Every action is one database transaction; if items moved, it is undone and crystals come back.
- Crystals and daily rolls belong to the active profile. Each book records the profile it was made in; the auction house refuses buys between your own profiles. The guide's collection is per account and season.
- Books, scrolls and crystals are never sold for gems or real money (no pay-to-win). Crystals can never be transferred (R3-5b).
- Performance: one random roll and a few array updates per action; counters are saved in batches; stacked-mob enchants log a warning above 5 ms of main-thread time per second.
- Bedrock: click-only menus, names up to 16 characters, Roman numerals.
