# Island size · id: B15 · status: Draft · version: 1

## In one sentence
Islands start small (3 x 3 chunks) and grow to 9 x 9 by upgrades; sizes up to 15 x 15 are a paid extra that lasts one season.

## How it works
- **Built: the size mechanics.** Start width 3 chunks (48 x 48 blocks). The border is per player and updates at once when the size changes. `IslandService.setSize` is the hook. Normal upgrades stop at 9 x 9 (`size.normal-max-chunks` 9). The hard cap is 15 x 15 (`size.max-chunks` 15 = 240 x 240). A grid slot is 512 blocks, so even 31 x 31 would fit.
- **Built: staff.** `/isadmin setsize <island> <size>` sets an odd width from 1 up to 15.
- **Planned (M5.4): Island Size upgrade**, 3 levels: 5 x 5, 7 x 7, 9 x 9. Task tiers 1, 3, 5. Price $3.4k up to $157k ($183k in all). Targets: 5 x 5 after about 45 min, 7 x 7 about day 1, 9 x 9 on day 2 to 5 for active players. Paid from the island bank. Needs the Upgrades right (B14).
- **Planned (decided, M5.6): sizes above 9 x 9.** 11 x 11, 13 x 13, 15 x 15, one step at a time, only after 9 x 9. Both ways are allowed (R3-19, REVIEW3 F6): shards (an "Island Charter" in the Black Market, expensive) or gems, bought or earned. The earlier plan had gems 250, 500, 750 and shards 150, 300, 450. A warning before buying: islands are wiped at season reset, so the size is lost then. These sizes give no island level points.
- **Planned: staff grant** of a paid size, and an audit line `progression/paid_size`. The gem shop and Black Market screens come with the rewards and store phases.

## What the player sees
- Island info shows the size in chunks. The border grows in front of you when you buy.
- (Planned) A confirm screen: "Buy Island Size II for $X from the island bank? Bank after: $Y."
- (Planned) For paid sizes: a gem price line, a "Black Market" note and the season-reset warning.

## Rules and limits
- Cross-border rules (B2) hold for every size. Explosions, liquids and growth never cross the border.
- Bigger islands cost more server work (more blocks, more loaded chunks). With no block or hopper limits (O1, O2), size is the main lever for space, so sizes bought with gems touch the Mojang rule: nothing sold may give a competitive gameplay advantage (principle 8). The owner chose to allow it; the current Mojang text must be read before the store opens (M11.1, a hard blocker). See open question 1.
- Gems are spent atomically through the economy service with a gem ledger line.
- Disband and reset clear the full max-size area plus one ring of chunks, so a big island leaves no leftovers.
- Bedrock: menus and confirm screens only.

## Open questions
1. Mojang's commercial usage guidelines: is a size bought with gems a "competitive gameplay advantage"? Default: keep as decided, check the current Mojang text before the store opens (M11.1) and fall back to shards only if it is a risk.
2. The owner called the shard price "expensive". Are 150, 300, 450 shards and 250, 500, 750 gems still right, or higher? Default: raise the shard prices after the economy model is rerun `(proposed default)`.
3. The Island Charter is an item. Is it tradeable (a way to move value between profiles, principle 2)? Default: bound to the buyer's profile and used on that profile's island `(proposed default)`.

## Rejected ideas
- Normal upgrades past 9 x 9 (157).
- Level points from bought sizes (P5-16, REVIEW A1).
- Shards only for big sizes (R3-19 suggestion): the owner wants gems as well.
