# Events · id: I3 · status: Draft · version: 1

## In one sentence
One event engine runs the server's scheduled moments (KOTH, envoys, automatic events, island competitions, chat games, fishing contests) with a countdown, a board at the Gatehouse and an `/events` menu.

## How it works
No event code exists yet. PHASE-9-EVENTS.md is an outline; the full plan comes after the owner answers. Everything below is from that outline and is a proposed default unless listed under Decided.
- **Built, waiting for events:**
  - The **Conqueror Crate** exists but is hidden until KOTH (`crates.yml: conqueror`, pity 20, 12 rewards, Conqueror Aegis set token on top).
  - The **Conquest pillar** exists but is hidden (`pillars.yml: conquest.enabled: false`).
  - The Conquest sets are locked as "Arrives with the Warzone".
  - The events portal at the Gatehouse stays closed.
- **Event engine** (M9.1, proposed default): one scheduler for all events with times in config (Europe/Oslo), boss-bar countdowns, an event board at the Gatehouse, an `/events` menu with what is next and the rewards. Never two big events at once. Phase 8 plans a small `EventSchedule` in `common` that this engine reuses.
- **KOTH** (M9.3, proposed default): 2-3 a day, 10 minute capture in the keep's courtyard (a 15 x 15 square with 4 entrances). Rewards: Conqueror key, shards and Conquest XP to the capturer's island. This unhides the Conquest pillar.
- **Envoys** (M9.4, proposed default): every 2 hours, 20-30 crates dropped on marked spots across the warzone field, with loot tiers.
- **Automatic events** (M9.6, proposed default): treasure hunt (40 hiding spots), money event (sell bonus), farming event, mining rush, double XP. Each 15-30 minutes, safe for islands, no gear needed for most.
- **Island competitions** (EV1): weekly, the category rotates (most crops, most mined, most mobs, most sold), top 3 get keys.
- **Fishing contests** (EV2): every 4 hours, 15 minutes (needs fishing, D8).
- **Chat games** (EV3): every 20 minutes, unscramble or math, $5k for the winner.
- **Final Week** of a season: double Conquest XP and extra KOTHs (I5).

## What the player sees
- Gatehouse event board: next KOTH, envoy, boss and event times.
- Planned: `/events` menu, boss-bar countdown, chat line like "KOTH starts in 10 minutes at the warzone." with the `» EVENT` tag (CHAT_BROADCASTS.md), and Discord announcements (I6).
- Teleports into events take 3 s. Exact menu and message texts are not written yet.

## Rules and limits
- Islands are always safe. Only KOTH and envoys are in the PvP world.
- Teleports in events take 3 s (345). No drop parties and no build contests (DESIGN §10).
- Event income is part of the ~15% "events, quests, crates" share of income. It must not become the main income (ECONOMY_BALANCE).
- Anti-abuse is not written for events yet. Boss fights use one reward per account, max 3 accounts per IP and no AFK rewards (I4). Events follow the same rules (proposed default). Per-IP rules need each Bedrock player's real IP, which Floodgate must pass (REVIEW3 §3, to check).
- Bedrock: board, menu and countdown are menus and boss bars. No chat clicks.
- Performance: one scheduler, no per-tick scans. Capture progress is a hologram and particles near the keep only.
