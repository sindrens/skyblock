# Generators and crops · id: D6 · status: Draft · version: 1

## In one sentence
Cobble generators on your island make better ores as you upgrade them, and 11 crop tiers grow even without light, so the island earns money by hand and, with fuel, while you are away.

## How it works
- **Built: crops grow without light (S25).** Planted crops are remembered per chunk. Every 30 s each dark crop has a 15% chance to grow one stage, about vanilla speed on watered farmland. Covered: wheat, carrots, potatoes, beetroots, melon and pumpkin stems, sweet berries, torchflower. Lit crops grow the vanilla way. (stacking.yml: crops.*)
- **Built: prices.** Generator drops sell for cobblestone $1, coal $10, raw copper $14, raw iron $18, raw gold $25, redstone $33, lapis $45, diamond $61 (emerald $82 is switched off for now). Crops sell from wheat $10 to pitcher plant $870. Island items pay half of the Depths item of the same tier. (sell.yml)
- **Built: stats.** Blocks made by cobble, stone or basalt generators count as island "generator output". Ripe crops a member breaks give Farming pillar XP. Today the generator still forms plain vanilla blocks.
- **Planned (Phase 7, M7.2): custom generator.** A cobble generator forms a block from the island's Generator level table (0 to 10, upgrade `Generator`). Level 0 is 100% cobble. Level 10 is 40% cobble, 6% each of coal, copper, iron, gold, redstone, lapis, diamond, emerald, 7% Ashstone ($180), 5% Emberite ($396). Ashstone and Emberite are the two custom ores (names are proposals). Basalt generators stay vanilla.
- **Planned: ores are mined by hand.** No drills in Phase 7. They give no Mining skill XP, Mining pillar XP at 25% of the Depths ore, and books at 25%. Area enchants like Vein Rend do not break real island blocks.
- **Planned (M7.3): 11 crop tiers** by Farming level: wheat 0, carrot and potato 5, beetroot 10, sugar cane 15, melon and pumpkin 20, cocoa 25, cactus 30, sweet berries 35, nether wart 42, torchflower 50, pitcher plant 58.
- **Planned: growth engine** takes over the dark-crop code for all tiers. Speed = vanilla x Crop Growth upgrade (+12% per level) x the crop growth family (cap +100%). Harvest Yield upgrade: +12% double-crop chance step per level.
- **Planned: Farming skill XP** only for ripe crops broken with a hoe. Bone-mealed crops give drops but no XP, set XP or books. Custom hoes roll Farming books.
- **Planned (M7.4): harvester hoe** from the Forge, 11 tiers. Right-click a ripe crop to harvest the area and replant it: 3x3 from tool level 1, 5x5 at level 15, 7x7 at level 30 (Wide Reap up to 9x9). Each tier gives +5% crop drops.
- **Planned:** Golden crop from the Golden Seed enchant (E6). Fully custom crop models wait for the resource pack.

## What the player sees
- Today: crops grow in the dark. `/skills` shows Farming as "Coming soon". The Forge has only the basic stone hoe.
- Planned: a locked seed is refused with "Farming 15 needed: 9/15". `/is production` has Generator and Crops tabs (crop tiers with a check or a cross). Forge Hoe tab.
- Planned: generator ores drop normal items (coal, raw iron) or the two custom items.

## Rules and limits
- Anti-abuse: bone-mealed crops, placed-then-broken blocks and coop players give no XP. The generator makes at most 40 custom rolls per second per island, above that it forms plain cobble. (generators.yml: max-rolls-per-second)
- Plan rule (PD36, not yet answered): production never beats The Depths. Mining your own generator pays under 25% of an active Depths hour, whole island output under 40%.
- Performance: generators work per form event. Crops grow in one budgeted task over the crop index, loaded chunks only (2,000 crops per tick at most). No per-tick scans. Offline islands are simulated (D7).
- Bedrock: hoe harvest is a right-click, upgrades are menus.

## Rejected ideas
- Drills or auto-miners for generator ores in Phase 7 (plan default, question 2): generators stay active play.
- Area enchants that break real island blocks (plan default PD9): grief and lag risk.
