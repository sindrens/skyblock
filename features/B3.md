# Members, invites, coop · id: B3 · status: Draft · version: 1

## In one sentence
A leader can invite players to form a team island (the leader plus 4 members to start), manage their roles, and give temporary trust to helpers without a slot.

## How it works
- **Built: team size.** `members.start-slots` 5 = leader plus 4; Member Slots upgrades can raise it to 9 = leader plus 8 (`members.max-slots`). Slots are stored on the island (`member_slots`).
- **Built: invite.** `/is invite <player>` (Leader, Admin, Mod): the target must be online, have no island on their profile, and not ignore you. Valid 5 min, max 8 open invites. The target gets a clickable `[Accept] [Deny]`. `/is accept [player]`, `/is deny [player]`, `/is invites` (menu to answer or cancel).
- **Built: solo to team.** The first invite that is sent makes the island a Team Island forever. A solo leader first sees a confirm: "Inviting makes your island a Team Island forever. Solo and team islands are on separate leaderboards." Cancel keeps it solo. Kicking everyone does not turn it back.
- **Built: roles.** Leader, Admin, Mod, Member. `/is promote`, `/is demote` (Leader: all steps; Admin: Member and Mod). Only a higher role kicks (`/is kick`, confirm screen). `/is leave` (confirm). `/is transfer <player>` (Leader only, two confirm screens; old leader becomes Admin).
- **Built: coop.** `/is coop <player>`, `/is uncoop` (Leader, Admin, Mod). Trust lasts only while a Leader, Admin or Mod is online. Max 4. No slot, no XP, no task progress, no bank. Never turns solo into team.
- **Built: members menu.** `/is members`: heads with role, join date, inviter, online state; click a head for the actions you may use. Buttons: Invites, Invite, Permissions, Settings.
- **Built: safety.** A kick saves a backup (membership, plus money and items when the profile is online). Staff restore with `/isadmin restore`. A kick or a leave removes only the membership: the player keeps money, items and skills. XP a member earned stays with the island.
- **Built (to be removed): join limit.** A player may join at most 3 islands per season (`members.max-joins-per-season`). Founding an island is not a join.
- **Planned (decided): remove the join limit** (N2-1). The live config still has `max-joins-per-season: 3`; this is a fix in REVIEW3 section 3.
- **Planned (decided): 48 h slot lock.** A slot freed by a leave or a kick stays locked for 48 h before the island can invite into it. Example: limit 4, the 4th member leaves, no invite for 48 h. It shows in the members menu and in the invite error, for example "A slot opens in 31h 12m (Alex left)". All new texts go into `messages/islands.yml`. (REVIEW3 F10)
- **Planned (decided): kick preview and contribution.** Before a leader kick, a screen shows what it costs the island, for example "Kicking Alex removes 21 points (Lv 38 -> 36). Bought perks stay. Alex gets $120,000 back." The island keeps 50% of the kicked member's contribution; the kicked player keeps personal progress and gets 50% of their unspent bank deposits back. Leaving by choice keeps 100% for the island. Details in B4. (REVIEW3 F9, F10)
- **Planned (decided): no leader kicks in the last 14 days of a season.** Staff still can. (REVIEW3 F11)
- **Planned:** Member Slots upgrade, 4 steps of +1 member (first after about 3 h; tasks tier 2, 4, 6, 8; $7.0k up to $2.2M), in B14.

## What the player sees
- "Your island is now a Team Island. It's on the team leaderboard from now on."
- "Your island is full (5 members)." when no slot is free.
- "<player> invited you to <island>. (3/5 members)" with Accept and Deny buttons.
- Online members are told about every join, leave, kick, promotion, transfer and coop change.
- (Planned) The 48 h lock line and the kick preview described above.

## Rules and limits
- One island per profile. Two of your own profiles can never share an island or be coop (P3, 119).
- An invite is accepted only on a profile without an island; the message hints to switch profile (133).
- Accept checks join limit, free slot and "no island yet" in one database transaction.
- A player who `/ignore`s you gets the same answer as "requests off", so ignore stays private.
- Staff can block a player from joining or creating islands (`/p` ladders); logged `island_join_block`.
- Staff kicks, bans and members inactive for 21 days or more do not cost the island any contribution (REVIEW3 section 4.2, accepted with F9).
- Everything is a menu or a clickable chat button, so Bedrock never needs long commands.

## Open questions
1. Which slot does the 48 h lock cover? Default: only the freed slot, so a half-empty island can still invite into its other free slots; staff kicks and `/isadmin restore` do not lock `(proposed default)`.
2. The live `islands.yml` also has `join-cooldown-hours: 24` (ignored by the code). Answers 121/161/258/259 say no cooldown at all. REVIEW3 asks to confirm. Default: no cooldown; the 48 h slot lock is the brake.
3. 141 says leaving wipes the profile; today leaving keeps everything and the owner's R3-12 answer is about the island's contribution. Default: leaving keeps personal progress.
4. Menus show "3/5" because the leader counts. Show "2/4 + leader" instead? Default: 3/5.

## Rejected ideas
- 24 h join cooldown and 24 h disband block (141, 258).
- Personal member homes (247).
- Counting coop players for XP, level or bank (REVIEW A4).
- Wiping a kicked member's money, items or skills (R3-12, SECFIX1): a kick removes only the membership.
