# Store and Discord · id: I6 · status: Draft · version: 1

## In one sentence
Players buy gems and ranks on the Tebex store, spend gems on cosmetics and convenience, and link their Discord account for announcements, tickets and appeals. Gameplay power is never sold.

## How it works
The plan is an outline (PHASE-11-STORE-DISCORD.md) plus the ranks plan. Only the groundwork is built.
- **Built:**
  - `/store`, `/discord` and `/vote` send clickable links (store.skybornmc.net, discord.skybornmc.net), with buttons in `/menu`.
  - Gems exist as an account-wide currency (one balance per player). Wardrobe sets can be bought with gems. Extra loadout slots are bought with "store credits" (`loadout.bought-max`).
  - `/freerank`, LuckPerms ranks, and a Discord webhook sink for punishments, appeals, reports, raid and economy alerts. It is **off** until Discord is set up (LATER.md).
  - Store tags already arrive as items that can be traded.
- **Planned (decided): two gem balances** (REVIEW3 F3). Gems bought in the store are **tradeable** (`/pay`, `/trade`). Gems earned in play (votes, streaks, prizes, thanks) are **bound** to the account. Crystals can never move. There are no sending limits (F4), but every gem transfer is logged and the economy-watch gem thresholds are lowered so big moves are seen.
- **Planned (decided): unclaimed items** (REVIEW3 F5, R3-14). Things bought with gems (skins, tags, sets, cosmetics) arrive **unclaimed**. Unclaimed, they can be sold or traded anywhere (AH, `/trade`, drop). Right-click claims one: it is added to the account and becomes bound.
- **Planned (decided): gem sets are looks only** (R3-20), worn over an earned set. Their bonus versions move to crates and crystals. Island sizes above 9x9 are sold for shards (Black Market, expensive) or gems (R3-19, REVIEW3 F6).
- **Planned (decided): ranks.** 7 bought ranks (Hatchling, Fledgling, Firewing, Flamecrest, Sunfeather, Pyrelord, Immortal), 5 to 100 EUR, account-wide, permanent, upgrades pay the difference.
  - A bought rank unlocks its cosmetic and convenience perks at once. Gameplay perks (sell %, skill XP %, kit fuel) are never sold (REVIEW3 F7, R3-7).
  - Bought ranks give a monthly cosmetic, not keys (R3-8). Won ranks keep their monthly keys and bonuses.
  - All perks sit on one progression track from start to end game, like a battle pass. This needs its own plan and is not built until it is locked.
- **Planned (decided): premium pass** is cosmetic only, with no extra reroll (R3-44). 1,000 credits per season.
- **Tebex link and gem shop** (M11.1, M11.3, proposed default): purchases arrive safely (queued, idempotent, logged), also offline, and ranks are granted by a console command into LuckPerms plus an audit row. A `/gems` shop sells rank upgrades, cosmetics, skins, tags, profile slots, the pass, streak save and extra loadout and wardrobe slots.
- **Gem sources:** store, voting (3 per vote), day-7 login streak (10), thanks (max 20 per booster, R3-43) and "gg" after a store purchase (1 gem, max 5 a day).
- **Discord** (M11.6, proposed default), first: account linking with a small cosmetic reward (a tag), punishment and appeal webhooks, event and store announcements. Later: ticket bot, each player's local time.
- **Chargeback** (decided, REVIEW3 F3): remove the rank, flag the account (RK15) and freeze the account's gem transfers.

## What the player sees
- Today: `/store` and `/discord` print a link; the menu buttons do the same. Planned (proposed default): a `/gems` menu, "Steve bought Firewing!" in chat, "Right-click to claim" on unclaimed items, a Discord link flow (not designed).

## Rules and limits
- Mojang's test is whether something sold is designed to give a **competitive gameplay advantage** (principle 8). Two risks are logged:
  - bought gems that can be traded for in-game money are a real-money to in-game-money path;
  - paid island sizes above 9x9 are paid progress.
- Hard blocker: read Mojang's current full commercial terms before the store opens (M11.1, RTA-1). Nothing in the repo quotes them yet.
- Keys are earned only, never sold (DESIGN §9). Gems never buy keys, upgrade levels, crystals or set XP.
- ECONOMY_BALANCE §9.2 still says gems are not tradeable and need updating.
- Webhook URLs are secrets in `secrets/discord.yml`. Sends are queued with retry and never delay a punishment.
- Bedrock: the gem shop and claiming are menus and plain chat, no clicks needed. Performance: store polling and webhooks run in the background.

## Open questions
1. Is "credits" dropped as a separate thing, so the store sells gems only (two balances: bought and earned)? Default: yes.
2. Which gem balance is spent first, earned (bound) or bought (tradeable)? No answer yet. Default: earned first, so bought gems keep their trade value (proposed).
3. Which perks sit on which step of the progression track, and which a bought rank unlocks at once? No default: needs its own plan.
4. Does a chargeback also remove gem items already bought and ban until settled (M11.7), or only the decided steps (rank, flag, freeze)? Default: only the decided steps; staff review.
5. Discord first: linking, punishment and appeal webhooks, event announcements, then the ticket bot; a tag for linking? Default: yes.

## Rejected ideas
- Selling crate keys: "keys are earned only" (DESIGN §9, 2026-10-29).
- Paid sell % or skill XP % on bought ranks, and monthly keys from bought ranks (R3-7, R3-8, REVIEW3 F7).
- Limits on sending gems (F4: owner said no).
