# Island bank · id: B11 · status: Draft · version: 1

## In one sentence
The island has one shared bank that the team pays into, that earns interest, that pays for island upgrades, and where the leader decides which role may take money out.

## How it works
- **Where:** `/bank` shows your personal bank and the island bank side by side. Also the Island menu "Island bank" button and the Banker NPC. The personal bank is C8.
- **Deposit and withdraw:** number picker, then a confirm screen for withdrawals (shows amount, fee and what you get). A deposit bigger than the free room deposits what fits and says so (600).
- **Capacity and interest cap** come from the Bank Capacity and Bank Interest upgrade levels 0 to 10 (`bank.yml: island.levels`). Level 0 is the start.

| Level | 0 | 1 | 2 | 3 | 4 | 5 | 10 |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| Capacity | $1M | $4M | $16M | $64M | $256M | $1.02B | $1.05T |
| Payout cap | $10k | $30k | $90k | $270k | $810k | $2.43M | $590M |

- **Interest:** 1.0% per payout, every 8 h at fixed times (00:00, 08:00, 16:00 UTC), also offline. Paid on the lowest balance during the period, up to the payout cap. It needs at least one member with 2 h played in the last 7 days. Each payout is one ledger transaction with an idempotency key, so restarts never pay twice.
- **Built today: withdrawal fee** is 2% (rounded up, leaves the economy), the current period's interest is lost, and the first 10 minutes after a payout are free.
- **Planned (decided): fee only.** The only rule is the 2% fee (lower with the Treasury mastery: 1.5%, 1.0%, 0.5%, see B14). Remove the free 10 minutes (`free-minutes` to 0 and out of the menu text) and the "a fee withdrawal forfeits the period's interest" rule. (R3-41, BANK_UX Q1 and Q2)
- **Planned (decided): interest activity.** Minutes spent in the AFK pool do not count toward the 2 h in 7 days rule. Other idle time still counts. (R3-25)
- **Role rights** (matrix rows): Deposit (Admin, Mod, Member), Withdraw (Admin), History (Admin, Mod). The Leader always has all three. Coop and visitors never.
- **Planned (decided): withdraw is fully the leader's choice per role.** There is no 3-day rule for new members. Every withdrawal stays in the bank history and pings the leader. (R3-11, REVIEW3 F8)
- **Daily withdraw limits today:** server config as % of capacity: Leader and Admin none, Mod 25%, Member 10%. They matter only if the leader gives that role Withdraw.
- **Planned (decided): deposits stay open.** Sending money to the island you are on, including bank deposits, is always allowed, even before the profile has earned $10k itself. (REVIEW3 F1)
- **Upgrades are paid from the island bank only** (169): no 2% fee and no lost interest (P5-11). Planned with B14.
- **Disband or reset:** the bank money is deleted (263, 591) with a staff-restorable backup.

## What the player sees
- "Put $X in the island bank." / "Took $X out of the island bank, no fee." or "(fee $X at 2%, this period's interest is lost)" today.
- Online members see each deposit and withdrawal in chat.
- "That's over your daily island bank limit." / "Your island role can't do that. The Leader sets it in /is permissions."

## Rules and limits
- Deposits and withdrawals are one atomic database transaction; capacity, balance and the daily limit are checked inside it.
- Blocked while combat-tagged and in the warzone (567), for frozen players and staff in staff mode (604), and when a sanction forbids it (fails closed if the check breaks).
- Coop players never touch the bank (REVIEW A4). The history menu shows who paid in or out.
- Interest runs as a background job (every 5 min, batches of 200), never on the main thread.
- Bedrock: the number picker and confirm screens are menus; no typing.

## Decided
- 169 and E4: upgrades from the island bank only; interest every 8 h; fee 2% lowered by masteries.
- 263 and 591: bank money deleted on disband or reset, backed up for staff.
- 590: starting rights: deposit Admin, Mod, Member; withdraw Admin only; history Admin and Mod.
- 589: starting daily limits Leader and Admin none, Mod 25%, Member 10%.
- 597: interest needs 2 h played in 7 days.
- 604, 567: frozen, staff mode, combat and warzone blocked.
- P5-11: paying an upgrade has no fee. P5-19: Treasury mastery steps.
- R3-11 ("Other: it has to be a permission, the leader is the leader of the island and we try not to limit the players") and REVIEW3 F8 ("No" to the 3-day rule): withdraw is fully the leader's choice per role; keep the history and the leader ping.
- R3-41 ("Recommended"): 2% fee only, no free window, no forfeited interest.
- R3-25 ("Recommended"): AFK pool minutes don't count for the interest activity rule.
- REVIEW3 F1: "yes unless its to island members on the island they are on": bank deposits are always allowed.

## Open questions
1. Daily limits: stay as server config (589), or does the leader set the % per role? R3-11 noted Admin has no limit at all. Default: the leader sets the % per role in the matrix, starting from 589 `(proposed default)`.
2. BANK_UX still has unanswered ideas: two pages instead of side by side, all/half/amount buttons, an Upgrade button on the main page, a payout line in chat, `/bank deposit` and `/bank withdraw` commands. Default: all yes.
3. Staff tool `/eco bank <player|island>` to view and fix banks. Default: yes, admins only, logged.

## Rejected ideas
- Paying the island bank to the leader on disband (263).
- Paying upgrades from a member's own money (169).
- The free 10 minutes after each payout (588) and losing the period's interest on a fee withdrawal: dropped by R3-41.
- A 3-day waiting rule before a new member may withdraw (REVIEW3 F8).
