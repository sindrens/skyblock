# Island storage / vault · id: B12 · status: Draft · version: 1

## In one sentence
A shared storage chest for the whole island team (`/is storage`), that grows from 1 to 6 rows through the Island Vault upgrade.

## How it works
Nothing is built yet. The Island Vault is part of Phase 5 upgrades (M5.4). The plan below is `docs/planning/ISLAND_STORAGE.md`, which is still marked "needs owner input". Lines marked `(proposed default)` are from that plan and not yet approved.
- **One shared live chest per island.** Everyone who opens it sees the same contents at once. Changes are saved within 2 s and on shutdown, each with a log line. `(proposed default)`
- **Size:** 1 row at Vault level 0, +1 row per level up to 6 rows (54 slots) over 5 levels (I7, O4). PHASE-5 gates the levels by task tiers 1, 3, 5, 7, 9 (the storage plan says island level 1, 3, 5, 7, 9: pick one). Prices run from $2.0k to $7.2M ($7.9M in all).
- **Access:** two role switches in the matrix, "Storage: put in" (default Leader, Admin, Mod, Member) and "Storage: take out" (default Leader, Admin, Mod). Coop and visitors never. `(proposed default)`
- **Daily take-out limit:** Member 64 items a day, Mod 512, Admin and Leader none. Putting in is never limited. `(proposed default)`
- **What may go in:** stackable normal items and Depths materials. Not unique items (tools, books, set tokens), filled shulkers, bundles or money notes. `(proposed default)`
- **Blocked** while frozen, in combat or in staff mode (I8).
- **Log:** members with the right see who put in and took out for 7 days (I9). Staff recall: staff can return what a kicked member took in their last 7 days (decided, staff only).
- **Mastery deliveries:** deliver tasks in the challenge book can take items straight from storage (decided).
- **Planned (decided): the Forge counts as a take-out.** When the Forge takes materials from storage (891), it uses the same "take out" permission and the same daily limit. It is built together with storage. The limits must fit Forge amounts: a tier 10 tool needs 2,824 items. (R3-23)
- **Disband or reset:** contents are deleted with a staff backup, like the bank. `(proposed default)`

## What the player sees
- Island menu button "Island storage" (or `/is storage`) opening a chest-style menu, 1 to 6 rows.
- The role lines in the matrix; a log menu for those with the right.
- (Proposed) One Sort button. See open question 3.

## Rules and limits
- Crash safety is the main risk: a shared chest holding a team's wealth must never lose or copy items. Changes are one database transaction per move with an item log, and two members editing at once must see the same state.
- Hand-ins to tasks remove items and save progress in one step (the task engine already has a `TaskStorage` hook for this).
- No scans: the contents are loaded when someone opens the chest and cached while it is open.
- Bedrock: a normal chest menu; no typing.
- Principle 3: if an island is removed, the backup lets staff restore it.

## Decided
- Idea 6 and I7: `/is storage`, 1 row to 6 rows over 5 levels, role toggle, a log of who took what.
- O4: `/is storage` is the Island Vault upgrade.
- I8: no collector overflow; blocked while frozen or in combat.
- ST1-ST6: declined: no auto-collect filter, no search or sort, no sell, no deposit-all, no compress, no pages. The owner is open to other ideas.
- I9: leader logs include storage (7 days).
- Round 3 follow-up: (a) mastery delivery tasks take from storage, (b) a daily take-out limit per role, (c) recall of a kicked member's items is a staff tool only.
- 891: the Forge takes materials from inventory, satchel and storage.
- R3-23 ("Recommended"): the Forge taking from storage counts as taking out: same permission, same daily limit.

## Open questions
1. One shared live chest that everyone sees at once? Default: yes.
2. Limits: the plan says Member 64 a day, Mod 512, Admin and Leader none, but a tier 10 Forge job needs 2,824 items, so these numbers are too small now that the Forge counts. Raise them, or exempt Forge amounts for roles that may take out? Default: set limits per role so one big Forge job fits for Mod and up `(proposed default)`. Unique items, shulkers, bundles and notes stay out.
3. Q5 asked for a Sort button, but ST2 declined sort. One Sort button, or none? Default: one Sort button (as the plan says).
4. Any other idea for storage (the owner is open)? Default: none.

## Rejected ideas

- Auto-collect filter, search, sort, sell, deposit-all, compress and pages (ST1-ST6).
