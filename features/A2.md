# Spawn portal to island · id: A2 · status: Draft · version: 1

## In one sentence
The "Your Island" portal in the spawn Gatehouse is the way into the game: the first time it creates your island and sends you there, and every later time it takes you home.

## How it works
- **Where:** portal `to_island` of the `spawn_gatehouse_v1` build, about 95 blocks north of the spawn dais. Its target is `island` `(worlds.yml: portals.island-portal; empty = taken from the build metadata)`.
- **Walking in** is checked against your **active profile** only:

| Your profile has | What happens |
|---|---|
| An island (any role) | Instant teleport home (3 s wait only while combat-tagged) |
| No island | The island is created and you are sent there |
| An island that is still being built | "Your island is still being built. You'll be teleported when it's ready." |
| No islands world loaded | Refused with a message |

- **Creating** uses the normal `/is create` flow without the confirm menu:
  - Title "Creating your island..." and a short sound.
  - Staff join block is checked first (a team block also stops creating).
  - The island is saved in one database step (next free slot in the 512×512 grid, start size 3×3 chunks).
  - The starter island is pasted with FastAsyncWorldEdit (`islands.yml: creation.schematic`, timeout 30 s, then a plain placeholder islet). The starter chest is filled (A4).
  - You are teleported to the island home. Title "YOUR ISLAND / Rise from the ashes", sound, and "Welcome to your island! Your starter chest is right in front of you."
  - The worlds module then fires `IslandPortalEnteredEvent(firstTime)`. The tutorial uses it to finish step 1 (A3).
- **Pending invites:** if you have no island but have invites, the portal first opens a 3-row menu "Your island" with **Create my own island** or **Join <island>** (newest 3 invites). Joining teleports you to that team island `(islands.yml: members.portal-invite-choice)`.
- **Every profile** can use it: a new profile without an island gets one the same way. Typing `/is` without an island still opens the "Create your island" confirm screen.
- **No double use:** while the island is being built, portals ignore you for up to 15 s `(portals.island-wait-millis)`.
- **Planned (decided):** three starter islands (Ember Meadow, Frost Peak, Ash Dunes) with a preview (decisions I1, I2). Today every island is Ember Meadow, and how the player picks one is still open (question 162).

## What the player sees
- The portal hall: frame with moss and oak, a floating islet model above the arch, a hologram "Your Island", happy-villager particles.
- During the tutorial: flame trail on the road, boss bar "» Your Island · 92m «", flame column at the portal.
- Chat after arrival: "Next: Open your starter chest".

## Rules and limits
- No creation cooldown and no confirm screen (answers 121, 161, 258).
- One island per profile. The portal never makes a second island for the same profile.
- If the build fails, you get "Couldn't build your island. Try /is again soon, or tell staff." The island row is kept, and the next portal use or /is home rebuilds it. A database error before saving creates nothing.
- The portal's **Join** button follows the normal island join rules (B3). Planned (decided): the 3 joins per season limit goes away (N2-1; the live server still has `max-joins-per-season: 3` and a 24 h cooldown, REVIEW3 fix 4), and a slot freed by a leave or kick stays locked for 48 h (REVIEW3 F10).
- Bedrock: the invite choice is a chest menu, no chat clicks.
- No per-tick work: the portal check is one hash lookup when a player changes block in the spawn world.

## Decided
- 159: first thing on the server is a portal at spawn that creates the island; the portal always teleports a player to their island. The tutorial starts there.
- 121 / 161 / 258: no creation or join cooldowns.
- TUT-19: with a pending invite the portal asks "create my own island or join that island".
- N1-2: an invited new player can skip the portal and do the tutorial on the team island.
- 254: a player with an untouched starter island must disband it first before accepting an invite, unless they have an unused profile.
- I1 and I2: three starter islands (Ember Meadow, Frost Peak, Ash Dunes) with a preview. Only Ember Meadow exists so far.
- N2-1: remove the 3 island joins per season limit (not applied on the server yet).
- REVIEW3 F10: a slot freed by a leave or kick stays locked for 48 h before the island can invite into it.

## Open questions

2. Should the island preview (decision I2: 15 s spectator flight) be offered at the portal? Not built.
3. Is the portal behaviour as built OK for every profile, including a new profile on an old account? Default: yes (docs/OPEN_QUESTIONS.md, PORTALS).

## Rejected ideas

- A confirm screen at the portal: it is skipped on purpose.
