# Essentials and perk commands · id: G12 · status: Draft · version: 1

## In one sentence
Small convenience commands: `/craft`, `/ec`, `/hat` and `/nick` for ranked players, `/trash` and `/repair` for staff, and the staff teleport, game mode and inventory tools. The commands work; who gets them follows a perk plan that is not written yet.

## How it works
**Perk commands (module perks).** Each is a permission node. Right now only staff and ops have them; donor ranks get them when the ranks feature (F2) is built.
- Planned (decided): perks unlock along one progression track from start game to end game, like a battle pass, not by hours played. A bought rank unlocks its cosmetic and convenience perks at once. Gameplay perks are never sold (REVIEW3 F7). This needs its own plan; nothing is built until it is locked.
- `/craft` (`/workbench`, `/wb`): open a crafting table anywhere.
- `/ec` (`/enderchest`): open your own ender chest anywhere. It is the real ender chest of your active profile.
- `/hat`: wear the held item as a hat. Safe swap; Curse of Binding is respected (`hat.blocked` can ban items).
- `/nick <nickname|off>`: letters, numbers and `_`, 3-16 letters (colours do not count). Shown with `~` in chat and tab. Rules: 60 s between changes, no reserved words (admin, owner, staff, helper, phoenix, system and others), no other player's name, must be unique, must pass the chat filter (304).
  - Tiers by permission: plain, `&` colours (`.color`), hex and gradients (`.hex`). Only safe tags are allowed, no click or hover.
  - The nickname is removed when the rank that allowed it is lost; colours are dropped if only the colour permission is lost.
  - Staff: `/setnick <player> <nick|off>` (mod), `/realname <nickname>` (`/whonick`, helper).
- **Combat rule (377):** `/ec` and `/craft` do not work in the warzone, in a boss arena, or for 10 s after combat. This applies to everyone, staff too.
- `/trash` (`/disposal`): admins and owner only (376). A 4-row menu; anything left inside is destroyed on close and logged, with a warning if custom items were in it.
- `/repair [hand|all] [player]` (`/fix`, mod): vanilla durability only. `/sudo` (owner only): run a command or chat as a player, always audited.

**Staff essentials (module essentials).** All audited (category staff).
- Teleport: `/tp`, `/tphere`, `/tppos`, `/top`, `/back`, `/shome` (max 5 staff homes). Players use `/spawn` and `/tpa` (G11).
- Game mode and body: `/gm`, `/fly`, `/speed`, `/god`, `/heal`, `/feed`, `/clear`, `/invsee`, `/ecsee`, `/vanish`.
- Items and world: `/i`, `/give`, `/skull`, `/more`, `/time`, `/weather`, `/build`, `/setspawn`.
- Rules (331-334): creative only for builders and admins and only in spawn and the Depths (mods use spectator). God mode, `/fly` and `/heal` only in staff mode (`/s`) or build mode. Items from `/give`, `/i`, `/more`, `/skull` and the creative inventory carry a hidden staff tag and can never be sold, traded or auctioned.
- Planned (decided, R3-39): admins, builders and the owner play on a separate account; mods and helpers keep one, with powers off (334). `/eco` cannot target your own account or a same-IP account.

## What the player sees
- `/hat`: "You're wearing Iron Helmet." `/craft` opens a "Crafting" window. `/trash` opens a menu named "Trash" with a red warning in the info item.
- `/nick Phoenix2`: "Nickname set to ~Phoenix2. Remove it with /nick off."
- Blocked: "You can't open that in the warzone." / "You're in combat. Try again in 8s."
- Bedrock: `/ec`, `/craft` and `/trash` are chest menus, so taps work. `/nick` needs one typed word.

## Rules and limits
- Convenience only, no money or gameplay advantage (principle 8): the ranks plan lists `/craft`, `/ec` and `/hat` as "no effect on money".
- Anti-dupe: `/hat` swaps in one step; the ender chest is the vanilla one, stored per profile (principle 2); `/trash` is logged and admin only; staff items cannot reach players' markets.
- Nicknames cannot impersonate: reserved words, other players' names and the `~` marker. `/realname` shows the real account.
- Performance: nick data is cached per player; no scans. Staff tool checks run once per second (`staff-powers.check-seconds`).
