# Warps, ratings, likes · id: B6 · status: Draft · version: 1

## In one sentence
Islands can have public warps that others travel to, and visitors can rate (1 to 5 stars) and like islands, which drives the Top rated and Most liked tabs of the visit browser.

## How it works
- **Warps:** `/is setwarp <name> [category]` (Leader, Admin, Mod) at your feet, inside the border, on a safe spot. Same name again moves the warp. `/is delwarp <name>`. 3 warps per island at the start (`warps.start-slots`), hard cap 10 (`warps.max-slots`). Names: letters, digits, `_`, `-`, up to 16, unique per island ignoring case, checked by the chat filter.
- **Categories:** shops, farms, parkour, builds, other (default). Each has a default icon. Icon picker (28 per page), public or private, delete: all in the warp edit menu (`/is warp` then Your warps then click a warp).
- **Sign warps:** a sign with `[warp]` on line 1, the name on line 2, optional category on line 3, written by Leader or Mod inside the border. It becomes a warp, counts toward the slots, can't be edited, and breaking it deletes the warp.
- **Use:** `/is warp` opens the warp browser (public warps of every unlocked island, most visited first, one button per category). `/is warp <player>` and `/is warp <player> <name>` go straight there. Private warps work only for members. Instant; 3 s in combat. A warp visit counts once per visitor per day.
- **Rating:** `/is rate <player> <1-5>`. One rating per player per island per season, changeable any time. Never your own island.
- **Like:** `/is like <player>`. Once per player (not per profile) per island per day. Never your own island. Members are told about likes.
- **Vote rules:** you must have visited the island first, have 60 min of playtime, and at most 3 accounts from one address may rate (and, separately, like) the same island per season. Only a SHA-256 hash of the address is stored.
- **Browser tabs (built):** Top rated needs at least 3 ratings and ranks by the season average. Most liked counts the season's likes.
- **Planned (decided): Top rated v2.** It needs ratings from at least 5 different IPs and ranks by a smoothed (Bayesian) average, so 3 five-star ratings don't beat 40 ratings at 4.9. (R3-33)
- **Planned:** Warp Slots upgrade 3 to 9 warps (6 levels, tasks tier 2 to 12), in B14.

## What the player sees
- "Warp <name> set." and "Your island has all 3 warps. Delete one first with /is delwarp."
- "You gave <island> 5/5." / "You liked <island>." / "You already liked <island> today. Come back tomorrow." / "Visit <island> before you rate it."
- Warp browser with category buttons; warp edit menu with icon, public/private, category, delete.

## Rules and limits
- Warp teleports check the spot is safe, else use the nearest safe spot inside the border.
- Ratings and likes reset with the season (islands are wiped, 296).
- Per-IP checks need the real IP of Bedrock players through Geyser/Floodgate (REVIEW3 section 3, item 9).
- Removing an island deletes its warps; visits, ratings and likes stay as history.
- Browser totals reload every 120 s (no per-click database work).
- Bedrock: the browser and edit menus are plain clicks. Making a new warp needs `/is setwarp <name>` or a sign; there is no "new warp" button yet (see open question 4).

## Decided
- 282: top rated needs at least 3 ratings (replaced by R3-33 once built).
- R3-33 ("Recommended"): Top rated needs ratings from at least 5 different IPs and ranks by a smoothed average. 10 IPs (the first suggestion) would leave the list nearly empty at 300 players.
- 292: max 3 accounts from one IP and 1 hour of playtime to rate or like.
- 295 and 296: ratings and likes reset with the season.
- O7: one like per player (not per profile) per island per day; ratings stay.
- I14: warp categories shops, farms, parkour, builds, other.
- I15: sign warps with `[warp]`, counting toward the slots.
- 285: warp teleports are instant, 3 s in combat.
