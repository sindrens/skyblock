# Tags and titles · id: F4 · status: Draft · version: 1

## In one sentence
Titles are earned and tags are owned. You wear one of each and they show next to your name in chat.

## How it works
- **Title = earned.** Sources today: first join (`newcomer`), tutorial finish (`firstflight`), 60 clean days (`honorable`, automatic), achievements (F3) and staff. Event titles (like King of the Hill) are in the list and wait for the events. Shown before the rank. Not tradeable.
- **Tag = owned.** Sources: crates, the free rank pick, staff, later the store. Shown after the name in small muted brackets.
- **Tag items:** a tag can come as a unique, unclaimed item (store tags work this way). Unclaimed, it can be traded and sold anywhere (AH, `/trade`, drop). Right-click and confirm to claim it: the tag is added to your account, bound, and the item is gone. `/tags item <tag>` makes one, admin only.
- **One title and one tag at a time.** Both belong to the account, work on every profile (PR1) and stay across seasons.
- **7 rarities:** common, uncommon, rare, epic, legendary, mythic, special. A secret title shows `???` until earned.
- **Registry:** `tags-titles.yml`. The base file has 12 titles and 8 tags. The achievements module adds about 35 titles when it starts. Admins can create, edit and delete entries in the game (`/tags` → Edit).
- **Chat line:** `<title><rank><name><tag> » message`. Tab shows rank, name and tag (the title was left out of tab in FIXES1).
- **Crate and free-rank tags today:** free pick ember, spark or ash. Crates give spark/ash (Ember), ashes (Cinder), ember (Blaze), skyborn (Inferno), phoenix (Phoenix), inferno (Eclipse).
- **Planned (plan TT1-TT9):**
  - Themed names for existing titles, ids kept (RTA-29).
  - New titles: First Flame, season titles like `Firecrown · S1`, event titles, Unbroken (30-day login streak), Voice of the Flame (500 votes).
  - New tags: free pick Spark, Smoke, Kindling; one tag per bought rank; more per crate; holiday tags only in their month; one new Eclipse tag each month that never returns.
  - Max 16 visible characters per name. Rank-name tags reserved so a store tag cannot fake a rank.

## What the player sees
- `/tags` (also `/tag`, `/titles`, `/title`): one menu, two tabs. Equip one title and one tag. Locked titles show how to earn them. A preview shows your chat and tab line. An Achievements button opens F3.
- Messages: `Title equipped: ...`, `New title: ... [Equip]`, `New tag: ... [Equip]`.
- Staff: an Edit button, `/tags give|take <player> <tag>`, `/titles give|take <player> <title>`, `/display` (chat, tab and nametag formats with live preview).

## Rules and limits
- Admins give any title or tag. Mods give only event titles from a short list (397). Automatic titles like `honorable` cannot be given or taken by hand.
- A crate duplicate re-rolls into a cosmetic you do not own. If you own them all it pays crystals.
- Tag items are unique and covered by dupe detection. A claimed tag and any title cannot be traded (REVIEW3 F5).
- Looks only, no gameplay power, so store tags are safe under Mojang's rules (principle 8).
- Every give, take and edit is audited. The editor saves `tags-titles.yml` in the background.
- Bedrock: both tabs are click-only. Gradient names must stay readable on a phone.
- Performance: ownership is one small table (`cosmetics_owned`), no scans.

## Connections
- Achievements (F3): titles. Ranks (F2): free pick, rank tags and the monthly cosmetic of bought ranks. Crates (F5): tag rewards.
- Progression track (F1): cosmetics can be earned along it.
- Battle pass (F9): premium tags and titles. Collection log (F10): Keeper of Ashes. Daily rewards (F7) and voting (F6): streak and vote titles.
- Chat (G1), tab list (G8), seasons (I5), store (I6), tutorial (A3).

## Decided
- 397: admins give titles and tags, mods give event titles from a short list.
- 398: titles and tags must be planned and made relevant to the theme (replaces a plain Founder title).
- 354: earned titles stay across seasons.
- PR1: achievement titles are account titles.
- O18: achievements give titles.
- RTA-29: give existing achievement titles themed names, ids stay.
- RTA-32: the First Flame title for 1 h played in Season 1's first week.
- DESIGN §9 (2026-10-29): titles are earned, tags are owned and come as tag items that can be sold on the AH (refined by REVIEW3 F5: only until claimed).
- R3-14: skins, tags and sets bought with gems or credits stay account-bound. Only things earned in play can become tradeable tokens.
- REVIEW3 F5 (final, refines R3-14): items bought with gems arrive as an unclaimed item. Unclaimed, it can be sold or traded anywhere. Right-click claims it: it is added to the account and becomes bound. This is how store tags already work. Use it for skins, tags, sets and cosmetics.
- REVIEW3 F3: gems bought in the store are tradeable, gems earned in play are bound to the account. Crystals are never tradeable.
- R3-8: bought ranks give a monthly cosmetic (F2).
- REVIEW3 F7: a bought rank unlocks its cosmetic perks at once, and everyone can earn them along the progression track (F1).
