# Punishments · id: H2 · status: Draft · version: 1

## In one sentence
Staff open one menu with `/p <player>` and click a reason. Each reason has a ladder (warn, mute, ban) so repeat offences get harsher by themselves, and every punishment can be undone, appealed, checked and audited.

## How it works
- **One menu** (`/p`, alias `/punish`, 6 rows): mutes top-left, 4 warn buttons bottom-left, bans top-right, small things bottom-right, the player's head and warn counter in the middle. Bottom row: Sanctions, Kick, Bulk, History, Close, More reasons, Silent, Dry run. Offline players work.
- **No confirm click.** One click applies the next ladder step and the menu refreshes. Safety is the 2-minute **[Undo]**, not a question.
- **20 reasons in 4 categories** (cheating, chat, gameplay, other) in `punishments.yml`. Examples: hacking = ban 30d, then perm. Spam = warn, mute 30m, 6h, 3d. Toxicity = warn, mute 1h, 1d, 7d, perm.
- **Offences** count earlier punishments of the same reason younger than 90 days (cheating reasons never expire). History is never deleted.
- **One pipeline** for every way to punish (menu, `/p <player> <reason>`, `/ban`, `/mute`, `/warn`, `/kick`, [Apply], bulk, auto rules): exempt, guard, probation, permission, training, approval, length limit, duplicate, never shorten, apply.
- **Warn rules:** warns count for 30 days. 2 active warns suggest a 1h mute. Another warn or mute within 7 days suggests 1 day. Staff click [Apply] or [Dismiss]; nothing is automatic (`warn-rules.mode: notify`).
- **Rank limits** (`moderation.yml: limits`): helper up to 1d, mod 30d, admin permanent. Helpers mute, warn and kick. Mods also ban.
- **Approval:** `ban perm`, `ipban`, `mute perm`, `ban >= 30d`, or anything over your limit becomes a request for an admin (waits 24h).
- **Evidence:** each mute and warning saves the player's last 10 chat lines plus 5 from others (public chat, IPs removed, kept 180 days).
  - **Planned (decided): DMs through `/report` only (R3-9a).** A report attaches the last 10 private messages between the two players (see H3).
- **Appeals:** players use `/appeal <text>` (20 to 500 characters). Staff use `/appeals`: Accept, Reduce or Deny. A permanent ban can be appealed after 30 days (cheating 90).
- **Probation:** after a mute or ban ends by time, a new offence within 14 days uses the next ladder step.
- **Sanctions** (account-wide): freeze balance, block trading, remove money, confiscate an item. Each has a reason and a restore. **Planned (decided, R3-28):** a system-detected dupe gives the holders an automatic 24 h trade block that expires by itself, and staff can upgrade it to a balance freeze here (see H7).
- **Island steps:** island kick, island ban, remove from team, team block.
- **Planned (decided): a "severe" reason (520)** with an instant permanent ban and admin approval, and **evidence for every ban of 7 days or more (521)**. Neither is built.
- **Tools around it:** `/pbulk` (max 20), `/watch`, `/pnote`, `/lockdown` (raid), `/pstats`, `/prollback`, `/ppurge` (owner), `/preport` (monthly), `/history`, `/alts`, `/warns`. The title "Honorable" after 60 clean days.

## What the player sees
- Warning: a title "WARNING" with the reason, plus [I understand] and [Rules] (or `/ack`). Bedrock gets a small menu with one button.
- Mute and ban screens show the rule number, time left, id and when an appeal is possible.
- A public line for bans and mutes with the rule number only, no staff name, unless the staff used `-s`.
- "Your mute has ended. Please follow the Rules." and, after a ban, "Welcome back."
- `/mypunishments`: active mute, warnings like "1.5 / 2", last 5 entries. Staff names are hidden.

## Rules and limits
- Punishments belong to the account (UUID), never a profile.
- Same player and same reason again within 15s is blocked unless confirmed or `-force`. A shorter mute or ban is refused while a longer one runs.
- Staff guard, per 5 minutes: helper 5 bans / 15 mutes / 25 total, mod 10 / 25 / 40 (both blocked when over), admin 30 / 60 / 100 (alert only). The owner is never limited. A block stays until an admin runs `/pguard allow`.
- Trainees (`skyblock.staff.punish.trainee`): bans, mutes, kicks and bulk become requests.
- Bedrock: plain left clicks, number pickers, and `/appeal <text>` instead of a text box.
- Performance: no database polling. One timer per online muted player; stats cached 60s; the Honorable check runs on join and every 6h.
- A broken `punishments.yml` edit is rejected and the old config stays.

## Connections
- Reports (H3): a punishment closes the reports you claimed; false reports lead to `report-abuse`.
- Logs (H5): punish from a chat line. Anticheat (H8): `/psuggest`. Anti-dupe (H7): a dupe suggests `exploiting`.
- Staff mode (H1): the Punish tool. Freeze (H4): a ban clears the freeze.
- Visiting (B5): a banned leader's island is closed to visitors. Economy (C2, C6, C7): sanctions block pay, trade and auction.

## Decided
- 423: permanent bans appealable after 30 days (cheating 90); the ban screen shows time left.
- 442, 443: only the owner can purge a player's data.
- 459, 460: helpers and up run lockdowns; staff, builders and donor ranks are exempt from them.
- 467, 470, 474: anticheat suggestions are never automatic. Mods and up bulk punish and read appeals.
- 450, 480: staff without approval rights are refused sanctions, not queued. Mod bans of 30 days or more need an admin.
- 493, 499: a shorter mute or ban is refused while a longer one is active. The ladders are as proposed (hacking 30 days, then permanent).
- 500, 519, 509: bans and mutes are announced, rule number only, unless `-s`. Only admins undo other staff's punishments.
- 513: private messages are not evidence. Changed by R3-9a: they count only through `/report`.
- R3-9a: `/report` attaches the last 10 DMs between reporter and reported, kept 180 days, stated on the privacy page.
- R3-28: a system-detected dupe gives an automatic 24 h trade block; staff can upgrade it to a freeze.
- 520, 521: a "severe" reason (instant permanent ban, admin approval) and evidence for bans of 7 days or more. Planned (decided), not built.
- REVIEW3 F11: leaders can't kick in the last 14 days of a season; staff still can.
- 522: a banned leader's island is closed to visitors.
