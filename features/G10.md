# Help and settings · id: G10 · status: Draft · version: 1

## In one sentence
`/help` explains every feature in short pages and can take you there, and `/settings` is one menu of on/off switches (chat, social, display, sounds, gameplay) that apply at once.

## How it works
- **`/help [topic|category|commands]`** (`/guide`):
  - 11 categories (Getting started, Islands, The Depths, Economy, Progression, Gear, Combat & Events, Rewards, Community, FAQ, All commands) and 41 topics. Combat & Events is locked "Coming soon".
  - Each topic has 3-6 short pages, a **Take me there** button (runs the feature's command), a **Show in chat** button, and a list of its commands.
  - Command lists are generated from the commands the player may use. Nobody lists commands by hand.
  - `/help <topic>` and topic aliases work (`/help tpa`, `/help settings`). Console and chat get a text version.
  - Every menu has a "?" that opens its topic. Help NPCs (A5) and sneak + right-click on an NPC show short help.
  - Texts are in `help.yml`. Reload: `/skyblock reload menus`.
- **`/settings [setting] [on|off|toggle]`** (`/options`, `/prefs`):
  - A menu in five groups. Click a switch: green ✔ on, red ✘ off. Saved at once, no relog.
  - Chat form for quick changes, with Tab completion: `/settings scoreboard off`.
  - Chat: Private messages, Mention sound, Join & leave messages, Chat colors, Chat timestamps (off by default), Hide ignored chat (on by default).
  - Social: Teleport requests, Trade requests, Island invites, AFK announcements, plus island switches.
  - Display: Scoreboard, Menu item, Tips, Particles, Players in The Depths (still "Coming soon").
  - Sounds: Menu sounds, Event sounds.
  - Gameplay: Island auto-pickup, Drop guard, Sell all skips hotbar.
  - Planned (decided): two new Gameplay switches, both on by default (REVIEW3 F14): "Undo last buy" (15 s) and a second click for buys over 50% of your money. The shop (C5) owns how they work. The switch names are `(proposed default)`.
  - Planned (decided): the "Players in The Depths" tile becomes a Visibility setting with three choices, Everyone (default), Friends + island, Nobody, plus pinning a player (REVIEW3 D4). Every switch today is on/off, so this needs a three-choice setting type. Details in E8.
  - Modules add their own switches through a shared settings service. Only differences from the default are stored, so changing a default later reaches everyone who never touched it. Every change is audited (`setting_change`).
- Staff also see staff-only switches, such as the island chat spy.
- New switch texts go in `messages/navigation.yml` (REVIEW3 F10).

## What the player sees
- Help main menu: category icons in flame orange; locked ones gray with "Coming soon".
- Settings title "Settings", info "Click a switch to turn it on or off. Changes are saved right away."
- Wrong setting name: "No setting X. Press Tab to see them all."
- Bedrock: both are chest menus. Switches are taps, no typing needed.

## Rules and limits
- A switch only changes the player's own experience (what they see, hear, receive or how their own clicks behave). It never changes the rules for other players.
- Staff can always reach a player who turned private messages off (staff are unignorable).
- A switch for a feature that is not built shows as "Coming soon" and unlocks by itself when the feature arrives.
- Performance: help pages and settings menus are built when opened. Setting reads are map lookups on the cached player row.
