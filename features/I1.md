# Warzone and combat tag · id: I1 · status: Draft · version: 1

## In one sentence
The warzone is the only PvP world: you keep your gear when you die and lose a small part of your money, and a combat tag stops you from escaping a fight with commands or by quitting.

## How it works
- **Built: the world.** `warzone` exists as an empty void world (PvP on, dusk, border 1000, keep inventory on). It is protected (no building, no containers). It can only be entered through its portal in the castle gatehouse (`worlds.yml: portals.warzone-portal-only`). There is no warzone build file yet, so the portal says "The warzone isn't built yet. Soon!". PHASE-9 and WORLD.md say "built"; at HEAD only the empty world exists.
- **Built: combat tag** (`CombatTagService`, `core.yml: combat.tag-seconds` = 10, `combat.mobs` = on). Hitting or being hit by a player or a hostile mob tags you for 10 s. It is used by:
  - teleports and portals (3 s wait instead of instant, never blocked),
  - `/loadout` and wardrobe changes,
  - `/ec` and `/craft` (blocked in the warzone and 10 s after combat),
  - `/trade` (no requests for 10 s, any damage ends an open trade).
- **Built: PvP normalization.** In the warzone damage enchants work at 40%, healing at 50%, area and movement enchants are off. Every set counts as 1x with diamond-like armor and 50% damage reduction; only conquest bonuses work (`enchants.yml: pvp-percent`).
- **Planned (decided): death rule.** Death in the warzone keeps your inventory and takes 2% of your purse (max $1M). Notes and bank are safe (682). PvE deaths lose nothing (#76).
- **Planned (decided): killer reward** (R3-42). The killer gets half of the victim's 2% loss (max $500k), once per killer-victim pair per hour, and nothing if both accounts share an IP. An empty purse pays nothing.
- **Planned: PvP tag** (proposed default): a second tag of 15 s from player hits only. The 10 s mob tag stays for teleports and loadouts. The PvP tag blocks `/ah /sell /shop /bank /trade /withdraw /pay /ec /craft`, profile switching, loadouts, flying and elytra.
- **Planned: combat logging** (proposed default): quitting while PvP-tagged counts as a death by the last attacker (2% purse loss, a kill and the R3-42 reward for them). No penalty on server stop, crash or staff kick. No NPC body is left behind.
- **Planned: kill streak lines** in the warzone chat, and kill and death effects (FI 90). The scoreboard note says kills and an event timer will be added.

## What the player sees
- Gatehouse portal hologram "Warzone · Soon" until the build is applied.
- Wrong entry: "The warzone can only be entered through its portal in the castle gatehouse."
- Tagged teleport: "You're in combat. Teleporting to X in 3s. Don't move."
- Blocked command: "You can't open that in the warzone." / "You're in combat. Try again in 8s."
- Warzone scoreboard (placeholder): combat level and money.
- Planned (proposed default): action bar `⚔ In combat 12s`, then "You're out of combat." Public line only inside the warzone: "Steve logged out in combat."

## Rules and limits
- PvP is only allowed here and in duels (822). Islands and the Depths are PvP off.
- The 2% loss is from the purse only, capped at $1M, so a rich player cannot lose a fortune and an empty purse loses nothing.
- Anti-abuse: the killer reward is once per pair per hour and never for same-IP accounts (R3-42). This needs the real player IP, so Floodgate must pass each Bedrock player's own IP (check before launch, REVIEW3 §3). 3 combat logs in 24 h alert staff (proposed default).
- Bedrock: tag time and blocks are shown in the action bar and plain messages. Nothing needs a chat click.
- Performance: the tag is one timestamp per player in memory. No per-tick scan; old tags are pruned.
- Tutorial: the portal opens at the tutorial finish (TUT-4), so new players read the rules first.

## Open questions
1. Two tags: a 10 s mob tag (teleports and loadouts only) and a 15 s PvP tag that blocks the economy commands? Default: yes. Without it, the 10 s tag from Depths mobs would block `/sell` for miners (567).
2. Which commands and moves does the PvP tag block (`/pay`, flying, elytra, profile switching)? Default: all listed above.
3. Quitting while PvP-tagged = death by the last attacker, with a public line and a staff alert after 3 in 24 h? Default: yes.
4. Open the warzone portal to the empty platform before the build exists? Default: no, closed until built.
5. PvP tag length 15 s, shown as the action bar line `⚔ In combat 12s`? Default: yes.

## Rejected ideas
- An NPC body left behind when a player logs out: keep inventory makes it pointless and it costs performance.
- Blocking teleports in combat: the owner chose a 3 s delay instead (345).
