# How the owner wants the game to work (patterns from 194 answers, 30 Oct 2026)

Use these to pick the **suggested** answer for any new question and to spot questions that earlier answers already settle. Source: OWNER_ANSWERS_ROUND1.md, OWNER_ANSWERS_ROUND2.md, DECISIONS_2026-10-30.md.

## Big principles
0. **Fun first, less is more (owner, 31 Oct).** The server must be fun, engaging and psychologically rewarding, and never hard to understand. If something gets too advanced, say so to the owner and offer the simpler version. Fewer, clearer systems beat many clever ones.
1. **No waiting, no busywork.** No cooldowns on creating/joining islands, no disband delay, no 30-minute new-account limit, teleports instant (3 s only in combat / combat zones / warzone / boss arena / events). Don't add delays unless they stop real abuse.
2. **Profiles are islands.** Money, inventory, skills, keys, collection log, pets, achievements, daily login reward: per profile. Deleting/leaving/resetting = that profile's progress is gone (staff can restore from backups). Never a way to move value between your own profiles (no buying your own other profile's AH listings, no transfers).
3. **Nothing is ever lost by accident.** Items never drop on the ground (overflow → `/box`). Kicks, resets, disbands keep staff-restorable backups. Bank money on disband/reset is deleted but backed up for staff.
4. **The tutorial unlocks the game.** First thing: walk into the portal at spawn → your island is created → the tutorial starts there (animation/camera, arrow guide, step by step); permissions unlock per step; full perms when finished. This replaces playtime gates.
5. **Help everywhere, lores clean.** NPCs and holograms explain things on right-click; everything reachable from menus (Bedrock never needs long commands); item lores short and compact for everyone.
6. **English only.** Game text and public chat in English; private messages any language. No Norwegian.
7. **Looks:** V2 bright palette, red identity, bold yellow `» ACTION` lines, red `|` bars. A **forced** resource pack (prompt before joining) will skin all GUIs, crates, sets and tools later; the owner draws the art. Don't over-design vanilla GUIs; pick items that can be retextured.
8. **Staff:** helpers limited (no secret island-chat watch, no socialspy, no bulk punish, no appeals queue); mods do most; admins approve big things; only the owner hands out staff ranks and purges data. Public punishment announcements on (rule number only, no staff name) unless the staff uses `-s` (silent). Ban screen shows time left and when an appeal is possible.
9. **Donations:** ranks give both convenience and some progress, "to a degree" (it's donations). Permanent purchased ranks plus season-only won ranks. 7 donor ranks with names that fit the phoenix/ember theme (the old names and "Ember" for the free rank must change). No Trainee staff rank. `/trash` is owner/admin only. Rank perks must be planned from how the server works.
10. **Islands:** 3x3 → 9x9 with normal upgrades, bigger only with gems or the Black Market. Team = 4 + leader. Upgrades paid from the island bank only. Bank: interest every 8 h, 2% fee lowered by masteries. Visiting: open by default, no visitor limit, browser shows every island (favour more playtime), max 3 accounts per IP and 1 h playtime to rate/like. Island level is dynamic from many sources (needs a plan). Achievements: personal ones (personal boost for that profile) and island ones (whole island) — needs a plan. Titles and tags must be themed — needs a plan.
11. **When a suggestion is just "keep as built" with no reason, think again**: pick what fits these principles and a 300-player skyblock with a strong economy, and say why in one line.
