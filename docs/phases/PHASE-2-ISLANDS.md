# Phase 2: Islands

**Goal:** every player has a protected, growing island they can share, manage and show off. Upgrades/levels come in Phase 5; this phase builds the island system they plug into.

Depends on Phases 0–1. Design: `DESIGN.md` §3, `WORLD.md` §3, `REVIEW.md` A4, A5, B1, B9, B10.

---

## M2.1 Grid, creation and home
- **Grid:** the `islands` world is divided into **512 × 512 slots** (one region file each), allocated in a spiral from 0,0. Slot table: slot id, x/z, state (free/used/clearing), island id.
- **Creation:** `/is` (no island yet) → short confirm menu ("Create your island") → island pasted from the configured schematic via FAWE (async, no lag) → teleport to the island home with title + sound + first objective.
- **Placeholder schematic** (owner decision: placeholders first): generated by code if no schematic file exists: small medieval islet (grass/dirt, oak tree, chest with starter items, sign "Open /menu"). Real schematics later: `plugins/PhoenixSkyblock/schematics/*.schem`.
- **Size:** starts at **3×3 chunks (48×48)** centered in the slot; grows by chunk rings up to **15×15** (upgrade in Phase 5). Shown with a **per-player world border** (only players on that island see its border).
- `/is home`, `/is sethome` (inside the island only, safe spot check), `/is go <member island?>`.
- Creation cooldown after disband: 24 h (configurable). One island per player.

## M2.2 Protection
- Inside an island, actions depend on role permissions (M2.3): break, place, interact (doors/buttons/levers), containers, redstone, animals, villagers (trade), item pickup/drop, armor stands/item frames, vehicles, fire/flint, ender pearls, spawners (later).
- **Visitors:** default can walk and look only (configurable per island in settings).
- **Build height: Y 0 to 256** (owner decision): placing/breaking outside that range on islands is blocked with a clear message.
- Cross-border protection: pistons, liquids, falling blocks, explosions, tree growth and portals can't cross the island border; entities pushed out are returned.
- PvP: off on islands.
- Bypass for staff: `skyblock.island.bypass` (+ `/is bypass` toggle, logged).

## M2.3 Members and roles
- Roles: **Leader, Mod, Member, Coop** (owner decision) with a **permission matrix GUI**: rows = roles, items = actions; leader toggles each (Mod can't edit Mod/Leader permissions).
- `/is invite <player>`: on a **solo** island, the leader first sees a confirm screen: "Inviting makes your island a **Team Island** forever. Solo and team islands are on separate leaderboards." Clickable invite for the target, expires in 5 min.
- `/is accept|deny`, `/is kick <player>` (confirm), `/is leave` (confirm), `/is promote|demote <player>`, `/is transfer <player>` (double confirm), `/is disband` (type-to-confirm + 24 h cooldown).
- **Coop:** `/is coop <player>`: temporary trust while the leader is online; never converts solo→team; gives no XP/bank access (REVIEW A4).
- **Limits:** 4 members at start, Member Slots upgrade to 8 (Phase 5); max **3 island joins per season** per player; 24 h cooldown between joins (REVIEW A5). XP a member earned stays with the island.
- Notifications to online members for every member change; all logged (`island/*`).

## M2.4 Visiting, warps and showcase
- `/is visit <player>` and **visit browser** (most visited, top rated, newest).
- `/is lock|unlock`, `/is expel <player>`, `/is ban|unban <player>` (banned visitors can't enter); notifications: visit attempt while locked, arrival, departure (each togglable).
- **3 warps** per island (more with upgrades later): `/is setwarp <name>`, icon picker, public/private, `/is warp <player> [name]`, warp browser GUI with visits and ratings (`/is rate <player> 1-5`, one rating per player per island per season).

## M2.5 Island menu and settings
- `/is` (has island) opens the **island menu** (layout below): Home, Members, Permissions, Warps, Settings, Upgrades (Phase 5, shown as coming soon), Stats, Bank (Phase 3), Visitors, Island info.
- **Settings:** visitor permissions, locked, notifications, island name (filtered), island description, biome display (Phase 7), island time (client-side day/night).
- `/is info [player]`: owner, members, created, size, level (Phase 5), warps.
- Island chat: `/is chat [message]` (toggle mode without text), color-coded, logged.

## M2.6 Stats tracking (foundation for island level)
- Counters per island **and** per member, per category (blocks mined, logs chopped, crops harvested, mobs killed, items sold, money earned, KOTH time …) with **daily and weekly buckets**. Written in batches.
- `/is stats` GUI: totals, per-member contribution, this week vs last week (categories show "—" until their features exist).
- Island top framework (solo vs team boards) ready; level calculation arrives in Phase 5.

## M2.7 Lifecycle and admin tools
- Disband/reset: island marked `clearing`, region emptied in slices by FAWE (async, throttled), then the slot is freed. Data rows keep `season_id` history.
- Offline islands are never kept loaded (simulation comes in Phase 7).
- `/isadmin`: `info`, `tp`, `delete`, `setsize`, `addmember`, `kick`, `setowner`, `reset`, `bypass`, `slots` (grid overview), `teleport-slot`. All logged.
