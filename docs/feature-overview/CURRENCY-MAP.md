# Currency map (proposal from the developer, owner asked 31 Oct for "what is used where")

Status: PROPOSAL, not decided. Built facts come from the code check in `disagreements/C1.md`. Principle: each currency must answer two questions in one line: "what work earns it?" and "what is it for?". Four currencies, no fifth (principle 0).

## The four currencies today and as proposed
| | Money $ | Crystals ◆ | Shards ✧ | Credits ✦ |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| One line | Everyday buying and selling | Your Depths work turned into gear power | Prize for danger (bosses, KOTH, events) | Store, loyalty and cosmetics |
| Earned by | Selling drops and farm output, spawners, collectors, AFK pool, quests, interest | Depths mine, forest and combat (chance per block or kill), island tasks and pillars | World boss top 3, KOTH capture, envoys, Depths tier 9+ rare drop (all planned, only the Conqueror crate exists today) | Store purchase, voting (all sites, each once a day), thanks, daily streak, battle pass |
| Spent on | /shop, Forge tools, bank tiers, AH, island upgrades (from the island bank), wardrobe money sets | Enchants and rolls, crystal sets and set merges, Forge extras | Black Market, island charters (size above 9x9) | Credit shop: ranks, cosmetics, tags, trails, slots, looks (progress items pending) |
| Tradeable | yes | never | yes | bought credits yes, earned no |
| Scope | profile (personal) plus island bank (shared) | profile | profile | player (all profiles) |
| Public | yes | no | yes | yes (total only) |
| Season end | wiped | wiped | wiped | kept |

Why different work for each: selling is the daily loop (Depths and production), crystals need Depths time, shards need risk and events, credits need money, votes or streaks. A player who only mines is short of shards; a player who only fights is short of crystals; so players trade money for shards and credits.

## The island aspect (your question)
Today the island's only currency is the island bank (money, shared, interest every 8 h, upgrades paid from it) and island level and pillars (progress, not a currency). Options:
- **A. Keep as built (recommended).** Personal money, crystals and shards; shared island bank; the Contribution menu (B4) shows who deposited and who earned island XP and tasks. No new currency. Why: personal currencies keep each player's progress visible and fair on kick or leave; the shared pool already exists; one more currency would break "easy to understand" for 1000 players.
- **B. Crystals become the island's currency.** Members' Depths work fills one island pool; it pays island upgrades, pillars, outposts and an island shop; enchants and sets move to money. Gives the island a clear second currency, but then personal gear no longer has its own currency, and one griefing member with the right permission can spend the pool.
- **C. Everything shared per island.** Rejected by me: breaks the $10k send gate and F1 logic, kick and leave split, offers a theft path, and removes the "my progress" feeling. Also makes solo and team play incomparable.
Either way a clear "who made what" menu on the island (contributions, deposits, tasks, pillars) is built in B4.

## Gaps this map shows (to fix in the later cards)
1. Shard sources are mostly unbuilt: until they exist shards cannot be earned in any meaningful way, so charters and Black Market stock are empty promises.
2. Crystals have many sinks and one source type; check against E1/E5 so every Depths world gives a different mix (owner request C1-R1-25).
3. Money has too many sources and few sinks; price overhaul planned (C1-R1-25).
4. Credits need an item catalogue before prices can be judged (I10).
