# Feature ideas for yes / no (2026-10-30)

Ideas taken from big skyblock plugins (SuperiorSkyblock2, BentoBox + addons, IridiumSkyblock, FabledSkyblock) and big servers, checked against our design.
**How to answer:** reply with numbers, e.g. `yes 1-12, 15, no 13 14, maybe 20`. Nothing here is built until you say yes.
"Planned" = already in our plan, the idea adds detail. "New" = not in our plan yet.
