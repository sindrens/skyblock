# Plan: the owner's picks from SUGGESTIONS_7NOV (7 Nov 2026)

Owner answers: Auto-Compactor YES, weekly island top rewards NO, Meteor at spawn YES, /fish tournament YES, lottery NO,
sell key NO, pets NO, visit rewards NO, tool prestige board YES, Shard Finder number in the lore YES, KOTH YES, tool
skins YES, "please implement this and everything else". Principle 0: simple, fun, less is more. Built in 4 releases.

## Release 1 (update156)
1. **Shard Finder number:** the tool lore and the perk tile show "+111% shards" (level x shards.finder-per-level).
2. **Top Tools board** (Hall of Heroes, the Foraging board: there is no axe any more): players ranked by their tool
   prestiges, a rebirth counting as 50 (all four tools added up). Source `tool_prestige all` (or one tool).
3. **Auto-Compactor** (hoe and pickaxe perk, one level, unlocks at tool level 15, 40,000 shards): 9 wheat become a hay
   bale, 9 iron ingots an iron block (coal, redstone, lapis, diamond, melon, slime too), in your bag and in the tool
   storage. A block sells for exactly 9 of the item and counts as 9 for the daily island price, so it is never worth more
   or less than the items.

## Release 2 (update157)
4. **Meteor at spawn**, every 60 minutes: a title and one chat line for everyone ("A meteor landed at spawn: /spawn"),
   five glowing fragments land on the spawn plaza. Hit a fragment (25 hits, everyone can help); the player who breaks
   it gets the loot: money, crystals, sometimes an Ember key. Gone after 2 minutes. No blocks change (display entities).
5. **Fishing Contest**, every 2 hours for 10 minutes at /fish: one boss bar ("Fishing Contest · 7:12 · You: 12, #3"),
   most catches win: #1 3 Ember keys + 300 crystals, #2 2 keys + 200, #3 1 key + 100. One chat line at the start and one
   with the top 3 at the end.

## Release 3 (update158)
6. **Tool skins:** the tool menu gets a Skins button: Diamond (free), Iron, Golden, Copper, Netherite looks for each tool,
   bought once with credits (250 / 500 / 500 / 1,000), switch any time. Only the look changes (vanilla item models until
   the pack v2 art exists).

## Release 4 (update159)
7. **KOTH at the Warzone:** every 3 hours for up to 15 minutes; `/koth` and the Warzone portal open while it runs. Stand
   alone on the hill (7x7 around the Warzone spawn) for 2 minutes in total to win (another island on the hill pauses
   it); boss bar with the holder and the time left. Win: 5 Ember keys + 500 crystals per player of the winning island who
   is in the Warzone. PvP is on in the Warzone; you keep your items when you die there.
