# Batch 8 part 2: /pve bosses, Fish and Combat Mastery (plan, 7 Nov 2026)

Owner, 7 Nov 2026: "keep working on everything, make it balanced, full implementation after it's planned".
This plan follows FULL_PLAN.md sections 4 and 6; numbers are balanced against /farm, /mine and /fish.

## Fish Mastery and Combat Mastery (island masteries, like Farm and Mine Mastery)
- 10 ranks each, paid from the island bank, same prices and island levels as Farm/Mine Mastery.
- Combat Mastery needs the island's combat pillar; Fish Mastery has no pillar (there is no fishing pillar).
- Each rank: +100 enchant levels on the members' rod (Fish) or sword (Combat).
- Requirements: the same two island tasks per rank as Farm/Mine Mastery (harvest island wheat, mine island
  cobblestone), so the island has to farm and mine to push every area.

## /pve bosses (personal)
- **Boss Sigils**: every 200 kills in a ring give one Sigil of that ring (per player, counted on the player).
  The scoreboard and the boss menu show them.
- **/pve boss** (and a button in the arena): a small menu with the ten rings: open ring, Sigils owned, the
  boss name and reward. Click = summon that ring's boss next to you (you must be in the arena and the ring
  must be open). One boss per player at a time, at most 20 bosses in the arena.
- **The boss**: that ring's mob, 30x its health, 1.5x its damage, glowing, a name, a boss bar for the
  summoner with its health and the time left (3 minutes). Only the summoner can hurt it, and other players
  can't see it. Leashed to its ring like the other mobs. Time out = the boss leaves (the Sigil is used up).
- **Reward** for a kill: money = 60x the ring's money per kill, 25 kills of progress for Combat XP and the
  island sword (shards, XP), and a crate key: 10% Ember (rings 1-5) / Cinder (rings 6-10), and every 10th boss
  without a key gives one for sure (pity). Chat line and a title.
- Names: Rotting King, Desert Tyrant, Bone Archer, Brood Mother, Frost Wraith, Tide Caller, Bastion Warlord,
  Infernal Core, Wither Knight, Ravager Alpha.

## Balance check
- Ring 1: 200 kills (about 7 min at 30 kills a minute) = $4,000 from kills; the boss pays 60 x $20 = $1,200.
- Ring 10: 200 kills = $70,000; the boss pays 60 x $350 = $21,000. A boss is about 30% on top of the kills,
  never the main income.
