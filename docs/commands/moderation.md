# Punishments (`modules/moderation`, M1.8; replace vanilla `/ban /kick /pardon`)

Flags in the reason: `-s` silent (staff only see it), `-ip` IP-wide. Durations: `30m`, `2h`, `7d`, `1w`. Templates/ladders, `/p` layout and warn rules: `punishments.yml` (texts in `messages/moderation.yml`). Length limits per rank: `moderation.yml → limits`.

Every way of punishing (menu, `/p <player> <reason>`, the direct commands below, [Apply] on a suggestion, automatic rules) goes through one decision pipeline: exempt → probation (P4) → permission → approval (P4) → length limit → duplicate (P2) → never shorten (an active longer mute/ban is kept; staff are told) → apply (or a request for an approver).

P2 extras (`punishments.yml → extras`, texts `messages/moderation.yml → undo.*, stats.*`):
- **[Undo]**: after every punishment you apply (menu, `/p`, [Apply], direct commands) you get a chat line with a clickable `[Undo]` for `undo-window` (2m). It is a normal pardon with the reason "Undone by <you> (mistake)": an undone punishment is not an offence and shows as reversed in `/pstats`. Undoing a warn also removes the pending suggestion it caused. Bedrock: the head in `/p` shows "Last action: ... (click to undo)" while the window is open. Only your own punishments, or anyone's with `skyblock.staff.punish.undo.others`. Kicks can't be undone. After the window: use `/unmute`, `/unban` or `/unwarn`.
- **Double-punish protection**: the same player + the same reason again within `duplicate-window` (15s, by anyone, also yourself) is blocked ("Steve was just punished for Spam by Alex 6s ago"); click/type it again within `duplicate-confirm` (5s) to apply anyway, or add `-force` (`skyblock.staff.punish.force`). Applies to the menu, More reasons and `/p <player> <reason>`; [Apply] is already first-click-wins.
- **Offences expire**: a ladder counts only earlier punishments for that reason younger than `offence-expire` (90d; cheating reasons `perm`; each reason can override). The menu says "(1 older offence no longer counts)". History is never deleted.
- **Public announcements**: bans and mutes are announced to all players unless `-s` (owner decision 2026-10-30; `moderation.yml → announce.public`).

P3 extras (`punishments.yml → extras`, texts `messages/moderation.yml → ack.*, notes.*, mine.*, entry.*, evidence.*, reports.*, discord.*`). Punishments are account-wide (player UUIDs, never profiles); all times in Europe/Oslo:
- **Evidence** (12.4): every mute and warning saves the player's last `evidence.chat-lines` (10) chat lines plus `context-lines` (5) lines of others, copied from the audit log (IP addresses are replaced, never stored). `/history <player>` → click an entry → Evidence (click it to print all lines in chat). Only `skyblock.staff.punish.history`. Deleted automatically after `evidence.retention` (180d).
- **Staff notes** (12.5): `/pnote`. The head of `/p` shows the newest 3 notes (author, date, 40 characters) and "N more". Removing keeps the row (who and when).
- **Acknowledgement** (12.6): a warned player gets `[I understand]` + `[Rules]` (`rules-link`) in chat, `/ack` does the same, Bedrock players also get a 3-row menu with one button. Reminder every `ack.repeat` (30s) up to `max-repeats` (3) and once on the next join. Never blocks gameplay (`block-until-ack: false`; true = only `/ack`, `/rules`, `/mypunishments` until confirmed). History shows "Acknowledged 14:03" / "Not acknowledged".
- **Warn weights** (12.7): warn buttons have `weight` (default 1, e.g. 0.5 or 2), a ladder step may be `warn 0.5`. The counter is the sum of the weights of active warns ("Warnings: 1.5 / 2"); a rule `at: 2` fires at 2 or more. Exact (tenths, no floating point).
- **Suggestion API** (12.8): other modules call `SuggestionService.suggest(target, reasonId, source, details)` (in `common`); staff get "Steve (chat filter: 3 spam hits in 10s) suggested: Spam, mute 30m [Apply] [Dismiss] [Open /p]". One pending suggestion per player + reason (a second call refreshes it); never applied automatically. Shows in `/p` (head) and `/warns`. Applying it counts as an offence of that reason. `extras.chat-suggestions` holds the detector settings; the chat-filter detector itself is a separate task.
- **Report link** (12.9): a punishment closes the reports about that player **you** claimed (outcome punished, reporters told; `close-reports: true`). Unclaimed reports stay open and you get "2 open reports on Steve [Open /reports player Steve]". Undo does not reopen reports. Needs the staff module (`ReportLink` service).
- **Discord log** (12.11): every punishment, pardon and undo as one embed (type, player, staff or "System", reason, length, id, rule; never IPs or evidence). **Off**: Discord is postponed (docs/LATER.md). It needs `extras.discord.enabled: true` **and** the webhook URL, a secret set as `DISCORD_WEBHOOK_PUNISHMENTS` in the VPS `.env` (written to the untracked `plugins/PhoenixSkyblock/secrets/discord.yml` by `server/scripts/apply-secrets.sh`). Queued and sent in the background with retry and Discord rate limits; a broken webhook never delays a punishment (one console warning per minute at most).

P4 extras (`punishments.yml → extras.approval / watchlist / probation / mute-ended`, texts `messages/moderation.yml → approval.*, watch.*, ended.*`). Account-wide (player UUIDs), times in Europe/Oslo:
- **Approval** (13.4): punishments matching `approval.rules` (`ban perm`, `ipban *`, `mute perm`, `ban >= 30d`) or longer than your rank limit become a **request** when you lack `skyblock.staff.punish.approve` (admins): nothing happens to the player and they are told nothing. You get "Sent for approval (#12, waits 24h)"; approvers online get `Alex requests ban perm for Steve (Hacked client) [Approve] [Deny] [Open /p]` (and the list on join). Approve applies it with by = the requester and "Approved by" = the approver; the approver's own rank limit is still the hard maximum. Deny needs a reason (preset buttons from `approval.deny-reasons`, or type one in chat); the requester is told (on join if offline). An approver can't approve their own request (`self-approve: false`); requests expire after `approval.expire` (24h); a second request for the same player and reason is merged. Pending requests show on the `/p` head (click = Approve / Deny menu, the Bedrock path). `/pstats` shows requests made / approved / denied / waiting / expired and the ones you handled. [Apply] on a suggestion works the same (the suggestion is marked "sent for approval"). The automatic rules never need approval.
- **"Mute ended"** (13.13): when a mute ends by time, an online player gets "Your mute has ended. Please follow the [Rules]" and a soft sound; offline players get it once on the next join. After a ban ended: "Welcome back. Please follow the [Rules]" (once). One timer per online muted player at the end of the mute (no database polling). Pardons and undos never send it.
- **Probation** (13.7): after a mute or ban ends by time (not pardoned, undone or perm) the player is on probation for `probation.duration` (14d): a new offence uses the ladder step + `step-bonus` (1), never past the last step, and still needs approval where the rules say so. The `/p` head says "On probation until 12 Nov (+1 step)" and every reason shows the step that will really be applied ("Probation: +1 step (normally Warning)"). The player is told once, in the same line as "mute ended" / "welcome back". Off per category or reason with `probation: false` (Name / build / skin and AFK farming ship with it off).
- **Watchlist** (13.5): `/watch`, `/watchlist`. When a watched player joins, staff with `skyblock.staff.notify` get one line: `Watching: Steve (suspected macros, added by Alex 2d ago) [Open /p]`. The player is never told. Entries end after `watchlist.expire` (30d). Shows on the `/p` head (staff with `skyblock.staff.watch`).

P5 extras (`punishments.yml → extras.bulk / appeal`, per reason `appealable:`, texts `messages/moderation.yml → bulk.*, appeal.*, log-evidence.*`). Account-wide (player UUIDs), times in Europe/Oslo:
- **Bulk punish** (13.8): `/pbulk <reason> <players...>` or the **Bulk** button on the `/p` bottom row (slot 47): pick players (the one you opened `/p` for is already selected; click heads to select, max `bulk.max` = 20), pick the reason, then **always** a confirm: "Punish 5 player(s)?" with every player and what they get ("Steve: Mute 30m", "Alex: Ban permanent (needs approval)", "Notch: skipped, already punished for longer"). Each player goes through the normal pipeline on their own (own offence count, probation, active punishments, approval per player: a `ban perm` in bulk becomes one request per player). Skipped with the reason: unknown names, exempt players, already muted/banned longer, just punished for this, too long for your rank. After it: one summary line with **[Undo all]** (the undo window, 2m; Bedrock players also get a small menu with the button), one staff line, one audit group (every punishment has `source bulk:<group>` plus one `bulk_punish` entry), one Discord embed with the count. Each punishment still counts in `/pstats` per player.
- **Punish from logs** (13.11): `/p <player> --evidence <log id>` opens the menu with that `/logs` chat line as evidence (shown on the head); `/p <player> <reason> --evidence <log id>` applies at once. In `/logs`, clicking a chat line does the same. The line must be a chat line written by that player and you must be allowed to see chat logs. The punishment then saves that line plus the lines before it (the normal evidence settings) instead of the newest lines at punish time, for every type (bans too, because you picked it). A request for approval keeps the line until it is approved. Tab completion offers the flag, not ids. Direct commands (`/ban`, `/mute` ...) don't take it.
- **Appeals** (13.10): players type `/appeal` (shows what they can appeal, the state of earlier appeals) and `/appeal <text>` (their active mute, else their newest active appealable punishment) or `/appeal <id> <text>`: a command instead of a text box, so Java and Bedrock work the same. Rules: types `appeal.appealable-types` (mute, ban), not for reasons with `appealable: false` (alt abuse, RMT), one open appeal per punishment, 20-500 characters, after a denied (or reduced) appeal the same punishment can be appealed again after `appeal.cooldown` (7d). Muted players get a hint line with the mute; `/mypunishments` [Appeal] runs `/appeal`. Staff with `skyblock.staff.notify` get "New appeal #14 by Steve (Mute #52, Spam) [Open]". `/appeals` opens the queue (oldest first); one appeal shows the punishment, evidence, history and the player's text with **Accept** (confirm; lifts it, pardon reason "Appeal accepted"; if another staff member gave it, their name is shown so you can talk first), **Reduce** (pick a shorter total length in minutes/hours/days with the number picker) and **Deny** (preset reason or type one). First click wins. The player is told at once, or on the next join. Open appeals older than `appeal.reminder` (48h) are listed to `skyblock.staff.appeals` on join. `/pstats` shows appeals handled (accepted / reduced / denied) and "Reversed by appeal" for the staff member who gave the punishment. **Discord (step 1)**: new appeals and decisions can be posted to the `appeals` webhook (id, punishment, reason, text, history counts, never IPs); **off** (`appeal.discord.enabled: false`) and it also needs the webhook URL secret. Banned players appealing from Discord (step 2) needs the Discord bot (later).

P6 extras (`punishments.yml → extras.never-auto / lockdown / raid / network`, texts `messages/moderation.yml → lockdown.*, psuggest.*`). Account-wide (player UUIDs), times in Europe/Oslo:
- **Raid lockdown** (13.9): `/lockdown chat [time]` (default 10m, max 60m): **new** players can't chat, use `/msg` (`lockdown.blocked-commands`: msg, tell, w, whisper, m, pm, r, reply, mail), write signs or books, and see "Chat is temporarily limited for new players (3m left)". `/lockdown join [time]` (default 5m, max 15m): accounts that **never joined before** get the friendly kick screen "The server is busy. Try again in a few minutes."; returning players always get in. New = total playtime below `new-player-playtime` (30m) **or** first join less than `new-player-age` (24h) ago; staff (`skyblock.staff.notify`) and every rank above default (`skyblock.lockdown.bypass`) are never affected; Bedrock players use the same rules. A lockdown never punishes anyone, ends by itself and is cleared by a restart. Every start and stop is audited (`lockdown_start`, `lockdown_end` with how it ended and, for a join lockdown, how many logins it refused, `raid_detected`, `raid_ignored`) and posted to Discord (off with the rest of Discord). Staff see it: the start line, a reminder every `staff-reminder` (60s) with [Status] [End now], the line on join and the `<ph:lockdown>` placeholder (`%phoenix_lockdown%`; for the HUD, empty for non-staff). **Raid detection** (`raid.detect: true`): more than `raid.accounts` (15) accounts joining for the very first time within `raid.window` (30s) → staff get "Possible raid: 18 new accounts in 30s [Start chat lockdown 10m] [Ignore]" (Bedrock: `/lockdown`). Detection only alerts; `raid.auto-lock: false`. If set to true: chat lockdown only, `auto-duration` (max 5m), once per `auto-cooldown` (min 30m), never the join lockdown, staff told at once.
- **Anticheat suggestions** (13.3): `/psuggest <player> <reason> [details]` from the console (GrimAC) or staff creates the normal suggestion: "Steve (Grim: Reach vl 12). Suggested: Hacked client, ban 30d [Apply] [Dismiss] [Punish menu]". Never applies anything (also in warn-rules auto mode; `extras.never-auto: [hacking, xray, macro]` documents and validates which reasons may never be automatic). One pending suggestion per player + reason: repeats are quiet and append their details (newest 5 lines). How GrimAC calls it: `server/CONFIG_NOTES.md` → GrimAC.
- **Network-ready** (13.12): every new punishment stores `server_id` (`network.server-id`, phoenix) and `scope` (bans and mutes `network.default-scope` = network; warnings and kicks server). With `network.enabled: false` (one server) nothing changes. With true, the join and chat checks only count bans/mutes with scope network (or no scope, older rows) or given on this server. No proxy, messaging or sync: the shared database is the sync.

P7 extras (`punishments.yml → extras.sanctions`, island ladder steps, texts `messages/moderation.yml → sanctions.*, island-punish.*, island-steps.*`). Account-wide (player UUIDs, every profile), times in Europe/Oslo:
- **Sanctions** (13.1): the **Sanctions** button on the `/p` bottom row (slot 45) opens a menu (Bedrock-safe, plain clicks): **Freeze balance** (no `/pay`, money notes, bank, shop, auction house or trades: `freeze-blocks`) and **Block trading** (no auction listings or purchases, `/pay`, money notes or trades: `trade-blocks`; their active listings are cancelled and the items go to their collection bin, never deleted): pick a length (`durations`: 1d, 3d, 7d, 30d, until lifted), then a reason (`reasons`). While active the button shows the end and a click lifts it; it also ends by itself (in-memory timer, no database polling). **Remove money**: pick the amount (number picker), then a reason; takes at most the balance of the player's active (offline: last active) profile and never goes negative; one database transaction with the sanction row (ledger reason `sanction_remove`). **Confiscate item**: the online player's inventory (hotbar, main, armor, off hand) or ender chest; click a stack, then a reason; the item is serialized into `confiscated_items` before it leaves the player, and only marked held once it is really taken (a moved item takes nothing). The last 14 sanctions are listed in the menu with Lift / Restore. Every sanction needs a reason, is audited (`sanction_freeze`, `sanction_trade`, `sanction_money`, `sanction_confiscate`, `sanction_lift`, `sanction_expired`, `sanction_restore`, `sanction_listings_cancelled`; the economy writes `sanction_blocked` for refused transactions), is shown in `/history <player>` (chat, under the punishments) and counted in `/pstats` ("Sanctions"). Approval: `approval.rules` may list `sanction freeze|trade|money|confiscate|*` (ships with money and confiscate): such a sanction needs `skyblock.staff.punish.approve` and is refused (not queued) for others. The economy also refuses every transaction that would break an active sanction (`freeze-currencies`, `freeze-exempt-reasons`, `trade-reasons`), so no other path can move value. A detected dupe (the dupe detector) creates a suggestion with the `dupe-suggestion.reason` ladder (exploiting) and "also freeze balance" in the details; never applied by itself.
- **Island punishments** (13.2): new ladder steps `island-kick` (sent off their team island now), `island-ban <time|perm>` (can't enter that one island, not even as a member; staff with the visit bypass still can), `coop-remove` (removed from the team) and `coop-block <time|perm>` (can't accept invites, be cooped or create an island). The island is the one of the player's active (offline: last active) profile. The island owner is never island-banned from their own island: for owners the island steps skip to the next `coop-block` (or the next normal step); without a team island, or without the islands module, the next possible step is used, and staff get "Island ban 1d isn't possible for Steve (owns the island): Team block 7d instead". The `/p` lore shows the step that will really be applied. Undo lifts an island ban or team block; island kicks and team removals can't be undone. Shipped example reason `coop-griefing`: warn → island-ban 1d → coop-block 7d → ban 7d (under More reasons). Needs `skyblock.staff.punish.island`.
