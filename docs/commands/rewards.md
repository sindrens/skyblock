# Rewards (`modules/rewards`)

Daily calendar, streak and playtime rewards (PHASE-10-REWARDS.md **M10.6**, owner 128, R5, R6, P10-17) and voting (**M10.5**, owner 129, 320, P10-13, P10-15). The calendar and playtime are **per profile**: each profile has its own calendar, streak and playtime (nothing moves between your own profiles, P4). Settings: `daily.yml` and `votes.yml`; texts: `messages/rewards.yml`. Reload: `/skyblock reload rewards`.

| Command | What it does | Permission | Status |
|---|---|---|---|
| `/daily` | Opens the daily rewards menu. **Calendar tab**: the 30 days (claimed = green glass ✔, today = the reward with *CLICK Claim*, later days = gray glass with the reward in the lore; the stack size is the day number), your streak (best, claims, next gems, streak titles) and the reset countdown. **Playtime tab** (no extra command, R6): active time of this profile, whether it counts right now, the hourly reward, the next key and the playtime titles. Also opened by the **Daily Rewards NPC** (role `daily`; sneak-click keeps the help text) and `/help daily`. No arguments, so there is nothing to tab-complete | `skyblock.rewards.daily` (everyone) | ✅ |

| `/vote` | Opens the vote menu (RX42; moved from the social module, where it was a plain link): one button per vote site (✘ not voted = *CLICK to get the link*; ✔ voted = when the next rewarded vote is possible), **All vote sites** (the vote page `skybornmc.net/vote`, owner 320), your **vote streak** (today ✔/✘, next streak key, the milestones) and the **vote party** progress. A click on a site closes the menu and sends the link: clickable in chat on Java, plus a popup with the address on Bedrock (GUI-5; a click in it sends the address as plain text). Also opened by the **Voting NPC** in the Tavern (role `vote`; sneak-click keeps the help text) and `/help vote`. From the console it prints the vote page. No arguments, so there is nothing to tab-complete | `skyblock.rewards.vote` (everyone) | ✅ |

No staff commands (`/daily admin` and `/vote admin test` are not in the approved list; staff see everything in `/logs`, category rewards).

**Voting** (M10.5, `votes.yml`)
- **Receiver:** our own Votifier receiver (NuVotifier protocol **v1** = RSA public key and **v2** = one token per site), on its own threads, never the main thread. **Off by default** (`receiver.enabled: false`) until the server is registered on the vote sites; the keys are made on the first start anyway: `plugins/PhoenixSkyblock/votifier/rsa/public.key` (paste into v1 sites) and `votifier/tokens.txt` (one token per site id, paste into v2 sites). Secrets are never in a config file, so `/admin` and `/skyblock config` never show them. Port `receiver.port` (8192), address `receiver.host` (0.0.0.0).
- **Limits:** at most `receiver.max-packet-bytes` (4096) read, the whole vote within `receiver.timeout-ms` (5 s, a hard deadline), `receiver.max-per-minute` (60) connections per address, `receiver.max-open` (16) at once. Anything malformed is closed (bad size, wrong key or token, wrong challenge, bad JSON, unknown site, strange names); rejects reach the console at most once a minute (`receiver.reject-log-seconds`, the line counts the rest). Unknown service names are logged so they can be added to `sites`.
- **Sites** (`sites`, one line each: `id | name | service names | link`): PlanetMinecraft, Minecraft-MP, MinecraftServers.org, TopG, Minecraft-Server-List. Service names match without case, dots or `www.`.
- **Per vote** (P10-13): 1 bound **Ember key** + **3 gems** + **25 pass XP** (pass XP only once the battle pass exists), **one reward per site per account per 24 hours** (`rewards.cooldown-hours`), for at most **3 accounts per voter address per day** (owner rule 292, `rewards.accounts-per-address`; only a SHA-256 hash of the address is stored). Paid to the **profile you are on** (129). Without the crates module a key pays its crystal fallback.
- **Offline votes** wait and are paid at your next join (to the profile you are on then); a name that never joined waits **7 days** (`rewards.unknown-name-days`), a known offline player 60 days (`rewards.offline-days`). **Bedrock names:** a vote for `Steve` is also looked up as `.Steve` (the Floodgate prefix), and a Bedrock player `.Steve` also gets waiting votes sent as `Steve`; a Java player never takes a `.Name` vote.
- **Vote streak** (per account, days in a row with at least one paid vote; days start at 00:00 Europe/Oslo): day **7 Cinder, 14 Blaze, 21 Cinder, 30 Inferno**, then the cycle repeats (`streak.rewards`, `streak.cycle-days`). A missed day starts it over. Streak keys are bound.
- **Vote party** (P10-15): a server-wide counter of paid votes, size **500** (`party.size`). When it fills, everyone **online who voted today, finished the tutorial and is not AFK** (`/afk`, the AFK pool, or no own action for `playtime.idle-minutes`) gets a bound **Blaze key**; the counter starts over. Progress on the Tavern vote board (hologram `vote_party`, written through the npcs module, at most every `party.hologram-seconds`) and in `/vote`.
- **/menu Rewards button:** votes pay on arrival, so only votes that waited (offline) and are still unpaid count as ready; claiming pays them through the normal vote payment.
- **Crash-safe, never double:** a vote is stored first under its **vote id** (hash of site + name + the site's timestamp; the arrival minute when the site sends none), so the same packet twice is stored and paid once. A payment is **one transaction**: the row moves `queued` → `paid` (only if still queued), the 24-hour rule is checked again inside, the streak moves only if unchanged since it was read, the party counter moves (row lock), gems go through the ledger with the idempotency key `vote:<id>:gems`, and keys / pass XP are written as pending grants (handed out after the commit like daily rewards). A filled party is recorded in the same transaction (`vote_parties`); each party reward is one transaction with a primary key per party and account. A party a stop interrupted is marked `review` at the next start (`vote_party_review` in /logs).

**Audit** (category `rewards`): `vote_received`, `vote` (paid: site, profile, rewards, streak, streak reward, party counter), `vote_duplicate`, `vote_limited`, `vote_expired`, `vote_party`, `vote_party_reward`, `vote_party_review`. The economy writes `vote_reward` ledger rows.

**Daily calendar**
- One claim per profile per day; the day resets at **00:00 Europe/Oslo** (`calendar.zone`), also on DST days.
- The calendar day moves **only when you claim**: missing a day never sends you back. After day 30 it starts at day 1 again.
- Rewards (`calendar.days`, one line per day, P10-17): money = minutes of reference income at your tier (`pouch.ref-per-minute`, the balance sheet's REF(t), same numbers as crates), crystal pouches = base x 1.3^(tier-1), bound keys through the crates module (days 3, 7, 10, 14, 17, 21, 24, 28 Inferno, 30 Eclipse), bound personal boosters through the boosters module, a random enchant book of a rarity and Disenchant Scrolls through the enchants module. **Island Fuel** and **Wardrobe Tomes** are not built yet: they pay their crystal `fallback` (shown in the menu as *pays ◆ N for now*) until they exist.
- **Streak** = days in a row with a claim. Every 7 streak days: **10 gems** (§9.2, `streak.gem-every`, `streak.gems`). Titles at streak **30** (*Devoted*) and **100** (*Unbroken*) through the cosmetics module (account titles). A missed day starts the streak over (the calendar day stays); the lost streak is stored for the **streak save** (100 store credits within 2 days, R5), whose button stays hidden until the store exists (Phase 11).
- A join reminder tells you when today's reward is still open (`calendar.join-reminder`; players can turn it off in `/settings` → Chat → *Daily reward reminder*).

**Playtime rewards** (R6)
- Only **active** time counts: you did something yourself in the last 5 minutes (`playtime.idle-minutes`: turning the camera, walking on the ground, chat, commands, clicks, breaking, placing, hitting; being pushed by water or riding does not count), you are not marked AFK (`/afk`) and you are **not in the AFK pool**. Frozen, staff mode and profile switches pause it.
- Every hour: Crystal Pouch S (`playtime.hourly-crystals` 20, tier-scaled) + 20 pass XP once the battle pass exists (`playtime.hourly-pass-xp`; the hook waits until then). No money.
- Every 10 hours: 1 bound **Cinder key** (`playtime.key-every-hours`, `playtime.key-crate`).
- Titles at **50, 100, 250, 500 hours** (*Regular, Veteran, Dedicated, Timeless*, account titles).
- Time is counted every 20 s and saved every 60 s; a crash loses at most the last minute of counted time.

**Rules (crash-safe, never double)**
- A claim is **one database transaction**: the calendar (or the paid playtime milestones) is checked against what was read and moved, a claim row is written (primary key profile + date: a second claim on the same day is impossible), money, crystals and gems are paid through the ledger (reasons `daily_reward` / `playtime_reward`, idempotency key per claim and currency) and the hand-outs are written as **pending** grant rows. All of it commits or nothing does.
- Hand-outs happen after the commit: a row is moved `pending` → `sending` in one atomic update (never handed out twice), then keys go through `CrateKeys` (reasons `daily` / `playtime`, bound), items to your inventory when you are online on that profile (the rest to `/box`, then the player file is saved) or straight into that profile's box, titles through cosmetics. Calendar items are **bound** to the profile (`calendar.items-bound`). When a module is missing for a moment (crates disabled ...) the row waits and is tried again every 5 minutes and at the next start.
- A stop or crash in the middle of a hand-out leaves the row `sending`: at the next start titles are granted again (idempotent) and everything else is marked **review** (`grant_review` in /logs) so staff can check `/box` and give it by hand. A reward can be lost, never doubled.
- Not while frozen, in staff mode or during a profile switch; one claim at a time per player; a `/box` at its hard cap refuses claims.

**Audit** (category `rewards`, `/logs`): `daily_claim` (day, streak, broken/lost streak, tier, money/crystals/gems, rewards), `playtime_reward` (hours paid, keys, titles, crystals, pass XP), `grant_delivered` (items, fuel, pass XP; keys and titles are logged by crates and cosmetics), `grant_failed`, `grant_review`, `profile_reset` (deleted profile). The economy writes `daily_reward` and `playtime_reward` ledger rows.

**For other modules:** `AfkPool` (common, published by the afk module) tells whether a player is in the AFK pool; `PassXp` (common) is the battle pass hook the pass module will publish (M10.8).
