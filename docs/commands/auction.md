# Auction house (`modules/auction`)

Players sell items to each other for money. Every listing, sale, cancel and expiry is logged (audit category `auction`, actions `ah_list`, `ah_buy`, `ah_cancel`, `ah_expire`, `ah_collect`, `ah_list_failed`, `ah_buy_gated`, `ah_collection_drain`) with the full item (incl. the serialized stack, so staff can restore it); payments are in the ledger as `ah_fee`, `ah_buy`, `ah_sale` (`/transactions`, `/logs pay <player>`). Settings: `auction.yml`; texts: `messages/auction.yml`.

**Sanctions (Phase P7, spec 13.1):** listing (`/ah sell` and the sell menu) and buying check the moderation module's sanctions (block trading, freeze balance). When staff block a player's trading, all their active listings (every profile) are cancelled as staff removals: the items go to their `/box` (From auction), nothing is deleted (`ah_cancel` with `by_staff`).

| Command | What it does | Permission | Status |
|---|---|---|---|
| `/ah` (`/auction`, `/auctionhouse`) | Browse menu: categories (automatic by item type), search (chat prompt: item name or a seller's exact name), sort (newest, ending soon, lowest/highest price), Sell held item, My listings (cancel), summary (items for sale, **your listings `3/7`**, balance, fee, tax), buy confirm dialog. Listing tiles show `| Typical: $x each` when the item has a typical price. **Similar to your hand** (slot 5, batch 9): shows only listings of the exact item you hold; click again to show all. No collection bin (ECON-SELL-SHOP-AH C3): items that come back go to `/box`. Staff with `skyblock.staff.auction.remove` can remove any listing from it (pick a reason) | `skyblock.auction.use` | ✅ |
| `/ah sell <price> [12h\|24h\|48h\|7d]` | List the item in your main hand. Without a duration a dialog shows the fee, tax, what you receive and the typical price (no typical price yet: **Cheapest now** from the current listings of other players, batch 9), with one button per duration (`listing.durations`, tab completion from the config). Prices like `1500`, `1.5k`, `2M` (tab shows the `<price>` hint, owner rule #48: no number suggestions). A price per item more than `hints.warn-factor` (10) times above or below the typical price asks for the same click or command again within `hints.confirm-seconds` (30 s) | `skyblock.auction.sell` | ✅ |

Rules (all in `auction.yml`): listing fee 1% (min $1, paid when listing, not refunded on cancel by default), sales tax 5% (taken from what the seller receives), price $10 to $1T, 5 listings at once (more with `skyblock.auction.slots.<n>`, highest wins, capped by `listing.max-slots`; the rank table is the ranks lane's `server/permissions/base.txt (Ranks V2 section)`: New 5, Ashborn 6, Hatchling 7, Fledgling 8, Firewing 9, Flamecrest 10, Sunfeather 12, Pyrelord 14, Immortal 16, plus up to +2 from Economy achievements), no pacing (owner C6-Q3: `anti-abuse.listing-cooldown-seconds` 0 and `relist.max-same-item` 0, config v2 moves untouched old defaults), listings last 12 h, 24 h, 48 h or 7 days (same 1% fee), no new-account limit for listing or buying (owner answers 131/550: the tutorial will unlock it instead), frozen players can't use it. You can never buy a listing made by one of your own other profiles (owner answer 118; refused in the menu and again inside the purchase, audited `profile_transfer_blocked`). Deleting a profile deletes its auction data too (owner answer 118): its active listings are cancelled and their items deleted (not returned), its collection bin is emptied; logged `ah_profile_delete` / `ah_collection_delete` with the full item data. Not listable: starter kit items (`items.blocked-ids`, `items.blocked-data-keys`), money notes (until the notes module allows them), dupe-flagged items, staff items (`/give`, `/i`, creative; owner answer 332, also inside containers), filled shulker boxes/bundles, admin blocks (`items.blocked-materials`).

Money goes to the seller even when they are offline; online sellers get a chat line plus an action bar `✔ <buyer> bought your <item> +$<net>` (`notify.popup`), offline sellers a summary on their next join. Expired, cancelled and staff-removed items, unpaid-fee items and items bought with a full inventory go to the owner's **`/box`** (source *From auction*), also offline (owner C6-Q2). How: the state change still writes the item into `ah_collection` in the same transaction (outbox); `AuctionBoxDrain` deletes each row exactly once and hands the items to the box; a box failure puts the rows back and retries every `tasks.box-drain-seconds` (60). Drained at enable, on join, on a profile switch and after every expiry/cancel/removal; audit `ah_collection_drain`. Join line: `3 of your listings expired. They're in /box (From auction). [Open /box]`.

**Buying gate (C1-R1-07):** a profile must have **earned** `economy.yml send-gate.required` ($10k; AH sale money does not count) before it can buy, unless the seller is a member of its island. Checked on the main thread after the confirm and again inside the purchase (before the listing is claimed); a missing or failing economy gate refuses (fail closed). Message `buy.gated` with the missing amount; audit `ah_buy_gated` (earned, required). Switch: `buy-gate.enabled`.

**Typical price (C5):** every `hints.refresh-minutes` (5) an async job takes the sales of the last `hints.median-days` (7) days and stores the median price per item (`ah_price_stats`) for items with at least 5 sales, 5 different sellers and 5 different buyers (`hints.min-*`). There is no account-link (same-IP) lookup yet, so that filter is skipped; the 5/5/5 rule applies.

Other permissions: `skyblock.staff.auction.remove` (mods and up).

For developers: the listing state machine is `AuctionEngine` (pending → active → buying → sold; active → cancelled/expired; pending → failed). Each change is a conditional `UPDATE … WHERE status = …`; payments use `EconomyService` with idempotency keys `ah_fee:<id>`, `ah_buy:<id>`, `ah_sale:<id>`; listings left in pending/buying by a crash are resolved on startup and every `tasks.cache-resync-seconds`.

## Profiles (PROF)
Listings, the collection bin and seller notices belong to a **profile**: `ah_listings.seller`/`buyer` and `ah_collection.owner` hold profile keys (profile 1 = the player UUID, so old rows are profile 1's). A sale always pays the profile that listed, never the player's active profile. Items for a profile that isn't active (or while its player switches) go to that profile's `/box`. Buying a listing of your own other profile is refused (`buy.own-profile`, engine outcome `OWN_PROFILE`, audited `profile_transfer_blocked`). A profile with active listings can't be deleted (`profiles.delete-listings`); its `/box` follows the box module's profile rules.
