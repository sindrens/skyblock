# Depths commands

`/depths` itself needs `skyblock.depths.command`; each sub-command also needs its own node.

| Command | Description | Permission | Status |
|---|---|---|---|
| `/depths debug [player]` | Region at your feet, region flags there (regions, denied flags, bypass), targeted block, depleted counts, timers, break-handling time, fast-break flags, combat mobs (total, this zone, mini-boss timer) and ambient mobs | `skyblock.depths.debug` | ✅ |
| `/depths generate [zone\|all]` | Builds the placeholder layout from `zones.yml` by code in throttled slices (`depths.yml → generate.*`): The Gate (`gate`), a tier zone with its causeway (`mine_t01` …), the boss arena (`boss_arena`) or everything (`all`, default, asks to confirm). Idempotent (a second run changes nothing); never touches anything marked `built: true`; audited (`world`) | `skyblock.depths.generate` | ✅ |
| `/depths palette [auto\|1\|2\|3] [player]` | DV2-SPIKE tier palette (only while `depths.yml → palette.enabled: true`). No tier: status (pilot zone, tiers and their levels, the player's shown tier and level, swap count, last/worst swap block count and ms, last throttled swap's extra ticks, slot index size). A tier: show that player a lower **open** tier (D2 staff/debug toggle; clamped to the highest tier their Mining level opens, `auto` = by level, reset on quit and profile switch); audited (`world`, `depths_palette_tier`) | `skyblock.depths.palette` | ✅ |
| `/depths tp <zone>` | Teleport to the spawn of The Gate (`gate`), a tier zone or the boss arena (`boss_arena`); ignores tier locks | `skyblock.depths.tp` | ✅ |
| `/dungeon` | Tier menu: tabs Mine / Forest / Combat, every tier of the wing; unlocked tiers teleport to their arrival pad after a `teleport.yml → dungeon.warmup-seconds` countdown (moving, damage, combat or a freeze cancels), locked tiers show "✘ Mining 15 needed · 9/15" with a progress bar. Opens on the wing you stand in | `skyblock.depths.dungeon` (everyone) | ✅ |

Protection (DEPTHSPROT, no command): the whole Depths world is protected by the worlds module's region flags
(`worlds.yml → protection.*`, see [worlds](worlds.md)): no breaking or placing outside the ore/log fields, no
containers, workstations, trapdoors or other usable blocks (doors, buttons, levers, gates stay usable), no buckets,
fire, block-breaking explosions or item frame/armor stand edits. Builders bypass in creative mode
(`skyblock.worlds.protection.bypass` or `skyblock.depths.build`). Inside the fields the engine decides as before. Combat
zones: no ender pearls or wind charges. Safe zones of the combat builds (`kind: safe`): mobs never target or hurt you;
combat mobs drop a target standing there and are sent back to a spawn spot if they get in; the graveyard's safe zones
and the crypt's rail bands also stop players hitting mobs.

Tier locks (M4.6, no command): a tier's zone needs its wing's skill level (`zones.yml → required-level`). Players below it see bars in that gate (only them), are pushed back when they walk, teleport or pearl in, get no XP or drops there and are sent to The Gate if they stand in a zone that became locked. `skyblock.depths.bypass` (builders, admins) ignores all locks and the `/dungeon` warmup; `/depths tp` gives a one-zone pass. First unlock of a tier is remembered per season and audited (`world`, `depths_tier_unlock`); the first visit shows a title. Placeholders: `depths_zone`, `depths_tier`.

Respawn timers survive relogs (SECFIX1 F-11, no command): a block you mined stays depleted for you until its own timer ends, even if you leave, rejoin or change world in between (running timers are remembered per player, at most `depths.yml → engine.remembered-per-player`, 4,096). A server restart resets everyone the same way. A satchel take-out saves the player file right after the items were given.

Profiles (PROF): Depths tier unlocks/first visits belong to the active profile (`depths_tier_unlocks.player` = profile key); access still follows the live skill level of the active profile. Deleted profiles' rows are removed.

Satchel (M4.4, no command; the phase doc names none): `/dungeon` → **Satchel** button (`/menu` → The Depths → Satchel), permission `skyblock.depths.satchel` (everyone). Depths drops go into a per-**profile** satchel (table `depths_satchel_items`): menu with every item, amount, today's price and value, **Sell all**, per item **Sell** / **Take one stack** / **Take all that fit** (`satchel.take-out`), and **Upgrade** (money, `satchel.upgrades`). Capacity = `satchel.base-capacity` + `per-mining-level` × Mining + `per-foraging-level` × Foraging + bought upgrades + bonus sources (`SatchelService#capacityBonus`, for Deep Pockets later), max `satchel.max-capacity`. R3-22: a full satchel no longer stops mining: drops (mining and combat kills) go to the inventory, and what does not fit waits a few seconds (`satchel.box-flush-seconds`, 5) and then goes to `/box` in one delivery (source `depths`). The action bar warns at most every `satchel.full-warning-ms` (3,000 ms). Breaking stops only while the satchel loads or when the satchel is full **and** `/box` is at its hard cap (`box.yml cap.hard-max-entries`, 5,000 stacks): the block stays, no XP, nothing lost. Overflow amounts are in the batched `depths_drops` audit row (`overflow`) and the box's `box_deliver` rows. New drops are saved in batches (`satchel.save-seconds`); every take-out and sale is one database transaction (a sale removes the items in the same transaction as the payment and the sale record). The Forge counts inventory + satchel and takes the inventory first. Audit (`item`): `depths_drops` (batched per `satchel.audit-seconds`), `satchel_take`, `satchel_use` (Forge), `satchel_upgrade`; sales are the economy's `sell` rows (source `satchel`). Placeholders: `satchel_used`, `satchel_max` (Depths scoreboards: `Satchel 412 / 640`).

Drops and crystals (M4.9, no command): drop tables per wing and tier in `depths.yml → drops` (item, amount, crystal-chance, crystal-amount, rare finds), items in `depths.yml → drop-items` (ids = `sell.yml` keys `custom:depths_coal` … and Forge materials). Fortune comes from the tool (`tools.yml → fortune.*` + extra-drops bonuses, max `drops.max-per-block`); crystal and rare-find bonus families scale their chances. Crystals are paid per profile every `crystals.batch-seconds` in one ledger row (reason `depths_drop`, retried with the same idempotency key) and shown on the action bar. Blueprint chances stay in `tools.yml → blueprints.<id>.drop`. Event `DepthsDropEvent` is the hook for E2 enchant effects.

Tier palette spike (DV2-SPIKE, Depths v2 §3.1, off by default: `depths.yml → palette.enabled`): the ore field of `palette.zone` (`mine_t01`) shows each player the block of their active tier at the same spots: coal (Mining 0), copper ore (5), iron ore (10), taken from the zones in `palette.tiers` (`mine_t01..03`: field block, depleted look, respawn time, `required-level`, drop table tier). The real block stays coal (the softest), so tier-1 players get no packets. Breaking a slot gives the shown tier's drops, crystals, rare finds, fragments, Mining XP (by the shown block), tool XP, blueprints, quest/task counts and ambient mobs (`ResourceBreakEvent#getShownType()` / `#getTier()`); the anti-fast-break check times the shown block (`BreakMath.scaledSpeed`). The active tier refreshes on a skill level-up (title "Iron unlocked!", `palette.unlock-sound`, a particle wave over the swapped blocks; only inside the zone with `palette.effects-only-in-zone`), a profile switch, joining and the periodic tier check (quiet). A swap re-sends every slot in the chunks the client has, nearest first: Java in one multi-block change (`palette.java-blocks-per-tick`, 0 = no limit), Bedrock at most `palette.bedrock-blocks-per-tick` (300) per tick. Every swap is logged when `palette.log-swaps` is on (block count, queued part, ms; slow-operation timers `depths-palette-swap`, `depths-palette-drain`). Slot positions are indexed lazily per chunk (re-scanned after a builder edit there or a reload; WorldEdit edits need `/skyblock reload depths`). Combat zones never get a palette (owner: simple tier areas).
