# Economy (`modules/economy`)

Currencies: Money, Crystals (island crystals, replaced tokens; **never transferable**: a hard rule in code, owner R3-5b, whatever `tradeable.crystals` says: no /pay, trade, notes or any account-to-account move), Gems, Shards (whole-unit `long`). Every change is one atomic database transaction with a ledger row (`eco_ledger`) and an audit row (category `economy`, action = reason), so `/logs pay <player> [other]` and `/logs player <name> economy` show it. Settings: `economy.yml`; texts: `messages/economy.yml`.

**Sanctions (Phase P7, spec 13.1):** `/pay`, `/withdraw`, redeeming money notes and banking (personal and island) check the moderation module's sanctions first (freeze balance, block trading; given in `/p` → Sanctions, see `docs/commands/moderation.md`); the player is told why. Every transaction is also checked as a safety net: one that would break an active sanction is refused without any change and audited (`economy / sanction_blocked`). Staff actions (`/eco take|set|reset`, removing money as a sanction) still work. **Fail closed (SECFIX1 F-15):** if the sanction check itself throws, every transaction that takes money out of an account (pay, trade, auction, notes, shop, bank) is refused and audited `economy / sanction_check_failed`; deposits (selling, rewards) and staff `admin_*` actions go through. The entry-point hooks (`SanctionService.denied/deniedOther`) refuse too and tell the player (`moderation.yml → sanctions.unavailable`). Ledger reasons `sanction_remove` / `sanction_restore`.

## Economy dashboard + anomaly alerts (M3.8)
Settings: `economy-watch.yml`; texts: `messages/economy.yml → watch.*`; data: `db/economy/V006__economy_watch.sql`.

**How the numbers are made.** A background job (every `job.interval-seconds`, 60 s, never on the main thread) reads only the ledger rows after its saved cursor (`eco_watch_state`, last processed ledger id), in batches (`job.batch-size` 5,000, at most `job.max-batches-per-run` 20 per run), and adds them to summary tables: money **created / removed / moved** per UTC hour, currency and ledger reason (`eco_watch_flows`), and per profile and hour what was **earned / spent** (`eco_watch_accounts`; own bank moves and staff changes are left out, `earnings.ignore-reasons`). The sums and the new cursor commit in **one transaction**, so every ledger row is counted exactly once, also after a crash. A missing ledger id (a transaction still committing) is waited for up to `job.gap-grace-seconds` (120 s). The money supply (every balance in player, island and bank accounts + money notes not cashed yet) is snapshotted every `job.supply-minutes` (60) for the trend (`eco_watch_supply`). Old rows are purged daily (`retention.*`). The dashboard only reads these tables (and the ledger in small indexed windows for the lists).

**Transit reasons** (`flows.transit-groups`): money that only passes through the server counts by its difference, so the sink ratio is honest: the auction (buyer pays `ah_buy`, seller gets `ah_sale`) counts only its sales tax as a sink, money notes (`note_withdraw` / `note_redeem`) only staff notes beyond those made.

**Inflation risk badge** (ECONOMY_BALANCE §7.2, BAL2): 14-day sink ratio = removed / created. **OK** at or above `risk.watch-below-percent` (75), **Watch** below it, **ACT** below `risk.act-below-percent` (60) on `risk.act-days` (3) days in a row (staff get a `sink-ratio` alert once a day), **Not enough data** before `risk.min-days` (3). **Sell price index**: today's sell table as a basket weighted by base price, in % of base (100 = all at base). **Velocity**: money moved between accounts in 7 days / money supply.

**Anomaly alerts** (all thresholds in `economy-watch.yml → rules.*`, per currency in `alerts.currencies`):
| Rule | Fires when | Default |
|---|---|---|
| `earn-rate` | a profile earned in one hour more than `multiplier` × the baseline (average earning per active profile-hour over `baseline-days`, 14; ECONOMY_BALANCE R6) and at least `min.<currency>` | 3×, $5M |
| `big-transfer` | one transfer between two players (pay, trade), or one amount created at once (not staff), at or above `supply-percent` of the supply and `min.<currency>` | 0.5%, $50M |
| `repeat-transfer` | the same profile sent the same receiver `count` amounts within `tolerance-percent` of each other in `window-minutes` | 5 in 10 min, 5%, from $10k |
| `note-redeem` | a profile cashed `count` money notes in one hour | 10 |
| `sell-spike` | the whole server sold more in one hour than `multiplier` × the hourly sell average (14 days) and at least `min-amount` | 4×, $50M |
| `balance-mismatch` | an account's balance before a transaction is not what its previous transaction left: it changed **without a ledger row** (money from nowhere) | on |
| `staff-transfer` | owner answer 334: a pay/trade between two players where one is a staff account (`skyblock.staff.economy.flagged`, builders/helpers and up, remembered from their join), at or above `min.<currency>`; shown only to `alert-permission` (`skyblock.admin.economy`, admins) | $5M |
| `sink-ratio` | the badge says ACT | once a day |

An alert goes to online staff with `skyblock.staff.economy.anomalies` (chat line with **[Open]** → `/eco admin <id>` and **[Punish]** → `/p <player>`), the console, the audit log (`economy / eco_anomaly`) and Discord channel `alerts.discord-channel` (off until a webhook with that name exists). The same rule + profile is sent at most once per `alerts.cooldown-minutes` (60). Only hours from the last `alerts.max-age-hours` (2) make alerts, so the first start (the job reads the whole existing ledger once, in batches) and catching up after a downtime fill the numbers without old alerts. **Alerts never punish.** The evidence screen shows the ledger rows behind the alert (earn-rate: the profile's biggest incoming rows of that hour; sell spike: the biggest sales of that hour; others: the stored rows), the profile's full history, **Open /p**, **Suggest punishment** (files a suggestion with reason `alerts.suggest-reason` through the moderation module; staff still press Apply or Dismiss), **Mark reviewed**, **False alarm** and **Open again** (audited: `eco_alert_review`, `eco_alert_dismiss`, `eco_alert_reopen`, `eco_alert_suggest`). `alerts.auto-suggest-rules` (empty) can file suggestions at once for chosen rules.

**Sell multiplier cap.** `economy.yml → sell.multiplier-cap-percent` (default `300` = at most ×4.0, owner decision #46). Every sell bonus (rank, Sell Multiplier upgrade, sets, personal/global boosters, events) is added up and capped once. Sell code reads the cap with `EconomyService.sellBonusCapPercent()` and applies it with `MultiplierCap.apply(base, totalBonusPercent, cap)` (`common/economy`; rounds down, never overflows, never negative).

**Money notes (M3.2).** Right-click a note (main hand) to cash it; no permission needed. Each note is a row in `money_notes` (id = the item's unique id); the amount comes from that row, never from the item. Cashing locks the row, marks it `redeemed` and deposits in one database transaction (idempotency key per note), so a note can never be cashed twice: a copy is taken from the hand, staff with `skyblock.staff.notify` get an alert, and an audit row `note_dupe` is written (`/logs`). Notes can't be crafted with, sold to villagers or used in a cartography table, and can't be listed on the auction house (`auction.yml → items.allow-money-notes: false`). Audit actions: `note_withdraw`, `note_redeem`, `admin_note`, `note_dupe`, `note_return_failed`.

Placeholders (cached, for the HUD and other plugins via PlaceholderAPI): `balance`, `balance_formatted`, `crystals`, `crystals_formatted`, `gems`, `gems_formatted`, `shards`, `shards_formatted` (`_formatted` = `12,500` / `1.2M`, no symbol).

For developers: `core.services().optional(EconomyService.class)` (`common/economy`): `execute(Transaction)` with reasons from `TxReason`, a `reference` and an `idempotencyKey` for anything that can be replayed. Never decide with the cached balance; run the transaction and handle `INSUFFICIENT_FUNDS`.

## Profiles (PROF)
Money, crystals and shards belong to the active **profile**; gems belong to the player (owner P2/PR2). `/bal`, `/pay`, `/eco` and `/withdraw` use the active profile (offline players: their last active profile); gem changes always go to the player. `/baltop` shows each player's **best** profile (P7). `/transactions` shows the profile's history plus the player's gem history. `/pay` between two profiles of the same player and cashing a `/withdraw` note on another own profile are refused (`pay.own-profile`, `note.own-profile`) and audited (`profile_transfer_blocked`). Every profile gets the start money (`start.money`, once per season and profile; owner answers 121/121b, no limit on recreating profiles: money is per profile and goes when a profile is deleted). Gems keep one start balance per player. A deleted profile's money, crystals and shards are set to 0 (ledger reason `profile_delete`). Gem purchases of profile slots use reason `profile_slot`.

## Selling (M3.4)
**Prices.** `sell.yml` holds the base price, category and on/off (`{ price: 82, sellable: false }`) of every sellable item; vanilla items by item id, custom items under `custom-items` by custom id (a custom item is never priced as the vanilla item it is made of). Defaults come from `docs/ECONOMY_BALANCE.md` (BAL2) and are a **draft the owner approves before launch**. Items not listed can't be sold. **Never sellable**, whatever `sell.yml` says: unique items (custom tools, enchant books, money notes, set pieces: anything with a unique item id), dupe-flagged items, `sell.blocked-ids` (`money_note`, `starter_*`, `tool_*`), items with a `sell.blocked-data-keys` key (starter kit), and custom items whose type is not in `sell.custom-types` (default: materials only).

**Dynamic prices** (`economy.yml → sell.dynamic.*`, ECONOMY_BALANCE §8): price = base × multiplier (floor 40%, ceiling 150%). Each item sold lowers the multiplier by an *impact*; inside one sale the price drops item by item, so splitting or bundling a sale pays the same. Every 10 min the gap to base closes by 2%; items without a sale for 60 min drift up +10% per day toward the ceiling. The impact is recomputed every hour from the rolling 24 h volume so a steadily sold item settles at 85% of base (default 1 ppm while there are fewer than 1,000 sales in 24 h; clamped 0.01–100 ppm). Steps, the hourly recompute and saving (`sell_prices`, every 5 min and on shutdown) run off the main thread; a restart applies the steps missed while the server was off (max 168 h). **Hot Item:** one item a day (Europe/Oslo date, picked from the date, `sell.hot-item.*`) sells for +25%; it changes the price, not the sell bonus. The menu shows the price per item, the trend over the last hour (Rising/Falling/Steady: words, the ▲▼ arrows are not Bedrock-safe) and the % from base.

**Sell bonus.** Every `BonusFamily.SELL` source of the `BonusService` (rank, island upgrades, sets, enchants such as Gilded Touch, boosters, events …) is added up and capped once at `EconomyService.sellBonusCapPercent()` (+300%), applied to the subtotal after dynamic prices. The menu lists each source and says when the cap cuts the total.

**Sell chest (SELL2).** Six rows: slots 0-35 are free space, row 5 a divider, the bottom row: 45 Back (when opened from the shop or satchel), 46 Sell inventory (every sellable stack of the inventory goes in; armor, offhand and, with `sell.chest.keep-hotbar-tools`, tools/weapons in the hotbar stay), 47 Total, 48 Info, 49 Close, 50 Help, 51 Your sell bonus, 52/53 Sell. Unsellable items are refused when you put them in (the action bar says why) and anything unsellable that still ends up inside is bounced back. Big sales (at least `sell.big-sale-chat-from`, $100k) post **one chat line** whose hover lists every item (most valuable first, `sell.big-sale-hover-lines` = 10), the bonus and the total; smaller sales only show an action bar line. This applies to `/sell hand` and `/sell all` too.

**Sell chest safety.** The menu framework cancels every click; the chest re-allows only plain clicks and drags that stay inside the free space and the player's own inventory (shift-click into the chest and Sell inventory are done by the server; double-click, number keys, drop and creative clicks never work there). While the chest is open its contents are copied into the player's own data (persistent data container, key `phoenixskyblock:sell_chest`), which vanilla saves in the same file as the inventory; a slot about to lose its item leaves the copy first, so a crash can neither dupe nor lose chest items. The player's data is saved at most every `sell.chest.save-player-data-ticks` (100) while the contents change. Closing the chest for any reason (Close, Esc, logout, death, teleport, profile switch, shutdown) hands every item back: inventory, then ender chest, then the feet (message; audited as `item / sell_return_overflow`). Items a crash left in the copy are handed back on the next join (`item / sell_chest_recovered`; to the profile they came from). While a sale runs the chest is locked; closing it meanwhile is safe.

**Safety.** On a sale the stacks are checked again, taken out on the main thread (escrow), the player's data is saved (`sell.save-player-data`, so a crash after the payment can't roll the inventory back to a state that still holds them), then **one** ledger transaction (reason `sell`, idempotency key `sell:<sale id>`) pays the active **profile** and writes the `sell_sales` record in the same database transaction. If it fails, every stack is handed back (inventory, then ender chest, then the feet; if the player left or switched profile, stored in `sell_refunds` and handed back on the next join/switch to that profile, exactly once). The price drop is reserved before paying and given back on failure, so two sales at once can't both get the old price. Frozen players and players switching profile can't sell.

**Logs.** One ledger row and one audit row (category `economy`, action `sell`) **per sale**, never per stack; the audit row holds every item and amount, the line values and multipliers, the subtotal, the bonus and the source (`chest`, `hand`, `all`, `satchel`, later `afk`). `/logs player <name> economy`, `/transactions` (type "Selling").

For developers: `core.services().optional(SellService.class)` (`common/sell`): `price(stack)`, `price(key)`, `sellable(stack)`, `quote(stacks, bonus)`, `bonusPercent(player)`, and `sell(SellRequest)` for stacks the caller already removed (give back `SellResult.giveBack()` afterwards), `openSellChest(player, parentMenu)` (SELL2: the shop and satchel Sell buttons). Satchel (M4.4), AFK pool and auto-sell chests use this.

## Banks (M3.3, BANK2)
`/bank` (and the island menu's **Bank** button) shows both banks. Settings: `bank.yml`; texts: `messages/economy.yml → bank.*`; data: `eco_accounts` (owner type `bank` = a profile's personal bank, `island` = an island bank), `eco_bank_state`, `eco_bank_withdrawn`, `eco_bank_activity`.

- **Personal bank:** one per **profile** (owner P2), only reachable from that profile (P4: no deposits to another own profile's bank; tried transfers are refused and audited `profile_transfer_blocked`). Kept when you leave or are kicked from an island. **10 tiers** (`personal.tiers`: capacity, unlock cost = 2% of capacity, payout cap; balance sheet §6.1). The next tier is bought in the tier menu (confirm screen; ledger reason `bank_tier`). Perks per tier: `messages/economy.yml → bank.perks.tier-N` (shown only; owner list pending, #45). A deleted profile's bank is set to 0 (`profile_delete`).
- **Island bank:** belongs to the island. Role permissions in `/is permissions`: **Bank deposit** (default Admin, Mod, Member), **Bank withdraw** (default Admin), **Bank history** (default Admin, Mod); the Leader always has all three, coop players and visitors never (REVIEW A4). Daily withdraw limit per role in % of the bank capacity (`island.withdraw-limit-percent.*`: Leader/Admin no limit, Mod 25%, Member 10%; Europe/Oslo day). Capacity by the Bank Capacity upgrade level, payout cap by the Bank Interest level (`island.levels`, §6.2; level 0 until Phase 5 upgrades exist). Online members see each deposit/withdrawal in chat. On `/is disband` what is left is deleted (owner answer 263: it belongs to the island; ledger reason `bank_island_deleted`, the online leader is told); a staff delete or reset keeps it in the closed bank for staff to decide. (`island.pay-leader-on-disband` was removed.)
- **Interest** (`interest.*`, BANK2 = owner #169): personal **0.6%** / island **1.0%** every **8 h**, at fixed times (00:00, 08:00, 16:00 UTC = 01/09/17 or 02/10/18 Oslo time), also offline; payout caps are 4× the M3.3 ones, so interest per day is +50% (balance sheet §6.0). The menu's **Interest** clock shows `| Interest every 8h`, rate, cap, a live next-payout countdown and the payout clock times. Paid on the **lowest balance during the period** (deposits count from the next period), capped per payout and by the free room, only when the profile (island: one member) played ≥ 2 h in the last 7 days (owner #38); minutes spent in the AFK pool don't count (owner R3-25, other idle time does). Paid by a background run every 5 min (off the main thread, batches of 200) and when a bank is opened or used; each payout is one ledger transaction (`interest`) with the idempotency key `bank_interest:<account>:<minutes>m:<period>` (3 h kept the old `bank_interest:<account>:<period>`), so restarts or double runs never pay twice. After downtime at most 3 missed payouts (one day) are paid. `interest.period-hours` takes effect after a restart: every bank state remembers its period length (`eco_bank_state.period_minutes`) and is converted once at start before the bank opens (`bank_period_convert` in the audit log; no stretch of time is paid twice, at most part of one period is skipped). An existing `bank.yml` with the untouched M3.3 values (3 h, 0.15/0.25%, old caps, 8 catch-up payouts) gets the BANK2 values on the first start (`bank_config_upgrade`); values the owner changed are kept.
- **Withdrawals:** **2% fee** on the personal and the island bank (`withdraw.fee-percent`; rounded up, leaves the economy, ledger `bank_fee` with `fee_percent` in the details) and that is the **only** rule (owner R3-41: no lost interest; `withdraw.free-minutes` is 0, the menu no longer mentions it). **Island masteries lower the fee** (owner #169): bank-fee mastery level 1 / 2 / 3 = 1.5% / 1.0% / 0.5% (`withdraw.mastery-fee-percent`, a step can never be above the one before). The island bank uses its island's level, a personal bank the level of the profile's island (no island = 2%). The Withdraw button shows the fee with the discount (`Fee: 1.5% (-0.5% mastery)`) or how low masteries can take it; the confirm screen shows amount, fee and what you get. Deposit and withdrawal are each one ledger transaction (`bank_deposit`, `bank_withdraw` + `bank_fee` in the same atomic batch); capacity, bank balance and the daily island limit are checked inside that database transaction.

For developers: `core.services().optional(BankService.class)` (`common/economy`): `open`, `openIslandBank`, `personalTier`, `personalBalance`, `islandBalance`. Accounts: `AccountRef.bank(profileKey)`, `AccountRef.island(islandId)`. Island hooks: `IslandService.hasIslandPermission`, `bankCapacityLevel`, `bankInterestLevel`, `bankFeeMasteryLevel` (BANK2; 0 until `/is masteries` calls `bankFeeMasteryProvider`), `IslandRemovedEvent`.
