# Crates (`modules/crates`)

Crates and keys (PHASE-10-REWARDS.md **M10.1**, owner decisions CA1-CA7). Keys are earned only and live per **profile** and crate as two counters: **bound** (votes, AFK pool, daily calendar, playtime, rank claims, tutorial: never withdrawn or traded) and **tradeable** (quests, battle pass, events, weekly top, fragments: can be withdrawn as key items, M10.4). Opening uses bound keys first. Crates and reward tables: `crates.yml`; settings: `crates-settings.yml`; texts: `messages/crates.yml`. Reload: `/skyblock reload crates`.

| Command | What it does | Permission | Status |
|---|---|---|---|
| `/crates [crate]` | Opens the crates menu: your keys and every crate with keys, pity and top-band odds. With a crate (`ember`, `cinder`, `blaze`, `inferno`, `phoenix`, `eclipse`; tab-completed) it opens that crate's reward preview straight away. Also in `/menu` (tripwire hook) and `/help crates` | `skyblock.crates.use` (everyone) | ✅ |
| `/crates admin give <player> <crate> <amount> [bound\|tradeable]` | Staff (P10-41): adds keys to the player's **active** profile (offline: the last active one). Type left out = **bound** (`admin.default-tradeable`). Amount 1 to `admin.max-amount` (1,000); more than `admin.confirm-above` (64) asks to confirm. Audited as `key_give` (actor = staff, reason `staff`, bound yes/no). An online player on that profile gets a chat line. Console OK | `skyblock.crates.admin` (admin) | ✅ |
| `/crates admin take <player> <crate> <amount>` | Staff (P10-41): takes keys from the active profile, **bound first, then tradeable**, all or nothing (never below 0; with too few keys nothing is taken and you see how many there are). Confirm above `admin.confirm-above`. Audited as `key_take` (bound/tradeable taken, before). Console OK | `skyblock.crates.admin` (admin) | ✅ |
| `/crates admin view <player>` | Staff (P10-41): keys (bound + tradeable) and pity per crate. In game a menu that starts on the active profile; the armor-stand button switches profile. Console: one block per profile. Audited as `crates_view` (category staff) | `skyblock.crates.admin` (admin) | ✅ |

**Menus** (6 rows, Bedrock-safe). `/crates`: keys summary in the top middle, the 7 crates in a row (Conqueror shows *Coming soon* until KOTH exists). Java: **left-click = preview**, **right-click = open 1** (with the spinner), **shift-click = open 10** (`open.bulk-amount`). Bedrock: a **tap opens the preview**, which has buttons for everything. **Preview**: every reward as its item with the V2 line `★ Name (xN) | chance%` (★ = top band; the chance is the reward's real share of the table), money and crystal pouches show what they pay **at your tier**, coming-soon rewards say what they pay now; keys and `Pity 7/23` in the top middle; buttons **Open 1**, **Open 10**, **Open all** (up to `open.max-per-click`, 64), and (M10.4) **Withdraw 1 key** / **Withdraw an amount** (number picker, Bedrock-safe) for tradeable keys. **Open 10 / Open all** skip any animation and show a **summary screen** (equal rewards merged, top rewards first) plus a short chat summary.

**Crate yard (M10.3).** The crates stand on the crate yard pedestals (build points `crates`: `common` … `monthly`, mapping `physical.points`) as **real vanilla blocks** (`physical.blocks`: Ember barrel, Cinder chest, Blaze copper chest, Inferno ender chest, Phoenix vault, Eclipse ominous vault), so Bedrock sees them too. They are placed when the pedestal's chunk loads (never force-loaded) and only over air or an older crate block (`physical.replace`); they can't be broken. After a start, reload or chunk load the pedestals are set up a few per tick (PERF10: `physical.place-per-tick` 2, `physical.place-budget-ms` 2; at least one per tick). A hologram above each shows the crate's name in its rarity color, **Click: Open** and **Sneak-click: View rewards**. A **plain click** (either button, a Bedrock tap) opens 1 with the in-world show; **sneak-click** shows the rewards; **without a key** a click shows the rewards and how to get keys. The **key board** (build hologram `key_board`, placed by the npcs module) lists how to get keys and the top-reward chance of every crate (`key-board.*`). **Conqueror** stays `/crates`-only until the builder adds a 7th pedestal: then add one line to `physical.points` (e.g. `conqueror:conqueror`).

**Key items (M10.4).** No new command: everything is in the crate preview of `/crates`.
- **Withdraw:** **Withdraw 1 key** (one click = 1) or **Withdraw an amount** (number picker: -/+ 1/10/64, Min/Max, type a value; up to `key-items.max-per-withdraw`, 64) turns **tradeable** keys into key items (`crate_key`, a tripwire hook named after the key in the crate's rarity color, unique id, dupe-checked). Items go to the inventory, the rest to `/box`. **Bound keys can never be withdrawn** (the button says "No tradeable keys"). Keys made from fragments are tradeable, so they can be withdrawn. Not while frozen, in staff mode, during a profile switch, with a trade block, or with a box at its hard cap.
- **Redeem:** **USE** a key item (Java right-click, Bedrock use/long-press) to put the key back on your **active** profile as a tradeable key, exactly once. The item remembers who withdrew it (account + profile): **it can't be redeemed on another profile of the same account** (owner P4; the item goes back and the refusal is logged). Any other player can redeem it.
- **Trading:** key items are normal unbound items: `/trade` and the AH accept them (behind the tutorial's existing `/ah` and `/trade` locks; AH category *Special*). They can't be crafted with (a tripwire hook in a recipe) and are never placed as a block.
- **Copies:** the item's unique id is the `crate_key_items` row. A used-up (or unknown) id is a copy: it stays taken, the holder is told, an audit row `key_item_copy` is written and it is reported to the DupeDetector (staff alert, audit `item/dupe`, R3-28 automatic trade block).
- **Crash safety (SECFIX1):** withdraw = one transaction (take the keys + write the active rows), then the items are given and the player file saved; a crash in between loses the items, never duplicates. A player who left or switched profile before the items were made gets the keys back on the profile they came from (`key_withdraw_refund`). Redeem = item out of the hand, player file saved, then one transaction (row active → redeemed + 1 key); a failed save or transaction gives the item back (offline: into that profile's `/box`).
- Settings: `crates-settings.yml → key-items.*` (`enabled`, `material` and `glint` read at start, `max-per-withdraw`).

**Animations (M10.3, cosmetic only).** The reward is rolled, saved and handed out first; the show starts afterwards, so closing the menu or leaving mid-show changes nothing (the result message comes at once).
- **Spinner (B40)** for **Open 1** in `/crates` and the preview: a 3-row menu where the crate's rewards scroll through slots 10-16 and slow down onto slot 13 (~3 s, `spinner.duration-ticks`, soft tick `spinner.tick-sound`). Click the row or the marker to skip. Bedrock: at most 20 menu updates (`spinner.bedrock-frames`, capped at 19 + the final screen).
- **In-world show** at a pedestal: rarity particles rise around the crate (`animation.build-ticks`), then the reward floats up above the crate with its name and the opener's name for 3 s (`animation.float-ticks`), visible to everyone nearby, with a firework for a ★ reward (harmless). The opener also gets the result as a chat line and a title (Bedrock may not show the floating item).
- **Fast crate opening** (`/settings` → Gameplay): skips the spinner and the build-up; at a pedestal the reward still floats up, without waiting.
- **Win messages:** a ★ reward from the crates in `broadcast.crates` (Inferno, Phoenix, Eclipse, Conqueror) is one line in global chat (Java: hover the reward to see the item; Bedrock: the same line as plain text). Everything else only goes to the opener. Open 10 / Open all: one line with the first ★ reward and how many more.

**Rules**
- **Rolls are decided first.** One database transaction per click takes the keys (bound first), moves the pity counter, writes one `crate_opens` row per open, adds won keys and pays money / crystals / shards through the ledger (reason `crate_reward`, idempotency key `crate_open:<batch>:<currency>`). Everything commits or nothing does; if the keys or pity changed meanwhile, nothing is taken and it is tried again (`open.retries`).
- **Items** (enchant books, Disenchant Scrolls, Set Tokens, spawners, elytra, boosters) are created by their own services with origin `crate:<id>` (dupe detection) after the commit, go to the inventory and the rest to `/box` (offline / other profile: straight into that profile's box), then the player file is saved (SECFIX1 PlayerSaves). **Cosmetics** (tags, titles) unlock account-wide; **Island Fuel** goes to your island.
- **Bound loot (REVIEW3 R3-2):** items from a bound key carry a hidden bound tag (`common/item/BoundItems`): they can't be traded, listed on the AH or picked up by another profile. Keys won with a bound key are bound too.
- **Pouches scale with your tier:** Money Pouch = N minutes of `REF(t)` (`pouch.ref-per-minute`), Crystal Pouch = base x 1.3^(t-1) (`pouch.crystal-growth`). `t` = your best stage tier (highest of mining/foraging/combat level, the AFK pool's rule).
- **Pity:** per profile and crate, counts opens without a top-band reward; at the crate's `pity` number the open is forced into the top band. Resets when `pity.season-id` is raised (season reset).
- **Duplicates:** a tag/title you own re-rolls into one you don't (same reward, then another cosmetic of the same band); own them all = its crystal value.
- **Boosters (M10.2):** `booster` rewards give a personal booster item (always bound to the opener, RX39) and `global-booster` rewards a global booster item (bound when opened with a bound key, and by default always), made by the boosters module (`BoosterService`) and handed out like other items. Without the boosters module (or numbers outside its limits) they pay their crystal value. Existing `crates.yml` files are upgraded at start: a `coming-soon` reward whose bundled twin (same crate and id) is live now gets the new type and its keys (`booster`, `percent`, `minutes`); your chance, top band and icon stay, and rewards you changed are never touched.
- **Coming soon:** rewards whose system is not built yet (tomes, skins, sell wands, collectors, blueprints, pet eggs) keep their chance and pay their crystal value (`fallback`) until then, so the odds never change.
- Not while frozen, in staff mode or during a profile switch; one open at a time per player. A box at its hard safety cap refuses opens.

**Audit** (category `crates`, `/logs`): `key_give` (every key grant, reason = source or `staff`, bound yes/no, actor), `key_take` (staff take: crate, profile, bound/tradeable taken, counters before), `crate_open` (one per click: crate, keys used, pity after, money/crystals/shards, rewards), `crate_grant_failed` (an item/cosmetic/fuel could not be handed out: staff give it by hand), `crate_review` (startup: an item reward was rolled while the server stopped mid hand-out; check inventory and `/box`), `crate_profile_reset` (deleted profile), `key_withdraw` (crate, profile, item ids, how many went to /box), `key_withdraw_refund`, `key_redeem` (crate, item, redeeming profile, who withdrew it), `key_redeem_refused` (P4: same account, other profile), `key_item_copy` (copy taken), `key_item_return_box` (a refused or failed redeem's item went to /box because the player was offline or on another profile). The economy writes `crate_reward` ledger rows.

**For other modules:** `CrateKeys` (common) `giveKeys(profileKey, crate, amount, reason, actor)`: `reason` decides bound vs tradeable (`keys.bound-sources` / `keys.tradeable-sources`; unknown = bound). The AFK pool's pending keys are delivered through it at the player's next join/check after this module runs.

**Staff tools (P10-41, owner approved):** `/crates admin give|take|view` above (admin only, tab completion on every argument, all crates incl. coming-soon ones, works from the console). Settings in `crates-settings.yml → admin.*`: `max-amount` (1,000), `confirm-above` (64), `confirm-seconds` (15, read at start), `default-tradeable` (false = bound), `notify-player` (true). `reset` and `test roll` from the original idea are not built (not in the approved list).

## Safety (for developers)
- `crate_opens.grant_state`: `done` (paid in the transaction), `pending` → `delivered` / `failed`. On startup pending **cosmetics** are granted again (idempotent); pending **items/fuel** become `review` and are never handed out twice (lose at worst, never duplicate).
- Key counters only change through conditional `UPDATE`s (`bound_amount >= ?`); pity through compare-and-set on the old value.
