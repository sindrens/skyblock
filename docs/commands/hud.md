# Scoreboard (M1.4, module `hud`)

No commands. Board shape (G7-R1-01, UI_STYLE §6.1): the title `PHOENIX SKYBLOCK` on every board, a `| You` section, one section named after the place (`| Island`, `| Visiting`, `| Spawn`, `| <Depths zone>`, `| Warzone`), the footer; 10 lines at most with the title. Each section takes at most `scoreboard.section-rows` (3) rows, a header with no row disappears, and the `<soft-blank>` line under the title shows only while the other lines are at most `scoreboard.blank-under-title-max-lines` (8). Built-ins: `<progress:key>` (a placeholder percent as `scoreboard.progress-length` dots), `<plain:key>`, `<timer>` and the condition key `board_timer`. Service `BoardTimers` (common `hud`, published by hud): `register(owner, player -> Optional<Timer>)` / `unregister(owner)`; the soonest-ending timer of all providers is the board's timer row (boosters register; events later). Providers run on the main thread about once a second per player: map reads only. Placeholder `island_here_owner` (islands) feeds the visiting board's Owner row. Layouts in `messages/hud.yml`, settings in `hud.yml` (`places`: which layout on your own island, visiting, The Depths and its zones, spawn, warzone; SB). Service `HudService`: `setEnabled(player, bool)` (sets the `/settings` toggle `scoreboard`, registered by hud; the sidebar hides/shows right away), `addLayout(owner, layout, predicate)` (events; wins over `places`), `scoreboard(player)` (nametag teams for M1.5).

No permissions.
