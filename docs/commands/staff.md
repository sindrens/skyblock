# Staff tools (M1.9, module `staff`)

Settings in `staff.yml`, texts in `messages/staff.yml`, data in `db/staff` (`staff_mode`, `staff_frozen`, `reports`, `report_dm_evidence`). Every action is audited (category `staff`; frozen chat in `chat`, logout while frozen in `punishment`).

Staff tools dispatch `/p` (moderation module), `/logs` (M1.10) and `/invsee`/`/ecsee` (essentials); missing ones say "isn't available yet".

Permission definitions: `modules/staff/src/main/resources/permissions/staff.yml`. Rank grants: `server/permissions/staff.txt`.

False reports and report blocks (Phase P 14.5): closing a report as `invalid` (the only outcome that counts; no-evidence and duplicate are honest mistakes) tells the staff member "This is Steve's 3rd invalid report in 30 days." and asks moderation's `FalseReports` service whether to suggest the `report-abuse` reason (threshold 3 in 30d, at most one suggestion per player per week, never automatic; settings in `punishments.yml → extras.false-reports`, fallback window `reports.invalid-window-days` without moderation). A `report-block` punishment stops `/report` until it ends ("You can't use /report for 2d (too many false reports)."); stored in `report_blocks`, lifted by undo/rollback; provided to moderation as the `ReportBlocking` service (`common/moderation`). Audit: `report_block`, `report_block_lift`.

Report link (Phase P 12.9): when a staff member punishes a player, the reports about that player **claimed by that staff member** close automatically with outcome punished (reporters are told as usual); unclaimed reports stay open. Provided to moderation as the `ReportLink` service (`common/moderation`).

DM evidence (STAFF-R3, R3-9a): `/report` copies the last `reports.dm-evidence.count` (10) private messages between the reporter and the reported player (both directions; audit category `dm`, action `dm`) into `report_dm_evidence`, and tells the reporter. That is the only way DMs become evidence. Staff with `skyblock.staff.logs.category.dm` (moderators and up, the same node as DMs in `/logs`) see a **Private messages** button in the report menu that prints them in chat; every view is audited (`report_dm_view`). Kept `reports.dm-evidence.keep-days` (180) days, then deleted by a background job every `purge-hours` (audit `report_dm_purge`). The `/rules` privacy lines say so.

Report reasons (R3-31): Scamming, Threats and Griefing were added before Other; a live `staff.yml` that still has the old default list is upgraded once (config version 2); an edited list is kept.

Staff creative on islands (F15, owner and admin, `skyblock.staff.island-creative`): entering `/s` turns the island protection bypass on by itself and leaving turns it off (logged, reason `staff_mode`). While in `/s`, `/gm c` works in the islands world (essentials creative gate) and the creative inventory can be used (kit tools stay locked). Every block placed or broken in creative on an island is written to the audit log in batches every `island-creative.log-flush-seconds` (actions `island_creative_place` / `island_creative_break`, target = island id, details = coordinates + material of every block). Items from the creative inventory carry the staff-item tag (essentials `staff-powers.tag-staff-items`: can't be sold, traded, auctioned or sent), creative-placed blocks on islands are remembered as staff blocks (mining them gives staff items back), and leaving `/s` restores the saved inventory and game mode as before (creative ends). No new command.
