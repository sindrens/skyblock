# Guide module (`modules/guide`): the island guide Kindle (docs/planning/ISLAND_GUIDE.md)

| Command | Description | Permission | Status |
|---|---|---|---|
| `/is guide` | Open Kindle's menu anywhere (plan 4 Nov C): his quests, the shared Next step (shows it and does it: `/start`, a teleport or a menu), his finds; island level with progress, upgrades, island tasks, milestones; island tools (`/tool`), `/farm`, `/mine`; Help on 50. Clicking Kindle on your island does the same. Kindle appears the moment you arrive (teleport, join, invite accepted, the spot's chunk loading), not on the next 5 s check | `skyblock.island.guide` | ✅ |
| `/is guide move` | Leader and admins: Kindle moves to where you stand (inside your island border) | `skyblock.island.guide.move` | ✅ |
