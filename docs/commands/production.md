# Production (`modules/production`)

**Switch: OFF by default.** `production.yml → fuel.enabled: false` until the owner enables production; then `/skyblock reload production` (or a restart). While off, nothing changes anywhere: spawners and crops work as before, the AFK pool keeps its fuel banked, the daily calendar and crates pay their fallback, the Fuel Tank upgrade shows "Coming soon" and the Fuel Efficiency mastery stays hidden. `fuel.pause-when-asleep` (default on) decides whether an Asleep island really pauses spawners and crops.

**Island Fuel** (owner DESIGN §5.4, PD1–PD5, R5 / rule 9)
- **States** (checked every `fuel.check-seconds`, 60): **Active** = a stored member is on the island of their **active profile** and did something real in the last `fuel.active-minutes` (15): break/place, hit a mob, harvest, fish, use a menu, chat, or walk `fuel.walk-blocks` (5) blocks not in water, a vehicle or a bubble column. The island earns **+1 min of fuel per minute** (per island, never per member). **Fueled** = nobody active, tank > 0: the tank burns. **Asleep** = nobody active, tank empty: **spawners stop (even next to a player) and crops don't grow** (vanilla growth and the stacking module's dark growth). Coop players, visitors, The Depths and spawn never earn fuel; standing AFK burns it. A member's action on an Asleep/Fueled island makes it Active right away.
- **Tank:** 12 h + 2 h per **Fuel Tank** upgrade level (36 h at level 12; `/is upgrade`, Production tab). **Burn:** `fuel.burn-rate` (1.0) ÷ (1 + Fuel Efficiency%). Fuel Efficiency = the **Fuel Efficiency** mastery (MA8: +10 / +20 / +30%) + island achievements (fuel boost, max +10%), one family `FUEL_EFFICIENCY`, cap **+40%** (`enchanter.yml → caps.fuel-efficiency`).
- **Fuel Cells** (1 h / 3 h / 6 h, `cells.*`): bound to the profile (never tradeable), unique (dupe scanner). **USE** (right-click) while standing on your own island. A cell is only used when it **fits completely** (otherwise "room for 2h 10m", item kept). The cell is taken first, then one transaction marks its id used and adds the fuel; a refusal or error gives it back (inventory, else `/box`). A copy of a used cell is removed and logged. Sources: the daily calendar's fuel days (4, 12, 23) and crate fuel rewards turn into cells while Island Fuel is on (largest cells first, the rest as one 1 h cell).
- **AFK pool:** its banked fuel (15 min per reward) is added to the island tank as soon as Island Fuel is on (on join, on the next reward, and once for everyone online when it switches on). A full tank refuses; the grant stays banked.
- **Shown:** gauge in the island menu (state, fuel / tank, burn rate, how to get fuel); placeholders `<ph:island_fuel>` ("Active", "7h 20m" or "Asleep") and `<ph:island_fuel_state>`; a join message when your island ran out of fuel ("ran out of fuel 2h ago") and one line to online members when it falls asleep. The scoreboard has no fuel line yet (the island layout is full); add `| Fuel: <ph:island_fuel>` to `messages/hud.yml` if wanted.

**Safety**
- Tanks live in memory and are saved every `fuel.save-seconds` (30) and on stop; the time since the last save burns at the next start (nobody was active while the server was down). Every write (saves, grants, cells) runs in one ordered chain.
- Grants: one transaction writes the current value, then `UPDATE … SET fuel_ms = LEAST(fuel_ms + ?, cap) WHERE … AND fuel_ms < cap`. Cells: `… SET fuel_ms = fuel_ms + ? WHERE … AND fuel_ms + ? <= cap` plus the used-cell row (`production_used_cells`); a cell that doesn't fit rolls back completely.
- Audit (category `production`, `/logs`): `fuel_add` (island, minutes, source, actor, tank after, cell id) and `fuel_asleep`.

**Custom generators (M7.2, owner V4, PD6–PD9). Switch: OFF by default** (`generators.yml → enabled: false`, own switch next to Island Fuel); `/skyblock reload production` applies it.
- **What it does:** any lava + water generator on an island that forms **cobblestone or stone** (`form-blocks`) forms a block rolled from the island's **Generator level** table instead (`levels`, 0–10, weights from the plan: level 1 = 90% cobble + 10% coal … level 10 = 40% cobble, 6% each vanilla ore, 7% Ashstone, 5% Emberite). The "cobble" share keeps the block that formed. Basalt and obsidian stay vanilla. Level 0 = vanilla.
- **Generator level** = the **Generator** upgrade (`/is upgrade` → Production; "Coming soon" while generators are off, live while on). Staff test switch: `test.level-override` (-1 = off) gives every island that level.
- **Drops:** vanilla ore blocks drop their vanilla items at the island prices of `sell.yml` (`categories.ores`). The two **custom ores** (ores with `item=`): **Ashstone** (gilded blackstone, level 9+, `custom:ashstone` $180) and **Emberite** (nether gold ore, level 10, `custom:emberite` $396), custom items of type material (`custom-items.island-ores` in `sell.yml`, so `/sell` and the satchel price them). A custom ore block on an island **always** drops exactly one custom item (Silk Touch too, no Fortune), players can't place those blocks on islands (creative excepted), explosions and mobs don't break them. This guard runs even while generators are off.
- **XP:** no Mining skill XP (M4.5). **Mining pillar XP at `pillar-xp.percent` (25%)** of the matching Depths ore (`depths-xp`: coal 1 … Ashstone 28 = amethyst, Emberite 40 = quartz) for blocks the generator **really made** (remembered per island, `memory.per-island` 512 positions, a restart forgets) and only for members on their own island in survival (coop players and visitors: nothing). Island stats `GENERATOR_OUTPUT` and the wardrobe's island-ore set XP keep counting ore results.
- **Books:** every member's generator ore fires `IslandGatherEvent` (player, block, island, ore, tier, generator-made; `common/production`). The enchants module rolls Mining books on it at `enchanter.yml → drops.island-generator-percent` (25%) of a Depths ore action, custom pickaxe and generator-made blocks only. Area enchants never run on islands (they only react to Depths blocks).
- **Island Fuel:** while Island Fuel is on, an **Asleep** island forms plain cobblestone (`fuel.plain-when-asleep`); a member's action wakes it at once.
- **Lag guard:** at most `lag-guard.max-rolls-per-second` (40) rolls per island per second, then the plain block.
- **Hooks:** `GeneratorService` (common, published while on: `generatorLevel`, `maxGeneratorLevel`), `GeneratorOutput` (blocks a generator can form, for later listeners), `IslandGatherEvent`.
- No new commands (nothing to tab-complete); no database tables (memory only).

**Farming (M7.3, owner PD10–PD12). Switch: OFF by default** (`crops.yml → enabled: false`, own switch next to Island Fuel and generators); `/skyblock reload production` applies it. While off, crops are plain vanilla, Farming stays hidden in `/skills`, Crop Growth / Harvest Yield stay "Coming soon" and no harvest quests are offered.
- **Crop tiers** (`crops.yml → crops`, WORLD §5.1): Wheat 0 · Carrots, Potatoes 5 · Beetroot 10 · Sugar Cane 15 · Melons, Pumpkins 20 · Cocoa 25 · Cactus 30 · Sweet Berries 35 · Nether Wart 42 · Torchflowers 50 · Pitcher Plants 58 (Farming level to plant). Farming XP per ripe crop: 1 / 1.5 / 2.5 / 4 / 6 / 9 / 13 / 19 / 28 / 40 / 58.
- **Planting gate** (`planting.gate`): on islands a seed only places its crop with the crop's Farming level: "Farming 15 needed to plant Sugar Cane: 9/15" (one message per 1.5 s, creative skips it). Melon/pumpkin blocks and torchflower blocks are building blocks (not gated); their seeds are.
- **Farming XP** (PD11): ripe crops broken (sweet berries also picked) **with any hoe** (`xp.need-hoe`) by a stored member on their **own** island in survival. Never: coop players, visitors, creative, unripe crops, **bone-mealed** crops (positions remembered until harvested or replanted), placed melons/pumpkins/sugar cane/cactus/torchflowers (only blocks that **grew** count; a broken cane/cactus counts every grown block of its column). Positions live in memory (`memory.per-island` 4,096, a restart forgets: a few crops give no XP, never extra).
- **Farming pillar:** the skill XP feeds the Farming pillar at `pillars.yml → skill-sources` (farming 1.0); the pillar's own crop source (`island-sources.crops`) pauses while Farming pays, so nothing counts twice.
- **Growth:** every natural growth step on an island (vanilla light growth and the stacking module's dark growth, S25) gets extra stages by **(1 + Crop Growth %) × (1 + crop growth family %)**. Crop Growth = the upgrade (`/is upgrade` → Production, +12% per level); the family = the best "nearby crop growth" bonus (Cropwarden Robes) of members standing on the island, capped by `enchanter.yml → caps.crop-growth` (+100%); refreshed every `growth.refresh-seconds` (10). Sugar cane, cactus and pitcher plants grow at vanilla speed (columns and two-block plants). **Asleep** islands (Island Fuel on, empty tank) never grow (M7.1 cancels the growth before this runs).
- **Harvest Yield:** every ripe harvest on the island (members and coop players) gets extra copies of the crop item by the upgrade's chance (+12% per level; 120% = always double + 20% triple). Seeds are not copied.
- **Hooks:** `FarmingService` (common, published while on: `tierLevels`, `plantLevel`; skills, quests, wardrobe and progression read "farming is on" from it), `IslandCropHarvestEvent` (player, block, island, crop, tier, blocks, tool, hoe, bone-mealed, `xpEligible`) for E6, `GameActionEvent.HARVEST_CROP` for harvest quests (ripe, own island, members, no bone meal; no hoe needed).
- **Other modules while on:** `/skills` shows Farming (next unlock = the next crop tier); `/quests` offers `harvest <n>` (daily 300, weekly 1,500, island 3,000 in the bundled pools); Cropwarden Robes open at crop tier 6 (Farming 25) in the wardrobe; the Crop Growth and Harvest Yield upgrades can be bought.
- No new commands (nothing to tab-complete); no database tables (memory only); no money paths (skill XP is the skills module's, audited there by staff changes).
