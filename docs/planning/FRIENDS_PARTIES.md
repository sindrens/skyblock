## What exists in the game now
`/msg`, `/r`, `/ignore`, `/tpa`, island chat, island invites and coop. No friends, parties or guest book.

## The problem / what to decide
Three social features with no plan. Parties touch combat rewards, so they must not become an alt farm.

## Our proposal
**Friends (`/friends`):**
- Add by request (both accept). Max 50 (more with ranks).
- Menu: online friends first, where they are (island, Depths tier, warzone), buttons: visit island, tpa, message.
- Join and quit notices for friends (setting).

**Parties (`/party`):**
- Up to 5 players, leader invites. Party chat (`/pc`).
- Shared kill credit in Depths combat zones; mini-boss damage counts for the party.
- Party joins events and duels (2v2 later) together.
- Party ends when the leader leaves or after 30 min with nobody online.

**Guest book:**
- A book item or `/is guestbook`: visitors write one line (max 60 characters), filtered, once a day per island.
- Leader can delete lines; last 50 kept.
