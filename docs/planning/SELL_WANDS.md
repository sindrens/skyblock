## What exists in the game now

## The problem / what to decide
Which wand types, which features, and how they stay inside the +300% cap and the push limit (SELL_PRICES.md).

## Our proposal
| Wand | Rarity | Uses | Feature |
|---|---|---|---|
| Copper Wand | Uncommon | 100 | sells one chest |
| Ember Wand | Rare | 500 | sells a double chest + "Keep Forge materials" |
| Blaze Wand | Epic | 2,000 | +5% sell bonus (counts toward the cap) |
| Phoenix Wand | Legendary | 5,000 | sells every chest in a 5-block radius |
| Eclipse Wand | Mythic | unlimited for one season | +10% sell bonus, radius 8 |

- Works only on your own island, in chests you have the Containers permission for.
- Unique items: tradeable and auctionable (like books); uses shown in the lore.
- Same prices and push limit as `/sell`. No crafting, only from crates, quests, events and the Black Market.
