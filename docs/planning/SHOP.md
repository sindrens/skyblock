## What exists in the game now
- `/shop`: 8 categories (building blocks, wood, stone bricks, glass and colors, decoration, redstone, farming, utility) → a quick-buy grid. Left-click 1, right-click 64, Bedrock amount screen. Sell button inside.
- Everything is available from the start. No spawners or progression items yet in the categories list.
- Shop NPC in the Market Square.

## The problem / what to decide
The shop is a builder shop. It doesn't help progress, has no reason to come back, and all items are open on day 1.

## Our proposal
**Three tabs in one `/shop`** (also three NPCs in the Market Square):
1. **Builder:** the current 8 categories. Always open.
2. **Island:** seeds and saplings, spawners, generator parts, fuel cells, collectors. **Items unlock with island level or Depths tier** ("Unlocks at island level 10"), so the shop grows with you.
3. **Daily deals:** 6 items a day at -20%, the same for everyone, refresh at 00:00 Oslo.

**Rules:**
- Buy price always above the max sell price (712).
- Confirm only spawners and buys over $1M (R3-29).
- Spawners from the shop are bound to the buying island (R3-4).
- Full inventory → refuse before paying (as now), never drop.
- Search (718) covers all tabs.
- Every price and unlock in `shop.yml`.
