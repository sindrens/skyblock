## Colours
- ColorGens: rainbow only for the logo, section headers and the top rank; everything else flat: grey labels, white
  values, one meaning colour per thing (green money and buttons, magenta crates, cyan fishing, gold rare drops, red
  slots/errors). Chat: coloured prefix, white text, key noun coloured.
- EnchantedMC lore: one accent colour per menu (green farming, gold mining, cyan checkpoints, magenta levels), grey
  details, one bright click line. A red accent on a harmless menu reads as an error.
- Tooltip pattern everywhere: bold title, a dim caps category line, one sentence, values, then the action.

## Scoreboard
- ColorGens: title `COLORGENS | Season 2`; blocks EVENT and STATISTICS; each row starts with a thin coloured bar
  (red, yellow, green, cyan, magenta); small-caps labels; blank line between blocks; IP at the bottom; ~10 lines.
- Avoid: too many currencies (EnchantedMC), date lines, tiny unreadable fonts (MC-Complex).

## Tab list
- ColorGens: pixel logo header, grouped by rank with coloured rank plates, names in rank colour, ping bars; footer
  "You are playing Season 2 with 1048 players" + store line.
- MC-Complex: section titles with counts (`STAFF (2)`, `PLUS (120)`), footer `Global • Here • Ping • TPS`.

## Store / ranks
- ColorGens store (pack GUI): category tabs on top, 3x2 rank cards (banner, 3-8 perk bullets, one Buy button), badges
  on the card edge (MOST POPULAR, POPULAR, BEST VALUE, NEW), a cart-rewards bar ("Add $10.01 more" for a locked
  bonus). Avoid: multipliers for money, "OVERPOWERED".
- EnchantedMC credit store: dark panel, balance on top, 3x2 tiers with growing gem icons.
- Purchase broadcast: rule line, STORE label, buyer + rank plate, 3 perk bullets, `[ CLICK TO BUY ]`.

## Other
- Events: big title + framed chat block with a one-line effect; the scoreboard switches to the event timer.
- Rare drop: big title, one subtitle, a chat line with what to do.
- Holograms: coloured name, one grey benefit line, a mouse-glyph action line.
- Progress ladders with "You are here" (vote streak, pay limit, upgrades).

## Resource pack (what the big servers use)
Pixel font (titles, small caps), a bitmap logo (scoreboard title, tab header), rank plates as glyphs, inline icons in
holograms/chat, full custom GUI backgrounds (store, credit store) drawn with a title glyph.
