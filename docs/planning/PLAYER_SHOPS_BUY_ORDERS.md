# Player shops and buy orders

**Status:** needs owner input

## What you already decided

- **Idea 58 (buy orders):** yes. "I buy 64 iron for $X", others fill it.
- **Idea 24 / 25:** warps with categories (shops, farms, parkour) and sign warps: built.
- **550-563:** auction house rules (fee, limits, collection bin never expires).
- **Principle 2:** never buy from your own other profile.

## What exists in the game now
Auction house (`/ah`) with listings, search, categories and a collection bin. Island warps with a Shops category. Nothing for player shops or buy orders.

## The problem / what to decide
Both ideas are not in any plan yet. They must be dupe-safe (escrow), fit next to the AH, and not become a boosting path.

## Our proposal
**Buy orders = a new AH tab** (`/ah orders`):
- Make an order: item, amount, price per item. The money is held in escrow.
- Anyone fills it from their inventory; items go to the buyer's collection bin, money to the seller.
- Same fee as AH listings; max 5 open orders (more with ranks).
- Same-IP accounts and your own profiles can't fill your orders.

**Player shops = chest shops on islands:**
- Put a sign `[shop]` on a chest: item, amount, price. Buy or sell mode.
- Visitors right-click to buy; money goes to the owner (or island bank, a toggle).
- Stock lives in the chest; the sale is one atomic step (item and money together).
- Islands with shops show in the visit browser under "Shops".
- Max 10 shops per island (more with an upgrade).

**Order:** buy orders first (smaller, uses the AH), then chest shops.
