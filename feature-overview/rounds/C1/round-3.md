## Developer opinion on the $10k gate (honest)
- What it stops: a brand-new alt receiving a main's wealth. What it does not stop: items traded to an alt who sells them (counts as `sell`), the island-member exception (an alt invited to the island can send freely), and vote or quest farming if those reasons count.
- Alternatives: (a) remove it and rely on alerts, flags and the trail: least friction, but the first line of defence is gone; (b) "tutorial finished + 2 h played" on the account: easy to explain, but AFK farming defeats it; (c) keep the earned gate with a narrow list.
- Recommendation: (c). It is already built, so this is config only. It is a cheap speed bump that costs honest players nothing (real play passes $10k in minutes, to be confirmed against ECONOMY_BALANCE), and the real protection is the accepted watch flags and the trail (C1-R1-06). Do not add layers.
