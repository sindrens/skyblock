# Owner profile (how the owner thinks; the developer checks every proposal against this before asking)

Built from `docs/OWNER_PRINCIPLES.md` (194 earlier answers) and the review sessions of 31 Oct 2026 (C1, B17). **Draft: the owner corrects it once; after that it is the default for every card.** Change a line by saying "profile: change X".

## What the owner wants
1. **Fun, visible progress.** Every action should give a feeling of progress and satisfaction; progress must be visible while it happens (popups, bars, trails). Less is more: few, clear systems.
2. **No single meta.** Mining, forest, combat/dungeon and island production each give different rewards, so nobody grinds one thing all season. Progress comes as MANY small upgrades (enchant slots, mining speed, skins, perks), not a few big ones.
3. **Easy for 1,000 players ("brukervennlighet").** Simple names, short lores, clear menus, helpful errors, nothing needs a long command, Bedrock never needs typing. Clean, organised, consistent look (red phoenix, bold where it helps).
4. **No waiting, no busywork.** No cooldowns unless they stop real abuse; teleports instant outside combat; tutorial must never be annoying.
5. **Nothing is lost by accident.** Keep inventory everywhere, no fall damage; only the PvP loadout resets on death. Deleted things are staff-restorable.
6. **The server allows everything; what is not allowed is not possible.** Security and dupe-proofing are done in code, not by rules. The owner expects the developer to know this better and decide.
7. **Money buys quality of life and "some" advantage, never the core grind.** Credits (about 100 per 1 USD, bigger packs give more) can buy ranks, cosmetics, convenience, island perks (timed or per season), boosters inside caps. Never money, crystals, shards, keys, levels or XP directly. PvP power must come from play. The owner accepts the grey-zone Mojang risk but wants a warning on every such item.
8. **Players trade.** Currencies have different purposes and sources so players trade them; tradeable things need a trail and alerts, not blocks.
9. **Everything configurable and staff-friendly.** Numbers, texts, items in config/messages; staff tools with permissions; audit logs; tab completion everywhere.
10. **English only. Resource pack skins GUIs later; vanilla fallback always.** Only 1.21.5-era blocks and items in shops, crates and icons.
11. **Overhauls come after the plan.** Prices, rewards and /shop are redone once the whole server is planned (C15). Until then, numbers are placeholders.

## How the developer decides (default rules)
- **Developer decides, owner can veto:** security, dupes, performance, data safety, migrations, tests, Bedrock handling, config keys, permission nodes, audit and alerts, command layout, technical limits.
- **Owner decides (asked, with a recommended answer):** feel and difficulty, speed of progress, what is fun, what is paid and its price, look and naming, anything that changes a locked owner answer.
- **Never ask about:** things already in this profile, `OWNER_PRINCIPLES.md` or the ledger. Apply them.
- **Always flag:** Mojang-risk items, reversals of earlier answers, anything that adds a new system (principle: less is more).
- If a feature is getting too advanced, say so and offer the simpler version.
